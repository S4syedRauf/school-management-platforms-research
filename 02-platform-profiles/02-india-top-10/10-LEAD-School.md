### Overview

LEAD School is an integrated school operating and learning system from Mumbai-based Leadership Boulevard Pvt. Ltd. It combines curriculum, pedagogy, teacher training, classroom hardware, student/parent learning, and an academic ERP instead of selling only a back-office SIS. The current product identity is **LEAD Nucleus**, described by LEAD as its Academic ERP and as a hybrid-mode platform. The public workflow covers school-owner visibility, teacher delivery, parent/student progress, admissions, attendance, assessments, homework, reports, fees, and communication [5] [6].

LEAD was founded by Sumeet Mehta and Smita Deorah in 2012. Its first school was Shantiniketan English School, and the founders expanded from operating schools to a partner-school model: teacher-app work began in 2016, the integrated system was offered to partner schools by 2017, and the partnership model scaled nationally. The company raised $28M Series C in 2020, $30M Series D in 2021, and $100M Series E in January 2022 at a $1.1B valuation. In 2023 it completed the acquisition of Pearson India’s K-12 learning business, adding curriculum/content capability [3] [4] [14] [15].

As of November 2026, independent company data still classifies LEAD as a private company. Inc42 reports Leadership Boulevard Private Limited as founded in 2012, with FY25 revenue above ₹367.4 crore and last funding listed as debt financing in 2023; SetterVC records the last disclosed equity round as January 2022 and approximately $166.5M total disclosed funding, subject to its own data caveats [16] [17]. These are business-status indicators, not proof of a public offering or of a current valuation above the 2022 funding mark.

### Platform Classification Explained

**Browser/cloud delivery.** Nucleus is accessed at https://nucleus.leadschool.in/ through a mobile-number/OTP login. LEAD describes it as a cloud-based, remote-access ERP with role-based access and encrypted storage. The teacher service is also browser-accessible at https://teacherapp.leadschool.in/ [5] [7]. No public evidence identifies AWS, Azure, GCP, a data-residency region, a desktop executable, or a self-hosted/on-premise deployment.

**Hybrid academic operation.** “Hybrid” means LEAD’s school workflow can toggle online, offline, or both modes, not that customers receive an on-premise ERP. The teacher app’s Play listing says lesson plans, audiovisual content, assessments, and practice tests can be bundled on an SD card with the teacher tablet for low-connectivity/offline classroom use. Smart TVs, teacher tablets, activity kits, printed books, and digital content are part of the integrated package [5] [12].

**Native mobile apps.** The LEAD Group Student App is listed on both Google Play and Apple’s App Store for iPhone/iPad. It supports parent/student access to unit progress, attendance, assessment scores, homework, practice, and videos. LEAD Teacher App is an Android app tied to a compatible Lenovo teacher tablet, while the School Owner app is an Android app for school metrics, student/parent/teacher reports, announcements, and LEAD support [10] [11] [12] [13]. A separately verified iOS owner or teacher app was not found.

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

LEAD publicly describes online admission forms, collection of student information and admission fees, a school listing page, enquiry generation, a personalised LEAD CRM, follow-up, walk-in conversion, and paper reduction. The ENROL/admissions offering is broader than a simple Nucleus form and is marketed as an admissions and school-visibility service [18].

#### Student information and records

Nucleus maintains a central student and teacher database, class-wise student lists, academic records, attendance, grades, progress data, and parent/teacher-linked information. The ERP’s privacy policy explicitly identifies student and teacher databases, notices to parents, online fee collection, reports, and analytics as platform uses [19].

#### Attendance

Teachers record attendance and parents can view daily attendance. School owners receive attendance and teacher-performance tracking, while the student app exposes attendance history. Public evidence does not establish biometric, RFID, facial-recognition, or gate-attendance support.

#### Timetable and master scheduling

LEAD describes daily timetable scheduling, school-mode configuration, subject/topic additions, and teacher allocation. The current public pages do not document a district-style constraint solver, room scheduling, substitute/cover module, or multi-campus master-scheduling engine [5] [6].

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Teachers can access assessments, prior submissions, grades, practice tests, quizzes, and remedial analysis. The current homepage markets **Jasper**, an AI Assessment Builder that lets teachers create assessments by topic and rigour. LEAD’s NEP/NCF material emphasizes competency-based, formative, practical, and 360-degree assessment; public evidence does not show a separate board-exam administration suite [1] [20] [21].

#### Report cards and transcripts

The Academic ERP and Student App generate progress/performance reports, and LEAD says its NCF-aligned system generates holistic report cards covering academic performance, skills, and overall growth. A conventional official-transcript module, transcript exchange, GPA engine, or alumni transcript service is not publicly documented [20].

#### Learning management and homework

The Student App provides live and recorded classes, videos, homework and assignments, practice tests, gamified quizzes, downloadable worksheets, and “Ask Doubts.” Teachers receive lesson plans, audiovisual materials, worksheets, syllabus plans, and assessments. School@Home connects home learning with classroom learning [5] [10] [12].

#### Communication

The verified channels are in-app alerts, school/teacher updates, notices, announcements, parent-teacher interaction, and app-based doubt resolution. The privacy and master-terms documents allow electronic communications and third-party payment processors. A dedicated named WhatsApp connector, SMS gateway, bulk-email engine, or pricing for messages is not publicly documented; therefore the guide’s implied SMS/Email/App bundle is only partly confirmed [19] [22].

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

The Student App serves parents and students; the Teacher App and teacher web login serve classroom staff; Nucleus serves school owners, principals, and academic coordinators; and the Owner App provides school-level metrics and contacts. Features vary by the school’s selected books/offering, so every partner may not see the same app modules [10] [13] [19].

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

LEAD confirms fees management, online collection, payment records, collection from parents on behalf of a customer, and use of third-party payment processors/gateways. No current official page names Razorpay, Paytm, UPI, GST invoicing, Tally, QuickBooks, general ledger, reconciliation, payroll accounting, or statutory accounting reports. Treat those as unverified rather than native capabilities [6] [22].

#### HR and staff

Staff-management data, teacher attendance/performance tracking, teacher onboarding, teacher training, LEAD Academy courses, certification, workshops, and support are evidenced. Payroll, leave approval, recruitment, appraisal workflows, and employee self-service are not publicly documented as Nucleus modules [1] [7].

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

School owners can view key metrics and drill into student, parent, and teacher reports. Nucleus produces progress reports, trackers, class analysis, and remedial analysis; LEAD positions these data-driven insights as part of academic planning and learning-outcome monitoring [5] [13].

#### AI features

Jasper is a production-facing AI Assessment Builder for customized assessment creation, and LEAD now describes its broader learning solutions as AI-based. Public sources do not substantiate a general-purpose conversational assistant, predictive early-warning model, natural-language BI, or no-code workflow builder.

#### Government reporting and compliance

LEAD publicly aligns curriculum and assessment with NEP 2020 and NCF 2023, describes 5+3+3+4, competency-based and holistic assessment, and says its systems adapt to CBSE, ICSE, and JEE tracks [2] [21]. No public evidence was found for UDISE+ submission, APAAR ID, state-census exports, CBSE SARAS integration, NAAC/NBA/NIRF/AISHE reporting, or higher-education administration.

#### Mobile apps, integrations and APIs

Verified integrations are the Google for Education ecosystem claim, Google authentication/YouTube connection in Nucleus privacy documentation, app-store distribution, and generic third-party payment processing [1] [19]. SaaSworthy records API support as “NA,” and no public API reference, OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever/ClassLink, Microsoft 365, or marketplace directory was found [8].

#### Modules not publicly substantiated

The guide lists library, transport, inventory, hostel, health, behavior, special education, alumni, and fundraising. The current official LEAD pages and legal documents reviewed do not provide enough primary evidence to count these as native Nucleus modules. There is also no public evidence of a dedicated cafeteria, clinic, boarding, asset-depreciation, safeguarding, or fundraising module.

#### Security and privacy

Nucleus uses OTP login and states role-based access, encrypted storage, consent, data-principal access/correction/erasure/withdrawal rights, deletion/anonymisation practices, processor agreements, breach notification obligations, and a grievance officer. The Nucleus privacy policy expressly references India’s DPDP Act 2023 and was last updated June 2, 2026. No SOC 2, ISO 27001, FERPA, COPPA, GDPR certification, hosting region, penetration-test report, or public incident disclosure was found [19].

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Integrated system rather than ERP-only:** Curriculum, printed books, classroom hardware, teacher development, apps, assessments, and Nucleus are sold as one school transformation proposition [1] [6].
- **Hybrid-mode continuity:** Online, offline, and combined school modes are supported, including offline teacher-tablet content for low-connectivity classrooms [5] [12].
- **Jasper AI Assessment Builder:** Teachers can customize assessment topic and rigour, with remedial analysis positioned alongside the assessment workflow [1] [12].
- **NEP/NCF-oriented holistic reporting:** LEAD connects academic results with skill development and overall growth rather than only marks [20] [21].
- **Affordable-private-school specialization:** The system targets schools serving moderate-fee families in smaller towns and attempts to lower non-learning operating costs [10].
- **Owner visibility:** School-owner dashboards and a dedicated owner app expose school, parent, student, and teacher metrics remotely [6] [13].

### Pricing Details

LEAD publishes no rate card, per-student INR price, USD price, tier names, setup fee, implementation fee, or free-trial terms for Nucleus or the bundled LEAD Learning System. Official pages use “Enquire Now,” “Book a demo,” or partner onboarding rather than self-serve checkout. The master terms confirm that each customer agreement sets the invoice value for books, onboarding, platform licensing, ancillary products/services, and taxes; the invoice is generally non-refundable [22].

SaaSworthy independently records “custom plan,” “quotation based,” and no free trial, with its pricing information last updated August 29, 2024; it does not supply a reliable INR or USD range [8]. Any guide or directory estimate should therefore be treated as unverified. Pricing is best described as a negotiated annual school/partner agreement, likely bundling software with curriculum, books, training, apps, and hardware rather than a standalone ERP subscription.

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

The public evidence supports Google for Education compatibility and Google authentication/YouTube connection. The master terms permit third-party payment processors/gateways, but name no gateway. No verified public API, developer portal, integration marketplace, OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, ClassLink, Microsoft 365, Tally, GST, UPI, Razorpay, or Paytm integration was found. Interoperability is therefore basic and partner-agreement dependent, not an open standards-led ecosystem [1] [8] [19] [22].

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

The June 2, 2026 Nucleus policy says Leadership Boulevard processes data for school customers, collects student/teacher records and location when permitted, requires verifiable parental/guardian consent for minors, provides access/correction/erasure/withdrawal/grievance/nomination rights, uses data-processing agreements, retains data only as necessary, and will notify the Data Protection Board and affected people for qualifying breaches. It expressly claims compliance with the DPDP Act 2023 and rules [19].

LEAD’s general website policy, last reviewed January 19, 2024, is weaker and more generic: it promises reasonable safeguards but no absolute security and allows retention for analytics, records, fraud prevention, and legal reasons [23]. Google Play reports encryption in transit and deletion requests for the Student App, but reports that the Owner App’s data was not encrypted and could not be deleted in its store disclosure at the time viewed [10] [13]. No SOC 2/ISO certificate, FERPA/COPPA/GDPR commitment, data-hosting region, or public breach history was located.

### Strengths

- Strong fit for affordable private Indian schools that need curriculum, teacher enablement, hardware, parent engagement, and academic administration together.
- Large claimed reach: 9,000+ schools and 4.1M+ students on the current homepage, with a 2022 unicorn funding history and 2023 Pearson K-12 acquisition.
- Clear hybrid/offline story for low-connectivity classrooms, with teacher tablets and downloadable or bundled content.
- Native student/parent, teacher, and owner touchpoints, plus useful attendance, homework, assessment, progress, and owner-dashboard workflows.
- NEP/NCF, CBSE/ICSE, holistic-progress, competency, and multilingual-policy positioning is more India-specific than a generic global SIS.

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- No public pricing, public API documentation, standards certification, or detailed module catalog makes procurement and interoperability diligence difficult.
- The current website presents conflicting scale figures: 9,000+/4.1M/65,000 on the homepage versus 8,500+/3.8M on several product and history pages.
- Scope is tightly integrated and partner-oriented; schools seeking a modular standalone SIS, higher-education ERP, or on-premise deployment may find it limiting.
- Google Play reviews include a November 2026 complaint that the Student App was slow, sometimes failed to open, and froze; a teacher-app review reported a service issue unresolved for a week. Apple reviews mention book-activation/access-code problems and lack of iPad horizontal mode [10] [11] [12].
- The Owner App’s store disclosure reported no encryption and no deletion option, while the newer Nucleus privacy policy makes stronger DPDP claims; buyers should reconcile app-level disclosures contractually.

### Key Milestones

- **2012:** Sumeet Mehta and Smita Deorah co-founded LEAD School in Mumbai; the first-school story centers on Shantiniketan English School.
- **2013:** LEAD’s newer corporate history describes the founders as starting with one school in this year; this is the first-school operating date, not the legal/company founding date.
- **2014:** ELGA English Language and General Awareness and the initial full curriculum were launched.
- **2016:** LEAD created its first teacher application and transitioned teachers to tablets; the partner-school learning-system expansion followed.
- **2017:** LEAD began presenting an integrated system for teachers, parents, students, and school owners.
- **2020:** Series C funding of $28M led by WestBridge Capital and Elevar Equity; nationwide digital/hybrid learning expanded.
- **2021:** Series D funding of $30M; LEAD’s integrated system was reported at 8,500+ schools and 1.2M students in the milestone history.
- **January 2022:** Series E raised $100M at a $1.1B valuation, making LEAD the first school-edtech unicorn of 2022 [14] [15].
- **January–March 2023:** LEAD raised reported debt financing and completed the acquisition of Pearson India’s K-12 learning business [16] [24].
- **2024:** LEAD’s corporate history says it expanded its product portfolio with tech-first and AI-enabled solutions.
- **2026:** Nucleus privacy policy updated June 2; Student App version 7.2.5 was updated in October; current website claims 9,000+ schools and 4.1M+ students [10] [19].
