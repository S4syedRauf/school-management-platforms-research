### Overview

MasterSoft ERP is an Indian education ERP/SIS suite from MasterSoft ERP Solutions. It combines admissions, academics, examinations, fees, scholarships, student services, alumni, LMS, OBE, HR/payroll, finance, procurement, hostel, library and administration for schools, colleges and universities. The older catalog names RF-CAMPUS, CCMS, CSMS and online admissions as its principal product lines [1] [4] [5].

Founder Sham Somani previously led Nagpur University’s computer center and built examination, accounts, payroll and administration software there. Gartner identifies the private company as founded in 1995, but no source gives a separate ERP first-launch year. No public acquisition or funding announcement was found [1] [11].

### Platform Classification Explained

- **Cloud/web SaaS:** Official pages describe centralized cloud delivery, anywhere access, automatic backups and no customer hardware; Business-Software independently classifies it as SaaS [4] [5].
- **Hosting:** The FAQ cites Government of India Meghraj-policy cloud hosting. The October 2026 policy names Microsoft Azure, with institution-selected regions and regional backup/disaster recovery [6] [14].
- **Browser access:** The registered-user student portal is a live web application owned by MasterSoft ERP Solutions [15].
- **Desktop/on-premise:** No current public evidence of a downloadable desktop client or customer-managed on-premise option. Gartner’s “hybrid experience” describes product access, not a documented deployment choice [4] [11].
- **Mobile:** Student Diary Cloud is linked to Google Play; the Faculty Management App page claims Google Play and iOS availability. Android/iOS store-level ratings for the official apps were not exposed [12] [13].

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

No-code/low-code forms with validation, conditional fields and uploads; programs, intakes, quotas and eligibility; entrance exams, interviews, LORs, approvals, offers, waitlists, SMS/email/WhatsApp reminders, funnel dashboards and direct handoff to enrollment, fees and academics [17].

#### Student information and records

Central profiles for demographic, academic, fee, scholarship, hostel, library and lifecycle data; role-based access, documents, exports, dashboards and reports for administrators, faculty, students and parents. Health records are mentioned, but not a detailed clinical module [6] [18].

#### Attendance

RFID, biometric, QR, OTP, mobile and geolocation attendance; course/session mapping, absentee alerts, parent visibility, hostel attendance and reports. HR attendance links to leave and payroll [6] [19].

#### Timetable and master scheduling

Conflict-managed class and exam timetables; faculty, room, course and session mapping; weekly schedules; curriculum execution; absence replacement; multi-campus scheduling; majors, minors, electives, prerequisites, credit-load checks and CBCS/NEP-aligned selection [12] [19].

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Offline/online exams; registration, permits, question banks, MCQ/descriptive/quiz/formative patterns, encryption, timers, randomization, webcam/proctoring, halls, evaluator workflows, bulk marks, absolute/relative/hybrid grades, SGPA/CGPA, grace/carry/backlog/classification, scrutiny, re-evaluation and grade-improvement petitions [20] [21].

#### Report cards and transcripts

Automated report cards, grade cards, merit lists, gazettes, transcripts, certificates and hall tickets; Word/Excel/PDF export; QR-verified digitally signed credentials; SMS/email delivery of results [20] [21].

#### Learning management and homework

Course/topic organization; PDF, presentation, audio and video content; syllabus, teaching plans, assignments, submissions, grading, rubrics, quizzes, polls, surveys, forums, internal chat, webinars/live classes and recordings; gradebook and learning-gap analytics. Microsoft Teams is mentioned for online lectures [5] [22].

#### Communication

SMS/email alerts for attendance, homework, events, exams, dues and results; push notifications; text, picture, PDF and voice messages; teacher-parent/student communication; meeting alerts. Admissions explicitly claims WhatsApp automation; named UPI/Razorpay/Paytm connectors were not verified [12] [17].

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

Role-based portals provide schedules, attendance, assignments, exams, fees, scholarships, documents, requests and progress. Parent tools cover attendance, fees, homework, events and performance; faculty tools cover attendance, schedules, notifications, leave and payroll [6] [12] [18].

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Course/campus fee structures; tuition, annual, exam, hostel, library, sports and miscellaneous fees; installments/EMIs, concessions, scholarships, sponsors, refunds, write-offs, dues, collection centers, invoices, receipts, reconciliation and collection reports. Cash, payment centers, gateways and wallets are described, but not named UPI/Razorpay/Paytm. Taxation appears in finance copy, without a specific GST-invoicing claim [5] [17] [23].

#### HR, staff and payroll

Employee records, onboarding-to-retirement, service books, biometric attendance, leave/OD/comp-off, salary revisions, arrears, supplementary bills, payslips, tax, Form 16, 24Q, PF, PT, statutory registers, self-service and payroll reports [1].

#### Transport

Vehicle, registration, insurance, permits, tax, logbook, routes, passengers, fuel, maintenance, trips, rented vehicles, bookings and alerts; school bus live tracking and parent notifications [6] [24].

#### Library

Procurement, accessioning, cataloging, classification, disposal, barcode/RFID circulation, reservations, renewals, kiosks/mobile self-check, OPAC/M-OPAC, fines, due reminders, visitor records, SMS/email, audit logs, AACR2/MARC21 and multi-campus reporting [25].

#### Hostel and boarding

Online admissions, dormitory/room/wing/floor definitions, allocation, occupancy, fees, defaulter reminders, tracking, attendance, leave/outdoor passes, parent updates, gate passes, visitor management, mess bills, canteen, refunds, certificates and end-semester settlement [26].

#### Inventory and assets

Procurement, suppliers, contracts, purchase book, internal invoices, taxation, stores, inventory, asset register, capitalization, disposal, depreciation, maintenance and accounting integration [23].

#### Health and medical

Health records and a university Health Center/Healthcare Management module are listed, but public pages do not describe appointments, prescriptions or clinical workflows [12] [18].

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

Student Services records offenses, disciplinary actions, appeals, academic holds and escalation; the portal supports grievances. Detailed K-12 pastoral-care controls are not documented [27].

#### Special education and safeguarding

No dedicated IEP, special-education or child-safeguarding module was publicly documented. The free offer for specially-abled schools is an eligibility criterion, not feature evidence [28].

#### Alumni and fundraising

Alumni onboarding, profiles, directory, news/events, jobs, internships, mentoring, referrals, chapters, donations, pledges, campaigns, receipts, donor dashboards, elections, e-voting and audit trails [29].

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

BI dashboards and exports for enrollment, attendance, academics, fees, payroll, finance, library, accreditation and engagement; audit logs, gap analysis, rubrics, scoring, dropout prediction and at-risk-student identification are claimed [1] [8] [11].

#### AI features

MS AI Copilot, AI Agents, universal search, adaptive advising, dropout/at-risk analytics, AI fee configuration, scholarship/document checks, payroll validation and query management are vendor claims; model providers and tier availability are not public [1] [6] [17] [30].

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Accreditation Data Management supports NAAC/NBA data, NIRF reports, multi-year evidence, proof uploads, Excel/PDF/link outputs, dashboards, rubrics and audit trails. School ERP supports CBSE, ICSE, State Board, IB and Cambridge and produces grade lists/mark sheets. AISHE workflows, UDISE+, APAAR ID and a NEP 2020 holistic progress-card template were not publicly verified [6] [8].

#### Mobile apps

Student Diary Cloud and Faculty Management App provide student/parent/faculty access to timetable, attendance, assignments, exams, fees, scholarships, library, grievances, notifications, leave, payslips, LMS and bus tracking. Android is directly linked; iOS is claimed for the faculty app [12] [13].

#### Integrations and APIs

Internal links cover SIS, admissions, finance, HR/payroll, LMS, examinations, OBE, accreditation, library, hostel and mobile; named technologies include payment gateways, net banking, RFID, biometrics, QR/OTP and Microsoft Teams. APIs are mentioned in policy materials, but no public API reference, marketplace, OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, ClassLink or Tally documentation was found [5] [14].

#### Security and privacy

Role-based access, OTP/password controls, HTTPS/TLS, encryption-at-rest capabilities, MFA where applicable, Azure privileged access, WAF/firewalls, backups, restore testing, audit logs, masking/pseudonymization and time-limited support access are documented. Institutions can export their data [14] [17].

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Accreditation Data Management:** dated evidence, proof attachments and NAAC/NBA/NIRF-ready reports are tailored to Indian higher education [8].
- **OBE/CBCS/NEP engine:** CO/PO/PSO/PEO mapping, Bloom’s Taxonomy, attainment and credit validation connect compliance with daily academics [18] [29].
- **Exam integrity:** configurable grading, proctoring signals, audit trails and QR-verified credentials support examination offices [19].
- **Agentic AI:** Copilot, workflow agents, universal search and adaptive advising go beyond conventional MIS reporting, but independent production evidence is limited [1].
- **Multi-campus and Indian operations:** affiliated-college data sharing, Meghraj-policy language, statutory payroll and RFID/biometric attendance are locally distinctive [5] [8] [18].

### Pricing Details

There is no public pricing page, plan table or implementation fee. The official FAQ gives a general Indian school-ERP range of ₹0–₹250 per student per year, not a MasterSoft quote; no USD price is listed [6]. Business-Software and TechJockey say contact vendor/price on request, with quotes varying by personalization, features, users and deployment [4] [9]. The M-Initiative offers free school software only to qualifying government girls’ schools, specially-abled schools, non-profit NGO schools and Sainik schools below 500 students [27]. Prices checked 24 November 2026; no reliable MasterSoft-specific third-party estimate was found.

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Public evidence is strongest for internal ERP links: admissions to enrollment/fees/academics; curriculum to registration, exams, LMS, attendance, fees and degree audit; accreditation to SIS/ERP; finance to fees, hostel, library and HR/payroll; and library to student/finance records [8] [18] [22] [24]. Payment gateways, net banking, RFID, biometrics, QR/OTP and Microsoft Teams are named. No public API reference, marketplace, OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, ClassLink, Google Workspace, Tally, UPI, Razorpay or Paytm certification was found.

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

The October 2026 policy treats the institution as controller and MasterSoft as processor, prohibits advertising/sale/data brokerage/general-purpose-AI training on institutional data, and describes Azure-region hosting, backups, TLS, encryption, MFA where applicable and incident response. The DPA adds just-in-time production access, approval records, 12-month support-access logs, masking/pseudonymization and regional controls [14] [15]. The material supports GDPR-oriented readiness and references ISO/IEC 27001:2022 controls, but does not prove ISO certification or SOC 2. FERPA/COPPA are not claimed; DPDP Act 2023 is not explicitly named. No public breach disclosure was found.

### Strengths

- Broad school and university coverage, with unusually deep Indian examination, accreditation, OBE, CBCS, affiliation and multi-campus functionality.
- Cloud delivery, mobile access, configurable workflows, role-based access and implementation/support options.
- Named institutional customers and substantial vendor-reported adoption, with positive Gartner and TechJockey ratings [7] [9] [11].

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Quote-only pricing and unclear plan boundaries make comparison difficult [4] [9].
- G2’s 3.4/5 sample reports repeated refreshes, app freezing, attendance glitches, weaker mobile compatibility, peak-time performance concerns and a need for a more advanced mobile app; it also says the LMS/CRM trail Blackboard/Canvas and Salesforce/HubSpot [10].
- Gartner’s public summary includes “cross integration challenges” and inability to cross-integrate modules despite praise for customization and analytics [11].
- Public documentation is marketing-led: no API reference, standards certifications, named payment connectors, public SLA or audited adoption dataset was found.
- Adoption claims conflict: 1,100+ institutions/1.5M active students versus 2,200+ implementations/10M+ users, so definitions should be requested [1] [7].
