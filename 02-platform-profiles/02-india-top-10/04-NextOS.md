### Overview

NextOS is Next Education’s cloud-first school operating system, combining NextERP, NextLMS, NextAssessment, digital content, communication, and mobile access for administrators, teachers, students, parents, transport attendants, and security guards. [1] [3]

The company was founded in Hyderabad in 2007 and remains presented as Next Education India Pvt. Ltd.; the public company pages identify founders and operating offices but do not disclose an acquisition history. Older Next Education material calls the suite NextLearningPlatform/NextERP, while the current product site calls it NextOS. The current rebranding therefore should not be confused with a separately verified 2007 NextOS product launch. [2] [14]

Its India-specific positioning combines board-aligned content with configurable report cards, holistic progress cards, DLT messaging, Indian payment gateways, Tally, and optional RFID/NFC/biometric/GPS devices. UDISE+, APAAR, GST invoicing, and higher-education reporting are not documented on the reviewed official pages and should be validated in procurement.

### Platform Classification Explained

**Browser and cloud:** NextOS describes the ERP and K-12 Suite as cloud-based/cloud-first SaaS, accessible from anywhere and on any device. No cloud provider, tenancy architecture, uptime SLA, or on-premise installer is named publicly. [1] [3]

**Desktop and on-premise:** No Windows/macOS desktop client, self-hosted package, or on-premise option was found; Windows access is browser-based.

**Native mobile:** “NextOS (NextLearningPlatform)” is listed for Android and iOS. The unified app covers administrator, teacher, parent, student, vehicle-attender, and security-guard workflows, and the K-12 FAQ says it can be white-labelled with a school’s name, logo, and colours. [3] [5] [6]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions, enrollment, and CRM

Admissions supports online/walk-in enquiries, OTP verification, configurable multi-tier workflows, online forms, document collection, bulk uploads, fee collection, campaign capture, lead tracking, counsellor follow-up, and multi-branch oversight. Built-in SEO/campaign tools capture leads; no separate CRM connector is established publicly. [3] [14]

#### Student information and records

The SIS provides a 360-degree view of personal, academic, fee, examination, document, wellness, attendance, and performance information, plus searchable records, sibling mapping, document storage, NOCs, certificates, ID cards, and provisional promotion. [3] [14]

#### Attendance and leave

Schools can mark daily and period-wise attendance, use teacher mobile roll-call, auto-calculate present/absent/half-day states, alert parents of absences, and generate attendance reports. Hardware and identity options include RFID, NFC, biometrics, face recognition, wireless gates, and mobile geo-facial recognition. Staff attendance can use facial recognition plus geo-fencing without external biometric hardware. Leave supports configurable leave types, holiday restrictions, gender-specific maternity/paternity rules, staff/parent applications, admin entry of offline applications, leave encashment, and mobile approvals. [3] [14]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

NextOS supports manual and automated timetable creation, teacher competency mapping, permanent and ad-hoc substitutions, proxy management, extra classes and holiday scheduling, and attendance/leave-linked scheduling. It advertises aSc Timetable integration and automated substitution notifications. [3] [14]

#### Gradebook, assessment, and examinations

NextAssessment includes a question bank publicly described as 650,000+ questions, organised by difficulty, Bloom’s Taxonomy, and Webb’s depth, with multiple languages and formats including MCQ, subjective, comprehension, annotation, and activity-based questions. Teachers can generate or upload tests for online, offline, or hybrid delivery; restrict author access; shuffle questions; permit controlled retries/calculators; upload handwritten answers; auto-mark MCQs; annotate subjective responses; and sync marks, remarks, and grades into the ERP gradebook. Adaptive assessment uses Item Response Theory and Knowledge Space Theory claims to vary difficulty and produce concept, time, effort, and outcome analytics. [10] [14]

#### Report cards, transcripts, and holistic progress

Examination workflows support configurable terms, subjects, activities, rubrics, grading systems, weighted scores, supplementary exams, grace marks, hall tickets, weekly/monthly/annual progress reports, year-wise performance, digital publication, and more than 100 report-card designs claimed in the brochure. Holistic Progress Cards (HPCs) are described as publishable in line with board guidelines. The site has a separate CBSE/ICSE Result Analyzer for school-uploaded CBSE gazette text or ICSE/ISC tabulation PDFs; it applies a Best-5 calculation and processes the file in the browser. [3] [11] [14]

#### Learning management and homework

NextLMS offers course and lesson planning, ready-made or customised plans, learning objectives, resource uploads, classwork/homework assignment, attachment and deadline management, online submission/evaluation, feedback, “Turned In” and “Returned” states, parent alerts, self-paced learning, moderated Q&A forums, activity feeds, and real-time performance views. Live lectures can use Zoom, Google Meet, or Microsoft Teams, with recorded sessions available for later access. [3] [4] [14]

#### Digital content and learning tools

TeachNext content is presented as board-aligned digital material covering CBSE, ICSE, IGCSE, Army Board, and 29 state boards, with multiple-language content claims. The LMS page lists 9,000+ hours, 25,000+ audiovisual modules, simulations, e-books, YouTube/Khan Academy links, SCORM and LTI support, an organism encyclopedia, periodic table, 2D graph plotter, log table, abacus, drawing/painting tools, and other subject tools. [3] [4]

#### Communication and engagement

Communication includes event-based SMS, email, push/in-app notifications, WhatsApp, in-app chat, group discussions, forums, document sharing, surveys, observation forms, templates, and a School Feed for photos, videos, voice notes, announcements, homework, lectures, and resources. DLT-enabled SMS is explicitly marketed. WhatsApp is publicly listed; Paytm is not. [1] [3] [14]

#### Parent, student, teacher, and administrator portals

The unified app and role-based web access give parents fee balances, payments, attendance, homework, reports, leave requests, messages, teacher chat, feeds, and bus tracking. Students receive schedules, resources, assignments, exams, answer sheets, reports, attendance, calendars, and quizzes. Teachers manage lessons, lectures, assignments, exams, grading, and parent communication; principals/admins get fee and operational dashboards. [5] [6]

#### Fees, billing, payments, and accounting

Fee management includes custom fee heads, concessions, scholarships, fines, sibling and staff-ward concessions, smart-pay rules, walk-in and online payments, cheque tracking, security deposits and refunds, collection locking, paid/due certificates, 45+ ready reports, and customised reporting. The Student Wallet lets parents pre-fund cashless fees, event charges, activities, inventory purchases, or pocket-money-like school expenses. Tally 4.1/Tally Prime integration is advertised. Gateways named by the official site include Razorpay, PayU, Atom, GrayQuest, HDFC CollectNow, Smart HDFC, eNACH, GQ/Zenda, and ICICI Eazypay; no public evidence was found for Paytm or a GST-invoice module. [1] [3] [14]

#### HR, staff, payroll, and leave

Staff records, hierarchy, competency mapping, staff ID cards, HRMS access, allowances/deductions, PF/ESIC formula logic, attendance/leave integration, salary locking, increments/decrements, loans/advances, payslips, full-and-final settlement, and bank-ready payroll reports are described. The brochure references 7th Pay Commission alignment. [14]

#### Transport

Transport covers routes, stops, trips, vehicle/fleet records, student allocation, driver/helper records, attendance and performance history, maintenance and fuel analysis, route-wise fees, receipts, live GPS, parent arrival/delay alerts, and targeted disruption messages. NFC cards tapped on a vehicle-attender phone can record boarding; mobile GPS is marketed as an alternative to dedicated GPS hardware. [3] [9] [14]

#### Library

The official reasons page and brochure describe barcode scanning, quick issue/return, ISBN auto-fetch, online browsing, location/category tagging including DDC, reservations, due reminders, and configurable membership policies. [14] [15]

#### Hostel and boarding

“Hostel Management” appears in the official feature navigation, but the reviewed public pages do not expose a detailed hostel workflow or sub-feature list. Room allocation, wardens, mess, and boarding reports should therefore be treated as unverified until shown in a demo. [1]

#### Inventory and assets

Inventory supports multi-store and supplier management, purchase workflows from requirement to payment, inward/outward and tagged items, stock transfers and adjustments, item issuance, school kits, asset/service management, location-based transfers, sales/returns, and audit reports. The official pages connect inventory transactions with fees and Tally. [14] [15]

#### Health, wellness, behavior, and discipline

The student profile and brochure describe medical/wellness records, health status, incidents, category-based discipline feedback and rewards, and discipline tracking. Public material does not establish a separate clinical, immunisation, safeguarding, or case-management subsystem. [14] [15]

#### Front office, visitors, projects, and tickets

Front Office provides OTP-verified visitor entry/exit, escort authentication, scheduled or on-the-spot visits, printed badges, event controls, security-guard mobile access, and audit history. Project and ticket tools track tasks, complaints, conversations, status, and resolution. [3] [14]

#### Analytics and reporting

Dashboards cover fees, attendance, examinations, student trends, class/subject performance, concept and learning-objective performance, discipline, and transport. NextLMS Analytics and Principal Reports are described as giving micro-level and school-wide views, with remedial-action planning and exports to Excel/PDF. Public evidence supports descriptive and diagnostic analytics; a separately documented predictive-risk engine was not found. [3] [4] [14]

#### AI features

AI-related capabilities include AI-proctored examinations, adaptive assessments, automated question-paper generation, mobile geo-facial staff attendance, and AI-powered doubt/learning assistance claims. A general-purpose production AI assistant, named predictive model, and public AI governance documentation were not found. [1] [10]

#### Mobile apps

The named app, NextOS (NextLearningPlatform), is available on Android and iOS. It supports multiple accounts/devices, automated alerts, fees, messages, homework, learning resources, exams, report cards, attendance, bus tracking, and role-specific workflows. White-labeling is offered for the K-12 Suite. [3] [5] [6]

#### Integrations and APIs

Documented integrations include Tally 4.1/Tally Prime, Razorpay, PayU, Atom, HDFC and ICICI payment services, GrayQuest, Zenda, eNACH, Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, YouTube, Khan Academy, aSc Timetable, RFID/biometric/face-recognition devices, GPS, SCORM, LTI, H5P, and ClassLink. The independent listing says no API is offered; no public developer portal, API reference, OneRoster/Ed-Fi/SIF certification, or app marketplace was found. [1] [3] [7]

#### Government reporting, standards, and India-specific compliance

Public evidence supports CBSE/ICSE result analysis, 29 state boards, NEP-aligned content, and holistic progress-card workflows. It does not verify UDISE+ export/API, APAAR ID, GST-compliant invoicing, Paytm, NAAC/NBA/NIRF/AISHE reporting, or formal DPDP readiness. DLT messaging and Indian payment/Tally workflows are not statutory-reporting certification. [3] [4] [11] [14]

#### Security and privacy

Role-based access, encryption, automatic backups, secure cloud storage, and 256-bit encryption are marketed; detailed evidence is assessed below. [12] [13] [15]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Integrated ERP–LMS–assessment–content stack:** administrative records, learning workflows, examinations, and board-aligned content share one product family. [3] [4]
- **AI-proctored and adaptive assessment:** online, offline, and hybrid exams combine proctoring signals, adaptive difficulty, analytics, and gradebook sync. [10]
- **Geo-facial staff attendance:** mobile face verification plus geo-fencing can reduce dependence on separate biometric terminals. [3]
- **Student Wallet and school eStore:** parents can fund a traceable wallet and use a no-code store for uniforms, books, or school purchases. [1] [3]
- **NFC transport attendance:** vehicle-attender phones can tap student NFC IDs while GPS and parent alerts cover the journey. [3] [14]
- **White-labelled role-based app and website:** schools can publish branded Android/iOS and website experiences. [3]
- **Large India-oriented content and question bank:** the vendor claims 29 state-board mappings, 650,000+ questions, and extensive audiovisual resources. [4] [10]

### Pricing Details

There is no public rate-card or tier table. The official School ERP FAQ gives **₹7–₹155 per student per month** and **₹60,000–₹2,00,000+ per year** for next-generation packages; the LMS page gives **₹20–₹50 per student per month**. These are indicative, quote-dependent ranges; no USD price is listed. [3] [4]

The sales model is modular: a school can start with a single module and add others. The FAQ says proposals disclose setup, training, and support commitments and claims no hidden/setup charges; the LMS page says onboarding, training, and support are included at specified intervals. No free self-serve trial, implementation SLA, or separately priced premium tier is published. [3] [4]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Documented connectors include Tally, Indian payment gateways, Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, aSc Timetable, GPS/RFID/biometric systems, SCORM, LTI, H5P, ClassLink, YouTube, and Khan Academy. [1] [3] No public API reference, developer portal, marketplace, OneRoster, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, or certification statement was found; SoftwareFinder answers “No” to an API question. [7]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

NextOS claims cloud encryption, role-based access, backups, secure servers, and 256-bit encryption. The privacy policy discusses gateway-processed financial details, optional face data, India-based storage language, overseas service-provider transfers, access/correction, and grievance contacts. [12] [13] It is not a DPDP Act statement; DPDP readiness, deletion/retention tools, processor contracts, certifications, penetration tests, named cloud provider, and incident history require due diligence.

Google Play says the app may collect location and personal information, encrypts data in transit, and cannot delete data, while Apple displays “Data Not Collected.” These store disclosures should be reconciled with enterprise data-lifecycle terms. No public breach or security incident was identified.

### Strengths

- Broad India-focused coverage across ERP, LMS, assessment, content, mobile, fees, transport, and communications.
- Strong board and curriculum positioning, including CBSE, ICSE, IGCSE, Army Board, and 29 state boards.
- Useful local workflows: DLT SMS, Indian gateways, Tally, UPI-enabled gateway claims, concessions, scholarships, sibling rules, and wallet payments.
- Mature help centre with hundreds of topic articles across admission, fee, examination, attendance, communication, LMS, transport, library, payroll, and student areas. [16]
- Native iOS/Android access and a high-volume Google Play presence, with 4.5/5 ratings on both major stores as viewed on November 24, 2026. [5] [6]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Pricing is quote-based and the official ranges are broad; there is no published module-by-module rate card, SLA, or USD price.
- Public product language is stronger than public technical evidence for APIs, predictive analytics, statutory reporting, and formal security certifications.
- Independent SoftwareFinder feedback is mixed: the three-review average is 2.7/5; complaints cite lengthy setup/training, weak reporting, few integration options, support gaps, and high cost. [7] [8]
- Google Play reviews include reports of repeated key-expiry problems, frozen video/crackling audio, slow opening, and missing homework; Apple reviews mention false outstanding-fee alerts, error messages, and infrequent upgrades. These are user reports, not independently reproduced defects. [5] [6]
- App-store privacy disclosures are inconsistent, and the public privacy statement does not expressly address DPDP Act readiness, retention/deletion controls, or a named hosting provider.
- Hostel, special-education, safeguarding, alumni/fundraising, UDISE+, APAAR, GST invoicing, and higher-education NAAC/NBA/NIRF/AISHE functions are not sufficiently documented for a confident feature claim.
