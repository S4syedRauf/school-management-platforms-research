### Overview

Entab CampusCare is an India-focused school ERP and student information system. Its current product family is branded CampusCare 10X and Entab One. It combines admissions, student records, attendance, assessments, report cards, fees, accounting, transport, library, HR/payroll, communication, portals, mobile applications, dashboards and school operations. Entab positions the system as a single source of truth for the lifecycle from enquiry and admission to alumni. [3] [4] [7]

The company says it was founded in New Delhi in 2000 and has focused on school technology for more than 25 years. Entab’s account of its history says the software moved from on-premises delivery to online/cloud delivery. Its post-NEP work added experiential-learning and competency-oriented assessment; the experiential-learning vertical launched on 24 September 2022. [2]

On 5 October 2026, Nelnet Business Services announced its acquisition of Entab. Entab continues under its own brand and leadership, with its New Delhi offices and approximately 290 associates retained. The acquisition is the only publicly verified ownership change found in this review; financial terms were not disclosed. [29] [30]

### Platform Classification Explained

**Browser/cloud delivery:** Entab describes CampusCare as completely web-based and cloud-facilitated, with access anywhere through an internet connection. Current product pages state that the SIS is hosted on AWS Cloud, uses SSL/encryption, automatic daily backup and a 99.9% uptime claim. These are vendor statements, not an independently verified SLA or audit report. [5] [15]

**Mobile:** The current native product is CampusCare 10x for Android and iPhone/iPad. Google Play identifies the developer as Entab Infotech Pvt. Ltd.; the listing showed 5L+ downloads and 4.0/5 from 3.79K reviews when checked. Apple lists CampusCare 10x as an iPhone/iPad education app, 3.7/5 from 1.1K ratings. [26] [27]

**Role-specific apps and portals:** Entab One claims dedicated parent, staff, principal and management apps, plus parent/student, staff, principal, management, central and utilisation dashboards. Publicly verified store pages clearly establish the CampusCare 10x app and a legacy CampusCare iOS app; separate public store records for every claimed role-specific app were not found. [7] [28]

**Desktop/on-premise:** No current native Windows/macOS client or current on-premise deployment offer is documented. Entab’s history says it evolved from on-premises software to online software, so “on-premise” should be treated as historical rather than a current product classification. [2]

#### Admissions and enrollment

The enquiry and registration modules capture multi-channel leads, generate enquiry IDs, tag lead sources, support custom forms by class/stream/branch, allocate counsellors, configure pipeline stages, log activities and reminders, track response time/conversion KPIs, and provide role-based counsellor dashboards. Registration adds document capture, admission numbers, onboarding and counselling workflows. [14] [4]

#### Student information and records

The SIS maintains a 360-degree student profile from admission through alumni, with automatic admission-number generation, sibling identification by mobile number, student master search, bulk field updates, bulk photo/data import through Excel, class/section lists, parent contacts, dropout details, academic history, certificates, transfer certificates, ID cards and custom reports. [15] [3]

#### Attendance

CampusCare supports bulk daily attendance, daily or period-wise configuration, student leave requests and approvals, configurable publishing cut-offs, holiday calendars, absence lists, class/student reports, percentage views and Excel/CSV/PDF export. Student attendance can notify parents through SMS and app notifications. Staff attendance can be manual, biometric, RFID or geo-tagged and can feed payroll. [16] [11] [19]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

The platform lists timetable management, timetable substitution, teacher workload and instant substitution alerts. The mobile app supports timetable access and staff/admin workflows; the public material does not expose a standalone constraint solver or detailed master-scheduling specification. [4] [7] [11]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Exam management includes subject-wise, student-wise, optional-subject and multiple-subject mark entry; class permissions; exam and report locking; PDF locking; hall-ticket generation; configurable report-card templates; mark-entry defaulter reports; comment conditions; subject-group totals; question-bank integration; offline/written and digital tests; analytics and Excel import. The assessment model includes competency and rubric-based evaluation. [13] [3]

#### Report cards and transcripts

Report cards are automatically generated by class, exam and student configuration, can be customised or uploaded, archived and viewed through parent/student portals. The system supports CBSE-aligned templates and transfer/certificate generation. Entab specifically documents NEP 2020 and NCF 2023-aligned 360-degree Holistic Progress Cards (HPC), covering academic, social, emotional, creative, sports, values and wellbeing dimensions, self-reflection, multi-source feedback and parent suggestions. No public evidence found for named ICSE or every state-board template. [13] [21] [3]

#### Learning management and homework

Current pages list LMS management, online classes, lesson and academic plans, homework/classwork, projects and assignments, school diary, online PTM, digital teaching resources, assessment and an experiential-learning repository. The Apple description adds AR-based interactive assets, hands-on activities, lab experiments, concept maps, practice worksheets, customisable lesson plans, short notes and competency-based question banks. [4] [27]

#### Communication

Communication includes in-app notifications, SMS, email and WhatsApp. The WhatsApp module offers approved templates, department/form-level toggles, attendance and leave alerts, fee reminders and confirmations, online-class links, circulars, event announcements, approval messages and audience targeting for parents, teachers and staff. Entab does not publicly name the underlying WhatsApp provider. [10] [7]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

Role-based access separates management, principals, teachers, staff, students, parents, counsellors and administrators. Parents can view attendance, grades, report cards, homework, diary remarks, circulars, fee history/receipts and live bus location. Students access assignments, study materials, timetable and their portal; teachers enter attendance, marks, lesson plans and assignments; management receives branch and school dashboards. [7] [11]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Fee functions include configurable fee structures, instalments, fee cards, concessions and approvals, defaulter management, fines/penalties, transport and hostel fees, full-and-final settlement, demand bills, receipts, cash/cheque/DD/NEFT and digital modes, reconciliation, carry-forward of dues or excess amounts, payment links through email/WhatsApp/SMS, online status, cheque-bounce records and fee analytics. The finance area includes accounts and bill entry, expenses/claims, audit trails, financial accounting and Tally app integration. The fee page says multiple configurable gateways and RBI-approved gateways are supported, and lists UPI integration in the module catalogue; it does not name Razorpay or Paytm. It explicitly claims GST calculation, summaries and GST-return export. [4] [12]

#### HR, staff and payroll

HR covers departments/designations, hiring, shifts, leave, attendance, biometric integration, IDs, training/orientation, holidays, policy, visitors, exit management, staff profiles and performance management. Payroll supports pay structures, earnings/deductions/arrears, payslips, PF, ESI, TDS, gratuity, Form 16, statutory registers, ECR files, loans/advances, bank-disbursement exports, multi-branch processing, appraisal/training links and attendance/leave integration. [4] [19]

#### Transport

Transport provides route and stop management, driver and vehicle records, live GPS, route playback, speed/stoppage data, route optimisation, route-change requests and approvals, transport billing, parent alerts, driver details, vehicle registration/licence information, RFID/biometric bus attendance, delay/deviation alerts and route-capacity, fuel, maintenance, attendance and driver-activity reports. [17]

#### Library

Library features include catalogue/acquisition, bulk book entry, membership, barcode printing, OPAC search, reservation and reservation approval, overdue notifications, stock verification, magazines, requisitions, circulation, digital resources, fines linked to fee ledgers, multi-library support and PDF/Excel/CSV reports. [18]

#### Hostel and boarding

The module catalogue lists hostel management and hostel fee management, but the current public feature pages provide little operational detail about rooms, beds, wardens, mess, boarding attendance or resident welfare. Treat hostel as a listed capability requiring demonstration rather than a fully evidenced current module. [4] [12]

#### Inventory and assets

Inventory tracks consumables and assets across stores/rooms, stock value, low-stock items, sales, orders, goods received, indents, purchase entries, vendors, quotations, purchase orders, issue/return slips, asset lifecycle, maintenance, depreciation, repair and disposal. It supports bulk import, request/approval workflows, finance linkage, GST-ready purchase documentation and audit-ready reports. CampusMart/tuck-shop or bookstore operations are also listed. [20] [4]

#### Health and medical

An infirmary/healthcare module is listed in the enterprise and operations categories. Entab One calls it Healthcare/Hospital Management, but public pages do not detail clinical records, medication, immunisation, consent or emergency workflows. [4] [7]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

The Anecdote module records structured observations about behaviour, discipline, achievements and interventions. The homepage also lists behaviour management, incident reporting and analytics. This is a useful pastoral/whole-child layer alongside HPC, although the public material does not describe a full safeguarding case-management workflow. [4] [1]

#### Special education and safeguarding

No dedicated special-education, IEP/SEN, child-protection or safeguarding module is publicly documented in the reviewed materials. Role-based access, audit logs, encrypted storage, incident/behaviour records and child-privacy language provide relevant controls but should not be represented as a complete safeguarding product. [1] [9]

#### Alumni and fundraising

Alumni management maintains a structured graduate database, academic history, locations and career milestones. Fundraising, donations, campaigns and advancement accounting are not publicly evidenced. [4] [7]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Entab One claims 500+ MIS reports, real-time admissions/attendance/academic/finance dashboards, multi-branch central dashboards, utilisation dashboards, fee and revenue tracking, performance trends, operational bottleneck detection and audit reports. Report exports commonly include Excel, CSV, PDF and other formats. [7] [4]

#### AI features

Current marketing pages claim an AI analytics dashboard, predictive analytics, early risk/trend detection, AI-assisted school planning, chatbot assistants for website/ERP/parent portal, personalised learning paths, auto-grading, content recommendations, plagiarism checking, behaviour analysis, performance forecasting and AI-generated questions/notes. These are vendor claims; no public technical documentation, model governance, accuracy benchmark or named production AI model was found. [1] [7]

#### Government, board and Indian compliance

CampusCare publicly documents CBSE-aligned report-card/question-bank workflows, NEP 2020/NCF 2023 HPC, 5+3+3+4 mapping, vocational/bagless-day tracking and CPD hours. It documents GST-ready billing, PF/ESI/TDS/gratuity payroll, audit trails and SQAAF quality-control support. I found no public product documentation confirming UDISE+ export, APAAR ID lifecycle support, a complete ICSE/state-board library, NAAC/NBA/NIRF/AISHE reporting, or vernacular-language packs. [3] [4] [12] [19]

#### Mobile apps

CampusCare 10x provides mobile attendance, marks, lesson plans, assignments, online classes, library insights, fees, approvals, transport, visitor approvals, diary remarks and notifications. Parents can view results, homework, fees, circulars and transport. The public stores verify iOS/iPadOS and Android availability; the Apple listing says the app language is English. [11] [26] [27]

#### Integrations and APIs

Documented integrations include AWS hosting, GPS devices, RFID/biometric devices, WhatsApp, SMS, email, UPI/payment gateways, Tally app integration, bank-disbursement exports and a generic geo-fencing/API-integration listing. A public developer API, API reference, marketplace or certified OneRoster/LTI/Ed-Fi/SIF/Clever/ClassLink connector is not documented. [4] [12] [19]

#### Security and privacy

Entab claims encryption in transit and at rest, AES-encrypted records on AWS, role-based access, MFA, audit logs, continuous monitoring, automatic backups, restore, regular updates, SSL and a 99.9% uptime target. Its privacy policy covers student, parent, teacher, usage and IP/device data; it describes access/correction/deletion rights, retention, third-party processors and child protections, and says COPPA applies where relevant. App-store disclosures are inconsistent: Google Play displayed “Data isn’t encrypted,” while Apple displayed “Data Not Collected”; these are store disclosures rather than a third-party security audit. No public SOC 2, ISO 27001, FERPA or GDPR certification was found. Entab is publicly engaging with DPDP Act issues through a Delhi NCR school-leader roundtable scheduled for 18 December 2026, but that is awareness/readiness activity, not proof of DPDP certification or completed compliance. [9] [15] [32] [26] [27]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **NEP-native holistic assessment:** 360-degree HPC, competency/rubric assessment, 5+3+3+4 mapping, vocational/bagless-day tracking and teacher CPD are explicitly positioned as core workflows rather than add-ons. [3] [21]
- **AI and predictive layer:** The current site claims predictive analytics, risk detection, forecasting, chatbots, auto-grading and personalised learning recommendations, although technical validation is not public. [1] [7]
- **No-code-style configurability:** Custom enquiry forms, fields, pipeline stages, report-card templates, message toggles, concessions and role permissions let schools adapt workflows without a published coding requirement. [14] [10]
- **Deep Indian finance/payroll localisation:** GST outputs, UPI/gateway flows, Tally linkage, PF/ESI/TDS/gratuity/Form 16/ECR support address India-specific administration. [12] [19]
- **Whole-campus operating depth:** Live GPS, RFID/biometric attendance, library OPAC/barcodes, inventory lifecycle, visitor management, infirmary and alumni extend beyond a basic SIS. [4] [17] [18] [20]
- **Multi-branch leadership visibility:** Central dashboards, branch permissions and cross-campus reporting target school groups such as the 45,000-student Aditya Birla network case study. [1] [7]

### Pricing Details

Entab’s current Entab One/CampusCare 10X page says **custom pricing based on school size**, with “no hidden costs,” one subscription, 35+ modules, unlimited users, mobile apps, WhatsApp/SMS/email, onboarding, migration, 500+ reports and a 99.9% uptime guarantee. It does not publish an INR per-student-per-year or per-school-per-month price, tier names, implementation fee or USD equivalent. The official price-information page likewise explains price factors rather than publishing a price card. [7] [6]

Third-party directories display stale or contradictory metadata. G2 and Capterra both show a nominal “Basic” price of **₹0.45 flat rate per month**, while Capterra records zero reviews and “contact vendor” in its comparison details. This figure is not corroborated by Entab and should not be treated as a credible buying price. GoodFirms says annual subscription, contact-vendor pricing and a 30-day free trial; its page does not state a rupee amount. An older 2014 company announcement described a pay-as-you-go monthly fee based on student/user counts with no upfront licensing fee or extra charges, but no current price was stated. [22] [23] [24] [31]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

The practical integration story is India-specific: Tally app integration; UPI and configurable/RBI-approved payment gateways; WhatsApp, SMS and email; GPS; RFID/biometrics; bank-disbursement exports; AWS hosting; and a generic API/geofencing feature listing. These are meaningful operational integrations for Indian schools. However, no public evidence was found for OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, ClassLink, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, a documented public API, SSO protocol, or an integration marketplace. The “Microsoft Gold Partner” language is a vendor claim and does not by itself establish Microsoft 365 interoperability. [4] [12] [19]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

CampusCare’s public security posture includes vendor-claimed AWS hosting, encryption, daily backups, role-based permissions, MFA, audit logs, monitoring and restore. The privacy policy describes educational-purpose processing, third-party processors, retention, access/correction/deletion requests and child safeguards, including a statement that student information is not used for advertising or profiling. [9] [15]

There is no public SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate, FERPA/COPPA audit report, GDPR certification, penetration-test summary or India data-residency statement. COPPA is mentioned in the privacy policy where applicable; the policy does not specifically name the DPDP Act. The December 2026 DPDP roundtable signals active engagement with consent, children’s data, vendor access, incident readiness and responsible AI, but should be scored as readiness activity rather than demonstrated compliance. [9] [32]

The reviewed sources disclosed no public security incident. App-store self-reports need caution: Google Play showed “Data isn’t encrypted,” whereas Apple showed “Data Not Collected,” and both conflict with Entab’s web privacy/security claims. Schools should request a current DPA, subprocessor list, hosting region, security assessment and incident-response commitments during procurement. [26] [27]

### Strengths

- Broad, integrated Indian-school scope spanning academics, administration, finance, transport, HR, communication and operations. [4] [7]
- Strong localisation for CBSE/NEP workflows, HPC, Indian payroll and GST-oriented finance. [3] [12] [19]
- Cloud/web access plus Android/iOS apps, parent engagement, GPS and WhatsApp/SMS communication. [5] [10] [11]
- Long operating history, New Delhi support footprint and acquisition by Nelnet, which adds international education-technology scale. [2] [29]
- Independent reviews, while few, commonly praise ease of use, comprehensive coverage, online fees, attendance, homework, timetables and responsive support. [23] [24] [25]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Public pricing is quote-only and directory pricing is unreliable; smaller schools may face higher cost or implementation complexity. [6] [7] [22] [23]
- G2’s sole reviewer cites a learning curve, limited customisation, variable support responsiveness and potentially high cost/implementation time for smaller schools. [23]
- Google Play reviews report slowness, crashes, results/assignment loading problems, a weak chatbot and limited support; Apple reviews similarly mention lag, login friction, bugs and inaccessible assignments. These are user reports, not independently measured performance results. [26] [27]
- Review coverage is thin and inconsistent: Capterra has no reviews, G2 has one, GoodFirms has one and SoftwareSuggest has eight, so aggregate satisfaction is not statistically robust. [22] [23] [24] [25]
- Public evidence is weak for UDISE+, APAAR, named Razorpay/Paytm connectors, vernacular languages, higher-education NAAC/NBA/NIRF/AISHE reporting, public APIs, open standards and audited certifications. [4] [12]
- The Google Play “Data isn’t encrypted” disclosure conflicts with Entab’s web claims of encryption, creating a procurement diligence issue. [26] [9]
