### Overview

Skolaro is Buzzyears Education’s cloud school and college ERP/LMS. It combines admissions, student records, academics, fees, HR/payroll, campus operations, communication, mobile portals, and reporting in one system. The vendor positions it for Indian, Gulf, and Middle Eastern institutions that want a single record across principals, teachers, parents, students, and finance teams rather than separate spreadsheets and registers. [1] [3]

The current product is marketed as AI-powered and as having 50+ modules. Strongest India-specific evidence covers board assessment, UPI/card/net-banking fees, statutory payroll, WhatsApp/SMS, Tally XML, and multi-account settlement. College pages advertise AICTE, UGC, and NAAC reporting, but do not publicly document UDISE+, APAAR, NEP 2020 holistic cards, GST/HSN-SAC, NBA/NIRF/AISHE, or DPDP readiness. [7] [9]

Guide-claim audit: the 2012 company claim, 1,800+ institutions, 100+ cities, four countries, 20,000-student group, 50+ modules, quote-based billing, seven gateways, Tally/NAV/Oracle, WhatsApp, and board templates are supported by current official pages. Product launch year remains undisclosed. The exact eight-language list, Aadhaar/PAN/EWS fields, CCE/FA/SA wording, UDISE+/APAAR, RTE, GST HSN/SAC, NEP 2020 holistic cards, dropout-risk alerts, and no-feature-gating claim are not all publicly evidenced. New Delhi is confirmed as registered office; a Gurugram office is not specifically confirmed. [2] [3] [4] [7]

### Platform Classification Explained

Browser access is explicit: the school ERP is available from a PC, smartphone, or tablet, with a web management portal and role-specific portals. Hosting is described as AWS cloud hosting, with daily backups and point-in-time recovery. [3] [4]

Native mobile delivery is explicit: parent, teacher, and student apps on iOS and Android, including a school-branded parent app. Public stores expose a general “Skolaro”/Buzzyears iBuzz listing rather than three clearly separate listings: Google Play package com.buzzyears.ibuzz and Apple listing “Skolaro.” [6] [16] [17]

No desktop executable or self-hosted installation guide was found. Capterra says cloud and on-premise, conflicting with the primary AWS/cloud description; treat on-premise as unverified directory metadata. [4] [15]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Skolaro AI assistant:** surfaces fee dues, attendance dips, admission bottlenecks, and exam outliers, and drafts fee reminders; a public technical model card is not available. [1]
- **Indian fee-operations depth:** seven named gateways, EDC reconciliation, multi-account settlement, scholarship rules, and voucher-level Tally XML sync. [7]
- **No-code configuration:** embeddable admission forms and a drag-and-drop report builder reduce dependence on developers. [20] [22]
- **Multi-channel parent engagement:** WhatsApp Business API, SMS fallback, push, email, delivery status, and branded apps operate in one communication flow. [6]
- **Board and higher-education breadth:** CBSE/ICSE/IB/IGCSE/Cambridge/state-board templates plus CBCS, AICTE, UGC, and NAAC-oriented college workflows. [8] [9]
- **Cross-campus finance:** class-wise merchants and multi-account settlement suit education trusts with several branches. [7]

### Pricing Details

Skolaro publishes no price card or fixed tier names. The official FAQ says pricing is per student, billed annually or term-wise, with all 50+ modules included in the base plan and automatic scale discounts; implementation, training, and migration are included in that base plan. Customisation is separately chargeable for unusual workflows. Pricing is therefore quote-based and should be requested from sales. [4]

A May 2023 Skolaro blog post gives a broad, industry-level Indian school-ERP range of ₹0–₹250 per student per year, not a Skolaro tariff; it should not be used as a current quote. No USD price, setup fee, or public plan comparison was found. [19]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Named integrations include Razorpay, Paytm, BillDesk, ICICI EazyPay, CCAvenue, Atom, Axis EazyPay, Razorpay-HDFC EDC, Tally XML, MS Dynamics NAV via SOAP, Oracle Cloud via REST, LeadSquared, Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Power BI, and Tableau. Biometric/RFID and GPS hardware are supported, and an official blog says the in-house team can integrate APIs. [7] [19] [20] [21] [22]

No evidence was found of 1EdTech OneRoster/LTI certification, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, ClassLink, a public developer portal, SSO documentation, or an integration marketplace. The site claims direct API access, but public endpoint documentation and authentication details are not available. Interoperability is therefore richer than a closed ERP but below a standards-certified ecosystem. [22]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

The FAQ and privacy policy state AWS hosting, ISO 27001-certified infrastructure, AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.2+ in transit, daily backups, point-in-time recovery, vulnerability scans, annual penetration tests, role-based access, and restricted/audited production access. The vendor targets 99.9% uptime. The policy says Buzzyears is a processor acting under an institution’s direction for children’s data, does not sell personal information, and supports access, correction, and deletion requests subject to legal retention. [4] [10] [11]

This is not evidence that Buzzyears itself holds ISO 27001 certification; the wording is “hosted on ISO 27001-certified infrastructure.” No SOC 2, FERPA, COPPA, GDPR, DPDP statement, India-only hosting region, or public incident was found. The privacy policy was last updated 1 Jan 2026. Google Play says data is not encrypted and cannot be deleted, while the vendor policy says encryption and deletion requests exist; schools should resolve this contractually. [10] [16]

### Strengths

- Broad native scope for Indian schools and colleges, with an official 50+ module index and role-specific portals. [3]
- Strong fee operations: UPI/card/net banking, seven named gateways, reconciliation, multi-account settlement, and Tally sync. [7]
- Useful board, exam, and report-card configuration across CBSE, ICSE, international curricula, and state boards. [8]
- Native parent/teacher/student apps and four-channel communication, including WhatsApp Business API. [6]
- Report builder, scheduled reporting, direct API claim, and cross-campus dashboards are distinctive for a mid-market Indian ERP. [22]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Pricing is quote-only; the only public ₹0–₹250 figure is a 2023 general-market estimate, not a current Skolaro price. [4] [19]
- Independent review depth is very thin: G2 has one review, Software Advice has no reviews, and Capterra has zero. [12] [13] [15]
- Mobile UX is uneven. Apple reviewers reported missing notifications, poor UI/performance, and inability to view or download PDFs; Google Play reviews include persistent loading and links that would not open, although other users praise homework usefulness. [16] [17]
- The vendor’s AI, 1,800+ adoption, outcome percentages, and standards/compliance positioning are primarily self-reported; public technical documentation and independent validation are limited. [1] [6] [7]
- UDISE+, APAAR, NEP 2020 holistic cards, DPDP readiness, and special-education/safeguarding capabilities are not publicly evidenced, despite the guide presenting several of them as available.
