### Overview

Academia is Serosoft’s centralized education ERP/SIS family. It spans enquiry through alumni across academics, attendance, fees, exams and student services. The site describes 40+ modules, AI dashboards, multi-campus, multi-currency and multi-language operations [1] [3]. It serves both higher education and K-12 through product variants such as Academia SIS, ERP, UMS, CMS, TMS, TVET and SMS [13].

Serosoft was founded in 2008. The company does not publish a separate Academia launch year; its CEO called Academia the flagship product in an October 2016 interview, so 2008 should not be misreported as the product launch year [4]. The current adoption figure is a vendor claim: 400+ institutions, 1M+ learners/educators and 32 countries as of 2026 [3]. No acquisition is identified in the official history or the independent sources reviewed.

### Platform Classification Explained

- **Browser/cloud:** Academia is described as cloud-native, with online portals, real-time data and SaaS access. Serosoft says its secure cloud infrastructure runs on Microsoft Azure data centers globally; its privacy policy identifies Central India, South Africa North and West Europe/North Europe production-backup arrangements [6] [14].
- **SaaS:** The SaaS model is an annual per-user-per-year subscription including hosting, updates, support and maintenance. Implementation covers configuration, data migration, API integrations, training and change management [5].
- **Perpetual/license option:** The license model is a five-year upfront license, usually linked to student volume, with fixed annual maintenance from year two. The page says “separate hosting setup,” but does not promise on-premise deployment or a desktop installer [7].
- **Mobile:** The platform has native institution-branded apps on both Android and iOS. Exact public examples include “Academia @ SIS,” “Academia @ CECOS,” and “Academia @ IIT.” Features include attendance, timetable, fees, results, assignments, documents and notifications; availability and feature scope vary by institution [11] [12].
- **Desktop:** No public desktop client was found. Administrative use is presented as web-based.

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

Campaigns and enquiry management, configurable web enquiry forms, multi-stage admissions, automatic stage progression, bulk application upload, applicant portal, mandatory/optional document collection, payment at application stages, offer-letter templates, accept/reject workflow and campaign-level dashboards are documented. The public REST API exposes enquiry, application, document, payment and status endpoints [15] [16].

#### Student information and records

A centralized student profile connects personal, guardian, address, identity, education-history, disability, nationality, caste-category, course, programme, document, hold and service-request data. The API includes student detail, documents, enrolments, courses, user roles and academic lookups [15].

#### Attendance

Attendance supports web entry, bulk upload, teacher mobile entry, QR scanning, RFID and biometric integrations. Reports can be student-wise, subject-wise, daily and monthly, with visual attendance insights and attendance-based detention/holds in examinations [17] [16].

#### Timetable and master scheduling

Programme, course, section and class scheduling are supported, with daily/weekly/monthly views in the student app. Public evidence confirms timetable management and course-wise/day-wise timetable access; a scheduling algorithm is not disclosed [11].

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Institutions can create assessment schemes, multi-level marking structures, sum/average/best-of rules, exam schedules, seating plans and marks-entry windows. Marks may be entered manually or in bulk. The examination page supports unit tests, midterms, finals, practicals and external exams, with pass/fail rules, SGPA/CGPA, course and section ranks and analytics [16].

#### Report cards and transcripts

Academia generates customizable marksheets, grade cards, certificates and transcripts in bulk. It can publish results through portal, email or SMS and supports configurable templates aligned to institutional or higher-authority formats [16]. CBSE/ICSE/state-board report-card templates and NEP 2020 holistic progress cards are not explicitly documented on the reviewed product pages; institutions should validate these in a demonstration.

#### Learning management and homework

Assignment management, homework, course-material/document sharing, submission, feedback, session diary and learning-management integration are present. Academia’s public material does not establish a full native content-authoring LMS, so it is better characterized as SIS-linked coursework plus LMS interoperability rather than a replacement for a specialist LMS [11] [12] [18].

#### Communication

The messaging engine supports notifications, email and SMS; examination results can be distributed through email, SMS or portal. Serosoft also documents WhatsApp API integration for broadcast messages, media/documents and stakeholder communication, with the article dated 9 Nov 2022 [19]. Push notifications are evidenced in the apps. A named vendor gateway list is not publicly disclosed.

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

Student, applicant, faculty/teacher and parent-facing portals are offered by plan and product variant. Students can view attendance, dues, schedules, assignments, results, hall tickets, placements and notifications; parents receive communication and performance visibility. Current K-12 plans list student, parent and teacher mobile applications, while higher-ed plans list student and faculty apps [11] [13].

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Fee plans can be linked to programmes, batches, seat types, admissions, services and exams, with late penalties, discounts, scholarships, auto-invoicing, reminders, receipts, payment-mode analysis, outstanding/aging reports and drill-down dashboards. Online payment gateways are supported and payments can be automatically matched to student accounts [20]. The school product explicitly cites Tally, accounting software and online payment gateways; Razorpay, Paytm and UPI are not named on the reviewed pages, so their availability is unverified. GST invoicing is not explicitly documented.

#### HR, staff and payroll

Employee/faculty management, staff profiles, role allocation, faculty portals, workload-related academic operations, HR and payroll-related use cases appear in the catalog and plan lists. Payroll depth is not publicly disclosed; do not assume a full HRIS [9] [13].

#### Transport

Transport management is a named module in both the higher-ed and K-12 plan maps. Route planning, GPS, vehicle maintenance and parent bus tracking are not publicly enumerated and should be verified.

#### Library

Library management is listed by Capterra and was specifically praised by a reviewer for organizing college books. The official current module map emphasizes resource booking rather than describing library sub-features, while the school page names KOHA interoperability [9] [13].

#### Hostel and boarding

Hostel management is a named module; the API and admissions flow include hostel-required data. Room allocation, occupancy, mess, maintenance and visitor workflows are not detailed [15].

#### Inventory and assets

Resource booking, facility-management and security-gate modules are evidenced. A standalone inventory, procurement or fixed-asset ledger is not clearly described in the official module map; do not assume a full inventory/asset-management suite without confirmation [13].

#### Health and medical

Infirmary/medical-care management is a named module in both the general and K-12 maps. Clinical records, medication, consent and emergency workflows are not specified [13].

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

Discipline management is named, and grievance management, surveys, tasks and committees provide adjacent student-support workflows. Detailed behavior points, pastoral case plans or safeguarding escalation rules are not publicly specified [13].

#### Special education and safeguarding

The Capterra catalog tags Special Education, but the official module pages reviewed do not describe an IEP, accommodations or child-safeguarding workflow. Disability fields exist in the admissions API, which is weaker evidence than a dedicated special-education module [9] [15].

#### Alumni and fundraising

Alumni management is part of the current product positioning and higher-ed lifecycle; scholarship and sponsorship management are named modules. Fundraising, donor CRM, campaigns and donation processing are not detailed publicly [1] [13].

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Academia claims 800+ customizable dashboards on its About page, while its 2026 India guide claims 800+ reports/dashboards and 400+ AI-powered reports. Analytics cover admissions, attendance, fees, performance, examinations, progression and operations, with drill-down, real-time data and multi-campus views [3] [21]. These are vendor claims, not independent audits.

#### AI features

The AI page names SERA-AI student assistant, natural-language reporting, real-time dashboards, predictive insights, AI enrollment bot, document-verification engine, workflow automation and retention-risk prediction. The vendor does not publish model-accuracy, data-retention or human-review benchmarks [22].

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Academia supports configurable reports and local workflows, and its API contains Indian-relevant fields such as caste category, national ID, home/second language and disability. However, no reviewed official page explicitly confirms CBSE, ICSE, a particular state board, NEP 2020 holistic progress cards, UDISE+, APAAR, NAAC, NBA, NIRF or AISHE submission connectors. These should be treated as unverified rather than assumed. GST invoicing and exact UPI/Razorpay/Paytm support are likewise not publicly confirmed.

#### Mobile apps

Android and iOS institution-specific apps provide student/faculty access, timetable, attendance, fees, assignments, marksheets/results, documents and announcements. Google Play shows 10K+ downloads for Academia @ SIS; Apple’s Academia @ IIT listing says the app is exclusive to its institution and has insufficient ratings for an overview [11] [12].

#### Integrations and APIs

Serosoft claims 50+ out-of-the-box integrations plus custom connectors and APIs. Public integration examples include LMS, accounting software, Tally, payment gateways, KOHA, WhatsApp, RFID/biometrics and LimeSurvey. The public REST API is extensive across admissions, finance, programmes, courses, students, documents, users, faculty attendance and receipts [9] [15] [18].

#### Security and privacy

The vendor states ISO 27001:2022 and ISO 9001:2015 certification, encryption in transit/at rest, role-based access, two-factor authentication, backups, monitoring, data-quality dashboards, subject-access support and Azure hosting [6] [14]. Its certifications page says SOC 2 is actively being worked toward, while other pages label it “SOC 2 compliant”; this inconsistency warrants requesting the current report. GDPR, POPIA, PDPO and NITA alignment are claimed. The privacy policy, last reviewed 11 Feb 2026, defines Serosoft as processor, customers as controllers, names EU/UK representatives, states deletion on contract termination and gives regional Azure locations [14]. No DPDP Act readiness statement or public security incident was found.

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- AI student assistant, AI reporting and AI agents/bots extend beyond conventional SIS reporting [22].
- Low/no-code configurability allows institutions to shape workflows, forms and rules without technical dependency [18].
- Multi-center capability gives groups a unified view across campuses or institutions [18].
- QR, RFID, biometric and mobile attendance provide several capture modes [17].
- Five-year licensing with fixed AMC from year two gives a non-SaaS budget option [7].
- Institution-specific Android/iOS apps let each deployment expose its selected modules and branding [11] [12].

### Pricing Details

No public numeric vendor price was found as of 24 Nov 2026. The official higher-ed and K-12 pricing pages show Pro, Premium and Enterprise module plans, but each routes to “Request Demo” and gives no INR or USD amount [5] [13]. The SaaS model is annual per user per year and includes software access, hosting, updates, technical support and maintenance; implementation services and the first year’s subscription are paid up front [5]. The license model is a five-year upfront payment, usually calculated per student/license with tiered pricing, plus fixed AMC from year two; the page does not state the amount [7].

G2 records two quote-only editions: “License Model — Contact Us / 500 Users With One-Time Purchase” and “SaaS Model — Contact Us / 500 Users Per Year,” with no numeric USD or INR price and no free trial [8]. Capterra and Software Advice display ₹1 per year, but this is a directory placeholder rather than a credible vendor quote; it should not be used as the product price [9] [10]. Setup, migration and implementation are separately discussed by the vendor, but no fee is published.

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Academia has a public REST API and claims 50+ out-of-the-box integrations, real-time synchronization and custom connectors [15] [18]. Named or evidenced connections include LMS platforms, finance/accounting tools, Tally, online gateways, KOHA, WhatsApp, LimeSurvey, RFID and biometrics. No public evidence was found for certified OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, ClassLink, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 or SSO. Interoperability is therefore strong at API/connector level but not standards-certified on the evidence available.

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

The strongest public controls are ISO 27001:2022, role-based permissions, secure login with optional two-factor authentication, encryption, backups, monitoring, Azure hosting and data-subject request handling [6] [14]. GDPR and regional privacy alignment are stated; FERPA and COPPA are not explicitly claimed. The privacy policy includes EU/UK representatives and regional Azure data locations, including Central India, but does not promise that every customer is hosted in India. DPDP Act readiness is not explicitly stated, and no publicly reported incident was located in the reviewed sources. SOC 2 wording is inconsistent: the security/about pages call it compliant, while the certifications page says Serosoft is actively working toward it [3] [6] [14].

### Strengths

- Broad, genuinely integrated coverage across admissions, academics, exams, fees, portals, transport, hostel, placement, alumni and analytics.
- Strong higher-ed orientation with programme/course structures, SGPA/CGPA, transcripts, OBE, thesis and placement workflows.
- Public API documentation is unusually concrete for an education ERP, including admissions, finance, student and faculty operations [15].
- Multiple delivery choices: annual SaaS or five-year license, plus Android/iOS apps and Azure hosting.
- Reviewers consistently praise breadth, support, automation, flexibility and campus-wide information flow [8] [9] [10].
- India-origin vendor with explicit Tally, payment-gateway, KOHA and WhatsApp references, although not every requested Indian connector is confirmed [13] [19].

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Pricing is quote-only; the public “₹1 per year” directory entries are placeholders and do not help buyers budget [8] [9] [10].
- G2 users report occasional bugs, slow loading, slower-than-needed fixes and reporting that could be more customizable; G2 also summarizes limited customization and learning difficulty [8].
- Capterra reviewers mention time-consuming customization, slow dynamic reports, dated UI/facelift needs, limited student-mobile features and an employee-app gap in older reviews [9].
- One Capterra reviewer reported a severe implementation failure and inability to deliver promised customization; this is an older, isolated review but is material procurement risk [9].
- No public proof was found for UDISE+, APAAR, board-specific report cards, NEP holistic progress cards, NAAC/NBA/NIRF/AISHE, GST, UPI, Razorpay, Paytm or DPDP readiness.
- Standards certification for OneRoster/LTI/Ed-Fi/SIF and public SSO/marketplace evidence are absent.
