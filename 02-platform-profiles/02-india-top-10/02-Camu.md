### Overview

Camu is Octoze’s cloud digital-campus platform: a unified student information system (SIS), learning management system (LMS), examination/Controller of Examinations (CoE), finance and fee collection, admissions, attendance, accreditation, and student-engagement environment. It is positioned most strongly for higher education and multi-campus universities, but the vendor also markets school ERP, K–12 LMS and government offerings. [1] [3]

The company started in 2013 after the founders’ healthcare software work; Camu’s first customer went live in 2014. The product added mobile in 2015, OBE and CBCS in 2016, virtual classroom in 2020, LMS 2.0 in 2022 and AI capabilities in 2025. Camu’s 2020 move of headquarters from Chennai to Singapore reflected ASEAN expansion, while development, support and business-development operations remained in Chennai. No acquisition is publicly disclosed in the reviewed company history. [2] [4]

### Platform Classification Explained

Camu is a hosted, multi-tenant SaaS product with microservices, auto-scaling, encrypted multi-region backups and a vendor-claimed 99.99% uptime. Octoze says it is hosted on Amazon Web Services and supports web access from a computer or mobile device. Its integration page describes secured, documented REST APIs, webhooks and an LTI-compliant LMS. [5] [12]

Native mobile delivery is evidenced by the iOS “MyCamu - Student Parent App,” iOS “Camu - Staff App,” Android “MyCamu Lite,” and the company’s generic Camu Mobile/ MyCamu descriptions. MyCamu covers assignments, exams, report cards, attendance, holidays/events and billing; the staff app exposes schedules, events, holidays, payment approvals and management analysis. [9] [10] [13]

No desktop application, customer-installed on-premise package or offline server edition was found in the official material. eLearning Industry labels the offering SaaS/cloud, mobile and desktop application, but its “desktop” entry is best interpreted as browser access rather than a separately installed client. [14]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

Online enquiry and application portals support configurable forms for multiple programs and intakes, UG/PG/diploma/lateral-entry workflows, document upload with validation/version control, eligibility rules, merit lists, quota/category/reservation handling, interviews, counselling, panel allocation, offer letters, waitlists, automated email/SMS/in-app status updates, fee collection, partial payments, refunds, scholarships, seat confirmation and SIS/LMS onboarding. [15]

#### Student information and records

Camu maintains centralized academic, personal, enrolment, document, account and staff records. Concrete tools include self-profile updates with approval, mandatory-document checklists, mail-merge document generation, e-portfolios/CV templates, student services with SLA and multi-level approvals, hold-and-clearance controls, scholarship applications and payment scheduling, advising/counselling notes, graduation lists and no-dues validation. [16]

#### Attendance

Attendance can be captured by session, course or day through QR code, biometric fingerprint, face recognition, mobile marking, check-in/check-out and geofencing. The system timestamps entries, supports late marking and retakes, sends absence notifications to parents, and provides dashboards, compliance reports and the “Heads Up” attendance summary. Faculty attendance is also supported through HR integration. [17]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

The platform provides course/class scheduling, timetable and academic-calendar views, workload and faculty allocation, conflict-aware course registration, schedule changes and timetable dashboards. Students can see lecture timetables and examination schedules in MyCamu; the review evidence also identifies class scheduling and online calendar features. [6] [9]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

LMS assessments include quizzes, reusable question banks, timed/randomized tests, auto-grading, rubrics, outcome mapping and continuous assessment. The CoE adds exam applications, automated multi-course scheduling, hall tickets, seat allocation, nominal rolls, MCQ/descriptive questions, remote and AI-assisted proctoring, anti-cheating activity tracking, mobile answer-sheet upload, dual/online mark entry, moderation, normalization, relative and multiple grading models, result compilation, publication, analytics, review and revaluation. [18] [19]

#### Report cards and transcripts

Camu publishes results and report cards through the student/parent portal, generates transcripts and consolidated mark statements, supports graduation/no-dues processing, and exposes report and document delivery through mobile. For K–12, the vendor says its CoE can generate holistic, all-round report cards, but a detailed CBSE/ICSE template catalogue was not publicly documented. [9] [20]

#### Learning management and homework

The unified LMS organizes lectures, documents, multimedia, SCORM packages, lesson plans, structured learning paths, assignments, submissions, deadlines, feedback, discussion/chatrooms, virtual classes with video/chat/recording, attendance, progress and performance analytics. It supports synchronous, asynchronous, blended and distance learning. [20] [14]

#### Communication (SMS, email, push, WhatsApp)

Camu documents automated email, SMS and in-app notifications for applicants, parents, staff and students, along with announcements, circulars, reminders, chatrooms, course messaging and push notifications. Email gateways include SendGrid, Microsoft Outlook SMTP and Google SMTP. Pay attention to scope: WhatsApp is not named in the reviewed official integration list, so WhatsApp support is not verified. [12] [15]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

MyCamu gives students and parents attendance, timetable, assignments, exams, report cards, fees/billing, holidays, events, announcements and teacher chat. Faculty can mark attendance, create plans and assignments, evaluate papers, compute marks and give feedback. Management dashboards cover academic, staff and financial monitoring. [13]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Fee functionality includes configurable structures and components, course/credit/flat fees, installments, concessions, scholarships, multiple payers, daily collection, fee registers, automated receipts, due reminders, outstanding/pending reports, reconciliation, billing history and financial dashboards. Official integration evidence names Paytm and Razorpay, plus regional gateways; MyCamu supports cards and net banking. The privacy policy says cardholder data is handled by PCI-DSS-compliant third-party gateways, not stored by Camu. Tally, GST e-invoicing and UPI were not specifically verified, although Paytm/Razorpay may expose local payment methods depending on gateway configuration. [21] [12] [22]

#### HR, staff and payroll

The HR module, delivered through partner systems, covers employee information, recruitment/onboarding, employee self-service, biometric integration, attendance, leave, payroll with tax/statutory compliance, performance management, workload analysis and exit management. Partner delivery means institutions should confirm the exact payroll engine, Indian statutory coverage and implementation boundary. [23]

#### Transport

SIS documentation says Camu covers transportation and the privacy policy describes GPS use for bus location when enabled. A dedicated transport page with route, vehicle, driver, fee and live-tracking subfeatures was not found, so transport is best treated as an available operational capability whose depth requires confirmation. [16] [22]

#### Library

The partner-integrated library capability provides centralized cataloguing, circulation, union catalogues, advanced search, issue/return/renewal, due notices, acquisitions, budgets, vendor tracking, multi-branch/multi-patron support, bulk operations and usage analytics; Koha is named as an integration. [23] [12]

#### Hostel and boarding

Hostel operations include online accommodation registration, room/bed allocation by availability, gender, course or preference, occupancy and maintenance visibility, student room history, biometric/RFID attendance, hostel fee structures, payment tracking, receipts, reminders, visitor logs, gate passes, notices and dashboards. [24]

#### Inventory and assets

Camu’s school knowledge center lists inventory management as a supported school resource, and the college ERP page names inventory among campus operations. Public detail on stock ledgers, purchase orders, asset depreciation or barcode workflows is limited; those subfeatures are not independently verified. [20] [25]

#### Health and medical

Health management is listed as a school knowledge-center resource and Camu’s privacy policy recognizes health/disability information as sensitive data where applicable. A dedicated public medical module specification, such as clinic visits, immunization or medication workflows, was not found. [25] [22]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

The “Hold and Clearance” feature can restrict access or services for anti-disciplinary activity, while student advising records counselling sessions, observed improvements and mentoring reports. [25] [16]

#### Special education and safeguarding

The platform can store disability information where applicable and offers role-based access, approvals, encryption and audit-oriented records. No public evidence was found for a dedicated special-education plan, individualized education plan, child-protection case workflow or statutory safeguarding register. [22]

#### Alumni and fundraising

Alumni management is listed by Capterra/GetApp, and the accreditation module includes an alumni database with activities, contributions and forums. The reviewed sources do not document a standalone donor CRM, campaigns, online giving or fundraising payments. [6] [7] [20]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Camu provides real-time academic, financial, admissions, attendance, examination, accreditation, HR, library, hostel and operational dashboards; drill-down/custom reports; scheduled/exportable reports; performance and retention analytics; audit-ready reporting; and Tableau, Google Data Studio and Power BI embedding. [5] [12] [18]

#### AI features

Camu Intelligence/AI Content Studio is embedded in the LMS. Faculty upload syllabi, PDFs, PPTs and documents to generate chapters, subchapters, quizzes, assignments and outcome-aligned courses, then edit, approve and publish. Other documented capabilities include adaptive assessments, contextual conversational learning, audio-visual/avatar experiences, flashcards, short video snippets and browser code editors/compilers. The vendor says Gemini and ChatGPT can be connected through open APIs and that personal data is not used for model training. [20] [26] [22]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Camu markets NAAC/NBA/ABET accreditation workflows, SSR/AQAR/criterion-wise/annexure reporting, OBE attainment, CBCS, NIRF readiness and multi-year evidence capture. Its India/NEP page documents UGC alignment, Academic Bank of Credits, DigiLocker/NAD templates, single student ID, bi-annual admissions, dual degrees, multilingual support and holistic K–12 progress assessment. Explicit UDISE+, APAAR ID, state-board report-card templates, GST invoicing and AISHE submission workflows were not publicly verified. [27] [28]

#### Mobile apps

The native iOS/Android family comprises MyCamu for students/parents, MyCamu Lite and Camu Staff App. Features include attendance, assignments, assessments, schedules, results/report cards, billing/payment, notifications, faculty marking, payment approvals, staff schedules and management analysis. Android shows 500K+ downloads for MyCamu Lite. [9] [10] [13]

#### Integrations and APIs

Documented interoperability includes REST APIs, webhooks, LTI, SCORM import/export, Microsoft AD, Google Authentication, Keycloak, OAuth 2.0, SendGrid, Outlook SMTP, Google SMTP, Canvas, Koha, Pi360, Jaspersoft, Power BI, Google Data Studio, Tableau, Paytm, Razorpay, AmBank and DragonPay. OneRoster, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, ClassLink, Google Workspace as a certified SIS standard, Microsoft 365 as a packaged suite integration, Tally and a public marketplace were not verified. GetApp’s profile says no API, which conflicts with Camu’s current official API claim; buyers should request current API documentation and tenant-specific availability. [12] [7]

#### Security and privacy

Official claims include AWS hosting, encryption, role-based access, multi-region backups, 99.99% uptime, ISO 27001 compliance and GDPR alignment. The April 2026 privacy policy identifies Octoze as processor for institution-owned data, describes PCI-DSS payment processors, data deletion/retention controls, overseas-access safeguards, and regional storage in India, Singapore, Ohio and potentially in-country deployments. DPDP Act readiness is not explicitly named, and no public SOC 2 or FERPA/COPPA certification was found. [5] [22] [11]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Unified SIS + LMS:** academic records, teaching, assessments, attendance and finance share one student lifecycle record. [1]
- **OBE/CBCS and choice-based enrollment:** outcome mapping, attainment and flexible course choice are unusually central to the product. [27]
- **Accreditation automation:** NAAC/NBA evidence capture, criterion mapping and SSR/AQAR generation are positioned as a core product rather than an export workaround. [27]
- **AI Content Studio:** source-material-to-course generation with faculty review, approval and one-click LMS publication. [26]
- **Flexible attendance:** QR, biometric, facial recognition, mobile and geofencing can be combined around institutional policy. [17]
- **NEP/ABC/DigiLocker alignment:** the vendor documents credit transfer, single student ID, dual degrees and bi-annual admissions. [28]

### Pricing Details

Camu has no public official pricing page, tier matrix or published implementation fee. The reviewed official site routes prospects to a demo/sales form. Third-party directories display a nominal **₹25 flat rate per year** as a starting/basic price, but do not state a per-student, per-school or per-campus unit, inclusions, limits or implementation charges; it should not be used as a dependable quote. Capterra and Software Advice both show ₹25/year, while eLearning Industry says “contact us for pricing.” No USD price was listed; figures were checked 24 November 2026. [6] [8] [14]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Camu has the strongest documented interoperability story in LTI/SCORM, webhooks, REST APIs, OAuth 2.0, Microsoft AD, Google Authentication and analytics/payment connectors. Named products include Canvas, Koha, Pi360, Jaspersoft, Tableau, Power BI, Google Data Studio, Paytm, Razorpay, AmBank, DragonPay, SendGrid and SMTP gateways. However, the public evidence does not establish certification for OneRoster, Ed-Fi or SIF; does not show Clever/ClassLink, Tally or an app marketplace; and conflicts with GetApp’s “no API” field. Treat API access as a sales-validation item rather than an assumed entitlement. [12] [7]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Camu claims ISO 27001 and GDPR compliance, encryption, RBAC, AWS hosting, encrypted backups and 99.99% uptime. Its April 2026 privacy policy provides regional hosting claims: India users’ data in India; users in the Philippines, Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand, UAE and Hong Kong in Singapore; the Americas/Africa in Ohio; and possible in-country hosting by contract. It describes institution ownership, processor/controller roles, PCI-DSS payment processors, retention/deletion, DPO contact and no use of personal data to train AI. The reviewed materials do not explicitly claim DPDP Act readiness, SOC 2, FERPA, COPPA, or a public incident history. [5] [22]

### Strengths

- Broad native higher-education coverage, especially SIS/LMS/CoE/OBE/CBCS/accreditation.
- Strong India relevance through NEP 2020, ABC/DigiLocker, UGC, NAAC/NBA, multilingual and regional-hosting claims.
- Mature mobile footprint for parents/students and staff, with Android scale and iOS counterparts.
- Detailed exam, fee, attendance and accreditation workflows rather than generic “ERP” labels.
- Customer evidence supports ease of use, reporting, payments and responsive support, with Capterra’s six reviews averaging 4.5/5. [6]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Pricing is opaque; the ₹25/year directory figure is not decision-useful, and implementation or module fees are undisclosed. [6] [8]
- Review volume is tiny: six directory reviews and no Apple overview, so ratings are not representative of a large installed base. [6] [7] [10]
- Capterra reviewers mention redirecting to another page to download subject modules, needing guidance from the vendor, slow updates, and a cumbersome staff-leave approval dependency; another reviewer says ease of use can improve and reports are limited/generic. [6]
- Google Play reviews report login/OTP failures, repeated logouts, crashes, stale details and slow loading. These are user reports, not independently reproduced defects, but they are material mobile pain points. [9]
