### Overview

Fedena is an education ERP/SIS rather than a pure learning-management system. It centralizes admissions, student records, academic scheduling, attendance, assessment, report cards, fees, HR/payroll, transport, hostel, library, inventory, communication and reporting for schools and higher-education organizations. Fedena’s help center explicitly says it is not an LMS, although it has assignment, online-exam, mobile-learning and video-conference functions and can integrate with LMS products such as Moodle. [11] [12]

Its history is unusually important for the Indian market. Foradian began in 2009, built a custom student-information system first called “Foredu,” installed Fedena in a Kasaragod school in August 2009, and released version 1.0 as Apache-licensed open source in April 2010. The commercial Fedena Pro/SaaS model subsequently funded hosted support, plugins, implementation and customization. [3] [5] The product launched a SaaS version in February 2013, a plugin marketplace in 2013, and raised $2 million from Fabindia managing director William Bissell in 2014. [3] Practically announced its acquisition of Fedena/Foradian in 2022; the acquisition report described Fedena as serving 40,000 institutions and 20 million users at that time. [1]

### Platform Classification Explained

* **Browser/web:** Fedena is explicitly described as entirely web-based, requiring no software installation. It runs across PCs, laptops, tablets and smartphones through a browser, with role-based logins for administrators, teachers, non-teaching staff, students, parents and management. [4] [6]
* **Cloud SaaS:** The current commercial plans include free cloud hosting, encrypted security, automatic updates, onboarding data/configuration and unlimited users. The product is described independently as cloud-based SaaS. [6] [12]
* **Self-hosted/on-premise:** Fedena’s official “101 reasons” page says customers can download and host it on their own server and URL; Docker installation is also documented. This is a deployment option, not a separate desktop client. [5]
* **Desktop client:** No native Windows, macOS or Linux desktop client is publicly documented; desktop access is through the browser.
* **Native mobile:** The current “Fedena Mobile App” is listed for iPhone/iPad and Android, with role-based experiences for administrators, teachers, parents and students. The older/demo Android listing is called “School Management App - Fedena” and describes FedenaConnect. [9] [10] [13]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

The Enquiry & Registration/add-on module captures online admission enquiries, embeds registration forms on an institution website, supports source/course/academic-year filtering, applicant stages (new, qualified, processed and rejected), counsellor assignment, subject-based registration, document verification, applicant communication by SMS/email, printable forms and fee receipts, and course/source/counsellor reports. Admission forms can be customized and accepted students assigned to courses or batches. [6] [14]

#### Student information and records

The SIS stores personal details, admission numbers, guardian/parent details, academic progress, documents, attendance, course and batch membership, user photos, health-related or extracurricular fields where configured, and historical academic-year data. Bulk CSV import/export supports student, parent, employee, examination score, attendance, library and inventory data; custom fields and user document storage support institution-specific records. [4] [5] [15]

#### Attendance

Teachers can mark day-wise or subject-wise attendance, view attendance history and leave records, and trigger parent alerts. Fedena supports biometric/RFID or swipe-card integration, biometric information APIs, leave applications, late/absence tracking and mobile attendance marking. [4] [5] [13]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

The timetable module manages courses, batches, subjects, teachers, class timings, custom weekdays, classroom allocation and teacher/subject allocation. Students, parents and teachers can view current and upcoming classes in dashboards and mobile apps; examination timetables and school calendars are separate scheduling surfaces. [4] [13]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Fedena supports exam groups, exam planners, term and planner exams, marks entry/import, grade calculation, CWA, GPA and CCE evaluation, group exams, grading levels, online objective and subjective exams, question creation/import, question rearrangement and randomization, publishing, student attempts, and exam reports. The online-exam help center documents question banks/workflows, while the history page records GPA/transcripts and online-exam enhancements. [3] [16]

#### Report cards and transcripts

The gradebook can produce exam reports, term reports and planner reports. Custom report-card templates, ICSE exam categories and IA/EA-style reporting are documented. Fedena 4.0 added GPA and transcripts. CCE reports are supported; the vendor says CCE was built after CBSE guidelines, including SA/FA marks. [3] [5] [17]

#### Learning management and homework

Fedena includes assignments/homework, teacher blogs, discussion groups, file attachments, online exams, mobile learning and a “Learn”/online-learning surface. It is not positioned as a full LMS; the help center explicitly distinguishes Fedena ERP from Uzity or other LMS products. Moodle integration is listed independently, and Zoom, Google Meet and BigBlueButton can support online classes. [11] [12] [18]

#### Communication

The system provides internal messaging, announcements/news, broadcast messages, email integration, bulk SMS, reminders, push notifications, blogs, polls, discussions and gallery posts. Messages can target parents, students, employees or groups, and alerts can cover exams, fees, attendance, assignments, events and announcements. WhatsApp integration is not publicly documented on the reviewed official integration/help pages; do not assume WhatsApp Business support. [5] [6] [13]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

Separate logins and privilege controls provide dashboards for parents, students, teachers, administrators and employees. Parents can view attendance, homework, results, fees, receipts, calendars and announcements; teachers can manage attendance, assignments, gradebook, timetable, leave and student performance; students can access schedules, exams, results, assignments and notifications. Parent accounts can manage multiple children. [5] [13]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Finance and fees handle fee structures, scheduled and instant fees, fines, discounts, tax calculation, fee defaulters, receipts, fee imports, fee transaction reports and academic-year rollover. Online payment covers student, application-registration, hostel and transport fees. Official documentation names PayPal, Authorize.net, Stripe, Braintree, PayU, CCAvenue and Interswitch, with custom gateways; current release notes add Razorpay, and BBPS integration is documented. Account splitting can divert fees to multiple bank accounts through Razorpay or ATOM. Tally synchronization exports transactions by date range. GST-specific invoicing is not explicitly documented, although tax and invoice features are. Paytm and generic UPI support are not confirmed in the reviewed sources. [3] [6] [14] [19]

#### HR, staff and payroll

The HR module stores employee details, employee documents, departments and user access; manages leave types, leave applications, attendance, payroll groups, salary processing and payslips. Employee attendance has create/update/search/delete API actions. [4] [5] [15]

#### Transport

Transport supports routes, stops, vehicles, route-specific fields, transport fee collection, vehicle/route reports and GPS integration for real-time fleet visibility and parent/staff tracking. Transport search and vehicle APIs are public in the API playground. [4] [6] [15]

#### Library

The library module manages books, categories/classification, issue and return limits, availability, barcode scanning, overdue fines and anti-theft/identification workflows. Library records can be imported in bulk, and the API exposes book search. [4] [14] [15]

#### Hostel and boarding

Hostel management covers hostels, rooms, room allocation, hostel-specific custom fields, hostel fees and records of residents. Hostel search is available through the API; the vendor describes hostel management as a core use case. [11] [15]

#### Inventory and assets

Inventory/store functions manage store categories and types, items, stock and issue/receipt workflows, with item-kit management added in Fedena 4.2. Inventory details can be bulk imported, although one reviewer specifically complained that customized CSV upload was not supported in the inventory system. [3] [14] [8]

#### Health and medical

The SIS can store institution-defined health-record fields and guardian details, but a distinct, named medical/nurse/medication module was not found in the official feature tour or help-center index. Treat health as configurable student data rather than a verified native clinical workflow.

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

The Discipline plugin allows complaints/issues against teachers or students, assignee selection, attachments/evidence, discussion and action/solution tracking. Discussion, poll, task, blog and messaging tools support pastoral or community workflows, but a safeguarding case-management module is not publicly documented. [14]

#### Special education and safeguarding

No dedicated special-education/IEP, safeguarding, child-protection or accessibility case-management module was publicly verified in the reviewed official pages. Custom fields, documents, roles and audit logs can be configured, but they should not be treated as a native safeguarding suite.

#### Alumni and fundraising

The Alumni module retains alumni students, supports alumni events and communication by email/SMS, and is intended to connect alumni with current students. A fundraising/donations module was not publicly verified. [14]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Dashlets provide role-specific dashboards for attendance, dues, exams, schedules, news and finance. Reports include attendance, fees pending, examination results, placement, finance, graphical performance analysis and custom reports with selectable filters/columns. Multi-school management provides consolidated dashboards for groups of institutions, although the dedicated page was not extractable during review. [5] [6] [12]

#### AI features

No production AI assistant, generative-AI authoring, chatbot, predictive-risk model or AI analytics feature was verified in current Fedena official materials. A third-party directory lists generic AI feature labels for Fedena, but those labels conflict with Fedena’s own published product documentation and are not used here as evidence. [12]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Fedena has Indian-board functionality: official materials cite CBSE, ICSE and regional state boards, and the help center documents ICSE grading/report workflows and CCE reports. However, I found no current public Fedena documentation confirming direct UDISE+ submission, APAAR ID generation/synchronization, NEP 2020 Holistic Progress Card templates, or NAAC/NBA/NIRF/AISHE reporting. Institutions should request a current implementation statement rather than infer these from generic custom-report capability. [4] [5] [17]

#### Mobile apps

The current “Fedena Mobile App” provides administrator, teacher, parent and student experiences: dashboards, attendance, assignments, gradebook, timetable, exams/results, fee payment/receipts, announcements, school calendar, leave approvals/requests, notifications and teacher communication. It is native on iOS and Android. The Android FedenaConnect demo listing separately exposes announcements with attachments, broadcasts, events, parent fee payment, teacher leave and attendance, and student switching for siblings. [9] [10] [13]

#### Integrations and APIs

The public API playground exposes token generation plus course, batch, attendance, employee leave/attendance, subjects, timetable, grading levels, exam groups/scores, books, events, finance transactions, biometric data, hostel, news, school, routes, vehicles, users and photo upload. OAuth/app-frame support lets third-party applications access Fedena with limitations. Google SSO and Microsoft Azure AD SSO are documented. [14] [15]

#### Security and privacy

Fedena documents encrypted stored data, backups, role/privilege controls, password recovery, custom URLs and audit plugins for activity, user and data changes. Its privacy policy provides access, rectification, deletion, portability, objection and restriction language, but the policy was last updated 24 May 2018. No public SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate, hosting-region statement, FERPA/COPPA attestation, DPDP Act readiness statement or public incident report was verified. [5] [6] [14] [20]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

* **Open-source core and plugin architecture:** Apache-licensed Fedena 1.0 and a plugin-friendly design allow self-hosting, custom development and third-party extensions. [3] [5]
* **Indian assessment support:** CCE, CBSE-oriented SA/FA workflows and ICSE IA/EA grading are unusually relevant for Indian schools. [5] [17]
* **Broad role coverage in one app:** The current mobile app unifies administrator, teacher, parent and student workflows rather than shipping only a parent companion app. [13]
* **Institution-group oversight:** Multi-school dashboards provide a consolidated management view for school groups. [5] [6]
* **No-code-style form and report customization:** Form Builder, custom reports, custom fields and import tools allow institutions to adapt data collection and reporting without changing the core code. [5] [14]
* **Public API and OAuth app frame:** Tokenized REST-style API actions and embedded external applications provide a practical extension path. [14] [15]

### Pricing Details

The official pricing page was checked 24 November 2026. It displayed a temporary 20% year-end sale ending 25 November 2026, with annual institutional pricing in USD and no per-student unit: Standard $800/year (approximately ₹74,778 at the 24 November 2026 Xe mid-market reference rate of $1 = ₹93.4728), Premium $1,120/year (approximately ₹104,690), and Ultimate $1,360/year (approximately ₹127,123). The INR figures are conversions, not official INR list prices. [6] [20]

Standard includes 20 core modules, 17 standard modules, unlimited users, one-to-one online training, automatic updates, email/phone support and onboarding data/configuration. Premium adds 10 premium modules; Ultimate adds 12 ultimate modules. Enterprise is quote-based and adds product source code; implementation is described in the FAQ as included, while mobile apps, gateways, SMS, biometric, GPS and other add-ons may depend on configuration and plan. A 14-day no-credit-card trial is offered. Third-party listings report older/alternative prices: Capterra $1,299/year, G2 $1,299/year and Software Advice $1,299/year; these should not override the official 2026 sale page. [7] [8] [9] [18]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Verified integrations include Tally, Moodle, Google SSO, Microsoft Azure AD SSO, Zoom, Google Meet, BigBlueButton, biometric devices, GPS/location services, bulk SMS and payment gateways including Razorpay, ATOM, BBPS, PayPal, Authorize.net, Stripe, Braintree, PayU, CCAvenue and Interswitch. Fedena has a public API playground, OAuth/app-frame support, XML/CSV export and a historical marketplace/plugin ecosystem. [5] [14] [15] [18] [19]

No evidence was found that Fedena is certified for OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi or SIF, or that it has Clever/ClassLink integrations or a current public marketplace with certification governance. Google Workspace and Microsoft 365 support is limited in the verified materials to Google/Azure SSO and Google Meet rather than a full productivity-suite integration.

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Fedena claims encrypted stored data, backups, role-based privileges and audit trails, and the current pricing page says cloud hosting includes encrypted security. Its privacy policy offers data access, rectification, deletion, portability, objection and processing restriction, but it is dated 24 May 2018 and is primarily a website/service privacy notice. [6] [20]

No public evidence was verified for SOC 2, ISO 27001, FERPA, COPPA or a named GDPR certification. The policy includes GDPR-style rights but does not establish current GDPR compliance. No data-hosting region or cloud provider is disclosed in the reviewed official materials. There is no verified public security-incident disclosure in the sources reviewed. DPDP Act 2023 readiness—especially child-consent, purpose limitation, data-principal requests, processor contracts and breach workflows—is not publicly documented; Indian buyers should obtain current contractual and technical assurances.

### Strengths

* Long operating history, open-source roots and a proven Indian education focus.
* Very broad ERP coverage across academic, administrative, finance, HR and campus operations.
* Strong fit for CBSE/CCE and documented ICSE grading, with regional-board positioning.
* Unlimited users, self-hosting/customization and a public API can reduce lock-in for capable IT teams.
* Current native iOS/Android app covers all four principal roles.
* Public pricing, a no-card trial and included onboarding make initial evaluation straightforward.

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

* Capterra’s 19-review profile gives 4.0/5 for ease of use, 3.6/5 for value for money and 3.8/5 for customer service, below the headline 4.2 overall. [8]
* Reviews praise the breadth, admissions, communication and simplicity, but complain that the interface can feel like a maze, gradebook and inventory navigation are difficult, support/training can be slow or poorly presented, and large-data loading can hang. [7] [8]
* Users report pricing pressure for small organizations and request lower entry pricing; Capterra lists $1,299/year while the official page’s 2026 sale is different, so buyers should confirm the quote and included modules. [7] [8]
* The Android FedenaConnect demo is only 3.6/5 from 79 reviews and includes login/demo-account complaints; the App Store Fedena Mobile App is 2.5/5 from six ratings. [9] [10]
* The open-source/community edition is attractive for control but may lag the hosted commercial edition and requires technical maintenance; current feature parity and support should be confirmed before self-hosting. [8]
* Direct evidence for UDISE+, APAAR, NEP 2020 HPC, WhatsApp, Paytm, GST invoicing, higher-education statutory reporting, AI and DPDP readiness is missing from public materials.

### Key Milestones

* **2009:** Foradian Technologies founded in April; custom student-information system development began in May as “Foredu”; first successful Fedena school test run in August. [3]
* **2010:** Fedena 1.0 released publicly as Apache License 2.0 open source; version 2.0 later added HR and finance to the open-source edition. [3]
* **2011:** Kerala Government’s Sampoorna project used the open-source Fedena codebase across more than 15,000 schools, according to Foradian’s history. [3]
* **2012:** Fedena 2.3 added CWA, GPA, CCE and eight languages; Foradian won NASSCOM Emerge 10 and MIT TR35 recognition. [3]
* **2013:** SaaS version launched; plugin marketplace and secure API/app-frame capabilities launched; Fedena became available in Italian. [3]
* **2014:** Fedena 3.0 launched; Foradian raised $2 million from William Bissell and reached its five-year claim of 20 million students/teachers worldwide. [3] [18]
* **2015–2017:** Enterprise division launched; Fedena 3.5 added major functionality; Fedena Connect launched in 2017. [3]
* **2018–2019:** ID-card/certificate, skill-based gradebook, custom report-card templates and enquiry analytics were added; Fedena 3.8 launched in 2019. [3]
* **2020–2022:** Online-exam/mobile and academic improvements continued; Practically announced its acquisition of Fedena/Foradian in 2022. [1] [3]
* **2024–2025:** Fedena 4.4, 4.5, 5.0 and 5.1 releases added reporting, workflow, attendance and UX improvements. [3]
* **March 2026:** Fedena 5.2 added library access in parent profiles, student documents in the mobile app and Razorpay payment-gateway integration. [3]
* **November 2026:** Official pricing page displayed a 20% year-end sale through 25 November 2026; current public adoption claim was 40,000+ institutions and 200+ countries. [4] [6]
