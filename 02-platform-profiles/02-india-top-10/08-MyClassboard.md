### Overview

MyClassboard (MCB) is an India-focused school ERP and LMS suite that combines student information, academics, admissions CRM, finance, HR/payroll and parent communication. Its current product catalogue is organized around ERP, LMS, Admissions, Finance, HR, Connect and the separate VED AI product, with safety/security and curriculum-specific solutions layered on top. [1] [3]

The company’s official history places establishment in 2008, first client in 2009 and major mobile milestones in 2013–2015. YourStory describes the business as founded in Hyderabad in 2010, names Ajay Sakhamuri as founder, and reports a 2021 pre-Series A round of ₹4.5 crore from ICICI Bank. No acquisition of MCB by another company was found; current third-party company profiles describe it as active, private and unlisted. [2] [25] [26]

MCB matters in the Indian market because it connects operational ERP with board-specific gradebooks, NEP-style holistic progress cards, local payment gateways, Tally, WhatsApp and Indian school safety hardware. Current scale claims have moved from the 2020 independent report of 2,500 schools and 1.5M students to the official 2026 claim of 3,200+ schools and 2.1M+ students; the figures are not directly comparable because they are different dates and sources. [1] [25]
### Platform Classification Explained

The official privacy policy describes MCB as a web-based and mobile application service, and the company markets cloud access from anywhere. Its public materials support browser access and SaaS delivery, but do not identify a cloud provider, region-specific tenancy architecture, customer-controlled hosting, or an on-premise edition. [15] [17]

The native mobile portfolio is role-oriented: MCB PARENT APP for parents, MCB SMART SCHOOL/SMART MCB for teachers and classroom updates, Smart MCB App for Admins, and MCB ADMISSIONS PLUS/Pre-Admission Mobile App for admissions. The official mobile page explicitly lists Parent Apps, Smart MCB App for Admins and Pre-Admission Mobile App; both major parent and teacher apps have Android listings, while the parent and Smart MCB apps have iOS listings. [14] [18] [19] [20] [21]

No Windows/macOS desktop client, packaged installer or verified on-premise option was found; admin and pre-admission apps are confirmed by the official catalogue, not independently reviewed store pages. [14] [22]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

Admissions Plus CRM covers lead capture, lead staging, lead nurturing, follow-up, click-to-call, IVRS, WhatsApp engagement, campaign management and chatbot-oriented admission journeys. The Pre-Admission Mobile App supports data collection, document uploads and application management. LeadSquared and Meritto are listed as admissions integrations. [3] [7]

#### Student information and records

The SIS stores student, parent/family and staff-linked information, supports filters, branch transfers, promotion/demotion, student-account controls, RTE and special-needs requests, parent information, portfolios and document attachments. It produces transfer, bonafide, no-due and income-tax certificates from templates. [29] [4]

#### Attendance

Attendance is available in the SIS and can be linked to parent communication. MCB advertises biometric and RFID integrations for attendance accuracy and security, with daily attendance conveyed to parents. [29] [30]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

Timetable management is listed by SoftwareSuggest and represented in parent-app functionality, although public official detail is limited. [20] [24]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

The LMS includes assessments, objective exams, assignments, Bloom’s Taxonomy tagging, online exams, online classes, course management and teaching plans. Curriculum-specific gradebooks are listed for CBSE/state boards, ICSE, IB, IGCSE, Cambridge, Montessori and higher education. Examcell creates curriculum-aligned question papers using AI plus manual question-bank selection, configurable sections and marks, difficulty balancing, exam scheduling, individualized papers, PDF output and web-based tests. [5] [9]

#### Report cards and transcripts

MCB supports standard report cards and a Holistic Report Card with foundational, preparatory, middle and secondary stages. Higher education adds semester/evaluation report cards, transcripts and course-attainment reports. [10] [13]

#### Learning management and homework

The LMS provides course management, teaching plans, content management, assignments, online classes, remote-learning connections, online exams and curriculum gradebooks; the parent app exposes assignments, events, announcements and progress report cards. [5] [18] [25]

#### Communication

Connect includes SMS, email, push notifications, school diary, announcements, Student Wall, school calendar, voice calls, click-to-call, WhatsApp and photo/video gallery functions. MCB also advertises multilingual translation through VED AI, including Telugu, Hindi, Tamil and Kannada; this is not the same as a fully localized administrative UI. [8] [11]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

The SIS describes separate student and parent login profiles, Google-account login, parent information and PTM collaboration. Public app evidence confirms parent and teacher mobile experiences; MCB positions the web ERP, parent app and Smart School teacher app as connected surfaces. [29] [18] [19]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Finance includes fee management, budgeting/reporting, connected banking, post-dated-cheque management, expense management, student loans, school store, cashless canteen and school services. The payment gateway page lists PayU, CCAvenue, Airpay, Rakbank, Ingenico and Razorpay; it does not explicitly name UPI or Paytm. Tally integration synchronizes fee data, receivables, collections and collection cancellations. GST-specific invoice generation was not publicly verified. [6] [11] [12]

#### HR, staff and payroll

HR includes core employee records and documentation, staff assessment, Staff Wall, praises and feedback, HR Plus payroll and compliance. Public pages do not publish detailed Indian payroll statutory tables or a clear PF, ESI, TDS and professional-tax rule list. [7]

#### Transport

Transport includes transportation management and VTS real-time GPS vehicle tracking, marketed as a parent-facing safety feature. [4] [14] [25]

#### Library

Library management is a named ERP module for cataloguing and school-resource access; independent listings also classify it as supported. [4] [22] [24]

#### Hostel and boarding

Hostel Management is a named ERP module for hostel administration and communication; detailed room, mess, attendance and warden workflows are not public. [4]

#### Inventory and assets

Inventory provides school-resource management; the catalogue also includes school store, merchandise transactions, Dynamic Forms and digital documents. Detailed asset depreciation and purchase-order functions are not public. [3] [4] [6]

#### Health and medical

Infirmary is a named safety/security solution for medical records and services; Visitor Management, RFID and vehicle tracking are also campus-safety controls. Immunization, medication and clinical interoperability were not verified. [14] [30]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

The SIS can record disciplinary infractions, alert parents, maintain student portfolios and archive behavior records for pattern analysis; it also supports reporting forms, inquiry procedures and anti-bullying investigations. [29]

#### Special education and safeguarding

MCB references special-needs requests, RTE, visitor management, RFID, biometrics, infirmary and anti-bullying investigations. A dedicated IEP/SEND module was not verified. [29] [30]

#### Alumni and fundraising

Alumni Management supports continuing contact, opinions, ideas, contacts and engagement with former students. A distinct fundraising, donor CRM or advancement module was not found. [4] [29]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

MCB advertises live dashboards, student-performance insights, fee/admissions reporting and VED AI data-driven/predictive insights. Exact KPI and data-warehouse details are not public. [8] [17] [27]

#### AI features

VED AI provides grammar correction, lesson-plan/content suggestions, YouTube summarization, text simplification, tone changes, multilingual translation and extraction from PDFs, images and spreadsheets. Examcell adds AI-generated questions, curriculum alignment and difficulty balancing; model providers and evaluation controls are not disclosed. [8] [9]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

MCB advertises CBSE/state/ICSE/IB/IGCSE/Cambridge gradebooks, NEP 2020-style Holistic Progress Cards, SQAA workflows, and higher-education NBA/NAAC reporting with outcome-based education, CO–PO/PSO mapping and attainment calculations. No public official evidence was found for UDISE+, APAAR ID, NIRF or AISHE exports. [5] [10] [13] The product also does not publicly confirm GST invoice compliance beyond payment/accounting integrations.

#### Mobile apps

The named mobile products are MCB PARENT APP, MCB SMART SCHOOL/SMART MCB, Smart MCB App for Admins and MCB ADMISSIONS PLUS/Pre-Admission Mobile App. Parent Android adoption is 1M+ downloads, but iOS and teacher-app ratings are materially lower than directory-review scores. [14] [18] [19] [20] [21]

#### Integrations and APIs

The public integration catalogue names Tally, payment gateways, Zoom, Google Meet, LeadSquared, Meritto, biometrics and RFID. The company also claims APIs and API integration for forms and applications. No public developer portal, API reference, marketplace, OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever or ClassLink certification was verified. [1] [3] [12] [17] [27]

#### Security and privacy

The SIS lists data masking, IP allow/deny lists, MAC-enabled security, role-based access control and two-factor authentication. The privacy policy says MCB acts as processor for customer data, supports access/correction/erasure requests, uses HTTPS and reasonable security measures, may transfer data cross-border, and does not store card data on its servers. [15] The company reports ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification for educational SaaS development, and a linked certificate records ISO/IEC 27701:2025, issued 9 November 2026 and expiring 8 November 2029. [16] [17] No public SOC 2, FERPA or COPPA attestation, India-specific DPDP Act statement, hosting-region commitment or publicly reported breach was found.

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **VED AI:** A school-oriented assistant suite combines writing support, multilingual translation, document extraction and teaching-content assistance rather than limiting AI to chat.
- **Examcell:** AI-generated, curriculum-aligned question papers can be mixed with a manually controlled question bank, custom sections, schedules and individualized papers. [9]
- **NEP Holistic Progress Card:** Stage-specific foundational, preparatory, middle and secondary assessment structures go beyond a conventional marksheet. [10]
- **Higher-ed OBE reporting:** The college module claims built-in CO–PO/PSO mapping, attainment calculation, backlog/supplementary handling and audit-ready NBA/NAAC reports. [13]
- **India-native communications:** SMS, WhatsApp, voice, click-to-call, diary and push channels are combined in one Connect suite. [11]
- **Franchise and multi-branch control:** Franchise Management provides centralized oversight for education chains. [4] [27]
- **Hardware-linked safety:** VTS, RFID, biometrics, visitor management and infirmary functions connect software workflows to campus operations. [14] [30]
- **Tally and local payments:** Fee receivables, collections and cancellations can be exchanged with Tally, alongside Indian payment gateways including Razorpay. [11] [12]

### Pricing Details

MCB has no public official rate card or pricing page; the official buying path is a demo and sales consultation. [1] [31] The company should therefore be treated as quote-based, with price determined by modules, student count, campuses, implementation and integrations. No official setup, migration, training, support or per-student tariff was found.

Third-party figures are inconsistent and should not be used as a budget without a written quote. YourStory reported ₹300–₹500 per student annually in 2021. [25] Capterra and Software Advice display a $3,000 per-user-per-month “Basic” card, not corroborated by an official MCB price sheet; treat it as unvalidated directory metadata. [22] [23] The supplied guide’s ₹4,720 entry point and ₹25,000–₹4,00,000 annual TCO bands were not corroborated. No official USD price is listed.

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Verified named integrations are Tally, Razorpay and other payment gateways, Zoom, Google Meet, LeadSquared, Meritto, biometrics and RFID. MCB claims APIs and forms/application integration, and PitchBook also describes an API. [3] [11] [12] [27] There is no public evidence of OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi or SIF certification; no Clever/ClassLink, Google Workspace or Microsoft 365 connector was documented in the current opened product pages, although YourStory reported Zoom, Google Meet and Microsoft Teams connections during the pandemic. [25] MCB therefore has useful Indian operational integrations but not a publicly evidenced standards-led interoperability ecosystem.

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

MCB’s public privacy policy identifies the school/customer as controller and MCB as processor, covers student, parent, staff, visitor, location, device, photo/video and document data, and permits cross-border transfers subject to stated safeguards. It describes HTTPS, restricted access, monitoring and retention/deletion controls. [15] Its data-privacy commitment says it does not sell customer data and requires TLS 1.2/1.3 for public-network connections. [16]

The official site reports ISO/IEC 27001:2022, and a linked certificate records ISO/IEC 27701:2025 issued 9 November 2026 through 8 November 2029. [17] The available material does not establish SOC 2, FERPA, COPPA, GDPR or PCI-DSS certification, a named hosting region or DPDP Act readiness beyond general privacy language. No public incident disclosure was found.

### Strengths

- Broad all-in-one coverage for Indian schools: SIS, LMS, admissions, finance, HR/payroll, communications and safety hardware.
- Strong local fit through board-specific gradebooks, NEP-style holistic reporting, WhatsApp/SMS, Tally, Razorpay and Indian school workflows. [5] [10] [11] [12]
- Meaningful scale evidence: 3,200+ schools and 2.1M+ students claimed by the official site as viewed 24 November 2026. [1]
- Higher-education capability is more substantial than a basic K–12 ERP because it includes OBE, CO–PO/PSO mapping, backlogs and NBA/NAAC reporting. [13]
- AI is visible in production-oriented workflows, especially VED AI and Examcell, rather than only in a roadmap. [8] [9]
- Older directory reviews praise the single-platform breadth, ease of use and support; SoftwareSuggest reviewers specifically praised admissions, finance and HR/payroll. [22] [23] [24]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Public pricing is opaque and contradictory: buyers must request a quote, while directory cards show an implausible $3,000 per user per month against a historical Indian per-student range. [22] [23] [25]
- Mobile UX is the clearest weakness. Google Play reviewers report lag, missing or empty notifications, stale calendars and sibling-account problems; teacher-app reviews mention constant hanging and missing class/profile data. [18] [19]
- iOS reviews report timetable loading failures, transport tracking that stays unavailable, awkward attachment handling, app crashes and unread-notification bugs. [20]
- Independent web reviews are positive but extremely thin: Capterra, Software Advice and SoftwareSuggest each expose only two reviews or similarly small samples, so their 4.5–5.0 scores are not robust evidence of broad satisfaction. [22] [23] [24]
- Public documentation is shallow for API schemas, hosting architecture, implementation, statutory payroll, data residency and government identifiers. UDISE+, APAAR, GST, NIRF and AISHE support should be treated as unverified until shown in a live product or contract; no verified OneRoster/LTI/Ed-Fi/SIF certification or marketplace was found.
