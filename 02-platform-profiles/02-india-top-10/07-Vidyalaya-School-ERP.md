### Overview

Vidyalaya is Sapphire Software Solutions’ integrated school and campus ERP. It combines admissions, student records, attendance, timetabling, assessment, fees, accounting, HR/payroll, transport, library, hostel, inventory, communication, portals, LMS and reporting in a configurable web system. The stated users are management, administrators, teaching and non-teaching staff, students and parents, with role-specific dashboards and access rights. [2] [6] [7]

The product began as a Windows solution after a ten-school Banaskantha pilot in 2002, then moved to web/cloud delivery. The history page records SMS integration, a national rollout, international channel partnerships in Africa and Qatar, e-exam, mobile apps, GPS tracking and virtual classroom as successive milestones. It says Admission CRM launched in 2023, LMS/Alumni/WhatsApp Business integration in 2024 and Vidyamitra in 2025. Sapphire’s company page presents Vidyalaya as an in-house product; no acquisition of Vidyalaya or Sapphire was found. [1] [4]

**Guide check.** The guide’s 2002 launch, ten-school Banaskantha origin, Ahmedabad location, 2,000+ institutions, 1.8M students, 40+ modules, Vidyamitra-2025, NEP holistic card, mobile platforms and ₹5–₹50/student/month FAQ range are supported by current or historical vendor pages. Material qualifications: the adoption totals are self-reported and vary by page/date (1,300+, 1,700+, 2,000+ and 1.5M versus 1.8M students); “company founded” and “product launched” both resolve to 2002 rather than two independently dated events; and I found no primary confirmation of the guide’s “OTP login” wording. The cited ₹1,000/year directory price is a third-party reference, not an official rate card. [2] [4] [6] [9] [16]

### Platform Classification Explained

The vendor describes Vidyalaya as a “web based” and cloud solution, with automated backups and access from anywhere. The current product is therefore best classified as browser-accessible Cloud SaaS. The historical Windows product is evidence of a legacy desktop lineage, not proof that a new on-premise Windows client remains available. Vendor pages instead say the web system can integrate on-premises apps and data; an independent TechnologyCounter listing labels deployment cloud, on-premises and hybrid, but does not document a current self-hosted installer. [4] [6] [13]

Mobile access is offered through a role-based “School App, Parent App & Management App” experience. The official site links to the Google Play listing named **Vidyalaya Student App** and Apple’s **Vidyalaya** app; the store descriptions include students, parents, teachers and administrators, with admissions, attendance, exams, fees, library, transport, messaging and dashboards. The evidence supports one multi-role app/custom-branded deployments rather than three separately named native parent, student and staff products. [2] [3] [17] [18]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

Admission inquiry, online admission forms, follow-up, conversion of inquiry to confirmation, document management, applicant tracking and website-linked admission forms are documented. Admission CRM was added as a named product milestone in 2023; AI-assisted admissions are marketed as an add-on. [4] [7]

#### Student information and records

Student profiles include photographs, family/guardian details, uploaded documents, bulk import, automatic roll numbers, ID cards, custom searches and reports. The online portal exposes student information, health, activities, fees, presence, exam schedule, results, library and holiday information. Certificates include leaving, bonafide, first-trial, transfer, transcript and character certificates, with custom templates. [7]

#### Attendance

The system supports subject-wise, day-wise/repeat and quick attendance, daily/monthly/yearly, cumulative and percentage reports, SMS alerts to absentees, staff attendance and integrations for biometric, UHF/RFID, barcode and face recognition. [7] [19]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

Timetable tools cover student and teacher schedules, teacher-occupancy alerts, subject-limit alerts, proxy/cover settings and AI timetable generation. [7] [20]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Assessment supports CBSE, ICSE, IB and state-board patterns, CCE, combined exams, marks conversion, user-defined mark sheets with 40+ designs, charts and statistics. E-exam supports question banks, term/chapter/practice tests, objective and subjective tests, online delivery and evaluation; bulk question import is a reported workflow. [7] [21] [22]

#### Report cards and transcripts

Report cards and mark sheets are generated from configurable designs, with result dashboards, transcripts and certificate templates. The Holistic Progress Card covers foundational, preparatory, middle and secondary stages, competency-based reporting, cognitive, physical, social-emotional and co-curricular development, teacher observations and parent feedback. The vendor says it aligns with NEP 2020, NIPUN Bharat and CBSE/ICSE/state-board needs; this is a vendor compliance claim, not a government certification. [7] [23]

#### Learning management and homework

Academic tools support daily classwork, homework, lesson plans, syllabus scheduling and completed-lesson updates. LMS and virtual classroom features provide digital assets, discussion, online classes, computerized tests, instant evaluation, reports, document storage and chat. [7] [24]

#### Communication

SMS and email alerts, app push/notice-board communication, circulars, announcements and parent-teacher interaction are supported. WhatsApp Business integration provides verified-school profiles, bulk broadcasts, rich media, encrypted chat, API integration and a chatbot for admissions and common queries. The vendor does not name a separate Paytm or Razorpay connector on the pages reviewed. [2] [25]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

Separate role logins/dashboards are documented for parents, students, teachers, management and employees. Students can view assignments, homework, classwork, results, timetables, events, calendars, news, classmates and teachers; parents can access attendance, health, fees, results and other records. [2] [6]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Fees covers multiple categories, deposits, student-specific concessions/additions, multiple receipt books, automatic receipts, payment SMS, account mapping and reports. Online payment supports bank accounts, cards, net banking, wallets and cash, with OTP and a vendor-described PCI-DSS v2.0-certified gateway. The site does not specifically identify UPI, Razorpay or Paytm by name, so those should be confirmed in a commercial demo. Accounting includes ledgers, income/expense tracking, charges, deposits, loans and school tax-planning pages. [7] [26]

#### HR, staff and payroll

HR stores personal, job, statutory and communication data, supports bulk upload, employee IDs, recruitment-to-retirement records and employee searches. Leave includes leave types, grade-wise settings, forms, approvals and reports. Payroll supports configurable pay elements, departmental payroll, PF/tax deductions, payslips and monthly/yearly reports. Staff performance/appraisal is present in the product catalogue. [7] [27]

#### Transport

Transport includes routes, student assignments, fee linkage, bus attendance and GPS vehicle tracking. The vendor also markets AI transport management. [2] [28]

#### Library

Library is a named core/resource module and a portal feature; independent listings describe cataloguing, circulation and inventory. Detailed public field-level documentation was not found. [7] [10] [13]

#### Hostel and boarding

Hostel management is listed as a product/resource module by the vendor and independent listings. The public pages do not expose detailed boarding workflows such as room allocation, mess billing or attendance, so implementation scope should be confirmed. [2] [10]

#### Inventory and assets

Inventory and asset management are named modules, with stock/resource management and inventory reporting. A Capterra review specifically requested an inventory dashboard, indicating that reporting depth may vary by deployment. [7] [9]

#### Health and medical

Health supports check-up records, student health histories, customizable templates, abnormality reporting and immunization charts. [7]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

Discipline management is listed in the official sitemap and independent directories; event/activity management includes calendars, enrollment and mobile integration. A detailed public discipline workflow or pastoral/safeguarding specification was not found. [2] [10] [13]

#### Special education and safeguarding

No publicly documented native IEP, 504, special-education case-management or safeguarding workflow was found. Health and role-based access should not be treated as a substitute for those capabilities.

#### Alumni and fundraising

Alumni Management is listed and was introduced as part of the 2024 next-generation solutions; AI alumni engagement is separately marketed. No dedicated fundraising, donor CRM or advancement module was found. [4] [7]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

MIS dashboards provide class capacity, new admissions, left-out students, student strength and attendance status, with graphical/real-time institutional and financial views. The platform advertises 1,800+ reports and customizable searches/templates. [2] [7]

#### AI features

Vidyamitra, launched in 2025, is described as a 24×7 administrative education assistant for instant staff queries. Separate AI pages cover timetable, lesson plans, fees, admissions, attendance, accounting, transport, inventory, alumni, homework checking, exam seating, holiday planning, payroll, assessment, push notifications, photo editing and happiness-index prediction. These are vendor feature claims; public technical model, governance, accuracy or deployment documentation was not found. [4] [6]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Board-pattern support covers CBSE, ICSE, IB and state boards, and the HPC page claims NEP 2020/NIPUN Bharat alignment. The higher-education IQAC module says it can create customizable reports aligned with NAAC and other accreditation requirements and its FAQ mentions NAAC/NBA. I found no public evidence of UDISE+, APAAR ID, NIRF or AISHE integrations/exports. GST invoicing is not specifically documented, although school tax planning and accounting features exist. [7] [23] [29]

#### Mobile apps

Native distribution is evidenced by the Google Play **Vidyalaya Student App** and Apple **Vidyalaya** listing, plus vendor links for a School App and Management App. Features include attendance, fees, circulars, homework, results, online tests, calendar, live notices, discussions, transport and dashboards. [2] [17] [18]

#### Integrations and APIs

Named integrations include SMS/email, WhatsApp Business, Tally, online payment gateway, biometric/UHF/RFID, barcode, GPS vehicle tracking, online exams, virtual classroom, LMS, mobile app and online portal. The official Tally page documents fee-transaction transfer, one-click voucher creation, fee-head/ledger mapping, data migration and Tally 7.2+ compatibility. No public developer API reference, OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, ClassLink or marketplace certification was found. [13] [25] [30]

#### Security and privacy

Vendor pages claim cloud storage, automated backup, encryption, role-based access and privacy controls; the payment page cites a PCI-DSS v2.0-certified gateway. Sapphire’s corporate page says the company is ISO 27001:2013 certified, but this is a company-level claim and not proof of a current product certification. The Apple listing says the developer reports no data collected, while Google Play’s data-safety panel reports no data shared/collected but also “data not encrypted” and “data cannot be deleted”; buyers should reconcile store disclosures with the contract and DPA. No public SOC 2, FERPA, COPPA, GDPR, DPDP Act readiness statement, hosting region, breach notice or incident report was found. [1] [17] [18] [31]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Vidyamitra AI assistant:** The vendor says the 2025 assistant answers administrative queries 24×7 and reduces staff workload. [4]
- **NEP/NIPUN holistic reporting:** A multidimensional HPC spans academic, cognitive, physical, social-emotional and co-curricular development. [23]
- **India-oriented board configuration:** The assessment module advertises CBSE, ICSE, IB and state-board patterns, CCE and 40+ mark-sheet designs. [7]
- **Tally fee posting:** Fee transactions can be transferred to Tally with mapped ledgers and one-click vouchers. [30]
- **WhatsApp chatbot and broadcasts:** WhatsApp Business API features include verified profiles, rich media, bulk messages and admission-response automation. [25]
- **Multi-modal attendance and safety:** Biometric, UHF/RFID, barcode, face-recognition and GPS vehicle tracking are offered as integrations. [19] [28]
- **Higher-education IQAC:** The IQAC module targets NAAC/NBA-style quality documentation, dashboards and accreditation reporting. [29]

### Pricing Details

Vidyalaya has no public vendor price sheet or named tier structure. Its official FAQ says Indian school ERP pricing generally ranges from **₹5 to ₹50 per student per month**, depending on modules and support; this is a market range, not a binding Vidyalaya quote. [6]

Third-party references are inconsistent and should not be treated as like-for-like plans. Capterra lists **₹1,000 flat rate per year** for Vidyalaya School Software, while Software Advice repeats ₹1,000/year; a separate Capterra record named **VidyalayaERP** lists Basic ₹7,500/year, Premium ₹13,500/year and Professional custom quote, which may be a different listing/product record. No setup, implementation or USD price was publicly stated. [9] [16] [32]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

The strongest public interoperability evidence is practical rather than standards-based: SMS/email, WhatsApp, Tally, online payments, biometric/UHF/RFID, barcode, vehicle tracking, online exam, virtual classroom, LMS and mobile/portal integration. Tally 7.2+ support and mapped fee-ledger posting are specifically documented. Google Workspace/Office 365 alignment is mentioned on the about page, but connector scope is not specified. No public OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, ClassLink certification, open API documentation, SSO specification or app marketplace was found. [6] [13] [30]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Sapphire states that it is ISO 27001:2013 certified; Vidyalaya separately claims encrypted cloud storage, automated backup and role-based access. The payment page claims a PCI-DSS v2.0-certified gateway. There is no publicly accessible product security whitepaper, SOC 2 report, ISO certificate scope, penetration-test summary, data-residency statement, DPDP Act readiness assessment, GDPR/FERPA/COPPA statement or breach/incident disclosure in the sources reviewed. Store privacy disclosures are limited and should be validated contractually, especially for minors’ data, retention, deletion and subprocessors. [1] [17] [18] [31]

### Strengths

- Broad native ERP coverage for Indian schools, with configurable board assessment, fee, attendance, HR and resource modules. [7]
- Strong practical India fit through Tally, SMS, WhatsApp, biometric and online-payment integrations. [13] [25] [30]
- 2002 product lineage and a credible migration path from Windows to web/cloud delivery. [4] [5]
- NEP/HPC and higher-education IQAC positioning broaden the product beyond a basic SIS. [23] [29]
- Independent reviews consistently praise ease of use, fee management, dashboards and support, although ratings are concentrated in older reviews. [8] [9] [10] [11] [12]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Pricing is not transparent: the vendor provides only a broad ₹5–₹50/student/month FAQ range, while directories show conflicting flat-rate figures and quote-based sales. [6] [9] [16] [32]
- Public adoption figures vary materially by page and year, and no independent institution/student audit was found. [2] [4] [6]
- Reviewers mention occasional server maintenance, lag, unknown errors, slow report opening, limited mobile-library functionality, Excel-heavy question entry, incomplete report/dashboard customization and occasional support delays. [8] [9] [10] [11] [12]
- An Apple reviewer reported crashes soon after opening and another reported the gallery not working; the App Store score was only 3.1/5 from 21 ratings when viewed. [18]
- There is no public evidence for UDISE+, APAAR, a developer API, 1EdTech/Ed-Fi standards, separate specialist-education workflows, or DPDP readiness. This limits verifiable interoperability and compliance confidence.
