### Overview

openSIS is OS4ED’s student information and school-management system. It centralizes student, family, staff, admissions, academic, attendance, grading, scheduling, communication, billing and reporting workflows for K–12, higher education, trade schools and virtual programs. The current commercial positioning is cloud-hosted, staff-account pricing with unlimited student and parent accounts, while the Community Edition remains open source under the GNU GPL. [1] [2] [5] [12]

OS4ED states that openSIS has served institutions since 2008 and now describes the product as AI-powered, with a live AI lesson-plan builder, natural-language search, reporting assistance, predictive insights and the “Piloo” guide. The company also offers openIntel for data warehousing and business intelligence. No public acquisition, institutional funding round, or separate product-company merger was found; Crunchbase lists OS4ED as active, private and for-profit. [2] [9] [15] [20]

The supplied guide is directionally right about the hybrid/open-source model and 2008 product history, but several claims need correction. It labels 2008 as the company founding year even though the primary source only dates openSIS service; its “thousands” adoption statement omits the current official 1,200+ institutions, 1M+ students and 80+ countries claims; its $500 setup fee is not on the current official price card; and its AI, openIntel, ISO 27001:2022 and WhatsApp claims are supported by current official pages, but should be described as vendor claims rather than independently audited product capabilities. The guide also implies a fuller ERP breadth than the public catalog supports: no native transport, library, hostel, inventory/assets, payroll/HR, alumni/fundraising or complete special-education/safeguarding module is publicly evidenced.

### Platform Classification Explained

The SaaS edition is browser-accessible and cloud-hosted. The Microsoft Marketplace listing says openSIS is hosted on Microsoft Azure and uses Azure security and scalability features; the “Why openSIS?” page claims 99.99% uptime backed by Azure. These are vendor/channel statements, not an independently verified SLA. [3] [21]

The Community Edition is a PHP/MySQL web application that can be installed independently. The GitHub repository identifies Community Edition 9.3, released June 2, 2026, and lists Apache 2.4+, MySQL/MariaDB and PHP 8.x requirements. It is GPL-licensed. OS4ED separately describes Professional Edition as licensed on-premises software and SaaS as a managed cloud service. [2] [12]

The official mobile product is **openSIS SaaS Mobile Connect**. The Apple listing supports iPhone and describes student, parent and teacher access to attendance, schedules, grades, assignments, announcements and institutional communication. Google Play lists the same named Android app and records Google and Microsoft SSO plus PayPal and Stripe payment support. There is one role-aware app rather than separately named Parent, Student and Teacher apps. [13] [14]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### School, institution and multi-school administration

The School Information module supports school dashboards, unlimited custom fields, marking periods (year, semester, quarter, trimester or custom ranges), summer-school dates, multi-calendar and bell-schedule management, holiday/event notifications, notices, course/program/subject/section setup, rooms and capacity, grade-level equivalencies, role-based access control, menu-level permissions, access logs, Excel export, reports, year-end rollover and “Copy School” templating for similar schools. Multiple school enrollment and district/enterprise rollups are available in the catalog; district dashboards and data warehouses are custom/enterprise options. [4] [6]

#### Student information and records

Student 360 stores identifiers, photos, demographics, contacts, family/custody and sibling links, documents and comments. It supports unlimited custom fields, masked Social Security numbers, enrollment/transfer history, rolling retention, multiple-school and multi-program enrollment, 504/SpEd/LEP and economic-disadvantage/free-lunch indicators, advisor assignment, mass updates, Excel imports, historical grades, saved searches, edit history, administrator impersonation and Boolean custom report queries. ID cards can include barcodes and QR codes. [4]

#### Admissions and applicant processing

Admissions includes dashboards for received, processing, enrolled and rejected applications; gender/program charts; customizable multi-page forms with CAPTCHA, custom fields, tags, staff assignment and export; process steps for academic/financial checks, interviews, acceptance/rejection and wait-listing; event-triggered email templates; medical fields; multilingual translation; conditional logic; multiple forms; CSS styling; fee refunds/subscriptions; and transfer of applicant fields into the enrolled student record. [4] [7]

#### Course, curriculum and timetable/master scheduling

Course Manager handles programs, subjects, courses, sections, prerequisites, learning outcomes and catalogs. Scheduling supports fixed, variable-day, custom-date, year-round/summer, block, rotating, mosaic and multi-period models. Administrators can group schedule/drop students and teachers, reassign teachers, manage team teaching, identify open periods, distinguish required/elective and Honors/AP/college tracks, support homeschooled and before/after-school students, permit conflict exceptions and accept course requests. [4] [19]

#### Attendance

Attendance provides unlimited codes and categories, excused/unexcused/tardy marks, teacher comments, administrator overrides and override comments, advance absence entry, missing-attendance monitoring, administrator entry on behalf of teachers, period or homeroom attendance, one-click whole-class marking, state/district minute rules, full-day/half-day recalculation and canned attendance reports. [4] [7]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

The gradebook supports multiple grade scales, percentage/letter grading, weighted assignments, teacher-defined scales, multi-term weighting, Common Core or school standards, effort/performance grades, honor roll, exemptions, rounding, exam grades, GPA, historical grades and prerequisite validation. The catalog documents exam-grade entry and calculation into final report-card grades, but not a separate examination-management suite. [4]

#### Report cards, transcripts and degree audit

Report cards can use canned and teacher comments, GPA and AI-assisted comment generation. One-click report-card and transcript generation, four-year consolidated academic history, trade-school transcript records, historical grade entry and progress reports are supported. Graduation/Degree Audit lets institutions define programs, credit categories and course requirements, support multi-program enrollment, schedule students into requirement-linked courses and expose Graduation Progress to students and administrators. [4] [19]

#### Learning management, homework and lesson planning

openSIS has lesson plans and curriculum tools, but LMS capability is primarily integration rather than a full native LMS. Teachers can create, copy, edit, align, attach and publish lesson plans for student/parent viewing; AI assists lesson-plan, curriculum and syllabus generation. Moodle, Canvas and Google Classroom integrations synchronize users, courses, assignments, lessons, schedules and grades with mapping, REST configuration, sync controls and LMS SSO. [4] [8] [9]

#### Communication

Communication supports SMTP/provider APIs, broadcast groups, email, attachments, notices, SMS through gateways such as Twilio and WhatsApp through Meta Cloud API. AI can translate messages by recipient language and draft in selectable tones. Push notifications are documented for the mobile app, but no separate push service is publicly described. [4] [8] [14]

#### Teacher, student and parent portals

Teacher Portal workflows cover attendance, gradebook, final grades, lesson plans, course information and classroom dashboards. Student Portal provides profile, schedules, grades, attendance, course requests, lesson plans, curricula, syllabi and graduation progress. Parent Portal provides linked-student information, academic progress, attendance, communications, lesson plans, curricula, syllabi and billing/payment access. Permissions, portal disablement, password rules and SSO are configurable. [4] [7]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Billing supports fee assignment, fee types/due dates, invoices, cash/check/card/EFT, ledgers, statements, aging, reminders, waivers/voids, refunds, deposits, scholarships/grants, payment links, Excel exports, recurring subscriptions and role-based fund deposits. Institutions can define a general-ledger chart and subaccounts. Stripe handles subscriptions; QuickBooks can synchronize charges, invoices and payments, and PayPal/Authorize.net are listed. This is school billing with accounting connectivity, not a full general-ledger ERP. [4] [8]

#### Staff and teacher management

Staff records include demographics, IDs, photos, masked SSNs, employment history, schools, grade levels, subjects, addresses, emergency contacts, documents and teacher certifications with expiry tracking. Administrators can search, filter, report, impersonate and act for teachers in attendance and grading. The public materials do not evidence a full HR recruitment, benefits, timekeeping or payroll module. [4]

#### Health and medical records

Health captures physicians, dentist and eye-doctor contacts, insurance and policy data, preferred facilities, critical alerts, immunization/physical dates, infirmary visits and nurse notes, with report filtering. Medical fields can also be collected in admissions. OS4ED describes these as education-administration health records, not a covered healthcare system. [4] [10] [11]

#### Behavior and discipline

Behavior records support incident categories, date/time/location, involved students and witnesses, action plans, detentions, suspensions, interventions, follow-up, parent notifications, custom forms, access-level controls, reports, exports, historical records and trend analysis. Public documentation does not establish a separate safeguarding/child-protection case-management suite. [4] [7]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Built-in dashboards cover admissions, collections, overdue balances, attendance and academic operations. Custom reports can query fields with Boolean/comparison logic, save filters, select columns and export to Excel. Academic Analytics is described as dashboards, KPIs, visual summaries and trends. District/enterprise customers can add advanced reporting, data visualization, data warehouse/BI and openIntel; the higher-education page claims enrollment, program-outcome, completion, retention and predictive insights. [4] [6] [8] [19]

#### AI features

Current production-facing AI claims include Piloo, natural-language Smart Search, reporting support, multilingual communication, workflow guidance, AI lesson-plan generation and AI-assisted report-card comments. OS4ED also describes predictive early-warning analytics for academic failure, attendance, enrollment and course demand. Curriculum Builder and Syllabus Builder are described as recently released, while broader intelligent workflows are marked coming soon. These are vendor claims; independent reviews do not yet validate their performance. [9] [20]

#### Mobile apps, integrations, APIs and security controls

The role-aware openSIS SaaS Mobile Connect apps cover attendance, schedules, grades, assignments, announcements, communication and payments. The integration catalog names Moodle, Canvas, Google Classroom, Microsoft Teams, Office 365, Microsoft Entra ID/Azure AD, Google Workspace, Zoho CRM, Salesforce, HubSpot, Stripe, PayPal, Authorize.net, QuickBooks, Twilio/SendGrid and openIntel. Advanced pricing includes API access with full documentation; the help center describes API endpoints, keys, OAuth, synchronization and integration logs. No public evidence was found for OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF certification, Clever or ClassLink. [5] [7] [8]

The platform has RBAC, custom profiles, menu-level permissions, audit/access logs, password controls, optional MFA, SSO, masked sensitive identifiers, data import/export and rollover. Transport, library, hostel/boarding, inventory/assets, alumni/fundraising and payroll/HR are not publicly evidenced as native modules; state reporting is a custom district/enterprise option rather than a broadly documented built-in capability. [4] [6]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- Staff-based pricing leaves student and parent accounts unlimited. [5]
- GPL Community Edition self-hosting sits alongside SaaS and Professional deployments. [2] [12]
- “Copy School,” rollover, conditional admissions workflows and bidirectional LMS integrations support institutional scale. [4] [8]
- AI spans lesson planning, communication, search, reporting and early-warning analytics; openIntel adds custom BI/data warehousing. [2] [9] [20]

### Pricing Details

The current official price page, checked November 24, 2026, offers a 15-day free trial and staff-based pricing in U.S. dollars. Essential annual is $480 total per year with five staff accounts included and additional staff at $8 per month billed annually; Essential monthly is $50 total per month with five staff included and additional staff at $10 per month. Advanced annual is $1,440 total per year with ten staff included and additional staff at $12 per month billed annually; Advanced monthly is $140 total per month with ten staff included and additional staff at $14 per month. The page’s repeated “per staff / month” label is a presentation error; the adjacent totals and included-staff language make clear that $480 and $1,440 are plan totals, not five- or ten-fold charges. [5]

Community Edition is free and GPL-licensed. Professional on-premises and District/State/Enterprise SaaS pricing is quote-based. Custom options can add white labeling, dedicated data isolation, custom modules/integrations, data warehouse/BI, district dashboards, state reporting, advanced reporting, implementation, training, account management and fixed annual or per-student pricing. The guide’s optional $500 setup-service claim was not found on the current official pricing page; it should be treated as unverified. [2] [5] [6]

Capterra and G2 display annual reference tiers of $4, $8 and $12 per user per month, corresponding to the public Core/Startup, Essential and Advanced labels, but both are marketplace listings rather than a separate official rate card. [16] [17]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Named integrations include Moodle, Canvas, Google Classroom, Microsoft Teams, Office 365/Microsoft 365, Microsoft Entra ID/Azure AD, Google Workspace, Zoho CRM, Salesforce, HubSpot, QuickBooks, Stripe, PayPal, Authorize.net, Twilio/SendGrid, WhatsApp via Meta Cloud API and openIntel. The product supports SSO, OAuth, documented API access on Advanced, REST-based LMS connectivity, Excel/CSV import-export and integration/synchronization logs. [7] [8]

No verified OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi or SIF certification was found, and no public Clever/ClassLink connector was found. Capterra lists seven integrations and G2 lists six, so marketplace counts vary by catalog scope. [16] [17]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

OS4ED states that it is ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certified and markets FERPA, GDPR and COPPA alignment. Its March 1, 2026 privacy policy describes AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.2+ in transit, RBAC, administrator MFA, penetration testing, network controls, Azure/Stripe subprocessors and 30-day post-termination student-data deletion. OS4ED acts as processor for institution data and offers DPA/SCC mechanisms for GDPR transfers. [1] [3] [10] [11]

The policy discusses FERPA, COPPA, PPRA, GDPR/UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA and state privacy laws. It describes HIPAA-consistent safeguards, not HIPAA certification. No public SOC 2 scope, data-region list, OneRoster/Ed-Fi certification, India DPDP statement or public security incident was found. Google Play says no third-party sharing, encryption in transit, possible financial/health collection and that data cannot be deleted, a notable tension with the web policy. [10] [14]

### Strengths

- Broad core SIS coverage and a choice of GPL self-hosting, SaaS and Professional deployment.
- Predictable staff-based pricing with unlimited student and parent accounts.
- Strong customization through fields, forms, reports, roles, workflows and school templates.
- Named integrations across LMS, identity, messaging, payments, CRM and accounting, plus a broad AI roadmap.

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Independent review volume is extremely small and ratings are weak: G2 is 2.9/5 from five reviews and Capterra is 1.0/5 from one review. [16] [17]
- G2 reviewers cite an outdated or less user-friendly interface, difficulty contacting support, limited support/resources, missing application management, limited rules/configuration, slow bug fixes and extra effort for scheduling changes. [17]
- A 2026 Capterra/G2 reviewer alleges that a discontinued free account led to deletion of four years of academic data without effective warning; the allegation conflicts with current Terms promising 14 days’ notice and export before inactive free-tier deletion, but it remains a material user-risk signal. [11] [16] [17]
- SourceForge users praise breadth and usefulness but report installation problems, bugs, low support/design scores and historical billing gaps in older Community Edition releases; those comments are dated and should not be read as a direct evaluation of SaaS 2026. [18]
- The public product is not a complete school ERP: transport, library, hostel, inventory, payroll/HR, alumni/fundraising and full special-education/safeguarding workflows are not evidenced.
- Standards interoperability is less transparent than leading district SIS vendors: no verified OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi or SIF certification was found.
