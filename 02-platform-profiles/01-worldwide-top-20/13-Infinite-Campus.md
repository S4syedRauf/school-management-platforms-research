### Overview

Infinite Campus is a U.S.-focused K–12 SIS combining a district student database with instruction, family engagement, communications, payments, food service, workflows, analytics and state exchange. Campus SIS is the core; Campus Learning adds an LMS, and Campus Parent/Student provide family access. The vendor advertises 1,500+ tools and a vetted Extension Marketplace. [4] [9]

The company began in 1993 when founder Charlie Kratsch worked with his former Minnesota district on student administration. Its history records expansion beyond the SIS into Food Service, Online Payments, Online Registration, Messenger and learning management, followed by machine-learning dropout prediction. [1] Infinite Campus says it has not acquired another SIS or company and remains employee-owned. [10]

### Platform Classification Explained

* **Browser and cloud delivery:** Campus SIS is described as web-based and as a single, cloud-based platform in independent and government descriptions. The North Carolina Department of Public Instruction says its statewide implementation was completed on 1 July 2025 as a unified cloud-based platform. [5] [11]
* **Hosting:** Customer data is in secure U.S.-based data centers, with separate databases and daily backups/30-day snapshots; no cloud provider is named publicly. [3]
* **Desktop/on-premise:** No current desktop client or customer-managed on-premise option was identified; treat it as web/cloud software unless a district contract says otherwise.
* **Native mobile:** The exact consumer app names are **Campus Parent** and **Campus Student**, available for iPhone/iPad and Android; **Campus Mobile Payments** is a separate iOS/Android app for Bluetooth card-reader payments. The parent and student apps expose announcements, assignments, attendance, grades, schedules, notifications, and other district-enabled items. [7] [8] [12]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

Campus Online Registration supports new and returning student applications, open enrollment, responsive questions, parent access through Campus Parent, district websites, mobile devices and kiosks, status emails, uploads such as utility bills, birth certificates and transcripts, application/enrollment reports, and approval that writes directly into Campus Census. It can collect tuition agreements and payment plans when paired with Campus Payments. [13]

#### Student information and records

Campus SIS is the real-time record for census/demographics, households, enrollments, calendars, schedules, attendance, grades, assessments, programs, documents, behavior, health, special programs, graduation plans, transcripts, staff and courses. Role permissions govern access. Data Suite adds validation rules, change history and access auditing. [3] [14]

#### Attendance

The SIS supports daily/class attendance and absence history. Workflow adds parent absence requests, badge/barcode/kiosk check-in and appointment-linked attendance; attendance also feeds Analytics risk predictions. [15] [16]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

The SIS covers course/section setup, calendars, student/staff schedules, course masters, schedule changes and schedule-driven notifications. Product and third-party materials reference a Schedule Wizard; independent reviews praise central scheduling but flag class scheduling usability. [6] [17]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Teacher tools include gradebook, assignment categories, standards-based grading, missing/late work, assessments, multi-type quizzes, resubmission, individualized assignments, discussions, Progress Monitor, Score Analysis and third-party grade passback. [9] [18]

#### Report cards and transcripts

Campus supports report cards, transcripts, graduation plans, standards, courses, historical records and parent/student delivery. CCS Backpack publishes custom documents; Report Translation supports teacher comments and multilingual report cards. [19] [20]

#### Learning management and homework

Campus Learning Suite provides Planner, Discussions, individualized assignments, Curriculum Library, enhanced Quizzes, resubmission, feedback-based reassignment, Progress Monitor, Engagement Check-In and Score Analysis. It is SIS-native but supports Canvas, Google Classroom, Schoology, Brightspace and other OneRoster/LTI partners. [9] [18]

#### Communication

Messenger sends scheduled, automated or emergency email, phone and SMS from current SIS contact data, with templates, sender controls and parent preferences. Parent/Student push notifications cover grades, assignment scores, attendance and schedules; translation tools address language access. [19] [21]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

Campus Parent and Campus Student are web portals plus mobile apps. Parents can view grades, assignments, attendance, schedules, announcements, food balances, fees, school stores and activities where enabled; students receive a learner view and notifications. Teachers use browser-based Instruction tools. District permissions control menu visibility. [12] [18]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Campus Payments covers course/lab/event/yearbook/trip fees, donations, activities, athletics, permission slips, stores, tickets, fundraisers, dependent care and tuition plans/discounts/scholarships. Food Service covers cafeteria POS, balances, limits, allergy alerts, FRAM meals and family payments. A full general-ledger accounting/ERP product was not publicly evidenced. [22] [23]

#### HR, staff and payroll

State Edition lists Staff Management and stores staff, qualification, employment and HR data; the ODS retains historical HR information. Public pages do not document standalone recruiting, payroll, benefits or time-and-attendance, so payroll should not be assumed native. [24]

#### Transport

No dedicated transportation-routing, fleet, bus GPS, eligibility or ridership module was identified in the reviewed official Campus SIS, Extension Marketplace or state-system pages. External transport integration is possible but unverified.

#### Library

No dedicated library-circulation, catalog, acquisitions or serials module was identified in the reviewed official sources. Any library integration is unverified.

#### Hostel and boarding

No hostel, dormitory, room-allocation or boarding-management module was publicly documented. It is not a residential-management suite.

#### Inventory and assets

School stores and cafeteria/POS inventory-adjacent transactions are documented, but no general equipment, fixed-asset, purchasing or warehouse-management module was identified. The Extension Marketplace page does not present inventory/assets as a standalone product. [25]

#### Health and medical

The SIS and State Edition include health data, health alerts and nurse workflows; Workflow can check in at a nurse location and the ODS retains historical health data. Clinical records, medication, immunization and telehealth were not specified publicly. [24]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

Campus SIS includes behavior records and behavior-management workflows. SpotLight adds positive/minor behavior entry, dashboards by district/school/classroom, filters by who/what/when/where, barcode mobile entry, multi-year reports, positive reinforcement, a School Store and drill-down to Behavior Management. BTAM adds multidisciplinary threat-assessment teams, staff concern entry, configurable triggers/notifications, dashboards, pattern/trend reporting and preventative workflows. [26] [27]

#### Special education and safeguarding

The statewide product inventory lists IEP and PLP; the system supports special-education status, IEP data, accommodations and state reporting. The 504 Module tracks eligibility, referrals, meetings, annual reviews, reevaluations, manifestations, forms, documents, accommodations, parent/staff input, electronic signatures, dashboards and automated state extracts. MTSS tracks academic/behavioral tiers, domains, interventions, meetings and progress monitoring. BTAM provides the safeguarding/threat-assessment layer. [24] [28] [29]

#### Alumni and fundraising

Campus Payments supports donations, fundraisers and donation drives, but no dedicated alumni CRM, constituent history, campaign management or advancement module was identified. Fundraising should therefore be understood as transaction functionality rather than a full alumni/foundation suite. [22]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Analytics provides daily, localized risk predictions from attendance, behavior, academics and stability, plus visualizations for assessment, attendance, behavior and classroom performance. Premium Surveys supports files, emotions, signatures, ranking, sortable lists, external links and 40+ languages; core reporting includes ad hoc queries, validation and certification. [16] [24]

#### AI features

The documented intelligence is predictive, not generative: algorithms forecast graduation persistence/dropout risk and support at-risk funding models. Agile Flow/Agile Learning are roadmap directions; no production conversational copilot was identified. [2] [10] [16]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

State Edition serves 10 state agencies with a real-time ODS, multi-year history, validation/certification, IDs, custom pages/forms, assessments, graduation pathways, overlap detection, cross-district enrollment, transfers and third-party exchange. North Carolina completed its cloud implementation on 1 July 2025; North Dakota selected it for 120,000 students in November 2024. [11] [24] [30]

#### Mobile apps

Campus Parent and Campus Student are the exact parent/student apps, each available on iOS and Android, with district-controlled notifications. Campus Mobile Payments supports iOS/Android card-reader transactions. Mobile functionality is strongest for family access, alerts, payments and selected workflow/behavior entry; teacher/admin operations remain primarily browser-based. [7] [8] [12] [22]

#### Integrations and APIs

The platform supports its Extension Marketplace, 1EdTech-certified OneRoster, LTI score passback/content selection, secure launch, and partners such as Canvas, Google Classroom, Microsoft Teams, Brightspace, Schoology, Clever, Edlink, Pearson, Renaissance and Turnitin. Direct API access is partnership-gated for many developers, not unrestricted public API access. [18] [31] [32]

#### Security and privacy

Security controls include role-based tool permissions, MFA for remote access, CAPTCHA and breached-password detection, separate customer databases, daily backups, vulnerability scanning, monitored logs, U.S. data centers, annual SOC audits and a SOC 2 report available under NDA. Infinite Campus states FERPA compliance, direct district control over education records, no sale/targeted advertising use of those records, annual privacy training and breach notification to the education agency. ISO 27001, formal GDPR/India DPDP commitments and a named public breach were not established. [3] [33]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

* **Single-database ecosystem:** SIS, learning, family access, payments, food service, messaging and workflow share current district data rather than relying on nightly file exchanges. [9] [23]
* **Predictive dropout prevention:** Campus Analytics Suite produces daily risk predictions localized to district context and reports category scores for attendance, behavior, academics and stability. [16]
* **Statewide real-time ODS:** State Edition links SEA and LEA data with cross-district enrollment, certification, custom forms, IDs and historical records in one operational store. [24]
* **Extension Marketplace:** The vendor’s differentiator is a vetted, integrated extension layer rather than a closed SIS or a loosely connected app directory. [10]
* **Workflow automation:** Events and Actions can trigger notifications and follow-up actions for enrollment, special-education status, scheduling/grading and homeless-status changes; appointments can auto-create hall passes. [15]
* **Strong family transaction surface:** One family app can combine academic updates with food balances, fees, activities, stores and donations where enabled. [22]
* **Data governance tools:** Data Validations, Change Tracker and Access Tracker make data quality and privacy auditing operational rather than purely policy-based. [14]

### Pricing Details

No current official price list, tiers or standard rate is published; buyers request a demo/quote. Gartner describes subscription pricing, typically per student per year, with module-dependent quotes and possible implementation/setup fees. [34] SaaSworthy lists a quotation-based “Infinite Campus Custom” plan with no free trial, but its details were last updated 11 November 2020 and are stale estimates. [35]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Infinite Campus says it was the first SIS certified by 1EdTech for OneRoster and CASE and requires certified digital-learning partners. The catalog documents roster provisioning, grade passback, LTI launch/content selection and OneRoster Assessments. Canvas confirms OneRoster 1.2 credentials, nightly provisioning and grade passback. [18] [31]

Named connections include Canvas, Google Classroom, Schoology, Brightspace, Microsoft Teams, Clever, Pearson, Renaissance, Turnitin, Edlink and others. The Extension Marketplace supplies an integrated partner model. Edlink’s independent technical review says Infinite Campus supports OneRoster 1.1 and 1.2 variants but direct API access generally requires a partnership-level commitment; therefore public API openness is weaker than the vendor’s marketplace and standards story. [32] No public evidence was found in the reviewed sources for a certified Ed-Fi or SIF implementation, a named accounting connector, WhatsApp integration, or a universally available district SSO standard.

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Infinite Campus states that districts control education records, the vendor acts as a contracted school official, data is housed in the U.S., and annual external SOC 2 Type II reports are available under NDA. [33]

The security page (20 January 2025) lists MFA, endpoint protection, intrusion prevention, monitoring, vulnerability scanning, separate databases and daily backups. The privacy policy (24 September 2026) says the apps collect limited crash/technical data, encrypt in transit and do not sell or use it for targeted advertising. [3] [36]

FERPA and COPPA-related handling is described, including education-agency control for children under 13. Public sources did not confirm ISO 27001, formal GDPR/India DPDP commitments, non-U.S. hosting or a public breach. Validate these contractually for non-U.S. use.

### Strengths

* Broad K–12 coverage on a single SIS foundation spanning enrollment, academics, family access, payments, food service, workflows, analytics and state reporting.
* Strong state-agency architecture: real-time SEA/LEA exchange, certification, historical ODS and cross-district operations are unusually deep. [24]
* Native Campus Learning avoids mandatory third-party LMS licensing while supporting OneRoster/LTI partner choice. [9] [18]
* Family engagement through distinct Campus Parent and Campus Student apps with real-time academic, attendance and notification access.
* The vendor reports 99% renewal and 3,400+ conversions; support is in-house and won HDI’s 2024 Best Service and Support Culture Award. [1] [37]
* Marketplace and governance tools reduce point-to-point integration and provide auditable data access/change controls.

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

* G2 users praise centralized records but report difficult navigation, permissions confusion, interface relearning and cumbersome grade/report workflows. [5]
* Capterra reviewers flag complex navigation, too many steps, limited customization, time-consuming grades, difficult reports, session timeouts and add-on fees for smaller districts. [6]
* Mobile ratings are materially lower than desktop review-site scores: Campus Parent 3.3 and Campus Student 3.6 on Google Play as viewed 24 November 2026. Review counts were not exposed in the opened listing, so app sentiment should not be over-generalized. [7] [8]
* Pricing opacity and module-by-module quoting make total cost difficult to compare; sources describe custom quotes and possible implementation fees. [34] [35]
* The product is strongest in K–12 SIS/state reporting; transport, library, boarding, general assets, alumni CRM, full payroll/accounting and a generative-AI assistant were not documented as native modules.
* Edlink says direct API access is partnership-gated and OneRoster implementations vary, increasing integration effort. [32]

### Key Milestones

* **1993:** Charlie Kratsch founded Infinite Campus after working with his former Minnesota district on student administration. [1]
* **1993–2001:** The company developed its relational-database/browser SIS foundation; the founder dates the first statewide data-sharing solution to 2001. [1] [2]
* **1996:** Infinite Campus says it introduced the first web-based SIS with integrated applications and SaaS pricing. [2]
* **2002:** The company moved to an Arden Hills office and began State Edition work with South Dakota. [1] [24]
* **2006–2007:** Bureau of Indian Education, Montana and Kentucky expanded statewide use. [24]
* **2008:** Infinite Campus opened its national headquarters. [1]
* **2009–2016:** Food Service, Online Payments, Online Registration, Messenger and learning-management capabilities expanded the product family. [1]
* **2015–2021:** Hawaii, Nevada and the Northern Mariana Islands joined State Edition. [24]
* **2018:** The founder describes a near-real-time collaborative-intelligence platform milestone; later Campus Analytics applied machine learning to dropout prevention. [2]
* **2023:** Delaware selected Infinite Campus as its statewide SIS/IEP and data-exchange partner. [24]
* **2023–2025:** North Carolina selected Infinite Campus in November 2023 and completed statewide cloud implementation on 1 July 2025. [11]
* **2024:** North Dakota selected Infinite Campus on 7 November 2024, becoming the tenth SEA customer for about 120,000 students. [30]
* **2024:** Infinite Campus won HDI’s Best Service and Support Culture Award. [37]
* **2026:** The vendor promotes the integrated Extension Marketplace and Agile Flow/Agile Learning roadmap. [10]
