### Overview

FACTS SIS is a cloud student information system for private and faith-based K–12 schools. It combines a school record, academic operations, family communications, health and behavior records, scheduling, admissions/enrollment, and links to FACTS tuition, financial aid, billing, fundraising and application products. The strategic value is the shared FACTS IQ data layer: a school can keep applicant, student, family, academic and financial workflows connected instead of reconciling separate systems [1] [2].

FACTS began in 1986 as a tuition-payment service, added financial aid in 2002, and joined Nelnet in 2005. The SIS lineage is RenWeb, acquired in June 2014 and renamed FACTS SIS in 2018. FACTS also acquired Dynamic Internet Applications in 2016, adding website/CMS and fundraising capabilities [6] [7] [26].

The product suits schools wanting one vendor for admissions, tuition and academic records, but some breadth comes from separately branded products or integrations rather than one native SIS module.

### Platform Classification Explained

- **Browser/cloud:** FACTS describes SIS as its first cloud-based SIS and provides separate browser logins for the SIS administrator console at https://sis.factsmgt.com/admin and Family Portal at https://sis.factsmgt.com/family-portal. The vendor describes the current platform as cloud SaaS and says its data centres use restricted physical access, redundancy and disaster-recovery controls [3] [16].
- **Desktop/on-premise:** No current Windows/macOS client or on-premise option was found. Historical reviews describe RenWeb’s transition from desktop/client-server to web delivery [9].
- **Mobile:** The named native app is **FACTS Family App** for iPhone/iPad and Android. It exposes announcements, attendance, classes, calendar, billing/tuition, homework, lunch, medical information, report cards and student schedule. FACTS also offers school-branded family-app experiences. The public evidence identifies a strong parent/student app, but not separate native teacher and administrator apps [4] [12] [25].

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

Application Builder supports no-code/custom online applications, branded inquiry/application pages, dynamic forms, automated touchpoints, task routing, status changes, document collection, review workflows and reports. Enrollment Management tracks inquiry-to-registration progression, digital contracts and packets, reenrollment campaigns, acceptance-to-payment-plan handoff, family accounts and data flowing into SIS. FACTS reports 6K+ schools using enrollment products and 1.3M+ enrollments processed in 2025, but those are product-family claims rather than SIS-only adoption [21] [22].

#### Student information and records

The SIS is a single system of record for student, family and staff records, demographic data, contact relationships, academic history, documents and historical transcripts. Records also feed health, behavior, attendance, communication, admissions and financial workflows. Role-based access and centralized data reduce duplicate entry, while the Family Portal exposes permitted student information [2] [3].

#### Attendance

Schools can configure day setups and attendance codes, capture attendance by class or day, monitor attendance in real time, generate attendance reports and automatically alert families. The Family App exposes attendance, and FACTS positions attendance alongside academic and compliance data [2] [4].

#### Timetable and master scheduling

Scheduling supports block schedules, complex schedules and multi-campus structures. The 2025 release added general-scheduling updates, templates/patterns and a person locator that finds students and staff by daily schedule. FACTS’s roadmap includes course requests, courses/classes/rooms, manual and automatic scheduling enhancements and schedule-conflict reporting; those later phases should not be treated as fully delivered without a current release note [5].

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Teacher tools include configurable gradebooks, grading scales, standards-based grading, assignments, missing-work visibility, lesson plans and classroom planning. A dedicated high-stakes examination engine is not clearly documented; exam administration should be validated in a demonstration [2] [9] [24].

#### Report cards and transcripts

Schools can customize report-card layouts, generate parent-ready reports, calculate GPAs and store historical transcripts. Transcript generation is tied to recorded grades and report cards. Reviewers value report-card workflows, while some report and transcript customization limitations remain common complaints [2] [10] [24].

#### Learning management and homework

SIS includes homework visibility, lesson plans, assignment/grade access and a learning-platform connection in the wider FACTS ecosystem. The Family App lists homework. Independent listings do not consistently evidence a full built-in LMS, so schools should treat FACTS SIS as an academic-management hub rather than a full course-content or LTI LMS [4] [9] [24].

#### Communication

Communication includes email, SMS/text, push, voice/text-to-speech alerts, targeted recipients, preferences, templates, scheduling, newsletters and announcements. WhatsApp is not advertised. Reviewers ask for better two-way communication and message history [4] [10] [11].

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

Family Portal gives parents/students access to grades, assignments, attendance, schedules, calendars, report cards, homework, announcements, medical-related information where authorized, tuition and school bills. Teachers and administrators use the browser SIS console for attendance, grades, lesson plans, records, reports, behavior and communications. Premier integration provides a single-login/SSO-style experience across Family Portal and FACTS financial services, though a standards-based enterprise SSO directory integration is not publicly specified [2] [3].

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Connected FACTS financial modules cover tuition payment plans, enrollment fees, incidental billing, prepay accounts, lunch/field-trip/childcare charges, configurable schedules and due dates, automated reminders, ACH, checks and debit/credit cards. Advanced Accounting acts as an accounts-receivable subsidiary ledger, linking charges, credits, payments, bank/institution accounts and adjustment reasons; financial-aid awards can be applied to tuition plans. Payment Forms provide branded online forms and data exports. FACTS states PCI DSS Level 1 validation and SSAE 18 auditing for its payment/SIS materials [3] [23].

#### Cafeteria and after-school care

Cafeteria functions include online menus, lunch balances/prepay, POS barcode scanning and lunch activity. The wider product description also names after-school-care management and student pickup/time-clock workflows. These are distinctive operational extensions, although they may be sold or configured separately by school [2] [11].

#### HR, staff and payroll

Staff/faculty records, permissions, schedules and staff lookup are present, including the scheduling person locator. A complete U.S. HR/payroll module is not publicly evidenced for FACTS SIS. FACTS’s Australia-oriented pages mention payroll and asset management, so regional availability should not be assumed for the U.S. product.

#### Transport, library, hostel and boarding

No first-party public SIS page verified native bus-routing, library circulation, hostel/boarding or dormitory-management modules. Transportation and library tools appear in third-party catalogs/integrations, but not as verified native modules [9] [24].

#### Inventory and assets

Asset-management language appears in Australia-oriented FACTS material, but the U.S. SIS page does not document a general inventory register; treat it as regional or unverified.

#### Health and medical

Health Information Management stores vaccination records, medical conditions, medications and medical notes, supports medication scheduling and parent notification for medical events. FACTS released a redesigned medical interface on 27 May 2025, added at-a-glance views and prevented overwriting of medical notes [2] [5].

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

Behavior workflows record incidents, conferences and interventions, automate family notifications and preserve centralized documentation. Dashboard Builder includes behavior and at-risk views. Pastoral-care terminology is not used consistently in public documentation, but discipline and intervention records are native [2] [5].

#### Special education and safeguarding

Role-based access, behavior/health records and compliance-oriented reporting can support safeguarding processes. A dedicated IEP/special-education case-management module, mandated-incident workflow or child-protection register was not publicly verified; schools requiring those functions should plan an integration or specialist tool.

#### Alumni and fundraising

FACTS Giving/Donor Services is a connected ecosystem product rather than a clearly native SIS screen. It can use SIS family/contact data, offer giving options and plan tiers, track donors/projects and provide campaign reporting. The 2016 DIA acquisition added website/CMS and fundraising tools [23] [26].

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

FACTS IQ provides cross-system enrollment, financial and academic dashboards. The SIS Dashboard Builder uses embedded Power BI-style reporting, live SIS data, configurable filters and sharing; prebuilt views include student information, medical, behavior, admissions and at-risk dashboards. Current FACTS materials claim 30+ dashboards, more than one million customization combinations and no extra dashboard charge. Analytics cover yield, retention, delinquency, affordability and financial-aid impact [5] [20].

#### AI features

FACTS advertises AI models and admissions yield predictions, plus an AI-supported roadmap for surfacing patterns and automating proactive alerts such as attendance changes. FACTS has said it is building secure data pipes for schools’ existing AI tools and targeting an AI beta, rather than describing a production conversational assistant. Therefore, predictive/decision analytics are evidenced, but a full production AI assistant is not [6] [20].

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Reporting exports support compliance reviews, and Australia-oriented Core Intelligence mentions government reporting. Public U.S. documentation does not enumerate every state/federal template; schools should confirm state and diocesan submissions during procurement [1] [5].

#### Mobile apps

FACTS Family App is available on iOS and Android with grades, homework, attendance, announcements, calendar, classes, schedules, report cards, lunch, medical and billing/tuition. School-branded apps can send push notifications and accept payments; separate native staff/teacher apps are not documented [4] [12] [13].

#### Integrations and APIs

FACTS exposes a documented OneRoster v1.1 REST API with OAuth authorization and endpoints for sessions, classes, courses, grading periods, demographics, enrollments, organizations, schools, students, teachers, terms and users. FACTS also claims Ed-Fi support and open APIs; SchoolDay documents a FACTS OAuth integration and CSV/SFTP alternative. Google Classroom and Canvas are listed integrations, while FACTS’s public materials do not verify LTI, SIF, Clever, ClassLink, Microsoft 365 or WhatsApp support [14] [15] [19].

#### Security and privacy

FACTS reports encryption at rest/in transit, testing, DDoS defenses, intrusion controls, restricted data-center access, backups, redundancy and disaster recovery. It cites PCI DSS Level 1 and SSAE 18, and describes ISO 27001 alignment; an independent ISO certificate or SOC 2 report was not found. The 4 Nov 2026 privacy policy addresses school-directed processing, FERPA, COPPA and deletion/access; GDPR is addressed in data terms, while India DPDP coverage and hosting regions are not expressly identified. No confirmed public breach was found; BBB complaints concern email and login operations [3] [16] [17] [18].

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Connected FACTS IQ operating layer:** Unifies SIS, admissions, finance, engagement, analytics and integrations around one record [1].
- **Single-database heritage:** FACTS says the original single-database design remains central, reducing duplicate entry between academics, enrollment and billing [3].
- **Power BI-style Dashboard Builder:** Lets school users build and share live-data dashboards without exporting spreadsheets [5] [20].
- **Admissions yield and retention intelligence:** Dashboards connect inquiry, yield, affordability, aid, delinquency and retention patterns [20] [22].
- **Family App plus payments:** Families can combine grades, attendance, alerts, forms and tuition payments in a branded mobile hub [4].
- **Medical and behavior workflow depth:** Medical notes, medication scheduling, incident documentation, interventions and family alerts sit alongside academic records [2] [5].
- **OneRoster v1.1 API:** The public Swagger endpoint is more concrete than a generic “API available” claim and exposes detailed roster resources [15].

### Pricing Details

FACTS does not publish a current SIS price card, tier table, per-student rate or standard implementation fee; sales quotation is required. Software Advice shows a “starting price” of $1,000 but labels pricing available upon request, so it is not a reliable unit price or official quote [9]. GetApp reports no price information [24].

The FACTS Family App listing states **$4.99 per family annually**, checked 24 Nov 2026; this is not the SIS license [12] [25]. SchoolDay reports **$500 annually for an API connection**, with CSV/SFTP as an alternative; this is a third-party charge [14]. G2’s review-derived implementation data most commonly shows 1–3 months; onboarding and conversion fees require a quote [11].

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

The strongest verified capability is the public OneRoster 1.1 API with OAuth and SchoolDay integration. FACTS claims OneRoster and Ed-Fi support plus open APIs. Google Classroom and Canvas are listed, and the ecosystem links admissions, enrollment, payment, aid, giving and Family App. No public evidence was found for LTI, SIF, Clever/ClassLink, Microsoft 365, WhatsApp or a broad marketplace; Premier integration’s SSO protocol is unspecified [14] [15] [19].

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

FACTS publishes encryption, secure facilities, monitoring, vulnerability and penetration testing, backups, redundancy and disaster recovery. The SIS fact sheet identifies SSAE 18 auditing and PCI DSS Level 1 validation. The newer security page discusses ISO 27001 alignment and regular audits but does not provide a certificate number; SOC 2 was not publicly confirmed. FERPA and COPPA are directly discussed in the 4 Nov 2026 privacy policy, while GDPR is addressed in the security/data-protection material. FACTS’s 20 Nov 2026 Data Protection Terms include a DPA, API/integration terms and AI feature terms. Data hosting regions, India DPDP coverage and a public incident register were not disclosed. Operational complaints in 2025–2026 include email delivery/authentication, repeated logins, account synchronization, bugs and support handoffs, with BBB records showing some issues resolved [16] [17] [18].

### Strengths

- Broad private-school coverage spanning academics, admissions, tuition, aid, family engagement, cafeteria and health.
- Deep FACTS-native connections reduce duplicate data between SIS, applications, enrollment, payment plans and aid.
- Strong practical recordkeeping: configurable grading, transcripts, attendance codes, medical records and behavior documentation.
- Public OneRoster API, OAuth roster endpoints and claimed Ed-Fi/open-API direction are better than a closed SIS.
- Reviewers frequently praise customer service, centralization and the ability to keep family, academic and financial data together [9] [10] [11] [24].

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Pricing is quote-only, and independent listings disagree or provide only ambiguous starting-price figures.
- Users report a learning curve, click-heavy navigation, bugs, outages and difficult reports [10] [11] [24].
- Capterra reviewers cite unclear roadmap communication, updates that prioritize parent experience over daily school users, clunky reports, limited trend views and no two-way parent/student communication [10].
- Apple reviews repeatedly report forced reauthentication, password resets, delayed grades, device switching problems, crashes and confusing navigation; the app is rated only 3.4/5 despite its broad feature list [13].
- Transport, library, boarding, payroll, IEP/special-education and state-reporting depth is not clearly documented as native.
- The wide ecosystem can feel like multiple products and logins; reviewers also describe setup work and a learning curve [11].

### Key Milestones

- **1986:** FACTS founded by David Byrnes and Stanley Phillips to serve one parochial school with tuition-payment services [8].
- **2002:** FACTS added Financial Aid Management [6].
- **2005:** Nelnet acquired an 80% interest in FACTS; the company reported more than 3,500 institutions and 650,000 families/students at the time [8].
- **2014:** FACTS acquired RenWeb; RenWeb then served more than 3,000 schools and brought admissions, scheduling, billing, attendance, gradebook, health and parent-portal capabilities [7].
- **2016:** RenWeb acquired Dynamic Internet Applications, adding School Site/CMS and fundraising-related capabilities [26].
- **2018:** FACTS renamed the RenWeb product FACTS SIS [3].
- **2025:** SIS released scheduling enhancements and a medical-module redesign; Dashboard Builder and MFA rollout were announced in the Q2 roadmap [5].
- **2026:** FACTS positioned FACTS IQ as the connected intelligence layer, refreshed its privacy/data-protection terms in November, and continued expanding dashboards, APIs and AI-supported insights [6] [17] [18] [20].
