### Overview

Alma is a US K–12 SIS combining records, attendance, scheduling, gradebook, report cards, enrollment, portals, communications, payments, analytics and state reporting. It also positions itself as an SIS/LMS environment, synchronizing assignments and grades with Google Classroom, Canvas, Microsoft Teams, Schoology and other classroom systems. [1] [2] [9]

The founders are Andrew Herman, Elizabeth Long and Michael Oliver. Founding-year databases conflict; Alma has no separate product-launch announcement, so 2014 is only a launch proxy. PitchBook reports one round and $699,000 raised; Crunchbase’s seed amount is obscured and Alma publishes no official total. Alma acquired Always Prepped in 2015 (terms undisclosed) and ESP Solutions Group in August 2026, extending into statewide data management, interoperability and reporting. [7] [8] [14] [17] [18] [23]

### Platform Classification Explained

* **Browser and cloud:** School accounts use school-specific Alma web addresses, and Alma describes the SIS as cloud-native with 99.99% scheduled uptime, multi-factor authentication, NIST 800-53 alignment, and releases approximately every two weeks. Alma does not publicly identify its cloud provider or school-data hosting regions. [2] [3]
* **Desktop/on-premise:** No installed desktop client or school-level on-premise Alma SIS edition was found. State-agency infrastructure documentation does describe Alma-hosted, SEA-hosted, and hybrid models, which should not be confused with a normal district subscription. [5]
* **Mobile:** Alma Now is Alma’s responsive mobile SIS/parent-student experience. It exposes assignments, schedules, teacher notes, grades, communications, and fee information on mobile devices. The reviewed official and independent pages did not provide a verified Alma SIS native iOS or Android store listing; therefore “mobile interface” is supported, while “native iOS+Android apps” is unverified. [4] [15]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment
Alma Start provides mobile-ready registration, re-enrollment, applications and admissions, customizable digital forms, secure document upload, saved progress, applicant-by-applicant review/editing, notifications and alerts, and a workflow intended to eliminate duplicate entry. The official package comparison also lists file-cabinet submissions and student/parent portals. [2] [3] [15]

#### Student information and records
Core SIS stores biographical and program data, household and associated contacts, emergency contacts, calendars, flexible groups, activities, critical medical data, immunizations, critical flags, custom fields, and a virtual/student filing cabinet. Data administrators get spreadsheet-style editing, validated bulk imports, bulk editing, mailing labels, stock reports, and historical enrollment records. [2] [3] [13]

#### Attendance
Attendance supports positive and negative attendance, teacher and administrator entry, attendance reports, attendance notifications, truancy reports, seating charts, and attendance dashboards. BeaconAI adds chronic-absence and submission-consistency views. [2] [3] [4]

#### Timetable, master scheduling, and academic operations
Alma lists advanced scheduling, walk-in scheduling, course and class management, course requests, calendars, graduation tracking, and multi-school/district oversight. “Advanced Scheduling” is an upgrade in the independent-school packages and included in the district package; no separate published scheduling price was found. [2] [12] [13]

#### Gradebook, assessment, and examinations
The gradebook supports traditional, standards-based and competency/proficiency grading; assignment management; missing-assignment tracking; calculated class grades; configurable competencies and rubrics; mastery and roll-up calculations; weighted assignments; skills-based learning; standards tracking; assessment tracking; and GPA equivalencies. Public materials do not describe a separate exam-engine module with question banks or proctoring. [2] [9] [10]

#### Report cards and transcripts
Academic records include report cards, progress reports, transcripts, enrollment history, imported historical grades, flexible GPA calculations, class ranks, graduation tracking, standards-based report cards, and electronic distribution. Report Builder and custom reports support school-specific layouts, although reviewers report that complex reporting can require CSV manipulation. [2] [10] [11]

#### Learning management and homework
Alma presents SIS and LMS tools together and lists built-in LMS, assignment management, lesson planning and blended learning. Its strongest documented learning workflow is interoperability: assignments, grades and feedback sync with Google Classroom, Canvas, Microsoft Teams, Schoology and Seesaw. Public pages do not document a full native course- or examination-authoring suite. [1] [9] [10] [16]

#### Communication
Communication includes student, staff and household messaging; group messaging; teacher-to-student messaging; embedded messaging in incidents; email-style notifications; and text alerts. The official integrations catalog includes ParentSquare, Remind, SchoolMessenger, OneCall Now, Signal Vine and other communications vendors. I found no public evidence of a native WhatsApp channel; SMS/text support should not be interpreted as WhatsApp support. [2] [3]

#### Parent, student, teacher and administrator portals
Parent and student portals expose grades, assignments, attendance, schedules, teacher notes, report cards, communications and, where enabled, fee plans. Alma supports role-based views, district/school dashboards, custom roles and permissions, and English/Spanish portals. A distinct teacher app is not marketed; teacher workflows are in the web platform and gradebook. [1] [2] [15] [16]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting
Fee Management supports any fee type, one-time/recurring invoices, configurable invoice and tuition plans, full or parity payments, credit-card, ACH and cash tracking, parent/mobile access, processing and reporting. No general-ledger accounting or payroll module is established. Payment integrations include e~Funds and PushCoin. [2] [3] [13]

#### Staff and user administration
Alma supports staff/teacher accounts, directories, role-based permissions, SSO, custom roles, account provisioning and district-level administrative controls. It is not a full HRIS: no native recruiting, benefits, time-and-attendance payroll, or payroll tax functions were documented. [2] [3] [9]

#### Health and medical
The native package includes medical notes and contacts, critical medical data and immunizations. Alma’s App Center also connects to CareDox, Health Etools, Health Office Anywhere and SNAP. Public documentation did not verify a comprehensive clinical record, medication administration, or nurse-case-management module. [2] [3]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care
Incident Tracking supports customizable incident codes, minor/major categories, workflow tools, multiple students on one report, and embedded messaging. BeaconAI provides incident heat maps, location/time comparisons, coding-gap identification and trend views. The platform does not publicly present a separate counseling or pastoral-care case-management product. [2] [4]

#### Special education and safeguarding
BeaconAI lists IEP and 504 tracking, ESL/ELL trends and intervention-timing signals; the App Center lists IEP Anywhere, IEP Direct and IEP Writer integrations. This is meaningful student-support and compliance visibility, but the reviewed sources do not prove that Alma natively authors IEP documents or provides a complete safeguarding/child-protection case system. [3] [4]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting
Operational analytics include custom reporting, embedded visualizations, a BI connector, district/school dashboards and summary views. BeaconAI provides attendance/truancy, enrollment, grading, family-engagement and behavior dashboards, subgroup/equity views, data-quality flags, cross-school comparisons and board-ready snapshots. [2] [4]

#### AI features
BeaconAI is Alma’s production analytics package and is marketed as using the live SIS data to surface patterns early, including chronic-absence risk, D/F trends, family disengagement, behavior hot spots, assessment gaps and data-quality problems. Alma’s 2023 CEO interview also described predictive analysis and AI-assisted state-reporting opportunities. No conversational AI assistant, generative lesson planner, or autonomous decision-maker was verified. [4] [16]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance
The platform offers state-specific fields, push-button reports, validations, a data dictionary, dedicated state support, compliance reporting, Ed-Fi and CEDS-aligned APIs, and state-level collection/validation/certification infrastructure. New Hampshire selected Alma for iNHDEX in 2022; the state says Alma users can submit with one click, receive validation-error navigation back to the SIS, run SASID checks and synchronize calendars. [2] [5] [6]

#### Mobile access
Alma Now provides mobile family/student access to learning activity, schedules, teacher notes, assignments, grades, communications and fees. Alma Start is mobile optimized for enrollment. There is no verified public evidence of separate native staff, parent and student apps in the Apple App Store or Google Play; mobile browser access is the safer classification. [4] [15]

#### Integrations and APIs
The App Center advertises 125+ push-button integrations, a searchable vendor library, configurable data sharing, an integrations dashboard, secure file transfer, public APIs and OneRoster API/SFTP. Examples span Google Classroom, Canvas, Microsoft Teams, Schoology, Blackboard, ClassLink, Clever, Google Drive/Docs, ParentSquare, Finalsite, Apple School Manager, Destiny, Raptor, transportation, cafeteria, health and Kindful fundraising systems. [2] [3]

#### Security and privacy
Controls publicly described include MFA, roles/permissions, cloud-native architecture, scheduled uptime, NIST 800-53 guidance, password controls and FERPA/COPPA provisions. Alma displays a GDPR badge and discusses children’s data and school/district control. SOC 2, ISO 27001, India DPDP readiness, hosting regions and a current independent audit were not verified. [2] [9] [13]

**Not verified as native modules:** transport routing, library management, hostel/boarding, inventory/assets, alumni/fundraising, payroll/accounting, WhatsApp communications, and full special-education case management are either integration-only or not documented. This distinction prevents the App Center’s broad connector list from being mistaken for native functionality.

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

* **BeaconAI:** production dashboards connect attendance, grades, enrollment, family engagement, incidents, assessments and data quality to early-warning operational action. [4]
* **Navigator onboarding:** step-by-step migration/project management with tasks, GPA setup, schedules, LMS integrations and progress monitoring. [16]
* **Spreadsheet Data Editor:** a bulk-edit workspace with validation, often singled out by administrators as a practical differentiator. [1] [11]
* **App Center:** self-service vendor selection, templates, data-sharing controls, update frequency and integration monitoring rather than one-off custom projects. [3] [16]
* **State-to-school data exchange:** iNHDEX and the ESP acquisition extend validation, certification, data dictionary and reporting capabilities beyond a conventional school SIS. [5] [18]
* **Configurable forms and workflows:** custom forms, fields, roles, permissions, incident workflows and reports allow school-specific processes without a separate development project. [2] [3]

### Pricing Details

Alma has no public price list, per-student/monthly rate, free trial, setup fee, implementation fee or tier price. The official package page names Foundations, Core and Essentials plus a District Solutions Package, but shows feature checkmarks rather than prices; Fee Management, Parent Messaging, School Messaging, Compliance Reporting and Advanced Scheduling are listed as upgrades/components. [12] [13]

Alma’s terms say school and district subscription length, estimated enrollment, fees and payment schedule are agreed in a separate written Statement of Work. Student, parent and teacher accounts are free to those users only when their school or district pays the subscription. Capterra and Software Advice both report “contact vendor”/custom quote and no free trial; neither opened source provides a defensible third-party Alma price range. [9] [10] [11]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Alma advertises a public API, 125+ App Center integrations, OneRoster via API/SFTP and secure file transfer. State materials mention Ed-Fi and CEDS. SSO connectors include ClassLink, Clever, GG4L and Pearson EasyBridge. Google Classroom/Drive/Docs cover Google Workspace; Microsoft Teams uses SDS and Graph for provisioning, rosters, assignments, grades and feedback. Canvas, Schoology, Seesaw, Blackboard and iReady are also listed. Payment connectors include e~Funds and PushCoin. No public LTI/SIF or 1EdTech certification evidence, or developer marketplace beyond App Center, was found. [2] [3] [5] [16]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Alma describes cloud-native delivery, MFA, configurable roles and permissions, 99.99% scheduled uptime and NIST 800-53 guidance. Its terms explicitly address FERPA “school official” handling and COPPA consent responsibilities, while the privacy policy describes children’s information, school/district control and deletion requests. Alma’s site displays a GDPR badge. [2] [13] [20]

No public SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certificate is linked from reviewed official pages; no provider or hosting region is disclosed, and India’s DPDP Act is not addressed. No publicly reported Alma SIS breach with a confirmed date was found in the reviewed sources, which is not evidence that none occurred. The privacy policy still shows an effective date of May 15, 2015; buyers should request current security and data-processing documents. [20]

### Strengths

* Broad native K–12 core coverage across records, attendance, grading, report cards, enrollment, communications, portals, payments and state reporting.
* Strong usability signal: Capterra reports 4.5/5 ease of use, Software Advice 4.5/5, and reviewers repeatedly call the interface intuitive and modern. [9] [10] [11]
* Practical interoperability through an API, OneRoster, SSO, App Center, LMS synchronization and secure file transfer.
* BeaconAI and the state-data/ESP expansion make analytics and compliance more distinctive than a basic small-school SIS.
* Customer support and onboarding are a recurring positive in independent reviews and Alma’s customer evidence. [1] [10] [11]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

* Pricing is quote-only, so schools cannot compare total cost, module cost, payment fees or implementation cost without a sales process. [9] [10] [11]
* Capterra and Software Advice reviewers report limited or difficult-to-customize reporting, CSV workarounds and trouble with complex higher-grade reporting. [9] [10]
* Users report occasional glitches or downtime; Software Advice includes a review describing a workday affected when Alma was unavailable, and Capterra flags occasional technical difficulties. [9] [11]
* Some reviewers describe standards-based grading and gradebook workflows as time-consuming or prone to sync/save/view issues, especially with Google Classroom or higher grade levels. [10]
* Support is generally praised, but reviews also mention slow response/follow-up during difficult issues and a need for trial-and-error. G2 includes a user who said it took time to get help; Capterra summarizes concerns about uneven response times and sensitive-data visibility. [9] [10]
* Native breadth has limits: transport, library, hostel, inventory, payroll, fundraising and full special-education workflows depend on integrations or are not publicly documented; native mobile app-store support is also unverified.

### Key Milestones

* **2012–2013:** PitchBook and Tracxn databases list earlier founding years; these conflict with the 2014 date supported by G2 and Tech.co. PitchBook reports one financing round and $699,000 raised; other database amounts are obscured. [7] [14] [17] [23]

* **2022:** Alma announced BeaconAI as the replacement for Data Lab, adding real-time operational analytics and pattern surfacing. [1] [16]
* **2023:** Alma’s CEO described expansion to 46 states and 54 countries, continued infrastructure scaling, Navigator, Data Lab/BeaconAI and predictive-analysis ambitions. [16]
* **March 2024:** Alma released Microsoft Teams Assignments sync using SDS and Microsoft Graph for provisioning, rosters, assignments, grades and feedback. [16]
* **August 6, 2026:** Alma announced acquisition of ESP Solutions Group, extending the connected platform to state agencies and strengthening data management, interoperability and state reporting. [18]
* **November 24, 2026:** Latest official homepage claim reviewed: more than 6 million students, 44 states and 61 countries; current institution count and an official Alma funding total remain undisclosed. [1] [17] [23]
