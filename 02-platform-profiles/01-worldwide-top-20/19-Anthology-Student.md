### Overview

Anthology Student is a higher-education student information system (SIS) and ERP-oriented platform that manages the lifecycle from prospect and admission through registration, academics, financial aid, student accounts and completion. Its strongest fit is an institution that needs a detailed, enrollment-centered record for conventional, online, flexible-term and competency-oriented programs rather than a K–12 school-management suite. The system’s newer web client adds responsive workflows, configurable views, forms, workflow automation, APIs and cloud delivery. [6] [10]

The product descends from Campus Management’s CampusNexus Student. Campus Management began as Integrated Solutions in 1988 and accumulated SIS, financial-aid, CRM, finance and HR capabilities; Anthology was formed in 2020 by combining Campus Management, Campus Labs and iModules. Anthology and Blackboard merged in October 2021. In 2025–2026, Anthology entered Chapter 11 restructuring: Blackboard retained the teaching-and-learning business, Ellucian acquired Student/Finance & HCM, and Encoura acquired Reach and the lifecycle-engagement/student-success portfolio. [1] [2] [4] [5]

### Platform Classification Explained

* **Browser/web:** The current Student web client is the primary user experience. Anthology’s help center documents a web app with tiles, student profiles, responsive self-service pages and role-based authorizations. The G2 profile describes the product as SaaS-based, and the 25.2 release was deployed to Anthology Cloud 1.0 and Cloud 2.0 customers. [6] [10]
* **Cloud hosting:** Anthology Student Cloud 1/2 is documented; AI services use Microsoft Azure OpenAI, with primary hosting in the United States and possible service resources in Canada, the United Kingdom or France. Ellucian separately states that its cloud services use AWS security controls and ISO/SOC programs, but product-specific hosting terms should be confirmed contractually. [11] [12]
* **Desktop/on-premise:** This is not a desktop-only product, but legacy CampusNet and on-premise clients remain supported in release documentation. The documentation suite also lists Installation Manager, replication/upgrade/availability groups and database tools; database tools are explicitly for older on-premise releases. [10]
* **Mobile:** Student self-service pages are mobile-friendly and Capterra lists web, Android and iPhone/iPad deployment, but no dedicated Anthology Student native app name or store listing was verified. Apple’s Anthology Inc. developer page showed “Blackboard Education View,” which is a different product. [7] [9]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment
Prospect and inquiry records, lead/source tracking, expected start dates, admissions representatives, interviews and tasks, document tracking with requested/received/approved/expiration status, programs and program versions, applicant-to-student conversion, application data, enrollment statuses and enrollment reporting are documented. Anthology Student supports configurable admissions-to-academic-record relationships and registration, including waitlists, registration restrictions and enrollment changes. [10] [13]

#### Student information and academic records
The Contact Manager/Student Profile centralizes personal, contact, recruitment, previous-education, citizenship/nationality, athlete, veteran and international-student indicators, notes and SMS subscription fields. Academic Records covers campuses, terms, programs, areas of study, courses, sections, prerequisites, transfer work, degree requirements, degree progress audit, degree pathways, elective pools, requirement rules, course repeats, graduation status and holds. [14] [15]

#### Attendance
Attendance records are attached to student courses and include date, start time, time attended, time absent, excused-absence status, type, unit and notes. Standard views include term-attendance grids/charts, and attendance violations can stop financial-aid disbursement through hold groups. [15] [16]

#### Timetable and master scheduling
Academic calendars, terms, class sections, meeting patterns, buildings, rooms, locations, instructors, secondary instructors, days/times, seat capacity, waitlists, registration relationships and cross-listed/course-sharing rules are supported. The Class Scheduling AI Assistant analyzes historical registration and section data to identify section demand and faculty-hire needs. [15] [17]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations
Student course records store letter, numeric, midterm and final grades, grade points, pass/fail, credits, hours, repeats, withdrawals and last date of attendance. Grade-change reasons and grade reports are available as standard views. A full LMS assessment/exam-authoring product is not part of the verified Student SIS feature set; Blackboard Learn is a separate portfolio product. [15]

#### Report cards and transcripts
Degree progress audits, academic history, grades, credits and degree-awarded records support transcript and completion operations. Hold codes can block transcript printing or release and can prevent graduation-status changes. K–12 report-card workflows are not documented as a native module. [16]

#### Learning management and homework
Anthology Student is an SIS/ERP, not a replacement for an LMS. Course and registration data can integrate with learning systems, while course materials, homework, discussion forums and online assessment belong to Blackboard Learn or another LMS. The G2 page contains user comments about learning resources, but those comments should not be read as proof that Student itself is Blackboard Learn. [6]

#### Communication and contact management
Contact Manager stores tasks, activities, notes, notification triggers and communication history. Student information includes SMS subscription and provider fields; the broader Anthology portfolio includes communication tools, but no verified native WhatsApp or push-notification module was found for Student. Email/SMS workflows and institution-configured alerts are supported; WhatsApp is not publicly documented. [14] [15]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals
The Student/Portal documentation supports student self-service, including registration, schedules, account summary, statements, payment access and other profile/account pages. The 25.2 release added a mobile-friendly My Finances account summary with posted balance, pending charges, expected funding and estimated balance. There is no evidence of a K–12 parent portal; staff/faculty access is role-based in the web client. [10] [18]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting
Student Accounts includes ledger cards, charges, payments, payment schedules, electronic drafts, ACH/credit-card holds, refunds, stipends, student statements, third-party invoices, subsidiary accounts, account aging, general-ledger posting detail and expected financial-aid funding. The product can export general-ledger files and process Title IV-related student-account transactions. Anthology Finance & HCM is the separate institutional finance/ERP product, not merely a Student billing screen. [10] [16] [18]

#### Financial aid
Financial Aid supports FAFSA/ISIR data, Student Aid Index, academic years, award years, term and non-term programs, clock-hour programs, loan periods, payment periods, packaging methods, auto-packaging, manual awards, Pell/SEOG/TEACH/Direct Loan workflows, disbursement schedules, MPN data, COD and NSLDS reporting, crossover packaging, satisfactory-academic-progress/pace evaluation and R2T4/refund calculations. [19]

#### HR, staff and payroll
Faculty Workload is a separately documented integrated product. Anthology Finance & HCM documentation covers human capital management and payroll, including employee/staff records and payroll processes; the 2025 release notes document synchronizing a worker’s “Known As” field into Student staff records. HR/payroll is therefore available in the broader Anthology ERP footprint, not necessarily in the core Student license. [10] [20]

#### Housing/hostel and boarding
Student Services includes Housing, housing status, roommate matching and room placement. Housing holds can prevent automatic roommate matching or placement, while manual placement remains possible. This is higher-education housing rather than a full K–12 hostel/boarding-management suite. [16] [18]

#### Career services
Career Services is a native help-center module with placement records, candidate search, employer-facing placement workflows, graduation/placement holds and placement salary reporting. [10] [16]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting
Views is a distinctive Student feature for building reusable grid and chart views from related data across Academic Records, Admissions, Career Services, Contact Manager, Financial Aid, Student Accounts and Student Services. Users can filter, pin, export and query related objects through OData; standard views cover prospects, documents, active students, grades, attendance, course sections, degrees and aid disbursements. Power BI configuration is documented. [15]

#### AI features
The production AI Product Guide/Help AI is an opt-in sidecar for Cloud 1/2 customers, powered by Azure OpenAI GPT-4o and curated product documentation; it does not access student or school data. The Class Scheduling AI Assistant uses Azure OpenAI to answer constrained section-demand questions from class-seat projections and recent registration history. These are scoped assistants, not a general autonomous student-success agent or proven predictive-retention engine. [11] [17]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance
Regulatory US and 1098-T Processing are separate documented release streams. Student includes Title IV, COD, NSLDS, FAFSA/ISIR, SSCR, R2T4, Gainful Employment, SAI, 1098-T and enrollment-reporting processes. Internationalization includes English, Spanish (Latin America) and French (Canada) system locales. State-specific reporting beyond documented regulatory products should be validated with the institution’s configuration. [10] [19] [20]

#### Integrations and APIs
Anthology Student provides REST APIs, OData queries, SOAP/WCF Service Catalog services, an object library, student data-model/ERD documentation, Connector Configuration and GitHub resources. REST access requires an Azure Active Directory account, a Student staff record, Security Console permissions and an application key. APIs are licensed-campus only; third-party public use is prohibited without Anthology/Ellucian consent. [10] [21]

#### Security, permissions and privacy
The Security Console provides staff groups, roles, authorizations, hold overrides and granular module permissions. Anthology Student’s AI documentation says the product is ISO 27001/27017/27018 certified and pursuing ISO 27701; Ellucian’s current cloud program reports SOC 1/2/3, ISO 27001:2022, PCI DSS and other controls. FERPA, GDPR and state privacy obligations remain shared institution/vendor contractual responsibilities; no Student-specific public certification for COPPA or India’s DPDP Act was verified. [12] [17]

#### Not native or not publicly verified
No native Student modules were found for school transport, library circulation, inventory/assets, health/medical records, behavior/discipline, special education/safeguarding, alumni fundraising or a dedicated homework/LMS product. Anthology/Encoura has separate Reach, Encompass, Advance, Raise and student-success/advancement products, and Blackboard has a separate LMS. [1] [2]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

* **Cloud 1/2 web client:** The modern browser interface combines role-based tiles, student profiles, responsive self-service and release-managed SaaS deployment.
* **Views and OData:** Administrators can build reusable grid/chart views across related objects rather than relying only on fixed reports. [15]
* **Complex aid engine:** Term/non-term, clock-hour, crossover, COD, MPN, R2T4 and automated packaging logic make it unusually specialized for U.S. postsecondary financial aid. [19]
* **AI Product Guide:** An opt-in documentation assistant uses Azure OpenAI GPT-4o without accessing student data. [11]
* **Scheduling AI Assistant:** Constrained natural-language analysis of seat projections identifies undersubscribed or over-demand sections and additional-section needs. [17]
* **No-code extension layer:** Forms Builder and Workflow Composer provide configurable forms and process automation; the exact workflow catalog is institution-specific.
* **Detailed control model:** Security Console, student groups and cross-module hold codes support operational controls such as blocking registration, transcripts, disbursements or housing placement. [16]

### Pricing Details

There is no public Anthology Student price list, free tier, trial or published per-student/per-year rate. Capterra listed “Contact vendor for pricing” and no free trial on August 20, 2026; G2 also showed no numerical public price. Implementation, migration, training, managed services, modules, hosting and support fees are quote-based and should be requested from Ellucian. A Capterra review reports that migration from CampusVue to CampusNexus was “tremendously expensive” and that managed services could also be expensive, but this is historical user commentary rather than a current price. [6] [7]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Documented interoperability includes Student REST APIs, OData, SOAP/WCF Service Catalog, Azure Active Directory authentication, an ERD/object library, Connector Configuration, Power BI and integration with Anthology Reach, Finance & HCM, Occupation Insight, Portal, Forms Builder, Workflow and Student Verification. Blackboard SIS integration is a separate pathway. I found no public evidence that Anthology Student is certified for OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi or SIF, nor a public Clever/ClassLink marketplace certification. SSO is supported through the documented AAD path; payment and accounting exports are institution/configuration dependent. [10] [21]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

The strongest public evidence is Anthology’s product AI documentation, which states ISO 27001/27017/27018 certification for Anthology Student and Azure OpenAI controls that do not use client prompts for model retraining. Current Ellucian cloud documentation reports SOC 1/2/3, ISO 27001:2022, PCI DSS, NIST 800-171, CSA and HECVAT-related controls, with AWS monitoring and incident-response tooling. These are vendor/cloud-program claims and do not replace a customer’s contract, DPA or security review. FERPA and GDPR are relevant obligations; the sources reviewed did not verify COPPA or India DPDP Act certification, a named Student data-hosting region, or a publicly reported Student-specific breach. The 2025–2026 restructuring and ownership transfer are material governance events, but the Ellucian FAQ says existing hosting, security protocols, compliance requirements, integrations and contracts continued without immediate change. [12] [17] [21]

### Strengths

* Deep higher-education SIS coverage, including admissions, academic records, financial aid, student accounts, housing and regulatory reporting.
* Handles complex U.S. aid and non-term/clock-hour program models that many general school platforms do not.
* Strong data model, configurable Views, OData, REST/SOAP APIs, Forms Builder, Workflow Composer and granular permissions.
* Cloud modernization is meaningful: Help AI, scheduling AI, responsive self-service and current release cadence are documented.
* Reviews recognize breadth, centralization, student records and routine usability; SoftwareReviews recorded 95% positive emotional sentiment across 17 reviews. [8]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

* UX is uneven across the installed base: G2 users cite occasional slowness, dated appearance, complexity, downtime and limited mobile functionality; the current G2 average is only 3.4/5. [6]
* Capterra users report slow or frustrating support, duplicated inquiries, unintuitive schedule lookup and difficult history filtering; ease of use is 4.1/5 and customer service 3.7/5. [7]
* SoftwareReviews users praise reliability and the portal but cite limited customization, unintuitive multi-step workflows and the need for training. [8]
* Pricing is opaque and implementation/managed-services costs can be significant; there is no public rate card or standard package. [7]
* The 2026 split among Ellucian, Encoura and Blackboard creates roadmap and contract-governance uncertainty, even though continuity was promised for existing SIS/ERP customers. [1] [2] [5]
* Core Student is not a full school-management suite: transport, library, medical, discipline, special education, alumni/fundraising and native LMS/homework functions require other products or integrations.

### Key Milestones

* **1988:** Campus Management’s predecessor, Integrated Solutions, was founded with Class Software. [4]
* **1995–2018:** Campus Management expanded through acquisitions including Spectrum Software, Donor2 Fundraising, Talisma and TopSchool. [4]
* **2020:** Campus Management, Campus Labs and iModules combined to form Anthology; CampusNexus Student became part of the unified portfolio. [3] [4]
* **2021:** Anthology announced its Blackboard merger on September 13 and completed the merger on October 25. [3] [5]
* **2025:** Anthology Student 25.2 added Help AI, data-view enhancements, aid and student-account changes; Anthology later filed Chapter 11 during its financial restructuring. [18]
* **February 2, 2026:** Encoura closed its acquisition of Anthology’s Lifecycle Engagement and Student Success portfolio, including Reach, Encompass, Advance and related products. [2]
* **March 2, 2026:** Blackboard announced emergence from Chapter 11 and confirmed the sale of Enterprise Operations, including Anthology Student and Finance & HCM, to Ellucian. [1]
* **2026:** Ellucian’s welcome page stated that 260+ Anthology SIS/ERP customers joined Ellucian, with existing contracts, integrations, hosting and security controls continuing immediately after close. [5]
