### Overview

PowerSchool SIS is the administrative system of record in a much broader K-12 portfolio. It centralizes enrollment, student and family records, attendance, schedules, grades, reports, fees, portals and state/provincial workflows, then connects those records to Schoology Learning, Performance Matters, Special Programs, Behavior Support, MTSS, Naviance CCLR, SchoolMessenger, analytics, HR and ERP products. [2] [3] [4]

The original SIS was developed in 1997, acquired by Apple in 2001 and Pearson in 2006, sold to Vista Equity Partners in 2015 for $350 million, taken public in 2021 and taken private again by Bain Capital on October 1, 2024 for approximately $5.6 billion. [6] [16] [25] [26]

### Platform Classification Explained

The core system is browser-accessed cloud software. PowerSchool describes its products as cloud-based; Analytics & Insights/MTSS materials describe cloud hosting, disaster recovery, 24/7/365 monitoring and up to 99.9% uptime. The privacy statement names AWS, Microsoft Azure, Oracle, Snowflake and Microsoft SQL infrastructure and says most customer data is hosted in the United States, with some products in Canada. [12] [14] [15]

The web Parent/Student Portal is accessible anywhere, while PowerSchool Mobile is native for iPhone/iPad/Apple Watch and Android. The ecosystem also has Schoology, SchoolMessenger, eSchoolPLUS Family, Unified Classroom Behavior, PowerSchool ERP Employee and Planboard apps. [21] [22] [23] No current desktop client or customer-managed on-premise SIS was evidenced.

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

PowerSchool Enrollment provides mobile-friendly, multilingual registration, configurable fields, workflows, document/form uploads, reenrollment, verification and automated SIS transfer. The flagship product is SIS-agnostic and supports school-choice/lottery processes; Enrollment Express works inside PowerSchool SIS. The vendor reports 3,500+ districts and 98M+ enrollment packets in 2025. [7]

#### Student information and records

SIS maintains demographics, contacts, programs, school/course history, schedules, alerts, logs, secure notes, data validation and reporting terms. Administrators get advanced/compound/wildcard/stored searches, configurable pages, tables and fields, mass entry, code sets, special operations and field-level security. [3] [13]

#### Attendance

Native features include daily/period attendance, attendance codes, bell schedules, teacher entry, searches, reports and family visibility. SchoolMessenger SafeArrival adds absence reporting and follow-up; Analytics and MTSS connect attendance to profiles and interventions. [3] [9] [12]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

The SIS manages periods, bell schedules, course sections, class and student schedules, with schedule visibility in portals and mobile. Public documentation confirms schedule administration, but detailed master-scheduling implementation is largely customer documentation/services material. [3] [13]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

PowerTeacher Pro supports assignments, grades, comments, standards-based/traditional grading, attendance and class data. Schoology adds assignments, discussions, standards mastery and conditional release. Performance Matters adds item banks, collaborative/online, paper, observational and performance assessments, accommodations, standards/item/student reporting, LockDown Browser, Kiosk Mode, AI item creation and rubric scoring. [4] [28]

#### Report cards and transcripts

SIS supports customizable reports, report cards, transcripts, GPA/class rank, schedules, history and state reporting. Schoology standards passback and Naviance electronic transcript/document sharing extend records into instruction and post-secondary workflows. [3] [11] [13]

#### Learning management and homework

Schoology Learning provides courses, curriculum, assignments, assessments, discussions, groups, templates, lesson planning, standards mastery, differentiated instruction, conditional release and family visibility. It integrates with SIS, Performance Matters, C&I, MTSS, Behavior, Naviance and analytics, plus Google Drive/Assignments, Microsoft Office/Assignments, LTI and OneRoster 1.2. [4]

#### Communication (SMS, email, push, WhatsApp)

SchoolMessenger supports phone, email, SMS/text, app and web messaging; mass/emergency alerts; two-way staff-family conversations; classroom posts; websites; events; multilingual behavior messaging; attendance alerts; and SecureFile delivery of report cards/schedules. WhatsApp was not identified as a native channel in reviewed official materials. [9]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

The web Parent/Student Portal supports multiple children, grades, assignments, scores, attendance, GPA where enabled, schedules, bulletins, fees, meal balances and notifications. PowerTeacher Pro is the teacher workspace. District administrators control which portal features are enabled. [3] [23]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

SIS includes student fees, meal/fee balances, transactions and mobile collection. ERP covers fund accounting, chart of accounts, budgets, encumbrances, general ledger, payables, receivables, contracts, fixed assets, grants/projects, purchasing and 400+ native K-12 reports. Named payment/invoicing subprocessors include Vanco, NMI, Authorize.Net, Worldpay and Stripe. [6] [15]

#### HR, staff and payroll

Recruiting/HR covers job distribution, applications, screening, interviews, hiring, onboarding, employee records and substitute management. ERP adds employee information, compensation, benefits, attendance, schedules, development, self-service and mobile access; payroll scope varies by licensed product. [6] [29]

#### Transport

SIS administrator resources include bus-route search, and the partner directory includes transportation integrations such as BatOnRoute. Public material does not establish a full native fleet-optimization/GPS module in SIS itself. [13] [14]

#### Inventory and assets

SIS documents asset management, inventory creation and checkout. ERP supports purchasing and fixed assets; SIS reporting can expose asset data alongside student and operational records. [6] [13]

#### Health and medical

Enrollment collects configurable health/safety/contact data; SIS reports include health and alerts, and health-center integrations appear in the partner directory. A complete clinical-record product was not evidenced as a standalone native module. [3] [7] [14]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

Behavior Support provides PBIS/SEL records, positive/corrective/neutral/referral dashboards, positivity ratios, trends by grade/location/time/group, family messaging and translation. MTSS adds profiles, goals, student plans, Tier 1–3 interventions, notes, attachments, progress charts and intervention-session attendance. [10] [12]

#### Special education and safeguarding

Special Programs covers IEP, 504, ELL, gifted/talented, service capture, digital signatures, forms, case management, deadlines, alerts, dashboards, family access and role-based documents. PowerSchool states it supports 25 state-specific models plus a federal model configurable for all 50 states. [8]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Analytics & Insights provides research-based dashboards, ad hoc reporting, role/row security, filters by school, grade, staff, ethnicity, gender, program and group, customizable KPIs/layouts/visualizations/language, historical profiles and individualized plans. It reports 560+ districts and 50,000+ interventions created in 2025. Connected Intelligence consolidates data for analytics and natural-language analysis. [5] [27]

#### AI features

PowerBuddy is a production assistant for students, teachers, families, administrators, college/career users and analysts. Features include conversational tutoring, lesson/assessment drafting, standards-aligned content, recommendations, college/career exploration, recommendation-letter drafting, natural-language data analysis, item generation and AI rubric scoring. [10] [28]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

SIS provides state/provincial reporting, data validation, reporting terms and compliance-oriented attendance, behavior, health, graduation and demographic reports. The SIS page claims support for 56 states/provinces; exact packages vary by jurisdiction. [3] [13]

#### Mobile apps

PowerSchool Mobile provides dashboard widgets for GPA, classes, assignments, attendance, bulletins, meal balance and fees; grade/attendance push notifications; teacher comments; calendars; schedules; and multi-child account linking. Separate apps include Schoology, SchoolMessenger, ERP Employee, Unified Classroom Behavior, eSchoolPLUS Family and Planboard. [21] [22] [23]

#### Integrations and APIs

PowerSchool advertises 75+ certified SIS integrations and a large partner directory covering learning, assessment, health, payments, transportation, library, cafeteria, safety and HR. Examples include Clever, Canvas, MasteryConnect, SNAP Health Center, Wonde and Vanco. A detailed, universally documented public SIS REST API contract was not located; buyers should validate API, sandbox, export, webhook and rate-limit terms. [4] [14] [18]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Connected Operating System:** unifies home, classroom, student support and central-office work. [2]
- **Contextual PowerBuddy AI:** spans tutoring, teacher planning, administration, CCLR, assessment and data analysis. [10]
- **Whole-child intervention loop:** combines attendance, assessment, behavior, MTSS, Special Programs and analytics. [5] [8] [12]
- **No-code configuration:** Enrollment, MTSS and Special Programs expose configurable fields, forms, plans and workflows. [7] [8] [12]
- **Large K-12 scale:** 15,000+ organizations/60M+ students claimed, with 56 states/provinces and 90+ countries in dated claims. [3] [6] [16]
- **Integrated ERP and workforce layer:** connects enrollment, budgets, HR, purchasing, payroll and staffing planning. [6]

### Pricing Details

No official public price list, tier table, per-student/year rate or implementation-fee schedule was found. PowerSchool directs prospects to a sales expert, so SIS, Schoology, Enrollment, analytics, ERP, support, implementation and add-ons are quote-based and configuration-dependent. [30]

Capterra’s listing, updated September 29, 2026, displays **$7 “Flat Rate”**, no free trial and no comparable unit such as student/year or school/month; treat it only as a marketplace estimate. G2 shows a five-dollar-sign perceived-cost indicator and three-month average implementation time from reviewers, not vendor pricing. [18] [20]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Public evidence includes Schoology OneRoster 1.2, LTI, Google/Microsoft integrations, 75+ certified SIS integrations, a large partner directory and 1EdTech TrustEd App verification. The 1EdTech vendor page confirms PowerSchool Group LLC membership but does not expose a simple current list of every active certification. Clever/ClassLink are relevant ecosystem options, but Ed-Fi, SIF and a universally documented public SIS API were not verified in the opened pages. [4] [14] [24]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

PowerSchool reports ISO 27001:2022, annual SOC 2 Type 2 examinations, 1EdTech TrustEd App verification, MFA, role-based controls, 24/7/365 security operations, logging, vulnerability scanning and 30+ annual penetration tests. It cites FERPA, COPPA and applicable state/provincial/federal privacy rules and describes itself as a FERPA school official/processor when handling education records. [14] [15]

The privacy statement says most customer data is hosted in the United States, lists AWS/Azure/Snowflake/Oracle subprocessors and describes international transfers using the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework where applicable. It references GDPR/international privacy alignment, but India’s DPDP Act was not expressly named in the reviewed public pages and should be confirmed contractually. [15]

**Breach chronology:** PowerSchool says it detected unauthorized exfiltration from certain SIS environments through the PowerSource support portal on December 28, 2024; notified affected SIS customers January 7, 2025; began applicable regulator/attorney-general notifications January 27; and issued a final update March 7. Potential data included name, contact information, date of birth, limited medical-alert information, SSN/SIN and related information, varying by person. PowerSchool reported no evidence of credit-card/bank data, other products being affected or continuing unauthorized activity. It offered two years of identity protection and adult credit monitoring; enrollment closed July 31, 2025. [27] [28]

### Strengths

- Broad K-12 coverage from SIS through LMS, assessment, special programs, communications, interventions, CCLR, analytics, ERP and HR. [2]
- Exceptional scale and procurement familiarity, with 15,000+/60M+ and 18,000+/90+ dated company claims. [6] [16]
- Strong standards/partner story through OneRoster, LTI, Google/Microsoft and 75+ integrations. [4] [14]
- Deep configurability in SIS pages/fields, reports, enrollment forms, intervention plans and special-program workflows. [3] [7] [8] [12]
- Production AI rather than a roadmap-only announcement. [10] [28]
- Native mobile access, although quality differs sharply between iOS and Android. [21] [22]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- G2 and Capterra reviews cite learning difficulty, technical issues, too many screens, confusing navigation and a feature set that intimidates new users. [18] [19]
- Reviewers report extra training/plugin costs, while official pricing is quote-only. [19] [20] [30]
- Capterra cites obtuse Enrollment migration/year rollover; G2 requests simpler reporting, more dependable sync and easier bulk workflows. [18] [19]
- Mobile reliability is polarized: Apple is 4.6/947K, but Google Play is 2.5/39.6K, with complaints about crashes, stale grades and notifications. Apple reviews also cite “missing” assignment confusion and sync delays. [21] [22]
- A 2026 G2 reviewer described a failed multi-district merge, billing problems and substantial customer-side cleanup; this is an individual account, not proof of a systemic failure. [18]
- The 2024/2025 SIS breach is a material trust weakness for a platform holding sensitive student, educator and limited medical data, even though PowerSchool reported no operational disruption. [27] [28]
- Library, hostel/boarding and alumni/fundraising were not substantiated as native SIS modules; treat them as partner or adjacent-system requirements.
