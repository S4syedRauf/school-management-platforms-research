### Overview

Workday Student is Workday’s cloud student information system for higher education. It brings recruiting, admissions, academic foundation, curriculum, registration, records, advising, financial aid, and student financials onto the same data model used by Workday Human Capital Management and Workday Financial Management [3] [8] [9].

The product matters because higher education has unusually complex academic calendars, program rules, transfer-credit policies, aid calculations, and financial transactions. Workday emphasizes real-time data, configurable business processes, mobile self-service, and AI-guided actions.

Workday was founded by former PeopleSoft executives Dave Duffield and Aneel Bhusri in 2005. Workday announced its intention to build Student in 2013, is described by independent market research as launching Student in 2016, and identifies 2018 as the first higher-education deployment. Workday’s later acquisitions—such as Platfora, Adaptive Insights, Peakon, and HiredScore—strengthened analytics, planning, engagement, and talent capabilities around the platform, but none is presented as the origin of the Student SIS [10] [12].

### Platform Classification Explained

* **Browser/cloud delivery:** Workday describes Student as a single cloud-based system. Student hubs and many self-service tasks are accessed in a browser, with role-based security domains and configurable profiles. The documentation specifically notes that Student Hubs are browser-only and are not available through the mobile app [2] [9].
* **Hosting:** Workday does not publicly identify one cloud provider or one hosting region for every Student tenant in the product pages reviewed. Hosting, regional availability, and tenant configuration therefore need to be confirmed in a contract and security review. Workday does publish enterprise cloud security/compliance material and a current trust center [23] [27].
* **Desktop/on-premise:** No native desktop client or on-premise Student edition is publicly documented. The delivery model is SaaS/browser-based.
* **Native mobile:** The exact shared app name is **Workday**. Workday links both the Apple App Store listing and Android package `com.workday.workdroidapp`; the app provides role-dependent mobile access, push notifications, dashboards, and selected student/employee transactions. Workday cautions that not every web feature is mobile-enabled [20] [21] [22].

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Academic foundation, curriculum, and master scheduling

The academic foundation defines academic units, levels, programs of study, academic calendars, periods, locations, and policies. Curriculum and registration support course catalogs, sections, prerequisites, eligibility rules, reserve capacity, registration appointments, waitlists, concurrent academic lifecycles, stackable credentials, program-of-study relationships, and schedule planning. The student-records page also describes course-demand visibility and planned-versus-actual enrollment analytics for staffing decisions [7] [8]. This is higher-education course scheduling rather than a K–12 bell timetable.

#### Recruiting, admissions, and enrollment

Student Recruiting and Admissions cover prospect and applicant profiles, recruitment notes, events, campaign effectiveness, application management, configurable online applications, routing and evaluation, admission decisions, cohort tracking, financial-aid action items, transfer-credit evaluation, and digital onboarding. Prospects can register for events, complete requirements, view financial-aid tasks, and continue into the student record without a separate handoff. Workday also supports admissions analytics such as application trends, recruitment spend, enrollment funnels, and unmet applicant need [5] [9].

#### Student information and academic records

Student profiles are configurable composite views with contact, personal, education, application, recruitment, financial-aid, history, holds, notes, athletics, and other conditional groups. Records cover academic history, multiple GPAs and GPA calculations, leave-of-absence questionnaires, documents, competencies, program changes, honors, privacy settings, student holds, duplicate management, and third-party access for family or friends. Transfer-credit tools maintain equivalencies individually or in bulk, capture context, support pre-matriculation evaluation, provide an administrator troubleshooting console, and re-evaluate equivalencies when a student changes program [7] [9].

#### Registration and academic progress

Students can check eligibility before registration, save planned schedules, register from a saved schedule or academic planner, join waitlists, and receive push notifications when a seat becomes available. Academic Requirements and Academic Planning evaluate degree requirements, planned and unregistered courses, ineligible courses, expected completion, cohorts, advisors, and progress toward credentials. Students can see holds, contacts, appointments, academic history, and registration tasks in the Academics Hub [7] [8] [9].

#### Grading, assessment, examinations, transcripts, and credentials

The public Student pages explicitly document grading and GPA calculations, grade entry/management, academic history, progress reports, transcript processing, academic requirements, honors, and credentials. The 2026 Student Administration Agent is designed to automate transcript processing, transfer-credit evaluations, and academic-requirements management [3] [7]. Workday’s public feature pages do not establish a standalone LMS-style gradebook, assessment authoring environment, or examination-proctoring module; those are scope questions for an institution’s integration design.

#### Academic advising and student journeys

Advisors can be assigned to students and use academic progress reports, academic requirements, academic plans, cohorts, and student profiles. Workday Student Journeys/engagement paths guide orientation, registration, financial-aid completion, program exploration, onboarding, and other milestones. Engagement overviews show whether students respond to journeys and meet milestones. Academic, financial, and extracurricular information can be carried from admissions into advising [5] [6] [8].

#### Financial aid

Financial Aid includes ISIR processing and verification, action-item assignment, cost-of-attendance assignment, need- and merit-based packaging, private-scholarship automation, a calculation engine, proactive alerts, real-time account refresh, built-in SAI calculation, enrollment-change recalculation, residency-aware changes, nonstandard academic periods, instruction-week calculations, Pell-table/regulatory updates, award rules, disbursement, work-study awards, job search/application, and links to Workday HCM and Payroll for hours, limits, and pay [4].

#### Student finance, fees, billing, payments, and accounting

Student Financials/Finance Hub brings tuition, housing fees where configured, charges, payments, balances, holds, refunds, billing schedules, financial-aid information, and payment actions together. Students can view real-time account information and make payments; authorized parents or payers can receive billing alerts through configured access. Student financial transactions connect to Workday Financial Management for broader accounting, reporting, and institutional finance, rather than representing a separate small-school bookkeeping product [3] [6] [8] [18].

#### Engagement and communication

Workday documents personalized communications through email, text/SMS, in-app messages, push notifications, alerts, reminders, targeted audiences, engagement plans, and journey milestones. Examples include registration reminders, financial-aid requirements, onboarding tasks, and waitlist availability. No WhatsApp channel is identified in the reviewed official documentation [3] [6] [7].

#### Portals and role-based access

The external student site provides an interactive portal for students and prospects. Academics Hub, Financials Hub, Campus Engagement Hub, and Student Config Hub centralize tasks and reports. Faculty, advisors, administrators, and student workers see role-specific tasks; configurable secure third-party access supports family and friends. Workday does not market separate named parent, teacher, and student apps; these personas are served through role-based browser experiences and the shared Workday mobile app [8] [9].

#### Analytics, dashboards, and reporting

Operational and academic insights include enrollment trends, course demand, applicant-pool health, recruitment spend, student progression, retention, degree progress, financial-aid awards, student revenue by academic period, refunds, budget, workload, and program profitability. Workday’s unified employee, finance, and student data model supports real-time reports, segmented dashboards, configurable reports, and cross-campus analysis [1] [3] [7].

#### AI features

Current materials describe AI-powered search and assistance in Student Hubs, AI guidance and recommendations, predictive views such as at-risk-student and top-application signals, and a Student Administration Agent for transcript processing, transfer-credit evaluation, and academic requirements. Workday’s broader responsible-AI platform supplies guardrails and deterministic, auditable business processes; some agents were announced as new capabilities, so institutions should distinguish generally available features from roadmap items in the order form [1] [3] [25] [26].

#### Workday HCM, staff, payroll, and employee-student identity

Student is unified with Workday HCM, Payroll, Financial Management, Adaptive Planning, and Grants Management. This enables one person/Universal ID across student and worker roles, campus-job eligibility and hours, pay slips, financial reporting, and workforce processes. These are powerful suite integrations, but HCM and payroll are not Student-only modules [8] [9] [26].

#### Mobile apps

The shared Workday iOS/Android app supports authorized role-based access, tasks, notifications, dashboards, time and pay actions, and selected Student functions. Native app scope is tenant- and role-dependent; the documentation says Student Hubs remain browser-only, and the app store listing warns that not all Workday features are available on mobile [9] [20] [21] [22].

#### Integrations, APIs, compliance reporting, security, and scope boundaries

Workday offers REST, SOAP, and Graph APIs, OpenAPI specifications, API Gateway/OAuth patterns, Workday Extend, and a partner Marketplace [19] [1]. Public materials support SSO/IAM and configurable domain security, but do not establish native OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, or ClassLink certification for Student. Government and regulatory support is strongest in financial aid (ISIR, Pell tables, compliance updates) and configurable institutional reporting. No native Student modules are publicly evidenced for attendance registers, K–12 homework/LMS, transport, library circulation, hostel/boarding administration, inventory/asset management, health/medical records, behavior/discipline, special education/safeguarding, or alumni/fundraising; those typically require other Workday products or integrations [3] [8] [18] [19].

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

* **Unified student-worker identity:** Universal ID connects a student’s academic and employee roles, including campus-job eligibility, hours, funding, and pay [9].
* **Financial-aid recalculation:** Registration, residency, or other student changes can trigger immediate attendance-cost and aid-package recalculation [4].
* **Transfer-credit lifecycle:** Applicants can evaluate transfer credit before matriculation, administrators can troubleshoot equivalencies, and program changes can trigger re-evaluation [7].
* **Configurable, no-custom-code processes:** Workday’s business-process framework and configurable application/academic rules adapt workflows across departments [3] [5].
* **AI administration agents:** Student Administration Agent targets transcript, transfer-credit, and academic-requirements work, while Student Hubs provide AI search/assistance [3].
* **Cross-campus data core:** Student, employee, finance, grants, and planning data can support real-time institutional decisions [1] [26].

### Pricing Details

Workday does not publish a Student price list, tier table, per-student annual price, per-school monthly price, implementation fee, or public free trial. G2 lists one “Basic” configuration as “Contact Vendor for Pricing,” while Software Advice reports one custom-priced plan and pricing available on request [15] [16]. The commercially relevant unit is therefore a negotiated institutional subscription, normally shaped by institution size, modules, countries, integrations, implementation services, and existing Workday HCM/Financials scope; no verified third-party dollar range was found in the reviewed sources. Pricing checked November 24, 2026.

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Workday exposes REST APIs for smaller self-service transactions, SOAP APIs for large scheduled imports/exports, and a Graph API for targeted data retrieval. The developer portal provides live API explorers and OpenAPI specifications [19]. Workday Extend and the Workday Marketplace support partner-built extensions, while Workday Student connects natively to HCM, Payroll, Financial Management, Adaptive Planning, Grants Management, and Workday identity/security [1] [26].

The reviewed public evidence does not verify 1EdTech OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, or ClassLink certification for Workday Student. Institutions should expect integration work for an LMS, alumni system, library, identity provider, payment gateway, data warehouse, and state reporting feed. SSO and role-based security are supported at platform level, but exact protocols, payment processors, accounting mappings, and API entitlements are tenant/contract dependent.

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Workday’s compliance page explicitly includes Student in the scope of its ISO/IEC 27001:2022 information-security, ISO/IEC 27017 cloud-security, ISO/IEC 27018 public-cloud PII, ISO/IEC 27701 privacy-management, and ISO 42001 AI-management coverage. Workday also publishes SOC 1/SOC 2/SOC 3 materials for Workday enterprise products and an updated trust center listing SOC, ISO, GDPR, FedRAMP Moderate, NIST, CSA STAR, and other badges [23] [27].

Workday’s privacy statement describes international-transfer safeguards, standard contractual clauses, EU–U.S./UK/Swiss Data Privacy Framework participation, access/correction/deletion/portability rights, and regional controllers including Workday Limited in Ireland and Workday, Inc. in the United States [24]. The reviewed public pages do not explicitly claim COPPA, India’s DPDP Act, or a FERPA certification for Workday Student; FERPA obligations remain a shared institutional/vendor-contract responsibility. Workday does not publish one universal Student hosting region, so data residency must be confirmed per tenant and order.

No verified Student-specific security incident was identified in the reviewed official, review, and higher-education news sources through November 24, 2026. This is not evidence that no incident has ever occurred; it means no incident was sufficiently verified for inclusion here.

### Strengths

* Broad, genuinely higher-education-native lifecycle coverage from recruiting through registration, aid, billing, advising, and graduation.
* Strong integration with Workday HCM, Payroll, Financial Management, planning, grants, identity, reporting, and security.
* Real-time academic and financial data, configurable rules, transfer-credit handling, and aid recalculation address complex university policies.
* Current AI direction is unusually concrete for an SIS, including search/assistance, recommendations, predictive signals, and administration agents [3] [26].
* Adoption is material and growing: more than 200 institutions and 5.8 million Student records were claimed in April 2026 [26].

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

* Reviews repeatedly describe confusing navigation, slow page loads, and a steep learning curve. G2 includes a 1/5 review calling registration clunky, slow, and dated; its review summary also asks for clearer navigation, faster performance, and better onboarding [15].
* Capterra and Software Advice both show ease of use around 4.0 despite higher overall/functionality scores; reviewers report duplicated paths, unintuitive menus, and the need to learn where tasks live [14] [16].
* Mobile access is useful but incomplete: reviewers report that mobile/tablet functionality is materially narrower than the web experience, with occasional loading delays and downtime around upgrades [14] [15].
* Large implementations are expensive and institutionally disruptive. Inside Higher Ed reported Ohio State abandoning its Workday Student effort in 2021, with analysts estimating sunk costs in the tens of millions and describing next-generation SIS deployments as unusually difficult [17].
* The product is not a complete K–12 school-management suite; schools needing transport, library, health, discipline, special education, boarding, or alumni functions will need integrations or other products.
