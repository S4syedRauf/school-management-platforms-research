### Overview

SIMS is the long-running UK school management information system (MIS) used to hold pupil, staff, attendance, assessment, behaviour, timetable, examination, safeguarding and statutory-return data. Its importance is less about being a general global SIS than about its deep fit with English, Welsh and Northern Irish school administration, including DfE census workflows and a large network of approved support units and technical integrators. [1] [6]

The current product is a two-track transition. SIMS 7 remains the established desktop/local product, while SIMS Next Gen is a ground-up cloud rebuild delivered through phased modules. ESS was carved out of Capita: Capita announced the sale in December 2020, Montagu completed the acquisition in 2021, and the CMA approved the merger with ParentPay in July 2021. [10] [11] ParentPay Group is the current owner and combines SIMS with ParentPay payments, Schoolcomms engagement, finance, catering, library and data-protection products.

### Platform Classification Explained

* **SIMS 7 desktop/on-premise:** The partner documentation describes local SIMS 7 installations, local backup services, .NET/WinForms-style integrations and applications sitting on a school server or proxy. The model can be locally hosted and is not a pure SaaS service. [5] [6]
* **SIMS Next Gen web/cloud:** ESS describes Next Gen as cloud-native and the independent 2025 rollout account describes 100% cloud access, automatic updates and use on any device. It is being rolled out module by module, so schools may run it alongside classic SIMS during transition. [1] [23]
* **Finance:** FMS is offered as cloud-based finance software with backups, upgrades, anytime access, automatic reconciliation, statutory reporting and local-authority data exchange; the partner portal also documents legacy FMS 6 local backup. [4] [6]
* **Native mobile apps:** SIMS Parent (iOS and Android) exposes homework, calendars, timetables, activities, assessment, attendance/truancy, behaviour, reports, messaging and safeguarding information; it requires a school invitation. SIMS Student is an iOS app for invited students. SIMS Teacher is an iPad app for registers, timetables, pupil details, conduct and assessment, with encrypted data. [17] [18] [19] [20]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment
SIMS 7 supports admissions import and update, including ATF/AIF workflows, applicants and pre-admission records. It is designed for school admissions and transfer rather than university-style applicant relationship management. [6]

#### Student information and records
The core database stores pupil and staff records, linked adults and contact priority, identifiers, groups, photographs, documents/attachments, medical information, attendance, behaviour, achievement, assessment and timetable data. SIMS Parent can expose selected records and let parents update information held by the school. [6] [17]

#### Attendance
SIMS provides class and lesson registers, attendance marks and codes, late minutes, historical register views, unexplained-absence workflows, attendance analysis and KPI/trend monitoring. Attendance is one of the live Next Gen modules and connects to statutory returns and parent messaging. [1] [6] [23]

#### Timetable and master scheduling
SIMS 7 Timetabling supports period-based timetables; the modern package includes timetabling, options and cover. Teacher access includes current timetables, while Next Gen roadmaps add flexible seating plans and real-time fire registers. [3] [6] [20]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations
Assessment Manager provides configurable marksheets, templates, assessment data collection, progress/performance analysis and intervention tracking. Examinations functionality covers entries, candidate information, exam timetables and results-related workflows. Assessment, exams and interventions are included in the current Optimise package. [1] [6] [15]

#### Report cards and transcripts
SIMS supports report creation using report templates/Word data tags, profile reports, exports and detailed report writing. Capterra users specifically value reports and assessment data, but describe report-building as training-intensive and sometimes error-prone. A dedicated higher-education transcript module is not publicly evidenced. [16]

#### Learning management and homework
SIMS is not documented as a full LMS with course authoring, content delivery or built-in lessons. Homework is visible in SIMS Parent/Student, and the current Engage package labels homework a priority roadmap feature rather than a mature core module. [1] [17]

#### Communication
The connected ecosystem supports SMS/email messaging, in-app notifications, attendance and unexplained-absence messaging, smart forms, consent collection, calendar updates and message history through Schoolcomms. WhatsApp integration is not publicly evidenced; native messaging is not presented as a WhatsApp channel. [1] [7]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals
SIMS Parent provides a multi-child, cross-school parent view; SIMS Student provides an invited student view; SIMS Teacher provides iPad classroom access. Browser access also exists for parent services, while SIMS ID and school-managed invitations provide identity and access control. [5] [17] [18] [20]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting
FMS covers suppliers and supplier payments, income and debtors, budgets, chart of accounts, period/year end, audit and statutory reporting. ParentPay/Schoolcomms add dinner money, meals, clubs, trips, wraparound care, shops, donations, single-basket checkout, top-up and payment reporting. Full tuition-fee billing is not described as a SIMS core feature. [1] [4] [7]

#### HR, staff and payroll
SIMS stores staff data and has Staff, HR and HR Assistant integration documentation; the current package includes staffing and personnel links. Payroll processing itself is not publicly documented as a native SIMS payroll engine. [1] [6]

#### Transport
No native transport-routing, fleet, bus-pass or vehicle-management module was identified in the official SIMS feature pages or partner documentation reviewed.

#### Library
Library/resource management is part of the wider ParentPay Group portfolio, including Reading Cloud, but it is not presented as a native SIMS MIS module. SIMS can participate in the wider group ecosystem and exchange selected data. [2] [22]

#### Hostel and boarding
No native boarding-house, room-allocation or residential-life module was publicly evidenced.

#### Inventory and assets
FMS includes equipment/asset management and loans, plus transactional reporting and audit trails. This is finance-linked asset control rather than a full facilities or warehouse-management suite. [1] [4]

#### Health and medical
Student medical information and conditions can be held in the MIS and shared selectively through connected engagement/reporting services. The public material does not describe clinical records, care-plan authoring or a medical scheduling module. [6] [22]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care
SIMS 7 supports behaviour and achievement records; Next Gen Conduct Management records incidents, automates workflows and provides dashboards/insights. The roadmap also names pastoral care for secondary schools. [1] [3] [6] [8]

#### Special education and safeguarding
SEND management is included in the Optimise package. The data model supports flags and pupil information used for safeguarding, while SIMS Parent highlights safeguarding-related updates and emergency information. A full statutory child-protection case-management product is not publicly specified. [1] [17]

#### Alumni and fundraising
No native alumni relations or fundraising module was found. ParentPay provides shops, donations pages and payments, but those are not an alumni CRM or donor-management system. [1]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting
Reporting and Insights combines attendance, behaviour, SEND and other data in filterable charts, drill-down pupil/staff views and benchmarking against national averages. MAT Group Hub is described as providing central access, trust-wide dashboards and configurable Power BI dashboards. [3] [8]

#### AI features
No production AI assistant, generative reporting assistant or documented predictive model was identified. ESS promotes an AI-themed education webinar, but EdTech Impact records that the SIMS team had not stated whether the product uses AI; score this conservatively as not publicly evidenced. [2] [24]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance
SIMS includes census and statutory returns, with school census guidance and support. FMS adds DfE validation and automatic submission for Consistent Financial Reporting and Accounts Returns. This UK compliance depth is a major differentiator. [1] [4]

#### Mobile apps
SIMS Parent, SIMS Student and SIMS Teacher are the named native apps. Parent supports homework, calendar, timetable, activities, assessment, attendance, behaviour, reports and messaging; Teacher supports registers, timetables, pupil details, conduct and assessment. Parent has 500K+ Android downloads but poor store ratings; the iOS app is free and has 1.8k ratings. [17] [18] [19] [20]

#### Integrations and APIs
SIMS 7 exposes partner APIs, reporting/command export, attendance, assessment, timetable, student, behaviour, admissions, staff/HR and dinner-money interfaces. Next Gen APIs are web-based and OData-compliant; the ecosystem documents OneRoster, SIMS ID single sign-on and a technical-integrator programme. ParentPay, Schoolcomms, FMS, e-procurement and budgeting are first-party connections. [5] [6] [7]

#### Security and privacy
ParentPay Group reports ISO 27001, ISO 9001, Cyber Essentials Plus and PCI-DSS Level 1 certifications for relevant group services. It describes UK/EEA data centres, UK GDPR/EU GDPR Article 28 processing, TLS 1.2, encryption at rest and in backups, RBAC/least privilege, MFA for employees, penetration testing, monitoring, incident response and disaster recovery. [21] [22]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

* **Deep UK statutory fit:** Census and DfE financial-return workflows are embedded rather than bolted on.
* **Two-speed migration:** Schools can keep SIMS 7 while adopting cloud Next Gen modules with shared data exchange.
* **Large partner ecosystem:** OneRoster, OData web APIs, SIMS ID SSO, local APIs and technical-integrator accreditation support third-party products.
* **Trust-level oversight:** Group Hub and Power BI dashboards are aimed at MAT-wide attendance, demographics, performance and policy control.
* **Integrated school operations:** SIMS, FMS, ParentPay and Schoolcomms connect records, engagement, money and reporting.
* **Long operational history:** ESS cites 40+ years of SIMS experience and a £50 million rebuild investment. [1] [3]

### Pricing Details

ESS publishes no public price list, per-pupil tariff, tier price or implementation-fee schedule. Current pages offer a demo or quote and describe packages such as Optimise, Engage, Pay, Finance and Secure without public amounts; support is sold in Foundation, Advanced and Complete levels, also without public prices. [1] [2] [6]

As a third-party estimate only, EdTech Impact’s page, last updated 23 November 2026, reports a paid subscription starting at £350 and example bands of £350 plus £2 per pupil and £400 plus £3 per pupil. The page does not clearly state the billing term or reconcile those bands with ESS’s current package structure, so it should not be used as an official quote. [24]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Public documentation confirms OneRoster, OData-compliant Next Gen web APIs, SIMS ID SSO, local SIMS 7 APIs, reporting/command export, and a technical-integrator programme with partner accreditation and support. ParentPay, Schoolcomms, FMS, e-procurement, budgeting and dinner-money connections are documented. The reviewed public sources do not claim 1EdTech certification for LTI or SIF, Ed-Fi certification, or named Clever/ClassLink connectors. Google Workspace/Microsoft 365 security reviews are offered through the group, but a deep native productivity-suite integration is not publicly detailed. [5] [6] [7]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

ParentPay Group reports ISO 27001 certification, ISO 9001, Cyber Essentials Plus and PCI-DSS Level 1 for relevant services; it states that customer data is held in UK or EEA data centres, uses UK/EU GDPR processor agreements, and maintains encryption, least-privilege access, MFA for employees, penetration testing, monitoring, backups and incident-response controls. [21] [22] The evidence reviewed does not establish SOC 2, FERPA, COPPA or India DPDP Act compliance for SIMS, and no product-specific hosting-region schedule beyond UK/EEA was found. No confirmed ESS/SIMS data breach was identified in the reviewed sources. The CMA investigated contract and switching conduct: its 2024 press release stated that no conclusion should be drawn that the law had been broken, while the earlier 2022 case closed in January 2023 after binding commitments concerning three-year contracts and break clauses. [12] [13]

### Strengths

* Exceptionally broad UK school administration coverage, especially attendance, assessment, behaviour, timetabling, examinations, census and reporting.
* Mature data model and partner documentation, with OneRoster, OData, local APIs and a substantial integrator ecosystem.
* Strong statutory and finance fit through census returns, FMS, DfE validation and ParentPay-linked payments.
* Current cloud rebuild offers a path away from local servers while preserving SIMS 7 data and workflows.
* Current official claims indicate substantial scale: 10,000+ schools, 700,000+ users and 40+ countries. [1]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

* The product is in a long phased transition: independent coverage said in 2025 that attendance, behaviour, census and assessment were live for early adopters while reporting, communications and finance were still on the roadmap, and WhichMIS reported that the final cloud modules were expected only by end-2026. [14] [23]
* Capterra reviewers praise registers, data depth and reporting but report a clunky or dated interface, difficult report/marksheet configuration, unreliable reports/glitches, slow performance and very poor support experiences. [16]
* G2 reviewers value timetabling, cover, attendance, fees and parent apps but mention frequent updates and limited troubleshooting resources. [15]
* SIMS Parent is rated only 1.3/5 from 1.8k UK App Store ratings; reviews describe login loops, random logouts, stalled messages, missing content and dinner-money access problems. [18]
* The iOS SIMS Student listing is 2.3/5 from three ratings, including a complaint that it is glitchy; SIMS Teacher has too few ratings to display a score. [19] [20]
* Contract and switching concerns have attracted CMA scrutiny, including complaints that database transfer could be complex or error-prone; the CMA’s notices record commitments but no finding of infringement. [12] [13]

### Key Milestones

* **1984:** Independent MIS-sector coverage dates SIMS’s origin to 1984; ESS’s current site describes more than 40 years of experience. [3] [14]
* **11 May 2020:** Education Software Solutions Ltd incorporated; the legal entity was formerly Capita ESS Ltd. [9]
* **December 2020:** Capita announced agreement to sell ESS to Tiger UK Bidco/Montagu for up to £400 million, with £298 million payable at completion. [10]
* **Early 2021:** Montagu acquired ESS in a carve-out from Capita. [11]
* **July 2021:** CMA approved the merger of ESS and ParentPay; ESS joined ParentPay Group, with databases then used in more than 19,000 schools according to Montagu’s announcement. [11]
* **April 2022–January 2023:** CMA investigated the move from one-year to three-year contracts and accepted binding commitments, including an adjudicated 12-month break clause for eligible schools; the case closed 10 January 2023. [13]
* **May 2024:** CMA opened a separate investigation into alleged switching/data-transfer conduct and described ESS as holding approximately 50% of England’s market; the press release expressly stated there was no finding of infringement. [12]
* **June 2025:** Independent School Business Services described Next Gen as rolling out gradually, with core attendance, behaviour, census and assessment live for early adopters and other modules still developing. [23]
* **July 2025:** WhichMIS reported 8,140 UK schools and 30.1% UK market share from summer 2025 census analysis, down from SIMS’s historic peak. [14]
* **November 2026:** ESS/ParentPay’s current official pages claim 10,000+ SIMS schools, 700,000+ users, 40+ countries and a £50 million Next Gen rebuild investment; pricing remains quote-based. [1] [3]
