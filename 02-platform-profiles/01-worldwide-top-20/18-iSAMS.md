### Overview

iSAMS is a UK-origin, web-based school management information system (MIS/SIS) for independent, international, online and multi-school K–12 organisations. It keeps student, academic, pastoral, admissions, communication, HR and financial information in a core database, then exposes it through administration screens, portals, apps, reporting and APIs. It is especially aimed at schools with multiple curricula, time zones, languages, campuses and fee-paying workflows [1] [2].

The company says it was founded by Rugby School alumni in 2005 and commercially launched its first web-based product that year. Later additions included HR, portals, SMS, mobile apps, fee billing, census, data protection, wellbeing, iFinance and Central analytics [3]. iSAMS acquired prep-school specialist CJM Software in September 2018 [5], then IRIS acquired iSAMS in October 2020; IRIS reported more than 1,000 schools across 80 countries at that point [4].

### Platform Classification Explained

iSAMS is primarily browser-based cloud software. The current site calls it 100% cloud-based, and EdTech Impact identifies Microsoft Azure as its hosting environment [1] [6]. No current desktop client is documented. A historical on-site hosting option is not a current on-premise offer [3].

The mobile layer includes native iParent, iStudent, iTeacher and iReport apps [29]. iParent and iStudent provide timetables, attendance, exams, reports, behaviour and notifications [30] [31]. Newer Parent and Student Cloud Portals are responsive progressive web apps with push notifications and custom branding [28].

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

The Admissions Cloud Portal is a branded, mobile-responsive website connected to the Admissions module and core MIS. It supports 24/7 enquiry and registration forms, multiple applicants on one account, applicant tracking, campaign/conversion reporting, registration-fee payment, outstanding-fee flags, document upload, personalised and automated email notifications, real-time reporting and automatic synchronisation into iSAMS [16].

#### Student information and records

Student Manager stores current/former-student records, family trees, admissions/census data, discipline, passports/visas, contacts, transport, health, SEN, documents and alumni information. It provides search, custom fields, result grids, group operations, exports, Email Wizard, section permissions and audit history [20].

#### Attendance and registration

Attendance is available through school and lesson registration, future absences, medical exemptions and off-games/absence information. Attendance appears in Student Manager, Parent/Student Portals, iParent/iStudent and Central reporting; the student portal also gives students real-time attendance visibility [26] [27] [30] [31].

#### Timetable, curriculum and cover

Timetable Manager creates unlimited draft, preliminary, published and archived timetables; supports students, forms, teachers, rooms, subjects, departments, years and teaching sets; adds meetings, tutorials, year schedules, preparation periods and teacher availability; detects clashes and missing links; logs changes; and exports/prints PDF, HTML and Excel. It imports Nova-T 4/6, TimeTabler, Edval, Timetabling Solutions, Skolaris, GP-Untis, ASC Timetable and Excel. Curriculum Manager links imported timetables and sets to automatically generate gradebooks. Cover Manager handles staff absence cover [21].

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

The product includes Curriculum Manager, Teaching Manager and Gradebooks for enrolment, courses, sets and academic tracking. Internal and external examination information includes entries, arrangements, candidate details, timetables and results. Students and parents can view these data through portals and apps [20] [26] [27] [30] [31]. iSAMS does not present itself as a full built-in LMS; instead, its LMS strategy is integration with Canvas, Google Classroom, ManageBac, Schoolbox, Toddle and Microsoft Teams [12].

#### Report cards and transcripts

School Reporting publishes reports and assessment data to the Parent and Student Portals. Recent portal controls allow staff to publish or withdraw reports for selected students and manage exceptions. iReport is the dedicated teacher reporting app, while portal/app users can view published reports and assessment history [26] [27] [28] [29].

#### Homework and learning-management boundary

The new Homework module lets staff create assignments, set deadlines and attach documents or links. Homework is surfaced in Student Cloud Portal and Parent Cloud Portal widgets. This is native homework/assignment management, but iSAMS explicitly says it does not offer a built-in LMS; it connects to external LMS/VLE products instead [12] [28].

#### Communication

Email Wizard sends database-driven email using live correspondence permissions, merges sibling households where appropriate, splits personalised child messages and logs every email to the student record. SMS Gateway supports templates, authorisations, sender identities, budget limits, detailed logs, scheduled messages and emergency broadcasts through third-party mobile providers. Portals and apps add email, SMS, push notifications, news, bulletins, calendars, photo galleries and electronic forms [11] [26] [30]. No official WhatsApp module was found in the reviewed product pages.

#### Parent, student, teacher and admissions portals

The Parent Portal supports branded content, timetables, teaching groups, attendance, future absences, reports, assessment, exams, activities, discipline, rewards, electronic forms, documents, online invoices/payments, email/SMS groups and visitor analytics. The Student Cloud Portal adds homework, push notifications, contact updates, timetable export, attendance, behaviour, exams, reports, forms, documents and usage analytics. The Admissions Portal is described above. Teacher-facing access is delivered through the web system and iTeacher/iReport apps rather than a separately documented teacher web portal [10] [26] [27] [29].

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Fee Billing generates bills and charges for applicants, current students and alumni, individually or by batch; supports split billing, recurring charges, discounts, VAT, nominal structures, multiple-student invoices, applicant filters, invoice history, notes, traffic-light status and export to Excel, Word and PDF. iSAMS Payments provides integrated cashless/online payments. iFinance adds general ledger, multi-currency, bank feeds, reconciliation, AP Inbox, invoice processing, workflow approvals, recurring invoicing, deferred income, BI dashboards, audit trails, fixed assets, stock, expenses and multi-entity/group consolidation [2] [19] [22].

#### HR, staff and payroll

HR provides employee records, automated outdated-data alerts, staff absence dashboards, calendars and reports, Single Central Record checks, multi-school SCR sharing, case management for flexible working/disputes/appeals, policy distribution and acceptance, read reminders and audit trails. IRIS/iSAMS also markets UK-focused payroll and pensions integration, although the detailed HR page reviewed concentrates on HR rather than pay-run mechanics [2] [18].

#### Activities, calendar and bookings

Activities Manager and Calendar Manager feed activity groups, events, sports and calendars into portals/apps. The November 2026 iSAMS Bookings launch adds parent-evening scheduling, video meetings, events, clubs and wraparound-care bookings; the new functionality is visible on the Parent Cloud Portal page [1] [26].

#### Inventory and assets

iFinance contains Stock Management, purchasing/ePOS/CRM integration, stock-level tracking, Fixed Asset Register, asset documents, leases/loans, maintenance/renewal dates, depreciation journals, fixed-asset schedules and CapEx forecasting [19].

#### Health and medical

Student records include dental, eye, hearing, vaccination, BMI, medical notes, public-health notes, conditions matrices, trip fields and health alerts with restricted access. The product navigation also identifies a Medical Centre module, and the portal can expose medical exemptions/attendance information. The Student Manager health record is the clearest publicly documented detail [20] [22].

#### Behaviour, discipline and pastoral care

Reward & Conduct supports configurable commendations, detentions, positive/negative incidents, house points, instant notifications, iTeacher entry and cross-record analysis. Student Wellbeing tracks life events, indicators, flags, care contributors and strict viewing permissions. Portals/apps expose appropriate rewards, detentions, conduct and pastoral records [17] [26] [27] [30].

#### Special education and safeguarding

SEN details are held in student records and Central provides an SEN report. Wellbeing analytics can compare conduct and concerns. Data Protection manages consent registers, parental consent, image-sharing permissions and DSAR workflows, while HR’s Single Central Record supports staff safeguarding checks [17] [18] [23].

#### Alumni and fundraising

Student Manager retains former-student records including occupation, family, education and donations. This is an alumni/contact and donation record capability, not evidence of a full fundraising CRM, campaign-management suite or donor-payment system [20].

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Central is built on Microsoft Power BI and iSAMS APIs. It provides visual dashboards, behaviour/wellbeing analytics, real-time updates, SEN reporting, assessment-data expansion and multi-school/group oversight. iSAMS announced Central in September 2023 and reported adoption by more than 100 schools by June 2024 [15].

#### AI features

Central’s 2024 AI update added a natural-language Q&A visualisation: users type a prompt and Central generates a report that can then be customised. This is a production AI reporting assistant, not evidence of an AI tutor, automated grading engine or predictive-admissions system [15].

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Census Manager was created for UK and international census requirements. Data Protection supports GDPR consent and DSAR processes; iFinance is UK VAT-ready; payroll is designed for UK HMRC and pension schemes according to the product overview [2] [3] [23]. No public evidence was found in the reviewed pages for Ed-Fi state reporting, US FERPA/COPPA certification or India DPDP Act-specific compliance.

#### Mobile apps

The native app set is iParent, iStudent, iTeacher and iReport. iParent/iStudent cover information access and push notifications; iTeacher supports classroom tasks such as registers, rewards, communication and cover/timetables; iReport supports teacher grades, assessments and reports. iParent and iStudent are listed on both iOS and Android channels; iSAMS also has cross-platform PWA portals [29] [30] [31] [32].

#### Integrations and APIs

iSAMS exposes REST API for real-time reads/writes and Batch API for standardised extraction and synchronisation. It offers SSO/social login, a developer portal and a marketplace with over 200 integration partners. Named LMS/VLE connections include Canvas, Google Classroom, ManageBac, Schoolbox, Toddle and Microsoft Teams; payment partners shown include Stripe, Esenda, Flywire and TransferMate [12] [19].

#### Security and privacy

The vendor states that iSAMS holds BSI ISO 27001:2022 and Cyber Essentials certification; its privacy policy identifies Azure as the hosting processor and describes GDPR-oriented safeguards. App listings state encryption in transit or encrypted app data. No SOC 2 report, FERPA/COPPA certification or precise hosting-region commitment was found [24] [25] [30] [32].

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Central natural-language Q&A:** prompt-generated Power BI report visualisations [15].
- **Single core database:** cross-functional academic, pastoral, finance and HR data [2] [4].
- **REST plus Batch APIs:** real-time integration and standardised synchronisation [12].
- **200+ partner marketplace:** extensions for LMS, facilities, security and sports [12].
- **International-school design:** multi-curriculum, multi-calendar and multi-currency support [1] [6].
- **Configurable wellbeing and consent:** indicators, role-based viewing, consent and DSAR records [17] [23].
- **PWA portals:** cross-device access, push alerts, branding and low maintenance [26] [27] [28].

### Pricing Details

No official public tiered price, per-student price, implementation fee or setup schedule was available when checked on 24 November 2026; the official pricing URL returned a verification page rather than rates. iSAMS remains quote/demo-led, with price depending on size, modules, integrations and implementation.

Third-party figures are estimates, not official quotes: GetApp lists a flat starting price of **6,000 per year** without a currency [8]; Software Advice Australia says **GBP 6,000/year** with no free version or trial [9]; EdTech Impact says **£5,000/year** and requests a quote [6]. These conflict and are not a standard price; implementation is reported at one to six months [6].

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

iSAMS has a public developer portal, REST API, Batch API, SSO/social login and a marketplace claiming more than 200 partners [12]. Named LMS/VLE integrations are Canvas, Google Classroom, ManageBac, Schoolbox, Toddle and Microsoft Teams; payment ecosystem references include Stripe, Esenda, Flywire and TransferMate [12] [19]. iSAMS says it connects through open APIs and roster standards, but the reviewed pages do not name a certified OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi or SIF implementation. No evidence was found for Clever or ClassLink certification, nor for a formal accounting marketplace beyond iFinance and payment partners. The practical interoperability proposition is therefore rich API plus marketplace/SSO, not standards certification.

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

iSAMS publicly claims BSI ISO 27001:2022 and Cyber Essentials certification [25]. Its privacy policy identifies Microsoft Azure as the hosting service for client data, lists subprocessors, describes security controls and supports GDPR subject-access requests and deletion requests [24]. The dedicated Data Protection module covers GDPR consent, parental consent, consent registers, data-subject access requests and portal visibility [23]. The app listings state encryption in transit and app-level confidentiality controls [30] [32].

The reviewed public material does not substantiate SOC 2, FERPA, COPPA or India DPDP Act compliance, and it does not publish a precise Azure region/tenant-location commitment. No publicly reported incident was identified in the reviewed official, review, app-store and press sources; this is an evidence limitation, not a guarantee that no incident has occurred.

### Strengths

- Broad native MIS coverage for independent and international schools, including admissions, academics, wellbeing, finance, HR and communications [2] [4].
- Strong international fit: multilingual portals/apps, multiple curricula, local time zones, multi-currency finance and a 95+ country vendor claim [1] [3] [6].
- Genuine interoperability through REST/Batch APIs, SSO and a 200+ partner marketplace [12].
- Central’s Power BI dashboards, natural-language Q&A, and ISO 27001:2022/Cyber Essentials claims provide credible analytics and assurance differentiators [15] [25].

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Independent review averages are modest: G2 2.5/5, GetApp 3.2/5 and Software Advice 3.2/5, with small samples [7] [8] [9].
- Reviewers describe navigation and workflows as clunky or long-winded, citing complicated registers, timetable setup, reporting and limited flexibility [7].
- EdTech Impact reviewers report an ageing interface, limited bulk import/export, paid custom reports, unreliable Email Wizard behaviour and unaddressed ideas [6].
- US reviewers report rigid regional formats and terminology; Software Advice reviewers cite poor support, difficult customisation and weak reporting/downloads [6] [9].
- iParent/iStudent reviews include login, loading, crashing and glitch complaints [30] [31].
- No built-in LMS or public price book means external learning-platform costs and a bespoke quote [12].

### Key Milestones

- **2005:** iSAMS is founded by Rugby School alumni and commercially launches its first web-based MIS with curriculum, timetable, assessment, reporting and admissions [3].
- **2006–2015:** HR, parent/student portals, cloud hosting, Reward & Conduct, Tracking Manager, international deployments, SMS Gateway, iSAMS mobile, iCommunity, Fee Billing, Census Manager and the iOS/Android app suite are added [3].
- **2016–2018:** Marketplace, Admissions Portal, ManageBac integration, iReport, GDPR/Data Protection, Wellbeing Manager and iFinance are added; iSAMS acquires CJM Software in 2018 [3] [5].
- **2020:** IRIS Software Group acquires iSAMS; the announcement reports over 500 UK independent and over 500 international schools across 80 countries [4].
- **2023–2024:** Central, powered by Microsoft Power BI and APIs, launches; natural-language AI Q&A is added in 2024, and iSAMS reports adoption by 100+ schools [15].
- **2025:** iSAMS launches the redesigned Student Cloud Portal/PWA and native Homework module [28].
- **2026:** The official site reports 1,800+ schools/groups, 1.7M+ student profiles and 95+ countries; recent roadmap items include Central Manage, AI reporting, Schools BI partnership and iSAMS Bookings [1] [3].
