### Overview

Blackbaud K–12 Education Management is a connected suite for private and independent schools. Its core is the Student Information System (SIS), with Learning Management System (LMS) included, plus Enrollment, Billing/Tuition, Financial Aid, Financial Edge NXT, Raiser’s Edge NXT, and School Website options. The design goal is one core record, role-based access, and data flow from inquiry through alumni engagement [4] [9].

Blackbaud began in 1981 with student billing [3]. It acquired WhippleHill in 2014, adding cloud front-office, student-information, enrollment, learning, and content-management capabilities; WhippleHill reported 450+ schools [10]. Its 2015 $190 million Smart Tuition acquisition added tuition payments and accounts receivable for thousands of schools and 300,000+ families [11]. Launch dates for today’s named SIS/LMS are not publicly disclosed.

### Platform Classification Explained

**Browser and cloud.** Current pages describe a cloud platform with role-based portals and shared SIS/LMS data [1] [4] [9].

**Hosting and deployment.** Blackbaud does not name the cloud provider or K–12 data-residency region in the reviewed pages. WhippleHill was cloud-based, while the broader 2014 portfolio included cloud and on-premise products [10]; no current K–12 desktop or on-premise option was found.

**Mobile.** SIS/LMS and Billing are mobile-friendly [4] [9] [12]. School Journey is a separate branded iOS/Android partner app using Blackbaud ID, SKY API, roles, and push notifications [13], not a native Blackbaud K–12 app. Blackbaud Mobile documentation found here is for Fundraiser Performance Management [14].

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment — Blackbaud Enrollment

Customizable application forms; school-level-specific checklists; inquiry and applicant tracking; automated reminders; configurable workflows; staff task management; committee review hub and candidate feedback; self-scheduling for visits, interviews, tours, and events; application status tracking; individualized enrollment contracts; deposits and payment-plan selection; integrated financial-aid awards; one-step reenrollment; dashboards and reports for inquiries, applications, contracts, and year-over-year enrollment progress [15].

#### Student information and records — Blackbaud Student Information System

A 360° Student View combines academics, activities, attendance, medical records, grades, graduation progress, and family information. Core records include students, parents/guardians, relationships, faculty, staff, advisors, classes, activities, athletics, and schedules. The family portal supports personal and medical information; roles limit records by relationship and security group [1] [4].

#### Attendance

Faculty can record class attendance, use daily bulk entry, and review attendance history. The suite includes dismissal and pre-excuse management; 2026 help-center updates document parent-submitted attendance. SIS/LMS screens and G2 reviews also describe attendance views, student photos, and attendance reporting [1] [5] [16].

#### Timetable and master scheduling

Administrators can configure complex schedules, assign classrooms and time slots, avoid conflicts, create schedule sets, and support rotations, custom days, and partial days. Students and advisors submit course requests; administrators review and approve them. “Find Me Now” locates students and teachers against the actual day schedule. Athletics adds game/practice locations, transportation information, and cancellation notices [1] [9].

#### Gradebook, assessment, and examinations

LMS provides a Gradebook with shortcuts, bulk actions, automated assessment grading, flexible grading methods, numeric or letter grades, criteria/mastery-oriented assessment, self-assessment, comments, quizzes, interactive assignments, discussions, online submissions, and assessment workflows. Blackbaud documents LockDown Browser support for assessments. The official pages do not establish a dedicated high-stakes examination-management module or public exam-board integrations [1] [9] [16].

#### Report cards and transcripts

SIS supports configurable grading formulas, role-based grade approval before faculty publication, report cards, recommendations, transcripts, graduation progress, and historical coursework. G2 users describe flexible grading output and report cards/transcripts, but also report that complex grading plans and customized reports require substantial configuration [5].

#### Learning management and homework

The integrated LMS includes a centralized assignment center, missing-work alerts, self-directed tasks, course pages, syllabus and content hosting, topics, stored coursework year over year, interactive assignments, group discussion, submissions, assessment, and feedback. It also supports advising, residential/community pages, groups, activities, extracurriculars, and athletics. Blackbaud positions it as included with SIS rather than a separate mandatory LMS purchase [9].

#### Communication

In-system messaging and real-time updates connect teachers, students, families, faculty, staff, administrators, advisors, coaches, and group leaders. Enrollment includes applicant-specific communication, automated reminders, and status updates. Financial Aid Management documents automated email and text reminders; Billing includes configurable payment notifications. Push notifications are evidenced for the third-party School Journey app. No official evidence was found for native WhatsApp integration; SMS/text is documented, while email and app push are supported in relevant workflows [13] [17].

#### Parent, student, and teacher portals

One parent/family portal shows all children’s records, grades, coursework, schedules, activities, messages, medical information, transcripts, contracts, financial aid, and tuition payments. Students get one login for assignments and resources; teachers get one login for classes, assignments, grades, attendance, and communication. The unified calendar is personalized by role and family relationship. Role-based access supports users with multiple personas, such as parent and teacher [1] [4].

#### Fees, billing, payments, and accounting

Billing Management is an accounts-receivable subledger: integrated enrollment contracts; payment plans and modalities; split payments by payer, percentage, or fee level; tuition, fees, incidentals, and shopping-cart items; bulk charges by class/program/team; financial holds; invoices; payment approvals; payment metrics; automated reconciliation; financial-aid discounts; and posting to Financial Edge NXT. Billing documents 256-bit encryption, PCI-DSS Level 1, MFA, and FDIC-insured custodial accounts [12].

#### Financial aid

Financial Aid Management supports need- and non-need-based awards, customizable applications and calculations, English and Spanish family applications, secure document upload, IRS integration for tax data, budgets by award source, disbursement tracking, batch awarding, automatic reallocation of unaccepted awards, verification/review services, reminders, reports, and integrated display of awards on contracts and payment portals [17].

#### HR, staff, and payroll

Staff/faculty records, role groups, faculty resources, and employee access are included in the record model. Native K–12 HR, payroll, benefits, or time-and-attendance was not evidenced [4] [18].

#### Transport, athletics, and activities

The LMS/SIS supports athletic rosters, practice/game scheduling, locations, transportation management, one-click cancellation notifications, team messaging, activities, advising, residential pages, and community groups. This is event/athletics transport coordination, not a documented standalone bus-routing or fleet module [9].

#### Library, hostel/boarding, inventory/assets, and health

The 360° view includes medical records; LMS includes residential/group pages; Marketplace partners cover medical records and lunch [4]. Native library circulation, hostel administration, inventory/assets, and full clinical-health modules were not documented, so schools should verify partner or configuration coverage.

#### Behavior, discipline, pastoral care, special education, and safeguarding

Student-life notes, advising, activities, role-based access, and conduct data are evidenced [5]. Capterra lists classroom management and special education, but no reviewed official page details dedicated special-education case management, safeguarding, incident, or pastoral-care modules; verify configuration or partners [7].

#### Alumni and fundraising — Raiser’s Edge NXT

The connected suite can carry family/student information into alumni status and the fundraising CRM. Raiser’s Edge NXT manages constituent records, giving history, campaigns, gifts, memberships, planned giving, events, donor reporting, prospect insights, stewardship, and donor communications. It integrates with enrollment and SIS so advancement teams can steward graduates and families; Financial Edge NXT connects fundraising and fund accounting [4] [18].

#### Analytics, dashboards, and reporting

Cross-department statistical dashboards cover enrollment, academics, finance, billing, and fundraising. Enrollment dashboards show funnel KPIs and trends; Billing dashboards show payments, cash flow, and net tuition; SIS/LMS provides lists, academic reports, report cards, transcripts, and school-wide calendars. Financial Edge NXT adds configurable financial dashboards, templates, drill-down reports, and role-based/view-only access. Users report that some report-building and query workflows are dated or less flexible than desired [5] [7].

#### AI features

Current Blackbaud positioning includes strategic automation, AI-driven financial-aid document review, AI-powered reconciliation and financial insights in Financial Edge NXT, predictive fundraising insights in Raiser’s Edge NXT, and natural-language assistance across Blackbaud AI page. The K–12 pages do not establish a production, K–12-specific AI tutor or universal student-facing assistant. AI is strongest in finance, fundraising, document review, and enterprise analytics rather than a dedicated classroom copilot [4] [17] [18] [19].

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

It provides configurable records, attendance, grades, transcripts, reports, and financial reporting. No reviewed page confirms state-reporting, Ed-Fi, SIF, or a named government package; verify extracts.

#### Mobile apps

The core experience is web-based and mobile-friendly. For K–12, School Journey is the documented partner-based native route, using Blackbaud ID, roles, SKY API, branded apps, and iOS/Android push notifications [13]. Blackbaud Mobile documentation is for Fundraiser Performance Management, not K–12 SIS/LMS [14].

#### Integrations and APIs

Marketplace provides vetted partners including Google, Canvas, Microsoft, Turnitin, Respondus, Schoology, Magnus Health, Orah, Ravenna, Tableau, and specialized apps. The open-source SKY API and Microsoft Power Platform support custom development and automation. Blackbaud Education Management supports OneRoster and LTI 1.3 on the LMS page. Blackbaud’s older ON API is REST-based but reaches end of life March 31, 2027; new work should use SKY API. JSON Web Token SSO allows third-party applications to use Education Management as the identity provider [9] [20] [21].

#### Security and privacy

Trust Center badges and documents show SOC 2 Type II, SOC 1 Type II, GDPR, HIPAA, PCI, CSA, HECVAT, NIST CSF, TX-RAMP, StateRAMP, and VPAT materials, plus MFA, centralized IAM/SSO, annual third-party penetration testing, incident response, subprocessors, deletion-on-request, and a DPA. Its Privacy Resource Center says customers remain responsible for controller obligations and applicable laws. No public K–12 hosting-region commitment was found [22] [23].

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **360° Student View:** unifies academics, activities, attendance, medical records, grades, and graduation progress.
- **Enrollment-to-cash workflow:** contracts, aid, billing, payments, and ledger posting share data and login.
- **Independent-school scheduling:** rotations, partial/custom days, approvals, athletics, and “Find Me Now.”
- **Standards and extensibility:** OneRoster/LTI 1.3, SKY API, SSO, Marketplace, Microsoft Power Platform, and 50+ vetted partners [3] [4] [9].
- **AI in finance and aid:** automated document review, reconciliation, dashboards, and insights [17] [18].
- **Advancement continuity:** student records can flow into alumni and donor stewardship through Raiser’s Edge NXT.

### Pricing Details

Blackbaud’s pricing is quote-based and varies by organization size, users, modules, support, and services. The official page gives no currency, tier, per-student rate, setup fee, or school-monthly price, though it says implementation, training, and support are typically included [24]. Capterra displayed a third-party $21,000-per-year starting estimate on November 24, 2026; scope was unspecified, so it is not an official price [7]. G2 reports seven months average SIS implementation time [5].

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Blackbaud documents OneRoster, LTI 1.3, SKY REST API, JSON Web Token SSO, Blackbaud ID, Marketplace apps, and Microsoft Power Platform [9] [20] [21]. Named connections include Google, Canvas, Microsoft, Turnitin, Respondus, Schoology, Tableau, Magnus Health, Orah, and Ravenna, plus Blackbaud Enrollment, Billing, Financial Aid, Financial Edge NXT, and Raiser’s Edge NXT. Ed-Fi and SIF certification, and certification on three or more listed standards, were not documented.

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Blackbaud advertises SOC 1, SOC 2, PCI DSS, HIPAA, GDPR, NIST CSF, HECVAT, TX-RAMP, StateRAMP, and VPAT materials, plus monitoring, MFA, OWASP-oriented development, and Trust Center documentation [22]. FERPA, COPPA, India DPDP Act, and K–12 hosting-region commitments were not specifically documented; schools remain responsible for applicable legal compliance.

The 2020 incident is verified. BBC reported a May ransomware attack affecting at least 10 universities and other organizations, ransom payment, and July external disclosure [25]. The FTC said the breach went undetected for three months, exposed personal data of millions, involved 24 Bitcoin (about $250,000), and led to an order requiring deletion, an information-security program, and a retention schedule [26]. Reuters reported a $3 million SEC settlement in 2023 over misleading disclosures affecting 13,000+ customers [27]. Blackbaud’s 2024 release says the FTC settlement required no payment and involved no admission or denial [28].

### Strengths

- Broad private-school coverage from admissions and academics through billing, accounting, websites, and advancement.
- Strong shared-record, role-based, single-login architecture.
- Deep scheduling, grading, athletics, advising, and enrollment workflows.
- Mature Financial Edge NXT/Raiser’s Edge NXT extensions.
- Public SKY API, LTI 1.3/OneRoster, Marketplace, SSO, and partners.
- G2 SIS is 4.2/5 from 29 reviews, with praise for centralization [5].

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Implementation is demanding: G2 reports seven months average SIS implementation, while reviews describe difficult startup and steep learning curves [5] [6].
- Capterra reviewers cite templated onboarding, inflexible configuration, and schools doing much of the implementation work [8].
- Users report inconsistent interfaces, dated/buried reporting, limited queries, slow imports, too many clicks, and clunky calendars [5] [6] [7].
- Support is mixed, with complaints about live chat and difficulty reaching higher-tier engineering [5] [6] [8].
- Cost, paid implementation, and cross-module dependencies recur in Capterra reviews [7] [8].
- The 2020 ransomware incident and later regulatory actions remain vendor-risk considerations [25] [26] [27].
- Native K–12 mobile coverage is unclear; schools may need a partner app [13] [14].
