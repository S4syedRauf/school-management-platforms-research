### Overview

Compass Education is an Australian school management information system that combines SIS/student records, attendance, wellbeing, teaching and learning, communications, family engagement, events, payments and financial management. It is positioned as a single source of truth for government, Catholic and independent K–12 schools, replacing a collection of administrative tools with a connected cloud platform and mobile experience. [2] [4]

John de la Motte and Lucas Filer founded the company in 2010 after beginning product work in 2009. The first documented product release was CompassKiosk in 2011; the mobile app followed in 2012, and CompassPay also launched in 2012. The timeline records 1,000 schools in 2015, Ireland in 2016, England in 2021 and a 2,000th school in 2021. [1] The business’s ownership changed materially when Advent Partners exited and EQT announced its 2024 investment. In February 2026, Compass acquired Inklass to strengthen school-branded mobile apps and connect communication, attendance, wellbeing, learning and payments in a more unified family experience. [2] [6]

### Platform Classification Explained

Compass is a multi-tenant, cloud-based SaaS platform. The official company description calls it a “full-suite, cloud-based school management platform,” and the parent guide says the service is available in any modern web browser through a school-specific portal. [2] [5] UK government procurement material describes horizontally scalable services, per-customer databases, multi-cloud and Tier-3 data centres, asynchronous worker pools, automated failover and a 99.9% availability commitment for that service. [11]

There is no public evidence of a Windows/macOS desktop client; staff and families use the browser portal. There is also no public on-premise edition. The platform has native mobile applications named **Compass School Manager** on Apple platforms and Google Play. The parent guide identifies iOS and Android apps and documents news, messages, timetables, calendars, Learning Tasks, conferences, attendance notes, reports, widgets, live class changes, Apple Pay in Australia, and light/dark/system appearance. [5] The acquired Inklass capability adds a school-branded mobile-app route, but the principal store listing remains Compass School Manager. [6] [9] [10]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrolment

**Enrolments Pro** supports an end-to-end journey from expression of interest through enrolment, with a mobile-friendly family experience, admissions pipeline dashboard, application-fee capture through finance integration and connections to third-party enrolment tools. [4] Compass also lists EnrolHQ, Digistorm Funnel and Enquiry Tracker integrations. [3]

#### Student information and records

People Management supplies the central people/student record and acts as the data foundation for enrolment, attendance, wellbeing, reporting and communications. Configurable forms, family/contact data, student profiles, documents, custom flags, role-based permissions and exportable records are evidenced across the product and procurement description. [4] [8] The parent-facing Student Profile brings together today’s classes, activities, observations, tasks, attendance, surveys and reports. [5]

#### Attendance

Attendance provides digital roll marking, centralised attendance management, absenteeism tracking, attendance notes, unexplained absence and lateness workflows, class-by-class summaries, daily snapshots of whereabouts, custom classes, events, Sickbay entries and early departures. Parents can submit reasons, times, comments and attachments, and schools can send absence SMS links for explanations. [4] [5]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

Schedule Builder creates and changes school timetables; Calendar manages class sessions and rolls; Room Swap manages room allocations and changes from a class page; Resource Bookings prevents double-booking of laptops and other resources. Compass also integrates with Edval and Timetabler. [3] [4]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Learning Tasks supports assessment-task creation, distribution, student submissions, marking, feedback and evidence. Compass supports configurable assessment frameworks, curriculum structures and reporting models, but a separate high-stakes examination module is not publicly described. [4] [8]

#### Report cards and transcripts

Semester Reports provides integrated end-of-semester report writing and publication. Progress Reports show performance over time; longitudinal reporting can follow a student from Kindergarten through Year 12. Families can download academic reports as PDFs. A dedicated higher-education transcript/degree-audit function is not evidenced. [4] [5]

#### Learning management and homework

Learning Tasks is the named learning-management module for assignments, due dates, submissions, marking, feedback, resources and parent visibility. Course overviews can link tasks, rubrics and materials; families can see upcoming and overdue work, and semester reporting can draw on learning evidence. Integrations include Canvas, Google Classroom/Hapara, Toddle, SchoolBox, Edrolo and Turnitin. [2] [3] [4]

#### Communication

News Feed provides a central announcement stream. SMS Communication supports bulk SMS, email and push notifications to staff, parents/carers and students; the official feature page explicitly names email, SMS and push, while the parent guide documents messages, alerts and school news. WhatsApp is not publicly listed as a native channel. [4] [5]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

The parent portal and mobile app expose news, attendance, timetables, calendar, tasks, reports, conferences, events, consent, payments, wallet and contact-detail updates. Staff use role-specific portal access for administration, attendance, wellbeing, teaching, meetings and finance. Students can access their timetable, Learning Tasks, feedback and reports where the school enables those functions. Portals can be school-branded with logos, colours and terminology. [4] [5] [8]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Billing Management, Debtor Management, CompassPay and CompassFMS cover contribution/fee invoices, debtor creation, fee splits, receipts, payment history, payment plans, donations, family Wallet top-ups and automatic top-ups. Event Payments and Consent handles excursions and approvals; Online Orders supports canteens; Shop supports uniforms and books. Finance capabilities include general ledger, accounts receivable/payable, fixed assets, budgets, purchase orders, cash flow, balance sheets, bank-transaction validation, receipting and financial dashboards. [2] [4] [5]

#### HR, staff and payroll

People Management, Time Card, Professional Development and Meetings cover staff records, arrivals/departures through Kiosk, development goals/training, meeting agendas, delegated actions and progress tracking. CompassFMS says it includes payroll, but the public feature material does not provide payroll workflows or a standalone HR/payroll product page; it should therefore be validated in procurement. [1] [4]

#### Activities, events and extracurricular management

Events supports excursions, permissions, consent, payments and attendee tracking. The calendar consolidates activities; Compass lists clubs, co-curricular programs, staffing allocations, extras, duty changes, ticketed events and online sales. Clipboard was announced as an extracurricular-management acquisition in the current site’s news feed, while the Inklass acquisition states that it will deepen Clipboard integration. [2] [6]

#### Transport, library, hostel and boarding, and inventory/assets

Compass lists Busminder as a transport integration and Oliver/Accessit for library-related connectivity, but no native route planning, fleet, library-catalogue, hostel or boarding-management module is publicly evidenced. Resource Bookings covers equipment/resource reservations, and finance includes fixed assets. These areas should not be counted as full native modules without a vendor confirmation. [3] [4]

#### Health and medical

Chronicle Sickbay records health incidents such as illness or injury, treatments and a student’s Sickbay presence. The feature set supports an audit trail, parent notifications and daily whereabouts visibility. [4] [5]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

Chronicle monitors student behaviour and wellbeing; Chronicle Meetings records student meetings and automatically generates attendance; Custom Flags highlight vital information; Digest gives selected-student wellbeing updates. Role-based access and audit trails protect sensitive pastoral records. [4]

#### Special education and safeguarding

Compass publicly describes confidential wellbeing workflows, granular permissions, audit trails and safeguarding/duty-of-care controls. A 2026 case study references sharing Individual Education Plans with base schools, indicating support for special-education workflows, but there is no separately branded special-education module or public IEP specification. [2] [4]

#### Alumni and fundraising

The independent-school offering describes alumni records, campaigns, donations, community outreach, events and a dedicated portal. Donations can also be requested alongside school contributions and payment plans. The public material is stronger on community/fundraising workflows than on a full advancement CRM. [2] [4]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Pulse provides data visualisation, dashboards and school-wide insights across attendance, learning and wellbeing, with cohort/year-level comparisons and exports. Chronicle and Digest provide operational wellbeing views; finance offers live reporting, cash flow and balance sheets. Public material does not claim predictive analytics, machine-learning forecasts or an AI assistant. [2] [4]

#### AI features

No production AI assistant, generative AI workflow, predictive model or AI tutor is publicly documented in the official Compass feature catalogue or the reviewed procurement listing. Capterra’s page labels some content “AI summary,” but that is Capterra editorial metadata, not a Compass product capability. [4] [8]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Compass advertises state curriculum reporting for government schools. The UK procurement listing documents statutory and non-statutory exports, including XML census returns, CTF, CSV and other formats, plus pre-built extracts for ParentPay. The Australian site does not publish a complete state-by-state return matrix, so schools should confirm their jurisdiction’s required reports. [2] [11]

#### Mobile apps

Compass School Manager provides native iOS/iPadOS and Android access for staff, students and parents. Features include news, alerts, messages, timetable, calendar, Learning Tasks, conferences, reports, attendance notes, live class changes, payments, Wallet and Apple Pay in Australia. Inklass adds branded app design and deeper real-time integrations. [5] [6] [9] [10]

#### Integrations and APIs

Compass publishes a robust public RESTful API, API documentation and a sandbox/test environment in the UK procurement record. Named integrations include TechnologyOne, Sage Intacct, Xero, MYOB Acumatica, Canvas, Turnitin, Google Classroom/Hapara, Edval, Timetabler, PaperCut, Oliver, Accessit, Busminder, Digistorm Funnel, EnrolHQ, Victorian Student Register, Edrolo, MSP Photography, RollCall, Clipboard and Music Monitor. [3] [11]

#### Security and privacy

The platform uses TLS/encryption, granular role groups, MFA, identity federation, audit information and Australian data centres for the Australian service. Compass states PCI DSS Level 1 compliance for payment handling. The UK service description adds ISO/IEC 27001, Cyber Essentials, annual external penetration testing, encrypted backups, secure erasure and at least 12 months of audit/system-log retention. [4] [5] [11] The Australian privacy policy explains Compass’s processor/service-provider role for client data and the categories of identity, contact, financial, transaction, technical and profile data it may process. [12]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Pulse school-wide analytics:** real-time visualisation of attendance, learning and wellbeing, with cohort and year-level comparisons and exports. [2]
- **50+ connected modules:** the company’s current positioning is an unusually broad K–12 suite spanning administration, learning, wellbeing, community and finance. [1] [4]
- **Configurable workflows and branded portals:** schools can configure wellbeing, pastoral, assessment, reporting and portal settings, with role-governed administration. [8]
- **Embedded school payments:** event consent, contributions, payment plans, donations, Wallet, canteen orders and Shop are connected to the family experience. [4] [5]
- **Public REST API with sandbox:** the API and test environment support custom integrations beyond the named partners. [3] [11]
- **Inklass mobile-app acquisition:** the 2026 transaction adds a school-branded, real-time app strategy across multiple school systems. [6]
- **Operational resilience:** the UK service describes per-customer databases, auto-scaled workers, failover and a 99.9% availability target. [11]

### Pricing Details

Compass does not publish list prices, tiers, per-student rates or implementation fees. The official site routes prospects to a demo and information pack, while G2 says pricing is unavailable and Capterra lists “Contact vendor for pricing,” with no free trial or free version shown. [7] [8] No reliable public third-party price range was found, so no estimate is supplied. Pricing and implementation scope should be requested as an AUD or local-currency school/group quote, with modules, data migration, training, support, payment processing and any school-branded app separated in the proposal. Checked 24 November 2026.

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Compass has the strongest public evidence for a rich REST API, documentation, sandbox and a large partner ecosystem rather than for formal education-data-standard certification. The named ecosystem covers finance/accounting (TechnologyOne, Sage Intacct, Xero and MYOB Acumatica), learning (Canvas, Turnitin, Google Classroom/Hapara, Toddle, SchoolBox and Edrolo), timetabling (Edval and Timetabler), enrolment, photos, transport, library, printing and co-curricular management. [3] [11]

No source reviewed confirms OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi or SIF certification, nor Clever/ClassLink marketplace certification. The UK listing does document identity federation with an existing provider such as Google Apps and built-in data exports in XML, CSV, CTF, PDF and other formats. Google Workspace/Microsoft 365 should be treated as integration-specific rather than assumed platform-wide; WhatsApp is not publicly documented. [3] [11]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Compass states that its Australian service is hosted in Australia-based data centres, uses industry-standard encryption and meets PCI DSS Level 1 obligations for payments. The privacy policy identifies Compass Education Pty Ltd as the Australian entity and describes school/client responsibility for the legal basis and notices applicable to client-uploaded student data. [4] [5] [12]

The UK government listing reports ISO/IEC 27001-aligned security governance, Cyber Essentials, MFA, identity federation, annual external penetration testing, TLS 1.2+, encrypted backups, secure deletion and audit logs retained for at least 12 months. It reports EEA storage/processing for that UK service. [11] This should not be generalized to every Compass geography without contract confirmation. No public SOC 2 report was found. The reviewed sources do not establish FERPA, COPPA or India DPDP Act certification/attestation; GDPR/UK GDPR controls are described in the UK procurement material, not as a global certification. No publicly reported security incident was identified in the reviewed official, procurement or review sources through 24 November 2026.

### Strengths

- Broad, genuinely connected coverage for Australian K–12 operations: SIS, attendance, wellbeing, learning, family communication, payments and finance. [2] [4]
- Current official adoption claim of 5,000 schools and 5M users, with a 99%+ retention claim and 270+ Australia-based staff. [1]
- Strong local fit: Australian data-centre positioning, Australian state reporting, local support, Apple Pay Australia and education-specific partners. [2] [4] [5]
- A large public partner catalogue and REST API reduce the risk of being completely locked into one vendor. [3] [11]
- Native mobile apps cover parents, students and staff, while Inklass expands branding and cross-system mobile access. [5] [6]
- Finance is unusually deep for a school MIS, extending from family billing and consent to general ledger, budgets, purchase orders and fixed assets. [2] [4]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Pricing is quote-only, with no public tiers, per-student rate or implementation price, making comparison and budgeting difficult. [7] [8]
- Review evidence is thin on G2: only one verified review is shown, so the 4.0/5 rating is not statistically meaningful. [7]
- The Android listing is materially weaker than iOS at 3.4/5 from 18.5K reviews; users report sign-outs, difficult login, attachment crashes, inconsistent navigation and a dated or confusing interface. [9]
- Apple reviews praise reliable schedules and frequent updates but report poor mobile access to resources/feedback, awkward payment confirmation, staff email-account behaviour and repeated card-entry/batch-payment friction. [10]
- The mobile experience can diverge from the browser experience, and some workflows depend on school configuration or optional modules; families may need workarounds when schools enable only part of the suite. [5] [10]
- Transport, library, hostel/boarding, formal examination management and standards certification are not publicly evidenced as native capabilities; integrations should not be mistaken for built-in modules. [3] [8]
