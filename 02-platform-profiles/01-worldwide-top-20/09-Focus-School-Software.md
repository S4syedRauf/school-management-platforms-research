### Overview

Focus School Software is a U.S.-focused student information and enterprise resource planning suite built primarily for large and midsize public-school districts. Its integrated SIS covers enrollment, student records, scheduling, attendance, gradebook, assessment, discipline, health, reporting and family portals, while Focus ERP adds finance, purchasing, assets, HR, payroll and employee self-service. The vendor also sells adjacent CTE/adult-education, special-student-services, analytics, communications, before/after-care, document-management, hall-pass and attendance-device solutions. [1] [2] [10]

The company says Andrew Schmadeke founded it in 2006 and launched the K-12 SIS that year. Current materials describe 180 employees, 3.7 million students across 16 states, and large implementations including Hillsborough, Fort Worth, Los Angeles Unified and Miami-Dade. No acquisition-led ownership history is disclosed. In February 2026, Focus added a state-approved Tennessee procurement route. [1] [3] [11]

### Platform Classification Explained

**Browser access and hosting.** Focus describes a web-based system available from any device through one login, interface and database. Its three-tier database/business-logic/presentation architecture uses a SQL relational database. Districts can use customer-owned servers or remote Focus servers, while the vendor also offers AWS-partner cloud hosting; this supports a hybrid rather than cloud-only classification. [2]

**Desktop and on-premise.** No installed desktop client is documented, but customer-owned servers are explicitly supported, so on-premise or district-hosted deployment exists. [2]

**Native mobile.** Focus describes a native Mobile Community/Focus App for iOS, Android and Windows. Apple’s developer page shows many district-branded Education View applications rather than one universal app. Mobile portals expose grades, attendance, schedules, assignments, messages, alerts, billing and other role-based functions. [2] [8] [12]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions, enrollment and school choice

Online enrollment and registration supports district-configured applications, supporting-document collection, multilingual forms, configurable edit rules, approvals, re-enrollment and school-choice applications. The school-choice workflow includes lottery assignment and parent/student notification. [13]

#### Student information and records

The core SIS holds configurable state/federal and district-specific fields, searchable student demographics, unlimited contacts and addresses, custody, emergency, legal-guardianship, pickup and communication-preference flags. Administrator documentation also exposes enrollment history, re-enrollment, vouchers, instructional method, threat classification, postsecondary placement, communication logs, letter logs, uploaded files, student summaries, change requests, seating charts, magnet/special assignments, Title I eligibility, ID badges and audit trails. [14]

#### Attendance and tardy management

Teachers can take attendance from desktop or phone in list or seating-chart views. Administrators receive missed-attendance alerts, edit records, manage excuse notes and contact parents; families receive real-time late/absence notifications and can upload excuse notes. Focus Kiosk handles sign-in/out for school, classes, clinics and offices, while the Mobile Scanner reads student-ID barcodes, marks tardies, prints slips and can print detention instructions. [15] [16]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

Elementary scheduling supports constraints such as students together/apart, teacher assignment, behavior and testing criteria, an Optimize function for distributing students using gender, discipline and assessment data, and drag-and-drop placement. Secondary Master Schedule Builder supports periods, teacher availability, room use, course defaults, automated, teacher-approved and student-entered requests, scenario/build reports, sections, mass scheduling and drag-and-drop teacher schedules. Students, parents and teachers can view course-list or weekly-planner schedules. [17]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Focus Gradebook supports configurable calculations, categories, assignment types, completion and extra credit, student groups, assignment uploads, annotation, grading by standards, rubrics, points, percentages, correct/incorrect responses and proficiency. The assessment system supports district, school and teacher-created formative and benchmark assessments, state/national result imports, Student Portal delivery, live progress reporting, immediate results, 52 item types, accommodations and IEP-driven text-to-speech assignment. Learnosity and Certica Navigate Item Bank are named assessment partners. [18] [19]

#### Report cards and transcripts

Report cards can be published to portals or printed/mailed at school or district level. Students and parents receive real-time grades and progress reports, and can download or print unofficial transcripts from the portal. [20]

#### Learning management and homework

Focus is not presented as a standalone full LMS, but includes lesson planning, assignments, class resources, work upload and a student planner. A standalone Gradebook and Scheduling solution is listed; Moodle, Canvas, Schoology, Blackboard, Google Apps for Education and OneNote connections are documented. [1] [2] [5]

#### Communication

The communications module provides two-way messaging among students, parents, teachers and administrators; targeted announcements; SMS, email, portal alerts and letters; secure messages; scheduled one-time or recurring sends; automatic translation into more than 70 languages; polls with multiple-choice, reactions, true/false and free-response modes; sign-ups; message reporting; and voice/text-to-speech calling. No official WhatsApp integration was found. [10] [12]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

Role-based portals provide alerts, announcements, news, recent/favorite programs, searches, knowledge-base access and the SIS menu. Student and parent portals expose enrollment, schedules, attendance, grades, assignments, calendar, news, events, class resources, links, forms, alerts, messages, report cards, transcripts and billing. Teacher portals support attendance, gradebook, lesson planning, messages, classroom resources and caseloads. [12]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Student Billing provides a sales/transaction-history/customer-ledger view and a portal/app shopping cart for courses, trips, parking permits, band, library and other fees. Parents, staff and teachers can pay by card or e-check; Focus Wallet stores payment methods, supports default settings, automatic payments and assigning cards to students through Authorize.net. ERP adds budgets, journal entries, purchase orders, invoices, checks, receipts, deposits, bank reconciliation, vendor tax forms and board-packet reports. [21] [22]

#### HR, staff and payroll

Focus ERP maintains pay steps, positions, leave banks, deductions and benefits; processes hourly/daily timecards, leave, adjustments, raises, bonuses and reimbursements; and gives employees self-service access to leave requests, pay stubs, benefits, workers’ compensation claims and tax forms. [22]

#### Transport

Transportation is not documented as a native operational module in the current feature pages. The technology page lists Versatrans, Trapeze Mapnet and EDULOG as integration targets, so transport interoperability exists but a native routing/fleet feature should not be claimed. [2]

#### Library

Library management is likewise not documented as a native Focus module. Follett Destiny Library Manager and Destiny Textbook Manager are listed as integrations. [2]

#### Hostel and boarding

No native hostel, dormitory or boarding-management module was found in the reviewed official feature pages or public administrator documentation.

#### Inventory and assets

ERP supports inventory reports, scheduled inventory, asset transfers and asset depreciation. The finance page also names purchasing, invoices, receipts and packing-slip document management. [22]

#### Health and medical

The Health module stores immunization and health records individually or in bulk, accepts parent-uploaded health documents, manages health forms, physician notes and alerts, medication needs and dispensing logs, screenings, consultations, assessments, medication administrations and clinic kiosk check-in/out. [23]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

Discipline/PBIS supports online referrals, district rules and consistent consequences, automatic detention for excessive tardies, portal alerts, minor-infraction logging, positive-behavior badges, parent opt-in notifications and trend reports combining discipline with demographics, attendance and assessment data. Pastoral-adjacent workflows include mental wellness and threat assessment in Special Student Services. [24] [25]

#### Special education and safeguarding

Special Student Services covers IEPs, gifted and service plans, Section 504, threat assessment, mental wellness, MTSS/RTI for academic and behavior response, English-language learners, student services and medical processes. The configurable Form Builder offers validation, cloning, configurable headers, text areas, radio/checkbox groups, e-signatures and dropdowns; caseloads, events, portal alerts and compliance checks are built into workflows. [25]

#### Alumni and fundraising

No native alumni-relations or fundraising/advancement module was found in the reviewed Focus materials.

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Every SIS module has built-in reports; Ad Hoc Reports provide drag-and-drop design; District Custom Reports use SQL queries. Optional Analytics provides seven data areas, more than 40 dashboards, district/school/student snapshots, subgroup filtering, raw-and-visual views, nightly refreshes and teacher-scoped reporting. “What-if Analysis” projects enrollments from five prior years and can incorporate county population projections for five years. [26] [27]

#### AI features

No production AI assistant, generative-AI copilot or machine-learning assistant was identified in the official product pages, documentation or current press material. Focus’s predictive “What-if Analysis” is a rules/statistical projection feature, not evidence of an AI assistant. [26]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Point-of-entry validation, verification summaries, drill-down corrections and upload-ready extracts are core features. Official coverage includes Arizona AzEDS/Ed-Fi; California CALPADS; Florida DOE/FES/WDIS/FTE, ELL, exceptional-student, assessment, discipline, SESIR and FLEID; Massachusetts SIMS/EPIMS/SCS/SSDR and SIF; Maryland; New Hampshire I4See; New Jersey NJ SMART; Pennsylvania PIMS; Texas PEIMS/TREx/TSDS/STAAR; Utah UTRex; Missouri MOSIS; Ohio EMIS; and developing Washington, Louisiana, New York and Tennessee coverage. [2]

#### Mobile apps

The native Mobile Community/Focus App and district-branded iOS/Android applications provide portal access, messages, alerts, grades, attendance, schedules, billing and hall-pass actions. The technology page also states Windows support. Focus documentation separately covers kiosk, handheld, mobile and attendance apps. [2] [8] [28]

#### Integrations and APIs

Focus states that it supports API calls, OneRoster and Ed-Fi certification, secure FTP, batch processing and Manage Integrations tools. Named integrations include Canvas, Schoology, Blackboard, Google Apps for Education, Microsoft OneNote, Clever, ClassLink, Moodle, special-education systems, food-service systems, transportation systems, library systems and alert providers. Administrator documentation exposes Ed-Fi setup, descriptor maps, resource synchronization, logs, Blackboard APIs, Moodle API setup, Google Drive/OneDrive export and SSO. [2] [14] [28]

#### Security and privacy

Public security claims include MFA, encryption at rest and in transit, field-level security, high-profile-student information hiding, automatic backups, SAML/SSO and audit trails. The May 20, 2024 privacy policy says the website is hosted in the United States and describes U.S. transfers, COPPA treatment and reasonable safeguards. The reviewed materials do not establish SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification, GDPR/India DPDP compliance, a more specific data-center region, or a public security incident. [2] [29] [30]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **State reporting at district scale:** validation, extracts, IDs, submissions and verification workflows span many jurisdictions. [2]
- **No-code configuration:** Form Builder and workflow triggers support validations, approvals, e-signatures and alerts. [25] [28]
- **Predictive enrollment planning:** What-if Analysis uses five years of history and county population inputs. [26]
- **Unified SIS/ERP:** student, finance, HR and payroll data share one login, interface and database. [2] [22]
- **Advanced scheduling and assessment:** elementary optimization, secondary scenarios, 52 item types and IEP-linked accommodations. [17] [19]
- **Accessible operations:** mobile tardy hardware and two-way translation in 70+ languages extend the core platform. [10] [16]

### Pricing Details

Focus publishes no district SIS/ERP subscription rate card, tier names, per-student price or implementation fee. Capterra and Software Advice list pricing as contact-vendor, with no free trial or free version. [5] [6] [7] The guide’s approximately $3,000 SourceForge reference could not be verified and is not treated as subscription pricing. Hardware prices checked November 24, 2026 are $569 for a Focus Attendance Kiosk and $329 plus $14 shipping for a Mobile Scanner; these are not SaaS rates. [16]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Focus publicly claims OneRoster and Ed-Fi certification, API calls, secure FTP, batch processing and a Manage Integrations tool. Ed-Fi Alliance independently lists Focus SIS as certified for the Student Information Systems API for Data Standard v5 through February 19, 2027, with implementation verification in Glendale Union High School District and Higley USD, Arizona; earlier v4 and Suite 3 certifications are also listed. [2] [28] The public administrator help center documents Ed-Fi ODS credentials, version detection, resource and school synchronization, descriptor maps, debug transfers and logs. [14] Named ecosystem connections include Clever, ClassLink, Google Apps for Education, Canvas, Schoology, Blackboard, OneNote, Moodle, food service, library, transportation, alert, special-education and assessment systems. No 1EdTech marketplace, LTI certification or SIF certification beyond the specific Massachusetts state-reporting note was established. [2]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

The technology page claims MFA, encryption at rest/in transit, field security, restricted visibility, AWS Partner status and backups. Its responsible-disclosure policy provides a vulnerability channel and typical 90-day remediation period. [2] [30] The May 20, 2024 privacy policy says the website is hosted in the U.S. and addresses COPPA, U.S. transfers and safeguards. Focus markets FERPA, HIPAA and COPPA alignment, but no public SOC 2, ISO 27001, GDPR, DPDP, PCI-DSS attestation or incident disclosure was found. [2] [29]

### Strengths

- Deep K-12 public-district coverage with documented support for complex state reporting across many U.S. jurisdictions. [2]
- Strong adoption evidence: more than 3.7 million students, 16 states and several very large districts as of 2026. [1] [3]
- Broad native SIS/ERP scope, including finance, HR, payroll, health, special services, assessment, communications and portals. [1] [22]
- Configurability through custom fields, SQL/ad-hoc reports, Form Builder, workflow triggers and district-specific forms. [25] [26]
- Ed-Fi and OneRoster interoperability, open API claims, SSO options and numerous legacy education-system integrations. [2] [28]
- Users commonly praise one-place access to student data, simple grade entry and attendance, and strong reporting flexibility. [5] [6] [7]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Pricing is quote-only; neither Focus nor the major review sites expose a district subscription baseline, complicating procurement comparisons. [5] [6] [7]
- Reviewers report crashes, slowness or intermittent outages, although one reviewer noted that stability improved after modifications and another said downtime might be district-specific. [5] [6]
- Navigation can feel cluttered or dated, with many tabs, too many clicks, unclear workflows and limited screen personalization. [6] [7]
- Teachers report feature discoverability, training and support gaps; Capterra users cite difficult report-card printing and support access. [5] [6] [7]
- The product has no publicly evidenced native transport, library, hostel/boarding, alumni/fundraising or AI-assistant module; several rely on integrations or are not documented. [2]
