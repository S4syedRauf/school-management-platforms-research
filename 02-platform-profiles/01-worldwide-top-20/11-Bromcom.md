### Overview

Bromcom is a UK school management information system (MIS) that combines pupil and staff records, attendance, assessment, examinations, behaviour, safeguarding, communication, parent and student engagement, reporting, finance and trust-level analytics. Its positioning is deliberately “one-stop shop”: the MIS, integrated school finance, MyChildAtSchool parent services, Student Portal, Teacher App, MAT Vision and Power BI are intended to reduce the number of bolt-on systems a school must operate. [1] [6] [8] [9]

The company was founded by Ali Guryel in 1986. Its milestones distinguish the complete Bromcom School MIS (2005) from the first UK cloud MIS (2011), so the company’s age should not be confused with the cloud product’s launch date. Bromcom also says MyChildAtSchool launched in 2002; Wikipedia’s independently sourced history dates the parent portal to June 2000. Public evidence reviewed here confirms Bromcom as a long-established supplier rather than a recently launched SaaS product. [2] [26]

Bromcom remains privately controlled according to independent market reporting, although the legal entity is a public limited company. It has expanded into integrated finance, MAT Vision, HR and AI-assisted workflows. Its latest public customer claim is 5,600+ schools, but independent reporting is more conservative and dated; buyers should reconcile “schools,” “users,” and active paid institutions. [1] [17] [18]

### Platform Classification Explained

- **Browser/cloud delivery:** The MIS and free apps are accessible from an online device with a web browser. Bromcom describes the service as fully managed and hosted on Microsoft Azure, with real-time access for staff, parents and students. [6] [7] [12]
- **Cloud provider and region:** Bromcom’s Trust Center identifies Microsoft Azure as the cloud provider and lists the hosting location as UK for the Bromcom MIS service. [24]
- **Desktop client:** No current Windows/macOS desktop client is identified in Bromcom’s current product pages or documentation portfolio. The reporting and administration tools are described as web-based.
- **On-premise option:** Bromcom markets cloud delivery and does not present a current on-premise edition. Hosting is included in the annual service charge and covers Azure costs. [12] [13]
- **Native mobile apps:** The exact named apps are **MyChildAtSchool - Parent App** for parents, **Bromcom Student App** for students, and **Bromcom Teacher App** for teachers. All three appear in Apple’s developer catalogue; the three corresponding Android listings are also present. [19] [20] [21] [22] [23]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrolment
Admissions is listed as a core module. Student tools support pre-admission search, onboarding, profiles, groups, contacts and school-specific fields; a full prospect CRM or online application funnel is not publicly evidenced. [4] [12]

#### Student information and records
The student record covers profiles, contacts, photographs, demographics, groups, characteristics, medical information, documents, permissions, flags, interventions, FSM and EAL. Dynamic groups can automatically add or remove students as attendance, assessment, behaviour or demographic criteria change. [4] [7] [12]

#### Attendance
Attendance covers statutory AM/PM, lesson, class, club and trip registers; quick registers; missing-register monitoring; code/subcode configuration; letters and notifications; scheduled reports; threshold groups; DfE benchmarking; dashboards; and mobile/tablet registration. [6] [7] [12]

#### Timetable and master scheduling
Cloud wTimeTable creates timetables from user-defined rules and relationships, flags clashes and blank sessions, supports timetable queries and connects to cover management. Parent/student timetable views, curriculum structures, room/equipment booking and a school diary are also included. [7] [12]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations
Assessment provides configurable marksheets, formative/summative tracking, headline figures, custom templates, calculations, colour-coded Primary Tracker marksheets, EYFS/test scores, statutory stages, cohort analysis and Excel import/export. Secondary examinations cover base-data imports, seating, submissions, results and internal/external workflows. [7] [12]

#### Report cards and transcripts
Student report cards are explicitly included for special schools. Reporting can attach Word templates, generate letters, certificates and reports, publish results to MyChildAtSchool and use mail merge. The reviewed public material does not confirm a higher-education-style transcript, credit or degree-audit function. [12]

#### Learning management and homework
Classroom Management includes assignments, homework and quizzes, lesson plans, seating plans, detentions and teacher dashboards. Bromcom AI can draft lesson plans, quizzes and homework; approved quizzes can be assigned to students and automatically marked. Bromcom is not presented as a full standalone LMS with a public LTI course catalogue. [10] [12]

#### Communication
The Communications module supports email, SMS, SMS Text Back, push notifications, school announcements, a school diary, parent/student/staff messaging, communication logs and automated messages driven by attendance, behaviour, watchlists or policies. SMS and email services can incur separate charges; the G-Cloud price sheet lists SMS bundles and SMTP packages. WhatsApp is not publicly documented as a native channel. [4] [12] [13]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals
MyChildAtSchool gives parents real-time timetable, homework, attendance, behaviour, assessment, clubs, trips, announcements, consent, parents’ evenings, payments, dinner money and school-shop access. Student Portal exposes attendance, timetable, behaviour, exams, lesson plans, events, clubs, documents and detentions. The Teacher Dashboard/App provides registers, behaviour events and messaging. [1] [4] [12] [19] [20]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting
Bromcom Finance is an integrated cloud accounting system with purchases, accounts payable/receivable, refunds, debit-card transactions, bank/VAT reconciliation, cost-centre and ledger links, budget profiles, payment mapping, dashboards, CFR/EFSA reporting and staff-contract salary data. MCAS supports dinner money, trips, clubs, wraparound care, instalments, shop products and stock control. [9] [12] [13]

#### HR, staff and payroll
The MAT Enterprise offer includes HR, while the MIS includes staff profiles, contracts, salary spine/base-pay structures, qualifications, background checks, CPD and Single Central Record (SCR) management. Bromcom documents workforce census and HR reporting. A complete native payroll engine is not clearly evidenced in the public material; Bromcom lists Access Education, Jane and other HR/payroll partners instead. [8] [11] [12]

#### Transport
No native transport-routing, fleet, bus-pass or vehicle-management module was found in Bromcom’s public product portfolio or reviewed documentation. Schools may integrate third-party systems through the API/partner ecosystem. [4] [5] [11]

#### Library
No native cataloguing, circulation, acquisitions or library-management module was identified in the reviewed public portfolio. [4] [5]

#### Hostel and boarding
No native hostel, boarding-house, room-allocation or residential-life module was identified in the reviewed public portfolio. [4] [5]

#### Inventory and assets
The core platform supports equipment and room booking, document management, school-shop product and stock control, and finance-linked purchasing. It is not publicly described as a full fixed-asset register or warehouse-management suite. [12] [13]

#### Health and medical
Health and Medical is a listed core feature for special schools. MyChildAtSchool lets parents update medical data; the MIS provides restricted access, documents and safeguarding records. Bromcom’s Trust Center states that customer personally identifiable, personal health and credit-card information are data categories it protects. [12] [24]

#### Behaviour, discipline and pastoral care
Behaviour Management records positive, challenging and neutral events, configurable policies, automated actions and follow-ups, points/tallies, certificates, dashboards, trend lines, rolling averages and cohort drill-down. Detention Management, suspensions, pathways, watchlists, alerts and behaviour communications are supported. [7] [12] [4]

#### Special education and safeguarding
SEND/SEN/ALN management, interventions, provision mapping, IEP/EHCP/IDP/PEP writing, statement-level tracking, student support and safeguarding are documented. Safeguarding includes incident logs, incident type, other agencies, body maps, attachments, timelines, restricted access and configurable alerts. [12] [5]

#### Alumni and fundraising
No alumni CRM, advancement, donor management or fundraising module was identified in the reviewed public portfolio. Online payments and school-shop functions are operational collections tools, not evidence of a fundraising suite. [4] [5] [12]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting
Analysis and Reporting combine ready-made reports, Quick Reports, Advanced Report Builder, ad-hoc joins, conditional formatting, charts, mail merge, scheduling and Excel/Word export. Dashboards cover attendance, behaviour, attainment, demographics, safeguarding, KS2/KS4/KS5, benchmarking, pupil groups and finance. MAT Vision adds cross-school consolidation, drill-down, central configuration, exports, live feeds and embedded Power BI; Tableau is also referenced. [1] [8] [12]

#### AI features
Bromcom AI is a production school co-pilot integrated into the MIS. It uses Microsoft Azure/OpenAI services for general queries, multilingual translation, lesson plans, quizzes, homework, letters and emails, with prompts, tone/length controls and automatic quiz marking. Internal-data insight, trend analysis and pupil-report summaries are publicly described as coming soon, so predictive analytics should not be treated as fully shipped. [10] [13]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance
Bromcom supports DfE School Census, Workforce Census, statutory returns, validation and summary reports, Consistent Financial Reporting, Welsh Census, Hwb integration and Catholic Census. The platform is designed around UK statutory reporting rather than US state reporting. [6] [9] [12]

#### Mobile apps
The parent, student and teacher apps provide native iOS and Android access. The Student App exposes attendance, timetable, behaviour, exams, lesson plans, events, clubs, documents and detentions, and supports Microsoft/Google SSO where enabled. The Parent App handles engagement, messaging, attendance, payments and school information; the Teacher App supports daily teacher tasks. [19] [20] [21] [22] [23]

#### Integrations and APIs
Bromcom publishes a secure web-service API and API-partner programme. Documented connections include Google, Office 365, Microsoft/Google/Active Directory login, Power BI, Tableau, Excel add-in, Hwb, SIMS Connector for Vision, payment providers and partners for HR/payroll, analytics, admissions, catering, identity, visitors, safeguarding and communications. OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever and ClassLink certification is not publicly evidenced. [4] [5] [11] [12]

#### Security and privacy
Role and field-level permissions, audit trails, time/location controls, two-factor authentication, SSO, restricted safeguarding areas and secure APIs are documented. Bromcom’s Trust Center lists ISO 27001:2022, Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus, Azure UK hosting and a public subprocessors list. Its privacy policy treats Bromcom as a processor for school/MAT data and cites UK GDPR rights. [12] [24]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Integrated MIS plus Finance:** A single cloud data model links pupil, staff, purchasing, budgeting, statutory finance and parent payments rather than requiring a separate FMS. [9]
- **MAT Vision:** Trust teams can consolidate data across Bromcom and non-Bromcom schools, drill from trust to school, cascade configuration and embed Power BI dashboards. [8]
- **AI content workflows:** Bromcom AI can draft lesson plans, quizzes, homework, emails and letters inside the MIS, translate them and auto-mark approved quizzes. [10]
- **Dynamic groups and automated workflows:** Attendance, behaviour, assessment and demographic rules can create cohorts, alerts, letters and follow-up actions without manual re-keying. [6] [7]
- **UK statutory depth:** DfE, workforce, Welsh, Catholic and CFR/EFSA reporting is unusually central to the platform’s UK-market design. [6] [9] [12]
- **One parent account across children:** Parents can view multiple children, book appointments, provide consent and pay for clubs, trips, meals and shop products from MCAS. [12]

### Pricing Details

Bromcom’s public pricing page is per-pupil and quote-based. Standard includes core MIS, assessment, attendance, census, communication, cover, exams, reports, statutory returns, teacher app and timetable; Plus adds analysis, behaviour, classroom management, clubs/trips, dinner, MCAS, Primary Tracker, Power BI and Student Portal; One-Stop-Shop adds advanced reporting, AI, CPD, Google/Office 365, Options and Safeguarding. [3]

The February 2025 G-Cloud 14 schedule is a dated public-sector reference. Combined MIS and Finance is **£6.55/£9.55/£11.55 per primary pupil** for Standard/Plus/One-Stop-Shop and **£9/£10/£12 per secondary pupil**; support is separate and Azure hosting is included. [13]

The schedule lists primary onboarding at £250/£600 and secondary at £250/£1,100 depending on migration, £500 academy-conversion fees per MIS or Finance, online payments at 1.275% per transaction, SMS from £300 per 5,000 and SMTP from £99 annually. Re-quote because the document is dated. [13]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Bromcom has a public API partner programme and secure web-service API. Documented connections include Azure, Microsoft 365, Google, Active Directory, Microsoft/Google SSO, Power BI, Tableau, Excel, Hwb, SIMS Connector for Vision, payments, SMS/SMTP and partners for HR/payroll, analytics, admissions, catering, identity, visitors, safeguarding and communications. [11] [12] [24]

The reviewed material does not verify OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever or ClassLink certification. Its API ecosystem is open, but public evidence does not support a claim of multi-standard certification.

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Bromcom’s Trust Center lists ISO 27001:2022, Cyber Essentials, Cyber Essentials Plus, UK Azure hosting, technical/organisational measures and subprocessors. Its privacy policy describes Bromcom as a processor for school, LA and MAT data under UK GDPR and recognises data-subject rights and transfer safeguards. [24]

No public evidence was found for SOC 2, FERPA, COPPA or India DPDP Act certification. Schools remain data controllers for pupil data.

Bromcom disclosed a September 2026 SSO-registration incident. Its FAQ says an unauthorised party accessed a separate component and retained some registration email addresses, but not passwords, tokens, MIS data or authentication services; Bromcom withdrew the legacy function, added checks and improved logging. [25]

### Strengths

- Broad native UK-school coverage spanning MIS, assessment, attendance, behaviour, safeguarding, parent engagement, finance and MAT oversight.
- Azure cloud delivery, browser access and native apps reduce dependence on school servers.
- Integrated Finance, parent payments, activities, statutory reporting, MAT Vision, Power BI, workflow automation and a public API ecosystem can replace bolt-ons.
- Public trust centre, ISO 27001:2022 and Cyber Essentials/Plus evidence support procurement due diligence.

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- G2 had no verified reviews and EdTech Impact had no reviews when checked, so independent enterprise-user evidence is thin. [14] [15]
- Trustpilot was 1.5/5 from 42 reviews on 24 November 2026; complaints included slow loading, crashes, login problems, a confusing interface, difficult reporting and term-start reliability. [16]
- Google Play and Apple reviews cited student login failures, lag, homework/attendance/timetable problems and crashes; an Apple Teacher review reported register-taking crashes. [20] [21] [23]
- The public pricing page is quote-based; only a dated G-Cloud schedule exposes numbers, making like-for-like budgeting difficult.
- Transport, library, hostel/boarding, alumni/fundraising and full payroll are not evidenced as native modules, and major interoperability standards are not publicly certified in the reviewed sources.
- The September 2026 SSO incident, although Bromcom says it was limited to registration email addresses and did not affect the MIS, creates additional due-diligence work for schools. [25]

### Key Milestones

- **1986:** Ali Guryel founded Bromcom in London; Companies House records incorporation on 24 July 1986, initially as Shoebox Ltd. [2] [18]
- **2000/2002:** MyChildAtSchool parent-portal launch is dated 2000 by Wikipedia’s independent history and 2002 by Bromcom’s own milestones. [2] [26]
- **2005:** Bromcom launched its complete School MIS. [2]
- **2011:** Bromcom launched the UK’s first cloud-based MIS for schools. [2] [17]
- **2012:** Bromcom won a place on a DfE £575m framework for cloud MIS and learning-platform services to local authorities. [2]
- **2024:** Bromcom reported an Education Authority Northern Ireland contract covering 1,100+ schools. [2]
- **2025:** Wrexham became the first Welsh local authority to select Bromcom’s cloud MIS for 71 schools; the G-Cloud pricing schedule was prepared in February. [2] [13]
- **2026:** Bromcom celebrated 40 years, launched/expanded Bromcom AI and disclosed the September SSO-registration incident in an October FAQ update. [1] [10] [25]
