### Overview

Arbor Education is a UK cloud school management information system (MIS) used by schools, MAT central teams and local authorities. It centralises student, staff, attendance, assessment, behaviour, safeguarding, census, communications, payments and operational data in a browser-accessible system. Arbor’s School MIS is sold in Core, Comms and Perform packages; the wider School Management Suite adds Arbor Finance, SAMpeople HR/recruitment, TimeTabler, Workflows and Arbor AI. [1] [5]

The company says Arbor MIS launched in 2011. Its 2020 sale to The Key Support Services transferred Arbor Education Partners to The Key/Darwin Acquisitions; Arbor MIS and Arbor Insight then served more than 1,200 schools and MATs. [2] [10] The Key acquired RM Integris, RM Finance and RM Accounts in June 2023, bringing the group’s cloud MIS brands above 7,000 schools and relaunching RM Finance as Arbor Finance. [11] The current Arbor site claims more than 12,500 schools and 930 MATs, not an independently audited count. [1]

### Platform Classification Explained

**Browser/cloud access.** Arbor says the MIS is not stored on a physical school server and is accessible anywhere with internet. Classroom365 likewise describes browser access, a single cloud login for MATs and dependence on reliable internet/Wi-Fi. [3] [12]

**Hosting.** Arbor says the MIS is hosted in Amazon Web Services’ London region, with personal data and backups in the UK, a private firewalled network, single-tenant database model and AES-256 encryption. [13]

**Desktop/on-premise.** No on-premise Arbor MIS deployment is documented. TimeTabler is listed by Arbor as a separate leading timetabling product, and the 2024/25 rate card states that its Options product is cloud-based but TimeTabler licences are sold separately because TimeTabler is not a cloud product. [1] [14]

**Native mobile.** Arbor has iOS/Android Parent/Guardian and Student apps; the Student App launched in November 2024. [8] [9] [15]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrolment

Admissions supports applications through enrolment, applicant records, admissions appeals and appeal numbers, new-student onboarding and year-end/new-school-year setup. Perform includes Admissions, while Workflows can automate new-student admissions and collect form data, reminders, approvals and documents. [5] [16]

#### Student information and records

Student Profiles provide the central record for contacts, relationships, education history, demographic and contextual data, documents, custom fields and data-quality flags. Arbor supports bulk actions, live feeds, duplicate detection, profile exports for subject-access requests, and a shared data model connecting attendance, behaviour, assessment, medical, SEN and communication records. [5] [13]

#### Attendance

The attendance module provides digital registers, configurable attendance codes, statutory attendance calculations, live attendance dashboards, threshold alerts, automated absence follow-up and persistent-absence analysis. The AI layer adds Auto Absence, which listens to, transcribes, translates for EAL families and logs absence-related voicemail for staff approval. [5] [17]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

Arbor displays student and staff timetables, calendars, lesson gaps and clashes, and supports cover management and staff absence workflows. Scheduling is complemented by separate TimeTabler and third-party Edval integrations; Arbor does not claim that its core MIS is a full master-scheduling engine. [1] [14] [18]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Assessments include flexible marksheets, assessment frameworks, progress tracking, curriculum tracking, intervention tracking, analysis over time, assessment-policy pushdown across MAT schools and attainment drill-down to individual profiles. Perform adds assignments and deeper assessment workflows. Examinations cover exam schedules, qualifications/units, awards, exam-related records and examination census preparation. [5] [6] [16]

#### Report cards and transcripts

Arbor supports student report cards, downloadable report cards in the Parent App, print-preview report cards, targets and progress views. [5] [8]

#### Learning management and homework

The Student App exposes assignments, homework and calendar information, while My Classroom supports classroom management, seating plans, random picker, lesson notes and key student information. Google Classroom integration synchronises Arbor data with a digital learning platform; specialist homework tools such as Show My Homework are available through partners. [5] [9] [18]

#### Communication

Staff can send searchable email and SMS, in-app messages and bulk communications to defined student, parent or staff groups. The Parent and Student Apps provide push-style in-app messages. Arbor’s documented integrations include ParentMail, Teachers2Parents, SchoolComms, Groupcall and other communication providers; WhatsApp is not publicly documented as a native channel. [5] [18]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

The Parent Portal and Arbor Parent & Guardian App expose attendance, behaviour points, academic progress, report cards, timetables, homework, medical updates, clubs, trips, consultation bookings and payments. The Student Portal/App provides calendar, announcements, attendance, behaviour points, assignments, biometric login and Ask Arbor. Staff use the browser MIS, My Classroom and role dashboards; no separate native teacher app is evidenced. [5] [8] [9] [15]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Payments Core handles trips, clubs, meals, wraparound care, account top-ups, cash/card/cheque/childcare-voucher payments, manual and bulk invoices, cashless-catering integrations, reconciliation reporting and multi-bank-account payouts. Payments Plus adds school shop, automatic student/group pricing, Apple Pay, Google Pay, rotating meal menus and parent meal choice. [19] [11]

#### HR, staff and payroll

Staff Management covers staff profiles, contracts, positions, qualifications, checks, absences, cover and appraisals; MAT MIS adds central-team HR records and a Single Central Record. Workforce Census workflows require positions, contracts and staff data. SAMpeople is the group’s separate HR/recruitment product; payroll is not listed as a core Arbor MIS module. [5] [6] [16]

#### Transport, library, hostel and boarding

No native transport routing, library circulation, hostel/boarding or dormitory module is publicly listed for Arbor MIS. These areas can be handled through specialist integrations or external systems. Independent-school and FE product pages do not establish a native boarding or library subsystem, so these capabilities should not be assumed. [1] [18]

#### Inventory and assets

Arbor Workflows includes an asset library and can capture data, documents, approvals and operational processes; MAT packages list an asset library. This is workflow-oriented asset/process management, not a clearly documented fixed-asset register, procurement or barcode inventory module. [6] [16]

#### Health and medical

Medical Tracker logs medical conditions, incidents, medication and care plans, with access and filtering from any device. Perform includes Medical Tracker, and medical information can be updated through the Parent App. [5] [8] [18]

#### Behaviour, discipline and pastoral care

Behaviour records positive and negative points, rewards, reports and detentions. Dashboards connect behaviour with attendance and attainment, while interventions record provision and effectiveness over time. Safeguarding/pastoral partners such as CPOMS and MyConcern integrate through Arbor’s partner ecosystem. [5] [18]

#### Special education and safeguarding

SEN tracks pupils, provision and funding, complex medical/educational needs, review evidence and alerts on attendance, behaviour or progress. Arbor’s granular permissions can restrict child-protection data, and safeguarding systems such as CPOMS and MyConcern are approved integrations. [5] [13] [18]

#### Alumni and fundraising

Alumni relations, fundraising campaigns and donor management are not publicly listed as native Arbor modules. Schools would need external systems or integrations for those functions. [1] [18]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Role dashboards, custom Report Writer, scheduled reports, live national benchmarking against DfE/Ofsted and Arbor’s national database, BI Connector, BI Viewer, live feeds, Data Warehouse and MAT trust-wide dashboards support operational and strategic analysis. MAT users can compare schools, drill down to staff and students, set attendance thresholds and push assessment, pay-scale, permissions and user-defined-field policies. [5] [6]

#### AI features

Arbor AI includes Ask Arbor natural-language answers, Smart Reports including an Ofsted Inspection Companion, instant student summaries, email/letter/SMS drafting and translation, formula suggestions, and Auto Absence transcription/translation/logging. Arbor reports 4,000+ schools using Arbor AI and says customer data is not used to train providers’ models; it identifies OpenAI, Microsoft Azure, Google Gemini and Snowflake among relevant subprocessors/providers. [17] [13]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

For England, Arbor supports School Census and Workforce Census preparation, dry runs, error/query resolution, census summaries, COLLECT/LA submission files and submission checklists. The Help Centre documents 2026/27 census dates and DfE guidance. Statutory reporting, custom reports, census audit and school-year setup audit are also packaged for MAT central teams. [5] [16] [20] [21]

#### Mobile apps

The Parent & Guardian app is available for iPhone and Android and manages family information, attendance, behaviour, progress, payments, clubs, trips, consultations, report cards and homework. The Student App is available for iPhone/iPad and Android, with announcements, calendar, attendance, behaviour, assignments, biometric login and Ask Arbor. App-store ratings are materially weaker than Arbor’s vendor satisfaction claims. [8] [9] [15]

#### Integrations and APIs

Arbor exposes an open RESTful API and partner-approval console with read/write permissions where supported, explicit data-access review, approval/rejection and revocation. Named integrations include Wonde, Groupcall, ParentPay, CPOMS, LGfL, OTrack, Cunninghams, ClassCharts, Studybugs, Scopay, Google Classroom, Microsoft 365/Teams, Office 365, G Suite, cashless catering, assessment, safeguarding, payments and BI tools. [6] [18]

#### Security and privacy

Arbor documents ISO 27001, ISO 9001, Cyber Essentials, DfE cloud-supplier checklist and G-Cloud status, UK AWS hosting, role-based access, password rules, optional/enforced two-factor authentication for staff, inactivity logout, audit trails, data-retention dashboards, SAR exports, 30-day restore capability and AES-256 encryption. It publicly states GDPR/UK GDPR compliance; FERPA, COPPA and India’s DPDP Act are not claimed as product certifications. No independently verified public breach was found in the sources reviewed; Arbor describes incident-response and breach-notification procedures. [3] [13] [22]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Ask Arbor and Ofsted Inspection Companion:** Natural-language answers and an editable inspection pack reduce report preparation and navigation time. [17]
- **Auto Absence:** Voicemail transcription, translation and absence-code suggestions turn a repetitive attendance workflow into an approval queue. [17]
- **MAT policy pushdown:** Trust teams can push assessment frameworks, pay scales, permissions and UDFs to schools while retaining local autonomy. [6]
- **Live national benchmarking:** Schools can compare their data with DfE/Ofsted benchmarks and Arbor’s live national dataset. [5]
- **Arbor Workflows:** A drag-and-drop builder combines triggers, forms, checklists, reminders, schedules, conditional logic, documents, emails and audit trails. [16]
- **Integrated payments:** Trips, clubs, meals, shops, childcare vouchers, Apple Pay and Google Pay sit beside student and communications data. [19]
- **UK statutory workflow depth:** Dry runs and error resolution for School and Workforce Census are designed around DfE/COLLECT processes. [20] [21]

### Pricing Details

Arbor has no current public web pricing page; the most specific official public source is the 2024/25 G-Cloud 14 rate card, so figures below are historical and not a November 2026 quote. Core, Comms and Perform were priced annually by student plus a school base fee: primary £3.15/£6.30/£10.50 per student plus £2,100 base; secondary £4.20/£7.35/£11.55 plus £4,200; FE/sixth-form/UTC £4.20/£7.35/£11.55 plus £4,725. Core included attendance, behaviour, progress, census, reports, profiles and email/SMS; Comms added portals/apps, parents’ evening, meals and payments; Perform added curriculum/intervention tracking, provision map, assignments, My Classroom, seating plans, shop and staff performance. [14]

The rate card listed MAT Essentials/Plus/Enterprise at a £1,500 year-one setup fee, annual per-student charges of £0.50/£1.00/£1.50 and base fees of £2,500/£2,500/£3,500, with extra data-warehouse/HR integration charges. Onboarding ranged from free for standard primary migration to £2,500 for secondary onboarding, plus optional training and programme management. Discounts scaled by institution count. Current tiers and prices are quote-based and may have changed. [14]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Arbor has an open RESTful API, partner-app permission management and an integration ecosystem exceeding 130 support partners/apps by the company’s count. It supports or connects to Wonde, Groupcall, ParentPay, CPOMS, ParentMail, Teachers2Parents, SchoolComms, cashless-catering providers, Edval, Google Classroom, Google Workspace/G Suite, Microsoft 365/Teams, Office 365, Excel, Power BI, Google Data Studio and BI/data-warehouse tooling. [6] [18]

The reviewed materials do not establish OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi or SIF certification, Clever/ClassLink certification or a formal marketplace. API access is often partner-mediated: schools must approve apps and cannot independently customise every permission set. [6] [13]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Arbor publicly reports ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certification, Cyber Essentials, PCI DSS 4.0 for payments, DfE cloud-supplier checklist and G-Cloud approval. It says data is hosted in AWS London, with UK personal-data storage and backups, UK GDPR/GDPR compliance, AES-256 encryption, RBAC, two-factor authentication, access/login histories, annual penetration testing and 30-day restore. [13] [22]

The compliance guide names AWS, Stripe, Microsoft, OpenAI, Google Gemini, Snowflake, Twilio/SendGrid, Vonage and other subprocessors in the UK, EEA and US. Public materials do not claim FERPA or COPPA certification, and no Arbor-specific India DPDP Act statement was found. No independently verified public security incident was identified; Arbor says it will notify customers of breaches affecting their data. [13]

### Strengths

- Broad UK school/MAT coverage, especially attendance, census, communications, assessment, behaviour and reporting.
- Coherent School MIS–MAT MIS data with central dashboards, policy pushdowns, native payments, parent/student apps and statutory workflows.
- Explicit UK AWS hosting, ISO 27001, GDPR tooling, API permission review, data-retention/SAR features and a large partner ecosystem.

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Parent and student mobile UX is a material weakness: Apple ratings were 1.9/5 for Parent and 1.3/5 for Student on 24 November 2026, with reviews describing confusing navigation, login failures, slow loading, random logouts and homework-submission problems. [8] [9]
- Trustpilot showed a 1.2/5 TrustScore from 173 reviews on the same date; its review summary reported complaints about confusing/intuitive design, slow performance, errors and support friction, although Trustpilot does not fact-check review claims. [7]
- Cloud delivery improves access but makes stable internet, Wi-Fi, browser/device configuration and account administration operational dependencies. [12]
- Several functions are not native in the MIS itself: payroll, full accounting, transport, library, boarding, alumni/fundraising and full master scheduling require separate suite products or third parties.
- Public interoperability is integration-rich but not standards-rich: the reviewed evidence does not show OneRoster/LTI/Ed-Fi/SIF certification, a formal marketplace or Clever/ClassLink certification.
- Pricing is not transparently maintained on the public site; schools generally need a quote, and historic rate-card prices should not be assumed current.
