### Overview

Synergy Education Platform is Edupoint’s integrated K–12 student-data, learning-management, assessment, student-support, communication, and district-productivity ecosystem. Its core design is a centralized database shared by Synergy SIS, Teaching & Learning, Special Services, and related modules, so enrollment, attendance, grades, interventions, special-education documents, and analytics can be used across role-based workflows without repeated data entry [1] [32]. Its users are district administrators, school leaders, teachers, counselors, nurses, special-education teams, students, and families.

Synergy’s importance is its combination of SIS, instruction, assessment, intervention, special services, and analytics. Edupoint reports 5.5M+ students in 21 states and 7,000+ schools; Tennessee alone had 36 districts and 185,000 students in March 2026 [1] [31]. Bob Weathers founded Edupoint in 2003, but no separate Synergy launch year, acquisition history, or funding history is publicly disclosed [3].

### Platform Classification Explained

**Browser and cloud.** ParentVUE and StudentVUE are explicitly available as web portals, while Edupoint’s cloud-hosting service manages the Synergy software and server OS, upgrades, fixes, monitoring, patching, backup, and disaster recovery [8] [12]. The hosting options are Synergy Cloud, Premium Cloud, Premium Plus Cloud, and Consortium Hosting. Edupoint says its cloud uses Amazon Web Services, geo-redundant data centers, and a 99.99% uptime SLA [11] [12].

**Desktop and on-premise.** Current public pages emphasize browser access and Edupoint Cloud. They do not clearly document a desktop client or a current on-premise licensing option. Districts can have direct database access in some Premium Cloud tiers through a point-to-point VPN, but that is not the same as an on-premise product [12].

**Native mobile.** Edupoint lists eight native iOS and Android apps: StudentVUE, TeacherVUE, ParentVUE, AdminVUE, SEVUE, HealthVUE, CounselVUE, and KioskVUE [4] [7]. They use the same credentials as the web portals and support device capabilities such as camera access. ParentVUE and StudentVUE support iPhone and iPad as well as Android; the new Apple listings require iOS/iPadOS 15.6 or later [8] [28] [29].

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Synergy SIS: student administration and records

The SIS covers enrollment, demographics, family/guardians, attendance, health/immunizations, discipline, courses/requests/history, mass and walk-in scheduling, report cards, academic/career plans, transcripts, graduation, lockers, groups, fees, and portals. District tools include calendars, role security, state reporting, emergency management, audit trails, inter-district exchange, CTE, surveys, workflows, dashboards, reports, and integrations; performance tools include test history, analysis, standards, LMS/assessment, and ELL support [2].

#### Enrollment, registration, lottery, and athletics

Online Registration provides family registration/re-enrollment, multilingual adaptive forms, uploads/approvals, school preferences, progress tracking, pause/resume, boundary/immunization validation, authorizations, queues, alerts, required/pre-filled/read-only fields, duplicate prevention, and real-time SIS enrollment [13]. Lottery handles open-enrollment, magnet, and charter selection with weighted/points rules, filters, waitlists, placement, multilingual communication, packets, audit trails, and Synergy Mail updates [14]. Athletics adds athlete registration, uploads/signatures, GPA/course/boundary/physical eligibility, approval queues, tryout rosters, team placement, coach access, and email notifications [15].

#### Scheduling, attendance, and school operations

The SIS includes calendars, course creation/requests, mass and walk-in scheduling, course history, graduation tracking, attendance, emergency management, student groups, and lockers [2]. TeacherVUE adds seating charts, attendance, hall passes, substitute access, discipline referrals, conferences, course recommendations, test-group analysis, and emergency response [16]. Transport and bus routing are not identified as native modules.

#### TeacherVUE Portal & Gradebook

TeacherVUE is the teacher portal and gradebook. It combines a 360-degree student profile, teacher-created and district course content, assignment content, online grading, traditional and standards-based grading, rubrics, report cards with AI-assisted comments, performance analysis, reports, Synergy Actions, Google Classroom integration, and third-party LMS integration [16]. Teachers can customize analysis bands, filters, color schemes, and gradebook organization; they can also use generative AI for lesson plans and content.

#### Synergy LMS and learning resources

Synergy LMS supports district course authoring and distribution, scope-and-sequence curriculum planning, drag-and-drop curriculum calendars, shared content banks, teacher teams, teacher modification of district content, synchronous and asynchronous/blended learning, recorded video classes, Quick Tests, badges, instructional-tool authorization and usage management, and personalized study guides. Content can embed videos, Google Drive files, Microsoft OneDrive files, assignments, images, calendars, graded discussions, and nearly 400 LTI learning apps. StudentVUE provides 24/7 access to course content and assignments [17].

#### Synergy Assessment, gradebook, and examinations

Synergy Assessment provides district/school/classroom test management, customizable banks, 12+ question types, technology-enhanced items, standards/scoring, live dashboards, breakout groups, online tests, bubble sheets/webcam scanning, PDF conversion, zoom/color/text-to-speech accommodations, and ParentVUE/StudentVUE feedback. Reporting includes 20+ pre-built reports, subgroup/standard analysis, drill-downs, and Synergy Analytics; optional banks are Inspect Plus and Mastery Item Bank [18].

#### Report cards, transcripts, and parent/student portals

The core SIS supports report cards, transcripts, course history, graduation status, academic/career plans, and progress tracking [2]. ParentVUE and StudentVUE provide one-login, real-time access to calendars, schedules, class websites, assignments, scores, grades, assessment results, attendance, administrative records, health and immunization records, discipline, transcripts, graduation status, fees, course requests, documents, and multilingual Synergy Mail. Depending on district configuration, StudentVUE supports assignment uploads, tests, flex periods, digital locker, hall passes, and counselor visits; ParentVUE supports absence reporting, alerts, conferences, fee payment, registration, information updates, lottery placement, athletics registration, MTSS progress, and special-education signatures/progress [8] [30].

#### Communication and workflow automation

Synergy Mail is an in-platform messaging system with automatic language translation and two-way translation in teacher/family communication. The platform also has notifications, reminders, automated tasks, business rules, mandatory fields, workflow customization, alerts, digital acknowledgements, and role-based dashboards [2] [3] [9]. Public pages document email, in-app messages, portal notifications, and push notifications. They do not document native WhatsApp messaging; SMS is not presented as a core platform channel, so districts should verify any local notification integration.

#### MTSS, early warning, behavior, discipline, and pastoral support

Synergy MTSS includes district screeners, scheduled screening windows, risk filtering, AI-assisted plan creation, configurable early-warning categories/data sources/weights, stakeholder notifications, referrals, tiered plans, accommodations, intervention delivery logs, progress graphs/flags, outcome capture, progress reports, fidelity analysis, custom forms, and complete history. PBIS/CICO features include goals, feedback schedules, behavior points, classroom/school reward stores, family visibility, and redemption in StudentVUE/TeacherVUE. It also includes incident documentation, recurring-behavior observations, and an Emotion Tracker for student-reported AM/PM emotions [19].

#### Special education, 504, health, and safeguarding-related records

Synergy SE supports collaborative IEP creation, including Gifted IEP, process and ad hoc documents, document packets, archives, compliance verification, guided workflows, translation, event notifications, progress alerts, Medicaid provider/service tracking and extracts, interactive error validation, simultaneous editing, caseloads, team summaries, service tracking, parent communication, goal libraries, mail merge, and configurable documents/data fields. Synergy 504 supports visual timelines, 504 process documents, accommodation plans, and shared data with SE. AI Assist applies to narrative fields such as prior written notices and progress summaries [6]. Health features include immunizations, health records, nurse visits, medication/refill alerts, screenings, health notes, and HealthVUE access [7] [23]. The reviewed pages support privacy, discipline, health, emergency, and student-support workflows, but do not claim a standalone safeguarding/child-protection case-management module.

#### Fees, payments, accounting, and other administrative domains

Synergy SIS and ParentVUE expose fee records, balances, and payment; MySchoolBucks and SchoolPay are listed integrations [10]. No native general ledger, procurement, HR/payroll, transport, library, hostel/boarding, inventory/assets, alumni, or fundraising module is identified publicly.

#### Analytics, dashboards, reporting, and compliance

Synergy Analytics supports pre-built/custom dashboards, longitudinal and subgroup analysis, and data from enrollment, attendance, discipline, GPA, tests, SEL, EWS, interventions, digital/parent engagement, demographics, Google Sheets, and other databases. Users can pivot, drill through, combine media with data, and restrict views by student, class, school, or district [20]. SIS adds 350+ reports, SSRS, report writer, ad hoc reporting, queries, audits, and role dashboards [2]. Federal/state reporting, CRDC, accountability, immunization, IDEA/504, standards, and mandated health services are supported [23].

#### AI features

Generative AI is in production across Synergy SIS, TeacherVUE, MTSS, LMS, Assessment, and Special Education/504. Documented uses include assignment/course/test/study-guide creation, lesson plans, intervention plans, report-card comments, discipline and health notes, special-education progress summaries, and help composing Synergy Mail. Edupoint says AI output requires user review, follows existing role permissions, and replaces personally identifiable information with non-identifying tokens before sending prompts to ChatGPT by OpenAI [9].

#### Mobile apps

The eight role apps cover core workflows: ParentVUE (grades, attendance, assignments, messages, health/discipline, fees), StudentVUE (uploads, assessments, course requests, hall passes), TeacherVUE (attendance, assignments, discipline), AdminVUE (ID scanning, tardies, discipline, emergency accountability), HealthVUE (medication, visits, screenings), SEVUE (IEP/BIP, meetings, behavior, Medicaid), KioskVUE (tardy/detention/health check-in, IDs, reunification), and CounselVUE (graduation, scheduling, conferences, CICO) [7].

#### Integrations, APIs, and extensibility

Synergy integrates with Google Classroom/Drive, Microsoft OneDrive, Zoom, Teams, Clever, MySchoolBucks, SchoolPay, Inspect, Mastery Item Bank, and nearly 400 LTI apps. Edupoint states that SIS is OneRoster 1.1 certified for rostering/grade passback, Ed-Fi certified, and SIF 2 enabled; it also provides a selected-LMS API and development toolkit/source-code capability [10] [3].

#### Security and privacy

Edupoint reports field-level security, encryption, role access, 2FA, audits, restoration, business rules, AWS hosting, 24/7 operations, vulnerability scanning, intrusion prevention, geo-redundant centers, 99.99% uptime, and annual SOC 2 recertification [11] [12]. Its privacy policy addresses FERPA, COPPA, district control, deletion/return, and no sale of confidential data [23]. ISO 27001, GDPR, India DPDP Act, and named incidents were not publicly claimed in reviewed sources.

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Single database:** Shared real-time data spans enrollment, instruction, assessment, interventions, special services, and reporting [1].
- **Guardrailed generative AI:** AI assists content, notes, plans, comments, and summaries with review and PII tokenization [9].
- **Configurable MTSS/EWS:** Districts define data, weights, workflows, forms, fidelity measures, and CICO supports [19].
- **Assessment-to-action:** Live results drive grouping, re-teaching, standards analysis, accommodations, and family feedback [18].
- **Special-education depth:** IEP/504, compliance, Medicaid, caseloads, alerts, mobile, and classroom context connect natively [6].
- **Eight role apps:** Mobile coverage spans families, students, teachers, administrators, nurses, counselors, special educators, and kiosks [7].
- **District configurability:** Custom fields, reports, rules, dashboards, and workflows support local processes [2] [19].

### Pricing Details

Edupoint publishes no product price list, tier table, per-student rate, per-school monthly price, or public implementation-fee schedule. Its official route is a district-specific demonstration and purchasing-timeline form, which confirms that pricing is quote-based and scoped to the SIS, special education, LMS, services, and selected modules [34]. Capterra lists Synergy LMS as “Get Price,” with no free trial and no free version [25]. Gartner describes subscription pricing generally based on enrolled students or school sites, with customized modules and possible implementation/support add-ons, but gives no verified dollar range [26]. Therefore, no reliable public currency, unit price, tier name, or setup fee can be reported as of November 24, 2026.

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Synergy is unusually standards-oriented for a district SIS. Edupoint states that Synergy SIS is 1EdTech OneRoster 1.1 certified for rostering and grade passback, Ed-Fi certified, and SIF 2 enabled. It also advertises Google for Education/Classroom, Microsoft OneDrive and Microsoft 365-related directory work, Clever, Zoom, Microsoft Teams, nearly 400 LTI learning tools, MySchoolBucks, SchoolPay, Inspect, and Mastery Item Bank integrations [10] [11]. An API supports secure exchange with select LMS products, and premium cloud customers can purchase point-to-point VPN database access; however, Edupoint does not present a broadly documented public developer portal or an open marketplace in the reviewed pages. SSO is supported, including district Google login for StudentVUE and role-based access across the platform [8].

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

The strongest public evidence is Edupoint’s SOC 2 certification, AWS-hosted cloud operations, field-level security, encryption, MFA/2FA, audit logging, vulnerability scanning, intrusion prevention, backup/disaster recovery, geo-redundant data centers, and 99.99% uptime claim [11] [12]. Edupoint states compliance with FERPA and HIPAA and describes COPPA handling in its privacy policy: districts retain control, Edupoint processes data for authorized educational purposes, and confidential data is not sold [23]. The platform supports IDEA/504 documentation, state reporting, CRDC, immunization tracking, learning standards, and Section 508/JAWS accessibility testing [23]. Hosting is described as AWS and geo-redundant, but no specific AWS region is published. No publicly reported breach or other security incident was identified in the sources reviewed. ISO 27001, GDPR, and India DPDP Act conformity are not publicly claimed on the reviewed pages and should be contractually verified.

### Strengths

- Broad K–12 coverage across SIS, enrollment, gradebook, LMS, assessment, MTSS, special education/504, analytics, portals, and mobile.
- Centralized data reduces duplicate entry across instructional and support workflows [32].
- Strong configurability, state reporting, health, special-education, and compliance support [6] [23].
- OneRoster, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, Google, Microsoft, LTI, payment, and LMS connections [10].
- Scale: 5.5M+ students, 7,000+ schools, and 750+ implementations claimed [1] [21].
- Gartner praises support; SoftwareReviews reports 7.2/10 composite and 7.6/10 CX [26] [27].

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- G2’s 72-review profile is 2.7/5; complaints cite slowness, errors, inconvenience, and wasted time, while attendance and gradebook integration receive praise [24].
- Capterra’s three Synergy LMS reviews average 3.0/5; value for money is 2.0/5 and customer service 2.5/5. Reviews cite a less-friendly new gradebook, glitches, and iPad printing limits [25].
- SoftwareReviews users praise customization but cite a steep learning curve, complex implementation, and payment-gateway concerns [27].
- Gartner calls it not the most user-friendly platform and notes some desired functions are unavailable, despite excellent support [26].
- ParentVUE and StudentVUE iOS apps are each rated 1.7/5; complaints include missing features, duplicate notifications, freezing/loading, and interface regressions [28] [29].
- Quote-only pricing and customer-gated documentation increase buying and onboarding effort [21] [22] [25] [30] [34].
