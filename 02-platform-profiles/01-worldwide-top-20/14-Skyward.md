### Overview

Skyward is a long-established K–12 administration platform combining a Student Information System (SIS) with a school-business ERP. Its current-generation Qmlativ Education Management System covers student administration, teacher and family access, finance, HR, payroll, assets, reporting, and mobile access; SMS 2.0 remains a supported legacy product line. The value proposition is consolidation: one district database for records, grades, attendance, family engagement, finance, and workforce operations rather than a collection of disconnected point systems [1] [3].

Skyward began in 1980 as Jim King & Associates in Stevens Point. The first school product was developed in 1981 for Merrill Area Public School District to manage personnel and salary negotiations, after which the software spread to other Wisconsin districts. It adopted the Skyward name in 1994, acquired Texas Educational Consultative Services in 2000, partnered with cloud host ISCorp, and became entirely web-based in 2009 [4].

Qmlativ was announced at the end of 2016, had been in live production for more than two years by January 2017, and was designed from scratch with a new database and technology stack rather than as a visual clone of SMS 2.0 [6]. Current public materials do not publish a consistently updated country-by-country customer table [2].

### Platform Classification Explained

- **Browser/web:** Skyward states that its solution became entirely web-based in 2009; Qmlativ presents browser dashboards, portals, and web-based payroll [4] [24].
- **Cloud:** Qmlativ’s product material strongly recommends secure cloud hosting. Skyward identifies ISCorp as its exclusive hosting provider and describes cloud hosting, backups, automatic updates, and security controls [3] [31].
- **Desktop/on-premise:** No standalone native desktop client is identified in the current product pages. SMS 2.0 has legacy district-hosting and Windows/IIS/MSSQL deployment references in third-party reviews, but Skyward’s current public material does not establish a new on-premise option for Qmlativ [34].
- **Mobile:** The exact primary app is **Skyward Mobile Access**, with Family Access, Student Access, Educator/Teacher Access, and Employee Access functions. The official app page lists iOS, Android, and historical Windows Mobile availability; the current Apple and Google Play listings are for Skyward Mobile Access [27] [36] [37].

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

Online Enrollment and Registration supports custom forms, new and returning student applications, registrar review/update/processing, personalized workflows, and requirements. Parents can register children online, reducing paper and office visits [9].

#### Student information and records

The core SIS stores student records, demographics, contacts, schedules, courses, academic history, health information, discipline, interventions, and other data in a district-wide record. The Family Access design exposes a controlled whole-child view, while privacy policy language confirms support for student, parent, employee, health, and financial information [15] [32].

#### Attendance

Attendance includes teacher entry, custom seating charts with student pictures, real-time counts by district/building/classroom, parent absence notifications, custom messages and intervention letters, audit/correction workflows, and dashboards showing attendance trends. Student ID scanning/wanding is reported as an available add-on [12] [35].

#### Timetable and master scheduling

Scheduling manages multiple entities, mass scheduling, auditing, real-time roster changes, student course requests, and conflict reduction. Online Course Requests are designed to give students more ownership and work with teacher recommendations; curriculum can be linked to graduation plans [10] [14].

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Teacher Gradebook provides quick scoring, teacher-controlled assignment/grade visibility, categories, weights, customized grading methods and scales, real-time progress reports for activities/athletics/groups, and standards-based or traditional grading. Third-party product documentation also reports assessment management, tests/assessments, online assignments, gradebook cloning, and automatic posting of grades to report cards; a Google Classroom grade-export integration is publicly described [11] [29] [35].

#### Report cards and transcripts

Report Cards & Transcripts provides real-time data without imports, multiple GPA storage, progress notes, customizable branding, extensive reporting options, and up-to-date transcripts [41]. Report Writer uses a drag-and-drop editor, access to every field, layouts/branding, and controlled distribution through email, Message Center, or a network location [7].

#### Learning management and homework

Skyward is not positioned as a full standalone LMS, but it has built-in classroom management, online assignments, course management, collaboration/message features, and assignment/grade visibility. Software Advice lists built-in LMS and learning-management capabilities [8] [35].

#### Communication

Message Center supports individual or class-wide secure messages, announcements, audit trails, and continuous parent-teacher communication. Family Access and the mobile app provide messages, notifications, attendance alerts, grade-threshold alerts, and missing-assignment alerts. Public materials document email/message-center workflows and push-style app notifications, but do not document a native WhatsApp channel [13] [16].

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

Family Access is the parent portal for grades, assignments, attendance, behavior, upcoming events, course requests, graduation planning, enrollment, fees, and payment/account activity. Student Access exposes grades, schedules, assignments, and communication. Teacher Access is a self-contained portal with a personalized dashboard, live alerts, gradebook navigation, attendance, behavior referrals, and mobile access [8] [14] [15] [27].

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Fee Management covers food-service, textbook, activity, tuition, and other fees; collection reports; account activity; payment plans/discounts; and parent visibility. The ERP adds accounts payable, accounts receivable, activity accounting, purchasing, budgeting, general ledger, automated approvals, real-time financial information, and financial controls [22] [42].

#### HR, staff and payroll

Human Resources includes applicant tracking, job openings, position requests, position management, staff planning, position budgeting and what-if scenarios, benefit enrollment/invoices, substitute tracking, employee growth/history, and ACA compliance including 1094/1095 submissions. Payroll supports complex wage plans, electronic timekeeping, job-code/time-status changes, approvals, and ACA-aware processing [23] [24].

#### Transport

No native student transportation or bus-routing module was identified in the public Skyward feature catalog reviewed. Treat transportation as an integration or separate system unless a district-specific proposal says otherwise.

#### Library

No native library-management module was identified in the reviewed official product pages or independent feature list.

#### Hostel and boarding

No native hostel, dormitory, or boarding module was identified. Skyward’s public positioning is K–12 district administration rather than residential-school management.

#### Inventory and assets

Asset Management tracks location, status, value, depreciation, audits, warehouse stock, ordering-to-depletion lifecycle, real-time inventory, and low-stock notifications [25].

#### Health and medical

Health Services tracks immunizations/vaccinations, nurse logs with freeform notes, medication administration, state-required health fields, and student accountability while in the health office. Skyward states that the module is HIPAA and FERPA compliant [18] [31].

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

Behavior Management removes referral paperwork, allows teachers to document non-escalated events, alerts administrators, exposes offenses to parents through Family Access, and supplies reporting and live dashboard tiles [19].

#### Special education and safeguarding

Skyward publicly identifies Special Education and has discussed Section 504 enhancements; Qmlativ updates also cover evaluation/IEP-manager visibility. MTSS supports observations, referrals, configurable concerns, intervention teams, Watchlist thresholds, intervention plans, progress tracking, and parent visibility. A distinct safeguarding case-management module is not publicly documented [6] [20] [33].

#### Alumni and fundraising

No native alumni-relations or fundraising module was found in the current public catalog.

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Skyward provides Report Writer, live tiles, state/federal reports, attendance/behavior dashboards, and DistrictPulse, a Qmlativ SIS analytics solution built with AWS and Amazon QuickSight. DistrictPulse supplies dashboards, author/reader roles, trend forecasting, staffing/intervention analysis, and custom reports; ERP availability was still listed as coming later [12] [19] [26] [43].

#### AI features

The Qmlativ Help Center uses AI to turn technical support into a guided learning experience. DistrictPulse uses AWS/QuickSight analytics and forecasting language, but Skyward does not publicly claim a general-purpose generative AI assistant embedded in district workflows or a validated predictive-risk model [3] [43].

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Skyward maintains state-reporting specialists, state-specific compliance updates, federal reporting including Civil Rights Data Collection, built-in state reports/e-files in budgeting, webinars, and preferred-provider status in Washington, Tennessee, and Rhode Island. It reports work with more than 20 states and multiple international locations [26].

#### Mobile apps

Skyward Mobile Access supports students, parents, and staff with grades, attendance, behavior, Family Access, payroll history, time sheets, tax forms, time off, and other employee functions. Family Mobile adds grades, messages, notifications, attendance, behavior, and calendar; Teacher Mobile and Employee Mobile bring role-specific access to phones [16] [27].

#### Integrations and APIs

Skyward supports OneRoster 1.1, Ed-Fi API v4 certification for Qmlativ, SIF certification, 1EdTech Trusted Apps data-privacy certification, partner integrations, LMS/API access, grade passback work, and Google Classroom grade export. SMS 2.0 has read-only LMS API access plus a licensed read/write option; Qmlativ’s OneRoster API was described as read-only in the cited announcement [28] [29] [30].

#### Security and privacy

Skyward documents encryption, firewalls, access limitations, physical controls, cloud backups, district-controlled permissions, annual employee training, security reporting, and return/destruction of customer data at contract end. It publicly claims COPPA, FERPA, and HIPAA compliance and identifies ISCorp as exclusive cloud host. The reviewed Trust Center did not publish a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate, and no public incident disclosure was found in the reviewed sources [31] [32].

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Qmlativ from-scratch architecture:** A new database and technology stack rather than a superficial redesign of SMS 2.0 [6].
- **DistrictPulse analytics:** AWS/QuickSight-backed dashboards, authoring, trend forecasting, staffing analysis, and intervention targeting inside Qmlativ [43].
- **Configurable intervention Watchlist:** District-defined thresholds can surface students based on grades, absences, and discipline, then route them into intervention plans [20].
- **Standards-based interoperability:** Ed-Fi API v4, OneRoster 1.1, SIF, and 1EdTech TrustEd Apps certification claims reduce bespoke rostering work [28] [29] [30].
- **User-configurable forms and workflows:** Enrollment forms, report layouts, approvals, and task workflows support district-specific processes [9] [22].
- **AI-assisted support:** The Help Center uses AI for technical-support guidance, but this is not the same as a classroom or administrative AI copilot [3].

### Pricing Details

Skyward does not publish official Qmlativ or SMS 2.0 price cards, per-student rates, tiers, implementation fees, or hosting fees. The official route is a school-pricing request to sales [7]. Software Advice, viewed 24 November 2026, reports one Basic plan at **$30,000 per year**; Capterra shows the same starting figure. These are third-party estimates, not a Skyward quote, and the unit is not verified as per-student [34] [35]. Setup, migration, training, and optional API/module fees are not publicly itemized [29] [43].

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Skyward’s strongest documented interoperability is standards-based: Qmlativ has Ed-Fi API v4 certification; SMS 2.0 and Qmlativ are listed as 1EdTech OneRoster-compliant and data-privacy certified; and the company lists SIF certification. OneRoster exports/APIs, LMS API access, Google Classroom grade export, and partner integrations are documented. A complete current connector matrix or developer marketplace is not public [28] [29] [30] [34].

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Skyward publicly claims COPPA, FERPA, and HIPAA compliance, district-controlled access permissions, cloud hosting, backups, encryption, firewalls, least-privilege access, physical controls, annual security/privacy training, and vulnerability reporting. The product privacy policy says the school/district owns and controls protected information and Skyward processes it under contract [31] [32]. No public SOC 2 Type II or ISO 27001 certificate, GDPR/DPDP statement, data-residency region, or dated breach register was located. This is a documentation gap, not evidence of non-compliance.

### Strengths

- Broad K–12 SIS plus finance/HR/ERP coverage, with one data estate spanning student and business operations [1] [5].
- Large, established customer footprint: 7.8 million students and 2,700 customers claimed as of the 2026 About page [2].
- Strong state-reporting specialization and a long U.S. K–12 implementation/support history [26].
- Useful native family, teacher, employee, mobile, fee, payroll, health, behavior, MTSS, asset, and reporting tools [8] [17] [21].
- Standards certification and API options are more open than a closed SIS, particularly for Ed-Fi and OneRoster [28] [29].
- Reviewers often value the consolidated data, comprehensive functionality, support, and ability to manage grades/attendance/records in one place [33] [34] [35].

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- User experience is uneven: G2 is 3.8/5 and Capterra’s ease-of-use score is 3.9; reviews repeatedly describe legacy screens, clutter, steep learning curves, and difficult navigation [33] [34] [35].
- Mobile sentiment is particularly weak: Apple is 1.8/5 and Google Play is 2.9/5; recent reviews cite 503/login failures, freezing, biometric problems, outdated UI, and repeated relogins [36] [37].
- Configuration and migration complexity matter: Skyward’s own Qmlativ transition guidance says reports, security, and workflows may not transfer directly, while feature parity and state readiness affect migration timing [6].
- Rostering can be awkward for A/B-day or period-based schedules, and some reviewers report slow or expensive reports, difficult reporting, and problems with expense reimbursement or student/teacher management [34].
- Price transparency is poor. Public sources show a $30,000/year catalog estimate, but not a verifiable per-student formula, implementation fee, or module-by-module price [34] [35].
- Skyward is not a clear replacement for a full LMS, transport, library, boarding, alumni, or fundraising suite; several of those modules are not publicly evidenced as native.

### Key Milestones

- **1980:** Jim and Jean King establish Jim King & Associates in Stevens Point, Wisconsin [4].
- **1981:** First school-management project for Merrill Area Public School District manages personnel and salary negotiations [4] [5].
- **1984:** Company incorporates as School Administration Software, Inc. (SASI) [4].
- **Late 1980s–1990s:** Expansion into school software; a 1994 Matrix Computers acquisition is reported in the independent history, and the company adopts the Skyward name [4] [38].
- **2000:** Skyward acquires Texas Educational Consultative Services and partners with ISCorp for cloud hosting [4].
- **2001:** WSIPC selects Skyward as Washington’s statewide SIS/ERP partner [4] [38].
- **2006:** First international customer, American Embassy School in New Delhi, according to the independent history [38].
- **2009:** Skyward solution becomes entirely web-based [4].
- **2011–2013:** Preferred-provider status expands through Texas, Rhode Island, and Tennessee [4] [26].
- **2016:** New Stevens Point world headquarters opens; Qmlativ announcement and pilot work begin [4] [6].
- **2017:** Qmlativ is in live production and supports fully web-based payroll at early districts; migration roadmap covers Illinois, Minnesota, Texas, and Washington [6].
- **2019:** OneRoster 1.1 certification work and API availability are announced; Skyward is named EdTech Digest Company of the Year [5] [30].
- **2020:** 40th anniversary release reports 2,000+ customers, 7+ million students, 21 states, and 10 countries [5].
- **2024:** Qmlativ receives Ed-Fi API v4 certification [29].
- **2025–2026:** Qmlativ continues feature releases, including IEP/evaluation visibility and DistrictPulse analytics; in October 2026 Mountain Brook Schools becomes Skyward’s first Alabama customer [2] [39] [43].
