### Overview

Classter is a modular, all-in-one student information system (SIS), school management system (SMS), learning management system (LMS), education ERP and academic CRM. It follows one student record from inquiry and admission through enrollment, attendance, grades, billing, graduation and alumni engagement. Its intended users are administrators, registrars, faculty, finance teams, students, parents, agents, employers and alumni. The product matters because it replaces disconnected spreadsheets and point systems with shared records, portals, workflows and role-based access [1] [9].

The company says it began in Greece in 2015 with Core, Academics, Billing and CRM modules [2]. The European Investment Fund reported more than 400 institutions by May 2018 after Odyssey Venture Partners and EIF-backed JEREMIE Greece investment [11]. In October 2025 Banyan Software acquired Vertitech, the legal entity operating Classter [10].

### Platform Classification Explained

* **Browser/web access:** Role-specific portals run online; an independent directory lists Safari, Chrome, Internet Explorer and Firefox and categorizes deployment as SaaS/cloud [6] [11].
* **Cloud hosting:** Classter documents Microsoft Azure cloud hosting, scalability, monitoring, backup and disaster recovery. The company’s SIS page also states a 99.9% uptime SLA and EU data residency on request; these are vendor claims rather than independently audited findings in the reviewed sources [8] [9].
* **Desktop client:** No separate Windows, macOS or Linux client was found; desktop use is browser-based [11].
* **On-premise:** Classter explicitly offers on-premise installation for institutions needing control of infrastructure and data. This makes the overall product hybrid in deployment options, although the primary commercial positioning is cloud SaaS [8].
* **Native mobile:** The companion is called “Classter” on Google Play and “Classter – School Management App” in Apple’s store. It supports Android, iPhone and iPad; the mobile app is complementary rather than feature-equivalent to the web platform. The original Android/iOS launch announcement dates to 4 March 2020 [17] [18] [19].

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Core, student information and records

Core centralizes student, parent, sibling, teacher, employee and contact records. It covers demographics, passport/nationality/language data, family relationships, multiple statuses/programs/classes, curriculum and study plans, subjects/groups, elective self-enrollment, year-to-year transfers, extracurricular/billable services, Azure Files documents, age/activity consents, permissions, accounts, templates, bulk import/export, CSV/PDF reports, custom fields and academic-period configuration. Medical data includes vaccinations and COVID testing, but this is not a full clinical record system [5] [30].

#### Admissions and enrollment

Admissions provides applicant, parent, student and agent portals; self-sign-up; quick admission and lead capture; multiple intakes/applications; program preferences; dynamic fields; qualification and eligibility rules; scoring/benchmarking; status workflows and checklists; interviews and entrance exams; document upload; GDPR, financial and data-sharing consents; fees; enrollment/re-enrollment; conditional offers; e-signed contracts; and record-linked communications. Family credentials can cover multiple children [31] [9].

#### Attendance, timetable and master scheduling

Attendance supports subject/session attendance, absence, presence, tardiness, QR-code and RFID check-in/out, attendance reports and parent/student visibility. Timetabling covers drag-and-drop editing, teacher/classroom/resource allocation, unlimited timetables, ad-hoc sessions, extra events and staff meetings, room and resource booking, teacher substitutions, clash and missing-session detection, and synchronization with attendance and academics. The help center lists an AI timetable scheduling engine; the public materials do not disclose its optimization method or an independent deployment case [5] [23].

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Academics & LMS includes configurable gradebooks/scales, weighted and hierarchical criteria, rubrics, outcomes, GPA, marking periods, feedback, blind marking, assessment workflows, external re-marking, assignment/quiz/presentation/exam scheduling, online exams, retakes, deferred classes, overload controls, outcomes tracking, grade exports and emailed reports. It also supports report-card PDFs, promotion, performance statistics, badges and behavior points [5].

#### Learning management and homework

The LMS side provides course/subject syllabi, lesson planning, sessions and lessons, learning rooms (course microsites), uploaded teaching materials, discussion boards, homework assignment/submission/feedback, learning feeds, LMS data feeds, online and hybrid learning, and connections to external Moodle, Canvas, Brightspace and Blackboard environments. Native authoring is less fully documented than the SIS functions; institutions should confirm content-authoring depth during evaluation [5] [9].

#### Communication and Academic CRM

Academic CRM connects students, parents, educators, employees, agents and alumni through announcements, events, meetings, internal messages, email, SMS, push notifications, feedback and surveys. Communication templates can be customized and messages can be logged on the relevant record. Mailgun is the documented email/SMS example; WhatsApp is not identified as a native channel in the reviewed official materials, so it should not be assumed [1] [4] [5].

#### Parent, student, teacher and other portals

Separate portals serve students, parents, applicants, educators, employees, alumni, agents and employer/partners. They expose role-specific classes/homework/grades/attendance, calendars, reports, payments, communications, consents, enrollment, timetables, leads, commissions and internships [1] [4].

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Billing covers arrangements, installment plans, contracts, flexible pricing, payers/activities, mass billing, invoices, online payments, cash-flow/balance reports, forecasts, expenditures, payment imports, SEPA and barcodes. Classter Payments, built with Nuvei, launched cards in Europe, the UK, Canada and the US in November 2026; standing orders, ACH and SEPA were roadmap items [20]. Stripe, PayPal, BlueSnap, DIAS and WooCommerce are named integrations. A full general ledger is not publicly evidenced.

#### HR, staff and payroll

The HR module now documents centralized employee records, personal data, qualifications, contracts and job roles; attendance and check-in/check-out; absence/late/early notifications; payroll calculations, deductions, bonuses and digital payslips; performance/appraisals; professional goals and training; recruitment; applicant tracking; and onboarding [26].

#### Transport

Transportation Management handles student transportation, bus routes, fleet and vehicle records, student assignments and integrated transport administration. The public page confirms routes, fleets and student transportation, while the help center exposes a dedicated transportation settings section. GPS/live vehicle tracking is not publicly confirmed [25] [6].

#### Library

Library covers physical and online libraries, barcode book records, lending and charging, online search and reservation, personal student/teacher library portals, course-specific libraries, course literature, teaching-session connections, ISBN integration and book-health/status tracking [24].

#### College and higher-education administration

The College module covers configurable curricula, programs, subjects, semesters, credits, enrollment, progression, transcripts, grading, examinations, thesis and internships. Public materials do not establish research administration, grants, faculty workload or campus-facilities ERP [4] [33].

#### Internship, thesis, alumni and careers

Internship & Thesis provides employer matching, opportunities, applications, dashboards, workflows, logbooks, contracts, thesis guidance and grading [27]. Alumni have records, portal, communications and community engagement; dedicated fundraising is not documented.

#### Surveys and quizzes

This module includes subject quizzes, smart marking, online questionnaires and surveys, admissions surveys, quiz builder, question banks, teacher evaluations, online exams and control tools. The page also states anonymous surveys, which are useful for course/teacher feedback [28].

#### Protocols and e-signatures

The module index lists Protocols and Signatures, and the help center provides configuration manuals. E-signed contracts are documented in admissions, but protocol numbering, routing and signature-vendor details require confirmation [4] [6] [9].

#### Agents

Agents is a dedicated module/portal for external representatives: lead capture, application tracking, applicant status, communications and commission visibility. It is especially relevant to international recruitment and higher education, but public evidence does not specify commission accounting rules or agent marketplace size [1] [4].

#### Analytics, dashboards, reporting, AI and advanced configuration

Advanced Features includes multi-campus management, adaptive terminology, BI dashboards, custom pages/fields, report builder, lists/reports, notifications, unique IDs, templates and open API [22]. The help center lists five AI articles: Early Warning, Attendance Summary, Exam Scheduling, Teaching Companion and Timetable Scheduling [23]. Public sources do not provide model names, accuracy benchmarks or a separate AI price.

#### Government/state reporting, compliance, special education and safeguarding

The SIS page claims QQI, Ofsted, EHEA and national-accreditation export templates plus audit logs [9]. Consents and permissions are configurable, but no reviewed public source establishes dedicated special-education case management, statutory safeguarding workflows or state-reporting certification. Behavior points/remarks/badges and pastoral mentors are documented [5].

#### Mobile apps

The mobile app provides dashboards, push notifications, calendars, announcements, attendance, timetables, assessments, homework, leave applications, digital cards, messaging, learning resources, consents, reports and financial overview; teachers also manage classwork and students. It requires an institution subscription and login [17] [18] [19].

#### Security, privacy and interoperability

Security features include role permissions, consent management, audit logs, Azure Files, backup/disaster recovery and configurable access. Vertitech’s privacy notice describes processing under applicable data-protection laws and says the services are not directed at children under 16 [21].

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

* **One longitudinal student record:** admissions through alumni can remain connected without re-keying [9].
* **Configurable academic model:** programs, terms, criteria, grading, credits and progression are institution-defined [5].
* **Multi-campus operation:** centralized administration can span locations with period-specific configuration [22].
* **AI scheduling and early warning:** the help center documents AI teaching, timetable/exam and Early Warning tools [23].
* **Deployment choice:** Azure SaaS and on-premise installation support different infrastructure needs [8].
* **Embedded payments:** Classter Payments is designed around recurring education payment terms [20].
* **Open Consumer API:** customers can read/write data and browse live API calls [7].

### Pricing Details

Classter has a public pricing page but not a standard tier card. The official example, checked 24 November 2026, prices a 1,000-student higher-education institution at **€8.50 per active student per year for Core**, **€6.50 for Academics & LMS**, and **€6.50 for Surveys & Quizzes**, totaling **€25,500 per year or €21.50 per active student per year** [3]. It is an example, not a universal rate. Annual or bi-annual billing is available, with up to 20% discount for two years; setup and implementation fees are not itemized [3].

Capterra and G2 label current pricing “contact vendor”; G2 describes per-student monthly/yearly pricing and one Core edition [12] [14]. An eLearning Industry directory last updated April 2023 lists an estimated €1.50 per active student per annum, but this is stale and conflicts with the current example [11].

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Classter advertises 40+ integrations and an open API, including Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Teams, Zoom, Webex, Moodle, Canvas, Brightspace, Blackboard, Turnitin, HubSpot, DocuSign, Mailgun, Stripe, PayPal, BlueSnap, DIAS, Xero, WooCommerce, OneLogin and Power BI [1] [13] [16] [34]. The Consumer API reads/writes data; bulk tools cover students, staff, groups, subjects, books, timetables, payments and grades [7] [23].

An independent 2023 directory lists AICC, IMS Common Cartridge, IMS LTI/QTI, PENS, RTWS, SCORM, Section 508 and xAPI [11]. These are directory claims, not current 1EdTech certifications. Public sources do not establish Ed-Fi, SIF, OneRoster, Clever/ClassLink or a public developer marketplace. Reviewers report Microsoft 365 SSO, but no complete SSO matrix is published [13] [16].

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Classter states that it runs on Azure with Azure Backup, role access, audit logs, secure documents and on-premise deployment. Its SIS page says “ISO 27001 & GDPR Ready,” 99.9% uptime and EU residency on request [8] [9]. “Ready” is not a published Classter ISO certificate or SOC 2 report. The reviewed sources do not verify SOC 2, FERPA, COPPA, DPDP or an independent penetration test. GDPR consents and the privacy notice are documented [5] [21]. No public incident report was located as of 24 November 2026.

### Strengths

* Broad, modular coverage across SIS, LMS, admissions, finance, CRM, HR, transport, library and higher education.
* Strong configuration of curricula, grading, admissions workflows, portals, terminology, reports and institution-specific fields.
* Azure cloud, on-premise choice, backup/disaster-recovery claims and a read/write API suit institutions replacing legacy systems.
* Reviewers consistently praise support, centralization, customization and breadth: Capterra reports 4.6/5 from 115 reviews, GetApp 4.7/5 from 114, and Software Advice 4.7 [12] [15] [16].
* A meaningful higher-education layer—credits, transcripts, internships, thesis and employer portal—differentiates it from basic K-12 administration tools.

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

* Implementation and configuration can be time-consuming. G2 reviewers praise support but mention setup difficulty, bugs, integration issues and limited customization in some workflows [13].
* Capterra reviewers report that nonstandard institutions may find workflows rigid and features non-obvious without training; one 2026 reviewer cited Canadian tax-receipting fit [12].
* Reviewers note finance/logistics reports may need tweaking, and that the platform’s many features can confuse less technical students or administrators [13] [15].
* Moodle integration has had interface problems for at least one higher-education reviewer, although the reviewer said Classter worked on improvements [13].
* Mobile sentiment is materially weaker than web review sentiment: Apple is 2.4/5 from 13 ratings and Google Play shows roughly 3.4–3.5/5, with complaints about limited app functionality, re-authentication after updates and homework not appearing reliably [17] [18].
* Public compliance evidence is incomplete: reviewed sources do not publish SOC 2, FERPA, COPPA or DPDP attestations.
* Pricing is customized and module-based, so buyers cannot calculate a reliable total cost from a standard rate card; implementation fees are not disclosed [3].

### Key Milestones

* **2015:** Classter/Vertitech founded in Greece by Nikolaos Nikou; the product began with Core, Academics, Billing and CRM [2] [10].
* **2015–2017:** Odyssey Venture Partners and EIF-backed JEREMIE Greece financing supported product development and international sales [11].
* **By 2018:** EIF reported more than 400 institutions across the Balkans, southern Europe, Africa, the UK and Germany [11].
* **2018 onward:** Admissions, transportation, library and alumni capabilities were added as the platform expanded; Classter later passed 400 active institutions [2].
* **4 March 2020:** Classter announced its Android and iOS mobile application [19].
* **2023:** Classter reported entering the United States and receiving early industry awards; the current About page presents this as a milestone [2].
* **2025:** Classter’s current site reports 500+ institutions and 300,000+ users; Banyan Software announced acquisition of Veritech on 10 October 2025 [9] [10].
* **17 November 2026:** Classter Payments card processing went live in Europe, the UK, Canada and the US with Nuvei; standing orders, ACH and SEPA were announced as staged roadmap items [20].
