### Overview

ManageBac+ is Faria’s curriculum-first learning and academic-management platform for international schools. It connects curriculum design, unit and lesson planning, classroom resources, tasks, assessment, gradebooks, portfolios, report cards, transcripts, attendance and school-to-home communication. It supports the IB continuum, Cambridge, IGCSE, Edexcel, AP and custom programmes through more than 600 standards sets. [1] [11]

The product is used by programme coordinators, teachers, students, parents and administrators. ManageBac began in 2006, launched CAS Manager in 2007, and adopted the ManageBac name in 2009. Its history includes automated IBIS registration (2011), OpenApply and iOS (2013), Faria’s reorganisation and LaunchPad SSO (2016), AWS Canada migration (2017), Atlas collaboration (2018) and Curriculum Trak/SchoolsBuddy joining Faria (2019). [2] ManageBac+ is an operating subsidiary of Faria Education Group, not an IB subsidiary or IB-endorsed software company. [2] [9]

### Platform Classification Explained

* **Browser/cloud:** ManageBac+ is explicitly web-based, requiring an internet connection, modern JavaScript-enabled browser and school-specific domain. The FAQ says it is hosted in Amazon Web Services data centres in Canada, the United States and China, with location dependent on school and local law. [9]
* **Desktop/on-premise:** No native Windows/macOS desktop client or customer-managed on-premise edition was found in the official product, FAQ or terms pages. The deployment model is hosted SaaS.
* **Native mobile:** The native app is named **ManageBac+** in current help documentation and is available for iPhone, iPad and Android. It supports students, parents, teachers, coordinators and administrators, including tasks, portfolios, attendance, messages, CAS and projects. [10] The public store listings still display the shorter app name **ManageBac**. [12] [13]
* **Mobile browser:** The 2026 AI Writing Assistant works in web and mobile browsers but, at the time checked, not inside the native iOS/Android apps. [17]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Curriculum management and academic standards

Collaborative real-time unit planners, curriculum mapping, lesson planning, resources, scope and sequence, learning outcomes, accreditation alignment and whole-school curriculum analytics are native. ManageBac advertises 600+ standards sets; its implementation guide reported 607 sets and more than 1.5 million licensed standards/benchmarks. [11] It supports IB PYP, MYP, DP and CP, British/Cambridge/IGCSE, Edexcel, AP and build-your-own programmes. The 2026 redesign added grid-based unit views, active/archived filters, Quick Add, week view, teacher notes, description/guidance, reusable resources and audience visibility controls. [8]

#### Teaching, learning and classroom/LMS

Classes contain units, lessons, resources, tasks, assignments, announcements, discussions and calendars. Teachers can share files, websites, video, photos, text notes, Google Docs and online lessons; students submit work and receive feedback. Presentation Mode and Zoom support remote lessons, while KeyChat supports school communication and social-emotional check-ins. [11] Portfolios hold files, photos, videos, links, goals, reflections, coursework and awards.

#### Admissions and enrolment

Admissions is not a full native ManageBac module. Faria’s separate OpenApply admissions system integrates with ManageBac for admissions, enrolment, re-enrolment and family information. The 2026 OpenApply Mini Profile can show tags, applicant-history status changes and configured general/custom fields on a synced student profile, including examples such as ESL, SEN and Medical Alert. [8] [15]

#### Student information and records

ManageBac provides student, parent, teacher/advisor, coordinator, administrator and read-only observer accounts; school-specific domains; profiles; year groups; classes; homerooms; programmes; permissions; contacts; academic records; attendance; portfolios and report data. It exports student information as PDF forms and CSV and supports synchronisation with SIS partners such as iSAMS, PowerSchool and Veracross. [9]

#### Attendance

Teachers record class and homeroom attendance by day or period; administrators configure attendance preferences, categories, days off, reminders and daily digests; parents can submit absence excusals. The 2026 release added confirmation per homeroom day and class period, missing-confirmation queues, reminder notifications, CSV import and iOS confirmation. [8] [11]

#### Timetable and scheduling

Users have calendars and timetables showing lessons, events, tasks, deadlines and exams, with student, class, group and year-group views. [10] A full master-scheduling/room-allocation engine is not publicly documented.

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

The gradebook supports points, criteria, comments-only, binary, interdisciplinary and custom assessment models; task and term grades; rubrics; differentiation; annotations; local-grade conversion; hidden results; and administrator tracking of submission progress. The assessment page adds Google Docs coursework, Turnitin plagiarism checking and Vantage Reader collaborative annotations. [4] AssessPrep, available as a Faria-linked online-assessment service/add-on, adds interactive questions, formative and mock exams, lockdown mode, live monitoring, analytics, AI question creation and AI grading, with gradebook sync; the current page advertises 20+ question types and 40,000+ ready-to-use questions. [4]

For IB, ManageBac supports DP plans, CAS, Extended Essay, Theory of Knowledge, Internal Assessments, Group 6 IA, eCoursework, exam calendars, candidate/session data and IBIS exam registration. The 2026 eCoursework dashboard covers IA, EA and Core components, missing submissions and links to IB upload requirements. [8]

#### Report cards and transcripts

Custom report-card templates support term grades, percentage equivalencies, exam scores, 21st-century skills, Cambridge Learner Attributes, service learning, portfolio highlights, advisor/student reflections, attendance, extra-curricular activity, class reports, cover sheets and grade descriptors. Schools can use multilingual UTF-8, right-to-left Arabic/Hebrew, custom layouts, 30+ patterns/wallpapers, error checks, online responsive reports and bulk print-ready PDFs. Transcripts include built-in checks for names, grades, comments and rubrics. [4] [11]

#### Learning management and homework

Tasks may be summative or formative, tied to units, categories, resources and differentiated classes. Students see upcoming/completed work and deadlines, upload coursework and access teacher feedback. Google Docs templates can be assigned in three clicks and saved automatically into portfolios; Zoom and Presentation Mode support synchronous/remote learning. [11]

#### Communication

Native communication includes announcements, class/year-group/group discussions, individual messages, 1-to-1 direct messaging, multi-user chat, notifications, push/mobile notifications, profanity filtering, parent alerts, daily/weekly digests and KeyChat. The evidence reviewed does not show a native SMS or WhatsApp gateway; those should not be assumed from the term “communications.” [1] [11]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

Role-based web and mobile experiences provide separate dashboards, calendars, classes, tasks, grades, attendance, portfolios, report cards, behaviour notes, messages and settings. Parents can switch between children, submit attendance excusals, see progress and join PTA activity; students manage goals, coursework, reflections, CAS and projects; teachers plan, teach, grade, message and record attendance. [10]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

ManageBac’s own subscription billing supports annual invoices, credit cards, purchase orders, cheques and China-only bank transfer, with actual or adjusted student-count billing. It offers 5% off for two years paid upfront and 10% off for three years paid upfront. This is not a school-fee ledger, payroll or general accounting module; SchoolsBuddy is Faria’s separate activities/bookings/payments product. [5] [11]

#### HR, staff and payroll

No native HR, recruitment, staff appraisal, payroll or workforce-payroll module was identified. ManageBac has staff/advisor accounts, roles, permissions and implementation/support workflows, but those are not an HRIS.

#### Transport

No native transport-routing or bus-tracking module was identified. Faria positions Vectare as its separate transport product, so transport should be treated as a suite integration/adjacent product rather than a ManageBac feature. [15]

#### Library, hostel/boarding, inventory/assets

No native library circulation/cataloguing, hostel/boarding, dormitory, inventory, asset-register or procurement modules were found in the reviewed official pages.

#### Health and medical

ManageBac can display configured OpenApply fields such as a Medical Alert tag in a synced Mini Profile, but a native nurse/clinic, immunisation, medication or medical-record workflow was not documented. [8]

#### Behaviour, discipline and pastoral care

Parents can view behaviour notes; teachers record Behaviour & Discipline through the app; KeyChat includes social-emotional support; homeroom/advisor comments and reviews support pastoral workflows. A dedicated safeguarding case-management or restorative-discipline module was not evidenced. [10] [11]

#### Special education and safeguarding

Role permissions, access controls, privacy settings, discussion controls, profanity filtering and configurable OpenApply tags can support SEN/safeguarding practice. ManageBac does not publicly describe a complete IEP/504 workflow, mandated-reporting case system or safeguarding register. [8] [17]

#### Alumni and fundraising

No native alumni-relations, donor, fundraising or advancement module was identified. OpenApply covers admissions/re-enrolment; it should not be counted as an alumni CRM without separate evidence.

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Whole-school curriculum analytics, engagement analytics, academic overview, DP/MYP academic analytics, tagged learning-outcome exports, eCoursework monitoring, submission-progress tracking, report-card error checks and parent/teacher dashboards are documented. The platform reports performance by student, class and group, but no predictive-risk score or publicly documented no-code workflow builder was found. [1] [8] [11]

#### AI features

ManageBac AI Writing Assistant can generate text, revise selected text, simplify, expand, correct grammar/spelling and translate; administrators enable it at school level and control teacher/advisor access. It is not enabled for China, does not pull page context, and the vendor says student/teacher personal information is not used to train models. AssessPrep adds AI question generation and AI grading as an associated assessment service. [4] [17]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

ManageBac provides IBIS integration and curriculum/reporting tools for international-school standards, but the reviewed pages do not document certified Ed-Fi, SIF, state reporting or national-government submissions. GDPR/ePrivacy controls and compliance support are documented; compliance is not the same as a state-reporting module. [8] [9]

#### Mobile apps

The native ManageBac+ app supports iPhone, iPad and Android for all major roles. Current functions include tasks, portfolios, goals, reflections, CAS/community engagement, projects, classes, messages, gradebook, attendance, behaviour and report cards. iOS supports coursework scanning and attendance confirmation; Android 2026 support added project worksheets, proposals, reflections, journals, notes, documents and presentations. [10]

#### Integrations and APIs

The official site advertises 50+ integrations: Google Workspace/Docs/Drive, Microsoft Office 365, Clever, ClassLink, Turnitin, Zoom, IBIS, IB eCoursework, Kognity, Concord Infinity, BridgeU, iSAMS, OpenApply, SchoolsBuddy and AssessPrep. Faria’s public Integrations Portal documents ManageBac+ APIs and an interactive “Test it” endpoint feature, with structured data retrieval/updating but no AI/LLM capability in the API itself. [1] [3] [18]

#### Security, privacy and access control

Faria reports ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification, encrypted connections and at-rest encryption, restricted production access, malware scanning, security training, 24/7 incident response, warm standby, backups and disaster recovery. ManageBac’s FAQ adds real-time redundant and daily/off-site backups, role permissions and cloud hosting. [9] [16] The reviewed public material does not establish SOC 2, OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi or SIF certification, or an India DPDP Act claim.

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

* **IB-native programme workflow:** IBIS registration, eCoursework, CAS, EE, ToK, IA and Core tracking reduce spreadsheet-based IB coordination. [2] [8]
* **Curriculum-first architecture:** More than 600 standards sets and curriculum mapping connect units, learning outcomes, assessment and reporting. [11]
* **Multi-curricula single account:** IB, Cambridge/IGCSE, Edexcel, AP and custom programmes can coexist in one international-school environment. [1] [11]
* **Holistic portfolio and service learning:** Portfolios, goals, reflections, CAS/community engagement and project-based learning capture evidence beyond grades.
* **Configurable international reporting:** Multilingual, right-to-left, branded web/PDF reports with proofing and rubric support are unusually deep for an LMS. [4]
* **Role-complete mobile access:** One native app serves students, parents, teachers, coordinators and administrators, rather than only a student companion app. [10]
* **ManageBac AI and AssessPrep AI:** Writing generation/revision is production functionality, while AssessPrep adds AI question creation and grading as a linked assessment product. [4] [17]
* **Faria family ecosystem:** OpenApply, SchoolsBuddy, Atlas, Vectare and other products can connect around the learning platform, although they are separate products. [15]

### Pricing Details

ManageBac+ has no public price card or currency-denominated tier price. Official documentation says it is an annual subscription priced according to total student numbers, with quotations available from sales; the terms state that recurring-service fees are based on enrolment and individual modules can vary with enrolment. [9] [7] The product page exposes Essential, Standard and Pro bundle names, but not prices or a public per-student/per-school rate. [3]

As of 24 November 2026, the official billing guide documents annual invoicing, net-30 terms, credit card/cheque payments and China-only bank transfer. Two-year prepaid agreements receive 5% off and three-year prepaid agreements 10% off. The guide does not publish setup or implementation prices; ManageBac says an Implementation Manager guides setup and training, and the About page states that it does not add surprise setup, training, maintenance or upgrade fees. [5] [9]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

ManageBac’s strongest interoperability is practical ecosystem connectivity: Google Workspace/Docs/Drive, Microsoft 365, Clever, ClassLink, Turnitin, Zoom, IBIS, eCoursework, Kognity, iSAMS, OpenApply, SchoolsBuddy, BridgeU, Concord Infinity and AssessPrep. [1] Faria maintains a public developer portal with ManageBac+ technical documentation, APIs and browser-based endpoint testing. [18]

The evidence reviewed does not show public certification on OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi or SIF, nor a conventional third-party app marketplace. Google, Microsoft, Faria and partner SSO options are supported in the app guidance, but a detailed public SAML/OIDC matrix was not located. [10]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Faria reports ISO/IEC 27001:2022 certification under BSI certificate IS 664562, TLS/SSL in transit, encryption at rest, restricted production access, malware scanning, annual security training, 24/7 incident response and disaster recovery. [16] ManageBac reports GDPR compliance, COPPA support, PIPEDA and UK DPA 2018 coverage, plus China Cybersecurity Law controls; its FAQ says hosting is in AWS Canada, the United States and China. Faria’s broader security page also refers to servers in Canada, China and the EU, so exact region is school- and contract-dependent. [9] [16]

Faria says it does not store card data and transmits payment information to Stripe, describing PCI DSS compliance. [16] FERPA and the India DPDP Act were not expressly claimed on the public pages checked, so they should be confirmed contractually. No public security incident disclosure was located in the reviewed official materials; absence of a disclosure is not proof that no incident has occurred.

### Strengths

* Deep, purpose-built IB workflow, including IBIS, CAS, EE, IA, Core and eCoursework.
* Strong curriculum-to-assessment-to-reporting continuity across IB and non-IB international curricula.
* Broad role coverage and unusually capable parent/student/teacher mobile access.
* Flexible multilingual report cards, portfolios, service learning and project-based learning.
* Practical integrations with the productivity, SIS, assessment and admissions tools international schools already use.
* Transparent public documentation for hosting, backups, privacy, billing mechanics and current releases.

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

* It is not a full SIS/ERP: admissions, transport, fees, HR/payroll, library, boarding, assets, health and alumni functions are absent or delivered by separate Faria products/partners. [11] [15]
* Pricing is quote-only; schools cannot compare a public per-student price or know tier limits without sales engagement. [5] [12]
* G2’s small but verified sample praised integrations and breadth while criticizing navigation/UI, maintenance interruptions and information density. [12]
* Mobile-store feedback is materially harsher: Google Play was 2.5/5, with complaints about unintuitive navigation, a finicky interface and changes to task/deadline organisation. [13]
* Apple reviewers described the app as confusing, difficult to navigate, inconsistent between dashboard and calendar, and reported a login error; the sample was only 12 ratings. [13]
* Trustpilot’s four reviews were all one-star and complained about confusing layout, updates, upload/task workflows and perceived downtime; Trustpilot warns the small unprompted sample may not be representative. [14]
* Advanced AI is constrained: the Writing Assistant is not in native mobile apps, is unavailable in China and only processes text explicitly sent to it rather than platform context. [17]
