### Overview

Toddle is an India-founded, cloud-based K–12 teaching-and-learning platform positioned as “more than an LMS.” It connects curriculum mapping, unit and lesson planning, assessment, gradebook, portfolios, progress reports, transcripts, attendance, timetables, communication, behaviour/well-being and accreditation evidence. Its strongest fit is an international or progressive school that wants teaching and reporting workflows to share data rather than operate as disconnected tools. [3] [4]

The founders were educators and school operators who built the first version inside their own school after experiencing fragmented tools. Toddle’s official history records a December 2018 inflection point when visiting educators asked the team to make the tool available more broadly; independent company sources identify 2019 as the founding year. [2] [3] A January 2023 Series A announcement said Toddle raised $17 million led by Sequoia Capital India (now Peak XV), with Tenacity Ventures, Trifecta Capital, Matrix Partners, BEENEXT and Better Capital participating. [6] No acquisition was identified in the sources reviewed.

Toddle began with IB schools but now markets to multiple curricula, including CBSE and ICSE. Its current public positioning is much closer to an academic operating layer and curriculum/LMS platform than to a full Indian school ERP: there is no public evidence in the reviewed materials for native fee accounting, payroll, transport, hostel, library, inventory or admissions management.

### Platform Classification Explained

Toddle is a browser-accessed SaaS service at web.toddleapp.com, and its privacy policy explicitly describes both a web end and a mobile end. It names three native mobile products: Toddle Family, Toddle Educator and Toddle Student. The policy describes separate teacher, student, parent and administrator roles; family users can view portfolios, calendars, news, policies and teacher communications. [5]

The platform is cloud-hosted and the integrations page identifies AWS regional hosting choices, including Australia, China, Europe/Ireland, Hong Kong, Singapore, the UAE and the United States, with backup locations. [11] No downloadable desktop client, self-hosted package or on-premise edition is publicly documented. Mobile capability is therefore native-app plus web, not a desktop/on-premise hybrid.

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment
No native admissions funnel, application management, entrance testing, seat allocation or enrollment workflow is publicly documented. SIS integrations can bring rosters into Toddle, but that is not evidence that Toddle replaces an admissions system. [11]

#### Student information and records
Toddle has centralized student profiles and connected evidence across portfolios, assessment, attendance, behaviour and reports. Rosters, term grades, timetables and attendance can synchronize from supported SISs. It is not publicly presented as a complete demographic, birth-record, transfer-certificate or statutory SIS. [4] [11]

#### Attendance
The Class Operations module supports daily, rotation-based and period-wise attendance, configurable codes/categories/reminders, SIS synchronization and attendance visibility in analytics and reports. [4]

#### Timetable and master scheduling
Toddle provides teacher/student schedules, calendars, workload views and cross-subject insights. Schedules can be imported from SISs and tools such as aSc and EdVal; supported SIS sync includes timetable/calendar in several connectors. [4] [11]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations
Assessments can be standards-, competency- or score-based and use rubrics, checklists, varied submission formats and multimodal feedback. The unified gradebook receives internal grades and can display uploaded external results such as NAPLAN, PAT and MAP. Assessment delivery includes timers, extra-time controls, browser focus mode and screen-absence alerts. [4] Toddle markets AI assessment creation, first-pass grading and inline feedback, but independent commentary flags that high-stakes lockdown capabilities may not be as deep as specialist exam systems. [12]

#### Report cards and transcripts
Schools can build customizable, visual, media-rich progress reports and transcripts. Evidence from portfolios, gradebook, behaviour and attendance can feed reports; grades flow into transcripts; reports can be published digitally or printed. AI can draft evidence-linked comments for teacher review. [4]

#### Learning management and homework
The LMS layer covers courses, units, lessons, assignments, worksheets, resource links, student submissions, differentiated tasks, class drives and learning experiences. AI can generate worksheets, questions, rubrics and differentiated activities. [4] [13]

#### Communication (SMS, email, push, WhatsApp)
The Communications Hub offers one-to-one messaging, group channels, announcements and an administrator conversation view. Announcements can be shared in Toddle or by email; AI drafts messages, summarizes threads and flags attention items. Auto-translation supports 100+ languages. Native SMS, WhatsApp Business, push-notification specifications and transactional messaging APIs are not publicly verified. [4] [11]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals
Teachers plan, assess, grade, communicate and curate portfolios; students submit work, set goals, self-evaluate and document learning; families receive portfolios, reports, announcements and messages through Toddle Family; administrators approve plans, manage permissions and view insights. Family invitation management includes bulk email, class invite links, printable PDFs, QR/text codes, approval states and removal. [5] [14]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting
No native fee ledger, invoicing, GST billing, online fee collection, UPI, Razorpay, Paytm or Tally integration is publicly documented. The integrations directory does list Sponte for fees and student services, but that is a third-party connector, not Toddle accounting. [11]

#### HR, staff and payroll
No public evidence of employee records, recruitment, leave, appraisal, payroll, statutory deductions or teacher payroll export.

#### Transport
No public evidence of bus routes, vehicle tracking, driver/attendant management, pickup permissions or transport fees.

#### Library
No public evidence of cataloguing, circulation, barcodes, acquisitions, fines or library-resources management.

#### Hostel and boarding
No public evidence of dormitory allocation, wardens, boarding attendance, leave permissions or residential pastoral workflows beyond general behaviour/well-being tracking.

#### Inventory and assets
No public evidence of procurement, stock, asset tagging, issue/return or maintenance modules.

#### Health and medical
No public evidence of clinic visits, immunization records, allergies, medication or nurse workflows.

#### Behaviour, discipline and pastoral care
Behaviour Management records and reports behaviour/well-being events, supports school-configurable approaches, uses AI to help write nuanced reports, and surfaces patterns in school culture and well-being. It is a pastoral/behaviour layer, not a documented safeguarding case-management system. [4]

#### Special education and safeguarding
Toddle supports differentiated learning, flexible evidence formats, accessibility and AI Tutors that adapt to pace and needs. It states WCAG 2.1/2.2 AA conformance and support for screen readers, keyboard navigation, VoiceOver, TalkBack and live captions. A dedicated IEP/SEN case-management or safeguarding incident module is not publicly documented. [4]

#### Alumni and fundraising
No public evidence of alumni relations, donations, campaigns, events or fundraising CRM.

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting
Ready-made dashboards cover curriculum coverage, student performance, attendance, behaviour and engagement. Leaders can correlate performance, attendance and behaviour; dashboards can be delivered daily, weekly or monthly. AI Insights accepts natural-language questions and returns charts, tables and suggested actions. [4]

#### AI features
Toddle AI is production-embedded rather than a separate chatbot. Publicly described tools include Curriculum Design Assistant, Assessment Builder, Grading Assistant, Report Writing Assistant, Writing Assistant, AI Tutors and AI Insights. They generate or differentiate plans and assessments, read multiple formats, draft evidence-backed report comments, summarize communications, analyze school data and provide teacher-configured student tutoring. Toddle says teachers remain in control and that AI Tutors are teacher-led, monitored and automatically flagged for unsafe/off-topic content. [13]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance
Toddle’s public FAQ says it supports Indian CBSE and ICSE curricula, but no reviewed page verifies CBSE/ICSE/state-board statutory report-card templates, NEP 2020 Holistic Progress Cards, UDISE+ export, APAAR ID, AISHE, NAAC, NBA or NIRF reporting. These should be treated as unconfirmed rather than assumed from curriculum support. Accreditation Management includes evidence, reviews, feedback and built-in standards for 15+ accrediting bodies including IB and NEASC. [4]

#### Mobile apps
The privacy policy names Toddle Family, Toddle Educator and Toddle Student. The web help center notes that some administrative family-invitation functions are web-only, so mobile parity is not complete. Apple’s public ratings show a significant gap between the well-rated web product and the lower-rated Student and Family apps. [5] [7] [8] [14]

#### Integrations and APIs
Toddle advertises 50+ integrations and two-way SIS synchronization. Named systems include Veracross, PowerSchool, Blackbaud, iSAMS, Compass, TASS, Sentral, Synergetic, Edumate, OpenApply, QuickSchools, SIMS, Skyward, Infinite Campus, Clever, ClassLink, Wonde, Edlink, Google, Microsoft, Teams, Zoom, Turnitin, Edpuzzle, H5P, ClickView, Educaplay, McGraw Hill, OneNote, Wayground and Kognity. The page documents OneRoster and LTI routes and says custom integrations can be evaluated; a fully public developer API specification was not located. [11]

#### Security and privacy
Toddle reports ISO 27001, ISO 27701, SOC 2, ISO 42001, 1EdTech Data Privacy, FERPA, COPPA and GDPR-related controls. Its privacy policy says it acts as processor/data fiduciary counterpart to schools and commits to India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA). It describes encryption, regional AWS hosting and role-controlled school access. No publicly reported security incident was identified in the reviewed sources. [5] [11]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- Curriculum-first design links maps, standards, units, lessons, assessments and evidence rather than treating the LMS as a file repository. [4]
- AI is embedded across planning, assessment, grading, reporting, communication and analytics, with teacher review and school-context grounding. [13]
- AI Tutors provide teacher-configured, immersive student experiences with conversation visibility and safety flags. [4] [13]
- Portfolio evidence flows into reports and transcripts, supporting a longitudinal K–12 learning record.
- Curriculum publishing creates a current public-facing curriculum view for families, boards and accreditation work.
- Accreditation Management centralizes evidence, reviews and feedback with 15+ built-in accrediting frameworks. [4]
- Toddle Buddy implementation support assigns a former educator to migration, onboarding and ongoing adoption. [12]
- Auto-translation supports family/community communication across 100+ languages, while the interface itself is available in nine languages. [4]

### Pricing Details

Toddle publishes no INR, USD or per-student price. The official page presents three plans: Planning Pro (curriculum planning), Advanced (curriculum planning plus assessments/gradebook, portfolios, worksheets, reports/transcripts, communications, attendance/timetable/calendar, analytics and accreditation) and Ultimate (Advanced plus AI Tutors, AI-powered reports, AI grading, AI feedback, conversational analytics and behaviour management). The page also says pricing is tailored to school/user count and requirements, offers a free trial with setup/training, and supports two- or three-year discounts. [15]

The FAQ says trials typically run 2–3 weeks; no setup, implementation, per-student/year, per-school/month, INR or USD amount is stated. G2 likewise records free trial and custom pricing, with final negotiation directly with Toddle. [9] No credible third-party numeric price range was found, so no estimate is supplied.

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Toddle’s strongest interoperability evidence is practical rather than certification-based: SSO through Google, Microsoft, Clever and ClassLink; two-way SIS roster/attendance/timetable/term-grade synchronization; OneRoster and LTI connections; and 50+ named integrations. Google Drive/Meet/Calendar, OneDrive/SharePoint/Teams, Zoom and Turnitin are explicit examples. [11] There is no reviewed evidence of Ed-Fi or SIF certification, a public developer portal/API reference, or a marketplace with independently installable apps. Payment/accounting interoperability for Indian gateways is not publicly verified.

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Toddle’s public compliance position is comparatively strong for an Indian-founded K–12 SaaS: ISO 27001, ISO 27701, SOC 2, ISO 42001, 1EdTech Data Privacy, FERPA, COPPA and GDPR/DPA support are described in its privacy and integrations materials. It also explicitly states DPDPA/DPDP Act 2023 readiness, data minimization, school-directed processing, governance processes and regional hosting choices. [5] [11] This is a vendor assertion; buyers should request current certificates, subprocessor lists, breach-notification terms, retention schedules, AI data-use terms and India-region availability during diligence. No public incident was found in the reviewed sources through November 24, 2026.

### Strengths

- Broad, connected academic workflow for curriculum-led K–12 schools.
- Very strong web-review sentiment: 4.7/5 on both G2 and Capterra as viewed in November 2026. [7] [8]
- Production AI covers several teacher workflows, not only content chat.
- Deep international-school and IB fit, with growing CBSE/ICSE and public/district positioning.
- Strong SIS, SSO, LTI and OneRoster integration story.
- Educator-led onboarding and “Toddle Buddy” support are repeatedly praised by reviewers. [7] [12]
- Clear privacy/compliance documentation, including explicit DPDPA language. [5]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Pricing is fully quote-based; no INR/USD benchmark is public, making procurement comparison difficult. [9] [15]
- The platform’s breadth creates a learning curve; G2 users describe it as overwhelming and Capterra users report multiple clicks, slow loading and frequent interface changes. [7] [8]
- Secondary/departmental workflows can feel card-heavy; one Capterra reviewer reported difficulty using the gradebook as a spreadsheet and reliance on Google Classroom/spreadsheets. [8]
- Customization and granular defaults are recurring requests, and some users say AI output needs correction or does not follow instructions exactly. [7] [8]
- Mobile sentiment is much weaker than web sentiment: Apple shows 2.8/5 for Toddle Student and 2.1/5 for Toddle Family, with complaints about lag, crashes, confusing navigation, missing assignments, login/region errors and deleted work. [7] [10]
- The product does not publicly demonstrate the Indian ERP capabilities expected in a full school-management suite: fees, GST/Tally, UPI/Razorpay/Paytm, payroll, transport, hostel, library, inventory, UDISE+, APAAR or higher-education statutory reporting are unverified.
