### Overview

MyClassCampus was positioned as an “operating system” for education: an India-origin, cloud and mobile-first ERP joining administration, academics, finance, communication and campus operations. Its stated users were school administrators, teachers, students, parents and staff. The product was designed for single- and multi-branch institutions, with role-based access and configurable settings. [2] [7]

The product history is a pre-acquisition timeline, not proof of a currently sold standalone product. A founder profile says the MVP launched in March 2016, while company records use 2015 as the founding year and acquisition reporting says 2017. Teachmint acquired MyClassCampus on 28 January 2022, intending to combine its ERP with Teachmint’s LMS. [3] [4] [5] [6] [7]

As of 24 November 2026, myclasscampus.com no longer exposes a current MyClassCampus catalogue, price list or documentation portal. It leads to Teachmint X classroom hardware and AI teaching content. A current independent comparison likewise reports that the old domain redirects and that it is not possible to establish from public primary pages whether the legacy ERP is still sold under its old name, folded into Teachmint, or retired. [1] [15]

### Platform Classification Explained

* **Browser/web access:** Historical descriptions call it a web-based school-management ERP and show web administration workflows for fees, inventory, transport, website building and system logs. [2] [8] [11]
* **Cloud hosting:** MyClassCampus was repeatedly described as cloud-based. No public source verified the cloud provider, hosting region, tenancy model or data-residency commitments. [2] [7] [8]
* **Native mobile applications:** LinkedIn describes advanced native iOS and Android apps. Historical Android package records identify “My Class Campus,” package `com.myclasscampus`, and version 15.6. [2] [13] The iOS app “My Class Campus” was free, version 1.5, and was removed from the App Store in February 2024. [12]
* **Desktop client:** SaaSworthy lists “Desktop App (Windows),” but labels the vendor profile unclaimed and does not supply primary evidence. Treat this as unverified rather than an established deployment mode. [9]
* **On-premise/hybrid:** No public evidence of an on-premise installer, customer-managed database or hybrid deployment was found.

### Complete Feature Inventory

The inventory below relies on historical product material; SaaSworthy describes 40+ modules, while StartupTalky, SoftwareStreets and the official video channel expose concrete sub-features. [7] [8] [9] [11]

#### Admissions, enquiry and enrollment

Online admissions, inquiry management and academic-year enrollment were documented, with inquiry dashboards, forms and Tawk.to chat integration. Certificates and dynamic certificate generation with field logic were shown in the official video archive. [7] [8] [11] Merit lists, OCR, waitlists and application-payment workflows were not publicly verified.

#### Student information and records

The student module stored profiles and records, supported certificates, progress information and QR-code profile scanning. Multi-branch institutions and role-based access were supported. [2] [7] [9]

#### Attendance

Attendance tracking covered students and staff, with attendance reports and advertised wireless biometric support. Hardware brands and offline behaviour were not publicly specified. [7] [8] [9]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

The system included a dynamic calendar, timetable, holiday and year planner, plus scheduling features for classes. Public material does not specify constraint-based scheduling, room/resource optimization, substitution management or timetable imports. [2] [7] [9]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Documented academic tools include exam scheduling, exams and results, online quizzes, question banks, an offline paper generator, target management with ratings and result generation. The platform could conduct online tests and notify users of result dates. [7] [8] [9]

#### Report cards and transcripts

Result reports, student progress reports and report-card generation were listed. CBSE, ICSE/CISCE, state-board, NIOS, IB, IGCSE, transcript, NEP 2020 or PARAKH templates were not publicly verified. [7] [9]

#### Learning management and homework

The LMS layer included study material, document libraries, quizzes, question banks and homework. “Pocket Study,” introduced in April 2020, was described as an integrated LMS, with Pocket Study and Teachmint Live Class workflows shown in the official video archive. [2] [11]

#### Communication: SMS, email, push and WhatsApp

The historical product advertised bulk SMS gateway support, an SMS panel and app-based communication for management, teachers, students and parents. Native email campaigns, push configuration, WhatsApp Business API, templates and named WhatsApp vendors were not publicly verified. [2] [7] [8] [11]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

The product had mobile/web access for parents, students and teachers, including fee history, online fee payment, learning material and communication. SaaSworthy lists parent and student portals; LinkedIn calls the product mobile-first so stakeholders do not need a web login for important daily updates. [2] [9] The exact staff-app name and role-by-role permission matrix are not publicly specified.

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Finance and fees were among the strongest documented modules: fee structures and collection, fee reports, passbook, income/expense and summary reports, bank and cash accounts, cheque management, company/accounts management and a student wallet. Online payment gateway support and fee payment through the app were listed, including fee history and settlement reporting. [7] [8] [9] Specific support for UPI, Razorpay, Paytm, GST invoicing, Tally integration, automated reconciliation or India e-invoicing was not publicly verified.

#### HR, staff and payroll

HRMS covered employee, vacancy, leave, loan, promotion/transfer, appraisal, staff attendance, payroll and salary, plus staff/HR reports. EPF/ESI, statutory filings, biometric-to-payroll automation and employee self-service were not specified. [7] [8]

#### Transport

Transport management included GPS-enabled buses, vehicles, routes, waypoints, waypoint-user transfer, a tracking dashboard and reports. The video archive shows an app view and SMS panel; vehicle vendor, hardware, geofencing and route-alert specifications were not published. [7] [9] [11]

#### Library

Library management included library/book reports and was listed as a core module. Barcode/RFID, circulation rules, overdue automation and cataloguing standards were not specified. [7] [8] [14]

#### Hostel and boarding

Hostel management with mess was explicitly listed, and SaaSworthy identifies housing/hostel management. Detailed boarding workflows were not publicly documented. [9]

#### Inventory and assets

Inventory covered requirements, purchase orders, stock, infrastructure/allocation, sales/invoices, item history, item-kit history, approvals and dashboards. [8] [11]

#### Health and medical

SaaSworthy’s generic taxonomy marks “Clinic Management,” but no vendor-specific clinic workflow or medical records, visits, immunization, medication or emergency management was verified. [9]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

Complaint/grievance, guest-user and gate-pass management were listed. SaaSworthy’s behavior label is generic; detailed discipline, counseling, safeguarding or pastoral workflows were not verified. [9] [11]

#### Special education and safeguarding

No source verified individualized education plans, special-needs accommodation, safeguarding case management or consent workflows; SaaSworthy’s “Special Education” tag is generic. [9]

#### Alumni and fundraising

No public evidence was found for alumni, donor, fundraising or advancement modules.

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

The product included analysis and reports, daily summaries, 40+ custom reports, fee reports, exam and attendance reports, inquiry reports, vehicle tracking reports, library/book reports, user-data reports and staff/HR reports. Dashboard videos exist for finance, inquiry, inventory, complaint, target and hostel modules. [7] [8] [9] [11]

#### AI features

No production AI assistant, predictive analytics or generative-AI feature was verified for the legacy ERP. Current Teachmint X EduAI features should not be attributed to MyClassCampus without a continuity statement. [1]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

No verified public evidence was found for CBSE/ICSE/state-board report-card standards, NEP 2020 Holistic Progress Cards, UDISE+, APAAR ID, GST invoicing, Tally, NAAC, NBA, NIRF or AISHE reporting. These requirements should be treated as quote-stage validation items, not assumed capabilities.

#### Mobile apps

Native iOS and Android apps were a central differentiator. The historical iOS app was “My Class Campus,” while the Android package was “My Class Campus” with package `com.myclasscampus`; the product also had Pocket Study for LMS use. Historical app evidence is stale: the iOS app was removed in 2024 and the Android listing preserved by APKPure was last updated in 2023. [2] [11] [12] [13]

#### Integrations and APIs

Documented integrations include online payment gateway, bulk SMS gateway, Tawk.to for inquiry/admission chat, GPS/transport hardware and wireless biometric devices. A DIY website builder and hosting service was also offered. SaaSworthy says API support is “NA” and answers its FAQ “Does MyClassCampus provide API?” with “No,” so no public API should be assumed. [8] [9] [11]

#### Security and privacy

Role-based access and data backup were documented. A current Teachmint privacy policy, updated 24 August 2026, describes collection of student, parent and teacher personal data, consent, data-subject rights, service providers and deletion within six months after termination for the Teachmint platform. It does not prove that the same terms, controls or retention policy applied to the legacy MyClassCampus ERP. [7] [16] No public MyClassCampus-specific SOC 2, ISO 27001, FERPA, COPPA, GDPR, India DPDP Act readiness statement, hosting region, penetration-test report or incident register was verified.

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

* **40+ configurable modules:** The broad ERP scope covered administration, academics, finance, HR and campus operations in one platform. [2] [7]
* **Mobile-first stakeholder access:** Native iOS and Android apps were designed to surface daily updates without relying on a browser login. [2]
* **Multi-branch administration:** One account could support single- and multi-branch institutions. [7]
* **GPS transport operations:** Vehicles, routes, waypoints, dashboards and reports were tied to GPS-enabled bus tracking. [7] [11]
* **Wireless biometric support:** Historical product listings included wireless biometric attendance integration. [9]
* **Integrated Pocket Study LMS:** The April 2020 LMS connected study material, quizzes and online classes to the ERP user base. [2] [11]
* **Dynamic website and certificate tools:** A DIY website builder/hosting module and field-logic certificate generation extended the ERP beyond back-office administration. [7] [11]
* **QR profile scan and student wallet:** These were distinctive convenience and identity/payment features in the historical premium module list. [9]

### Pricing Details

No current official price was publicly available on 24 November 2026. The standalone site no longer publishes a pricing page, and the current destination does not quote a MyClassCampus rate. [1] [15]

The best dated historical figure is StartupTalky’s 13 April 2021 profile, which reported ₹100–₹150 per student per year, with Basic, Advance and Premium packages. It did not publish a package-by-package feature matrix, minimum enrolment, implementation fee or taxes. [7] SaaSworthy currently labels the product “Premium Plans (Quotation Based)” and a “Custom” plan, but warns that its feature/pricing details were last updated 4 December 2019; it is not a current official quote. [9] [10] No reliable USD price, setup fee, migration fee, support fee or renewal schedule was found.

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

The public record supports payment-gateway connectivity, bulk SMS, Tawk.to inquiry chat, GPS/transport hardware, wireless biometrics, app-based fee payments and a hosted website builder. [8] [9] [11] No verified evidence supports OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever, ClassLink, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, Tally, Razorpay, Paytm, WhatsApp Business API or an open public API. SaaSworthy explicitly reports API support as unavailable. [9]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

MyClassCampus publicly described role-based access and data backup, but did not publish a security certification or compliance matrix. [7] The current Teachmint privacy policy, last updated 24 August 2026, is the closest live parent-company statement and discusses PII, consent, processors, access/correction/deletion rights and retention; it should not be read as a MyClassCampus-specific DPDP Act attestation. [16]

No public evidence was verified for SOC 2, ISO 27001, FERPA, COPPA, GDPR, India DPDP Act readiness, Indian data-hosting region or a public security-incident history. The absence of evidence is not proof that controls do not exist; it means a buyer must request the DPA, subprocessor list, hosting location, incident-notification terms and export/deletion procedure in writing.

### Strengths

* Broad historical ERP coverage across admissions, academics, finance, HR, transport, library, hostel, inventory and communication. [7] [8] [9]
* Strong India-market orientation with mobile apps, fee collection, GPS buses, biometric support, SMS and multi-branch configuration. [2] [7] [9]
* Mobile-first delivery and an integrated LMS/Pocket Study layer made the product more than a back-office database. [2] [11]
* Reported scale was meaningful by 2021–2022: 1,500–2,000+ institutions and 300,000 to 2m+ students depending on the dated claim and definition. [5] [6] [7]
* Acquisition by Teachmint supplied a larger education-technology parent and a potential ERP-plus-LMS route. [4] [5]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

The major current weakness is verifiability: the original site no longer exposes a standalone catalogue, current price, roadmap or data-export documentation. [1] [15] Buyers cannot reliably tell whether the legacy ERP is still sold under the MyClassCampus name or folded into Teachmint. [15]

Public review evidence is thin and not fully current. SaaSworthy shows a high 4.9/5 from 75 ratings, but the profile is unclaimed and carries a 2019 data-freshness warning. [9] [10] AppBrain shows only two iOS ratings averaging 3.0/5 and records that the app was removed in February 2024. [12] I found no dependable review corpus identifying recurring operational complaints; therefore widespread usability or support complaints should not be invented. The most defensible user pain point is the uncertainty created by stale listings, discontinued/removed app evidence and quote-only current access.
