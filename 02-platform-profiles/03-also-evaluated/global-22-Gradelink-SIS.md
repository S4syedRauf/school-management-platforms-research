### Overview

Gradelink SIS is a school information and administration system built around a single web-based record for students, families, staff, classes, grades, attendance, communication and tuition. Its stated value proposition is operational simplicity for smaller and mid-sized schools: administrators enter information once, teachers manage gradebooks and lesson plans, and families access grades, assignments, attendance, conduct, schedules and billing through portals or the mobile app. [1] [2] [14]

The product is especially relevant to private, parochial, charter and international K–12 schools that want admissions, academics, parent communication and basic billing in one subscription rather than a large district ERP. Gradelink says it has served schools for 24 years and currently claims 2,200+ schools and 400K users, but it does not publish a country-by-country adoption table. The company was founded in 2001 according to LinkedIn and Crunchbase; a separate product launch year, acquisition history, and funding history were not publicly disclosed. [1] [10] [13] [14]

### Platform Classification Explained

Gradelink is browser-first Cloud SaaS. The company says it runs entirely online, works on major browsers and operating systems, requires only an internet connection, and cannot currently be downloaded or used offline. It supports Macs, PCs, tablets and mobile browsers. [4] [13] The security page says customer servers are hosted in a locked data-center facility in Irvine, California, with U.S.-based IT staff; it does not name AWS, Azure, Google Cloud or another public cloud provider. [8]

There is no evidence of a Windows/macOS desktop client, self-hosted package, or customer-managed on-premise edition. Native mobile software is the **Gradelink Student/Parent App** for iPhone/iPad and Android. It exposes grades, assignments, attendance, homework, schedules, news, discipline, report cards, billing and configurable alerts. The product pages do not identify a separate native teacher or administrator app; staff use the responsive browser service. [7] [19] [20]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

The core SIS includes a prospective-student inquiry form and prospect workflow. The paid EnrollMe add-on provides online applications, re-enrollment, custom enrollment forms, document collection and automatic fee collection when paired with Gradelink Payments. Help documentation covers application management, importing applicants, enrollment sessions, reports and setup for a new academic year. [2] [3] [6]

#### Student information and records

Student records cover demographics, status, photos, family and sibling links, campus assignment, tags, emergency-card information, contact details, religious information, languages, government IDs, testing IDs, files and documents. Administrators can withdraw or promote students, lock or reset student/family accounts, view login information and export records. The security model isolates each school in a separate database. [6] [8]

#### Attendance

Attendance includes daily and per-class registers, attendance and conduct reports, holiday scheduling, custom and detailed reports, total-school attendance, extended-care tracking and preschool/child check-in. The 2026 product news documents parent absence reporting through the portal or app, with opt-in email notifications to teachers and administrators, and batch check-in/check-out for groups. [2] [10]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

Classes and Scheduling supports block scheduling, concurrent mixed terms such as quarters and trimesters, class calendars, custom class types, custom and managed grade scales, standards per class, simple or advanced teacher schedules, and student scheduling. The evidence supports class scheduling rather than a full transportation-style master-scheduling optimizer. [2] [5]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Teacher Gradebooks provide assignment collections, assignment-type weighting, file attachments, automatic lowest-grade drops, extra credit, curves/grade boosts, custom grade codes and comments, preset comments, low-grade highlighting, projected semester grades, semester/year averages, effort and citizenship, team teaching/grading, and unified multi-class views. Teachers can record homework, tests, projects and quizzes; reports include GPA/scores, honor roll and sports eligibility. Standards-based grading and class/teacher/student standards metrics are included. A separate high-stakes examination-management module was not found. [2] [6]

#### Report cards and transcripts

The platform generates comprehensive, conduct, deficiency, progress and standards-based report cards, with customizable templates and paperless distribution. It produces transcripts and transcript labels, preserves academic history, and supports export of transcript records as PDF. A 2026 Award Certificates tool adds dynamic templates, drag-and-drop design, smart data fields such as name, term, principal and GPA, and batch PDF export. [2] [6] [10]

#### Learning management and homework

This is an SIS with learning-support functions, not a full standalone LMS. Native tools include lesson plans, assignment collection, attachments, homework/test/project/quiz tracking, teacher pages and upcoming-assignment visibility. Google Classroom can synchronize assignment and quiz grades into the Gradelink gradebook. No public evidence was found for SCORM, LTI, course authoring, discussion forums or a full LMS product. [2] [5] [11]

#### Communication

Built-in SmartSend supports email blasts, group building, school calendars, school news, teacher pages and automated parent alerts. SmartSend Pro is an optional add-on for unlimited text and voice messaging, message scheduling and history logs, with automatic synchronization of students, parents, staff and alumni. The 2026 news page reports full STIR/SHAKEN “A” attestation for voice caller-ID trust. WhatsApp and native push broadcast support were not documented. [3] [10]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

Separate parent/family and student accounts provide grades, assignment details and teacher comments, attendance and conduct, transcripts, schedules, calendars, news, downloadable attachments, absence reporting and grade/attendance alerts. Teacher functionality is delivered through the web interface and Teacher Pages rather than a separate teacher portal or app. The redesigned Parent and Student Portal launched before the 2026 review period. [2] [6] [10]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Tuition & Billing includes accounts receivable, multiple tuition rates, billing statements, aging reports, convenience fees, automated lunch billing and incidental billing. Gradelink Payments, integrated with PaySimple, accepts ACH and credit cards, supports one-time or recurring donations, AutoPay, AutoPost and multi-month installment plans; families can pay tuition, lunch, sports and other charges. This is school billing and payment collection, not a complete general-ledger, procurement or fund-accounting suite. [3] [5] [11]

#### HR and staff

Staff accounts store credentials, employment status, contact information and pay grade, with configurable access levels and notifications. Staff rosters and staff reports are available. No native payroll, benefits, recruiting, performance-management or employee self-service module was found. [2] [6]

#### Health and medical

Medical records include allergies, immunizations/immune status, insurance information, medical history, medications, physical exams, office/injury visits, vision, hearing and scoliosis exams, plus medical reports. [2] [6]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

Discipline tools store incidents, demerits and counseling/discipline entries, support configurable menu choices and discipline email alerts, and expose discipline reports and exports. A broad pastoral-care or safeguarding case-management product was not evidenced. [2] [6]

#### Special education

IEP tracking is an administrative report and student-record feature. Help documentation covers IEP and 504 plans, custom dropdown menus, adding plans and IEP reports. The public materials do not establish a full special-education workflow comparable to a district SpEd case-management suite. [2] [6]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Native reporting includes address labels, birthday lists, classes by attendance or average grade, family directory, IEP tracking, lunch, staff roster, standards-met-by-teacher, volunteer hours, security activity, student rosters, transcripts, attendance and financial reports. District Login adds cross-school demographics, academic performance, attendance, census data, maps, subgroup reports, staff/student details and group email. Predictive analytics, data warehousing and natural-language BI were not found. [2] [12]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Public integration materials identify Ed-Fi certification as a student-information capability and describe Wisconsin WISEdata/Ed-Fi reporting for Choice schools. Other named exports include Indiana DOE Data Exchange and Choice Scholarship reporting, Michigan MSDS Early Roster, Georgia HOPE Scholarship and NCEA reporting. These are targeted state or association workflows, not a universal international statutory-reporting engine. [2] [5] [11]

#### Mobile apps

The native app is the Gradelink Student/Parent App for iOS/iPadOS and Android. It offers grades, assignment and category-weight graphs, report cards, attendance history, discipline, billing statements, school news, schedules, grade alerts, attendance alerts, absence reporting and multi-child switching. The app requires the school to subscribe and some features depend on school settings. [7] [19] [20]

#### Integrations and APIs

Named integrations include Google Classroom, ClassLink, Schoology, Clever, Microsoft School Data Sync, Apple School Manager, Renaissance RDI/Star, FACTS Tuition, PaySimple, Edmentum, Canvas, ClassWallet, Indiana Data Exchange, Michigan MSDS and Wisconsin WISEdata. GetApp’s product FAQ explicitly says no API is available, so these are vendor-supported connectors and exports rather than a documented public developer platform. No public LTI, OneRoster, SIF, marketplace or QuickBooks Online integration was verified. [5] [11] [17]

#### Modules not evidenced as native Gradelink modules

The reviewed primary materials did not substantiate native transport management, library management, hostel/boarding, inventory and asset management, payroll, alumni/fundraising CRM, AI assistant, predictive analytics, WhatsApp messaging, or a dedicated teacher mobile app. Some third-party catalogs list generic category labels, but those listings were not accepted as proof against the vendor’s actual feature pages. [2] [5] [17] [21]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **District/Diocese Login:** Cross-school reports, subgroup filters, census/map views, staff and student detail, and district-wide email give a small-school product a useful multi-school oversight layer. [12]
- **School-isolated databases:** Gradelink states that schools are kept in separate complete databases rather than shared tables, a distinctive privacy boundary for a multi-tenant service. [8]
- **Fast implementation:** The vendor targets a five-to-ten-business-day setup after receiving the roster and report-card information, with standard current-record transfer included. [3] [4]
- **Integrated private-school workflow:** EnrollMe, SIS academics, family communication, billing and PaySimple payments connect the admissions-to-revenue path without a long-term contract. [3]
- **Targeted compliance exports:** Ed-Fi/WISEdata, Indiana, Michigan, Georgia and NCEA connections address specific U.S. state and Catholic-school reporting needs. [5] [11]
- **Configurable family alerts:** Parents can set grade and attendance thresholds in the app, while schools can send scheduled email, SMS and voice notifications. [3] [19]
- **Dynamic award certificates:** The 2026 certificate editor turns report data into branded, batch-printable awards without external design software. [10]

### Pricing Details

The official calculator displayed **US$106 per month plus a one-time US$275 setup fee** when checked on November 24, 2026. Pricing is based on actively enrolled students, billed monthly, with no long-term contract; the calculator does not present a simple public annual per-student rate. The official page lists a free trial and says standard data transfer, staff training, support and updates are included. [3] [4]

Optional published add-ons are **EnrollMe: US$20.83/month plus US$497 setup**; **SmartSend Pro: US$16.67/month**; and **Gradelink Payments: US$0/month software fee plus payment processing**. Clever Sync, Renaissance Learning, transcript import and educational standards are listed as additional options requiring a sales quote. Extended historical-data transfer may also incur a fee. [3]

For comparison, Capterra and GetApp report **US$121/month for up to 50 students, US$164/month for 51–100, and US$210/month for 101–150**; these are third-party displayed tiers, not a replacement for the official calculator. [15] [17]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Gradelink supports Google Classroom grade sync, ClassLink and Clever rostering, Schoology, Microsoft School Data Sync, Apple School Manager, Renaissance RDI/Star, FACTS Tuition, PaySimple, Edmentum and Canvas. State/sector connections include NCEA, Indiana DOE Data Exchange, Michigan MSDS, Georgia HOPE and Wisconsin WISEdata. [5] [11]

The official feature page labels Gradelink “Ed-Fi Certified,” and the Wisconsin integration is described as an Ed-Fi/WISEdata pathway. Google SSO is supported. However, no public API is documented; GetApp explicitly answers “No” to API availability. No verified OneRoster, LTI or SIF certification, public integration marketplace, Microsoft Graph API, accounting-platform connector or QuickBooks Online connector was found. [2] [3] [17]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

The security page reports SSL encryption for web and mobile connections, nightly redundant backups using local and remotely encrypted storage, an additional off-site backup, audited device/IP requests, network segregation, firewalls, intrusion detection, log aggregation, VPN access, configurable timeouts, password complexity and lockout rules, teacher-level permissions and separate school databases. The pricing page also advertises MFA, Google SSO, encrypted service, login/IP/DNS tracking, daily backups and student-privacy compliance. [3] [8]

Gradelink says servers are in an Irvine, California data center and IT staff are in the United States. Schools retain ownership of their data and can export Excel-compatible student data, PDF academic records or an encrypted SQL backup. Its privacy/DPA language commits to reasonable industry-standard technical and organizational safeguards, applicable privacy laws, CCPA, school data policies, subcontractor controls, incident notification and deletion/return on request. [8] [9]

No public SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certificate was located. FERPA is referenced contractually, including directory-information language, but a public FERPA certification does not exist in the materials reviewed; COPPA, GDPR and India’s DPDP Act were not publicly claimed as certifications. No publicly reported Gradelink security incident was found in the sources reviewed through November 24, 2026. Schools should obtain the current DPA and security plan directly before procurement. [8] [9]

### Strengths

- Broad, coherent SIS coverage for private and faith-based K–12 schools: records, attendance, gradebook, report cards, transcripts, communication, admissions and tuition.
- Public calculator pricing and a monthly, no-long-term-contract model are unusually transparent for a commercial SIS. [3]
- Very strong support reputation: Capterra shows 4.9/5 for customer service, while GetApp reviewers frequently praise rapid, knowledgeable help. [15] [17]
- High review usability scores, especially Capterra ease of use at 4.6/5 and strong Apple app feedback. [15] [19]
- Practical state/association exports and a district/diocese rollup for organizations that do not need a large district ERP. [5] [12]

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Reviewers report occasional freezes, unexplained returns to the home page, glitches/timeouts and limited comment space. [15] [17]
- The interface is considered less modern or less streamlined than larger SIS products, and several reviewers describe report customization or complex reporting as limited. [16] [18]
- The native app is family/student-focused; a Software Advice reviewer said staff sometimes must use a computer because the web software is not usable on mobile, while a Capterra reviewer reported parent complaints about app glitches and bill-pay usability. [16] [17]
- Billing can become confusing when a family pays tuition, lunch and activities in one transaction, and GetApp reviewers complain that PaySimple setup/importing is not sufficiently user-friendly. [15] [17]
- Add-on pricing and sales scope can make total cost harder to predict, and advanced enterprise integrations, API access, predictive analytics and ERP modules are limited or absent. [17] [18]
