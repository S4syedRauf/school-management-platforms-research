### Overview

Edunext ERP is a broad, India-oriented school-management suite combining admissions, student records, attendance, academics, examinations, report cards, fee collection, finance, HR/payroll, transport, library, inventory, hostel operations, e-learning and parent/teacher/management mobile apps. The vendor positions it for schools that want a single cloud system instead of separate administrative, academic and communication tools. The official site says it serves 1,400+ schools globally; its historical timeline records expansion to Dubai/Sharjah in 2015, Nigeria and Ghana in 2018, Japan in 2022 and Bangladesh in 2023. [1] [2]

The company was incorporated in 2012, but the ERP’s first-launch year is not stated. EducationWorld reports the copyrighted Parent App launched in 2013 and Alexa integration in 2018. The official timeline records Tally (2014), Version II (2015), e-learning (2020), AI proctoring (2021), WhatsApp/admission CRM (2023), and event, cafeteria, safety, task, grievance and appraisal capabilities (2024). Tracxn reports no disclosed funding rounds or acquisitions. [1] [13] [15]

### Platform Classification Explained

Edunext is a cloud/web ERP: the official technical page says there is no client-machine installation, the system is accessible through the internet 24x7, and it is built with Java, React JS, MySQL and Apache. The terms page describes the service as cloud-based and names AWS, SSL and encrypted passwords; the official timeline says the technology shifted to Amazon Web Services in 2017. Browser access is through each school’s Edunext ERP login, with Chrome and Firefox explicitly supported in the parent help page. [1] [7] [17]

There is no public evidence of a Windows/macOS desktop client or an on-premise deployment option. The product has native Android and iOS mobile delivery. The principal parent-facing listing is **Edunext Parent**; Apple also lists **Edunext Teacher**, **Edunext Management**, **Edunext Alumni**, **SecureNext**, and **EdunextCafe** under the developer. The vendor describes role-based student, parent, teacher and management apps, plus separate visitor and transport applications. [8] [9] [10] [12]

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment

The admissions module is a full enquiry-to-enrolment workflow: user-defined enquiry and admission forms, custom fields, mandatory-field sequencing, publish/unpublish controls, class-wise age rules, configurable stages, applicant login, document uploads and stage tracking. It adds lead assignment, follow-ups, reminders, status tracking, counsellor dashboards, interaction and campus-tour scheduling, entrance-test question-paper generation, online evaluation, weighted selection criteria, OTP and document validation, audit trails, online invoicing, multi-bank/gateway settlement and admission conversion reports. The page names CBSE, CISCE, IB, IGCSE, state and national curricula and says age–class checks are aligned with NEP and CBSE guidance. [4]

#### Student information and records

The SIS supports user-defined student profiles, ID-card generation, document-expiry alerts, certificates, birthdays, clubs and houses, status changes for suspension, struck-off, withdrawal and transfer certificates, data mining, dynamic reports, completeness statistics and compliance reports. Historical records, previous-year data and Excel migration are supported. [5] [17] [29]

#### Attendance

Attendance is available for students, staff, online classes, libraries and transport. Teachers can mark students in the app; staff attendance supports department calendars, shifts, leave and late-coming MIS; biometric/face-recognition devices can feed employee attendance; RFID gates and Android bus phones support student movement/attendance. Online-class attendance can be marked automatically when a student joins a scheduled class. [1] [6] [9] [18]

#### Timetable and master scheduling

The public materials document student and teacher timetable access, teacher calendars, lesson-plan schedules, class-wise online-class scheduling, merged sections, remedial classes, observers and period-wise online attendance. A detailed public description of constraint-based master scheduling or room/resource optimization is not available. [3] [8] [9]

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations

Teachers can enter marks, grades and remarks; schools can configure assessment terms, grading structures, weightages and rounding, create subjective or objective papers, run quizzes, annotate checked papers, publish results, and compare performance across years, classmates, classes and subjects. Examination pages explicitly support CBSE, CISCE, IB and IGCSE workflows and CBSE result TXT-file import. [3] [19]

#### Report cards and transcripts

Schools can customize, print and publish report cards online and in the mobile app; the academic page claims 1,100+ report-card formats. The SIS also generates customized certificates and transfer certificates. No separate transcript-credit-management product is publicly described. [3] [5] [19]

#### Learning management and homework

The e-learning layer stores audio, video, PDF, Word, PowerPoint, web links and chapter/topic resources; teachers assign homework, assignments and projects to classes, sections, groups or individuals; students submit attachments; teachers annotate, comment and award marks/credits. Discussion boards, quizzes, online assessments, live classes and automatic results are included. [3] [9] [15]

#### Communication

Communication includes in-app parent–teacher and intra-school messaging, SMS, email, push alerts, circulars, news, calendar, remarks, photo galleries and automated birthday/fee/overdue reminders. Business WhatsApp integration supports broadcast messages, automated replies, PDFs, URLs, audio, video, payment links, read receipts and fee reminders. The admission CRM adds WhatsApp, SMS and email logs and stage notifications. [4] [5] [8] [20]

#### Parent, student and teacher portals

The browser portal is school-branded and reached from a school website. The parent app covers profiles, attendance, homework, fees and receipts, communication, circulars, calendar, remarks, achievements, report cards, leave, syllabus, e-content, online classes, quizzes, transport and bus location. A single family login can switch between multiple wards. Teacher app functions include attendance, remarks, homework, syllabus, e-resources, timetable, leave and parent communication; management app functions include fee/admission summaries, attendance, circulars, SMS, library status and staff inspection. [8] [9] [10]

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting

Fee management provides day-wise collection, dues/receipts, projected collection, concessions and waivers, overdue reminders, caution/security-deposit refunds, ECS/NACH, 40+ reports and branded challans/receipts. The current fee page documents cards, net banking and UPI, while the payment page documents web/mobile integration, registration and miscellaneous receipts, transaction synchronization and bank settlement. Finance supports user-defined ledgers, groups, vouchers and schemes, student auto-ledgers, cheque printing, day books, trial balances, income/expenditure, cashbooks, bankbooks, petty cash, balance sheets and drill-down. Tally ERP 9 integration is documented. Razorpay and Paytm are not named in the opened official pages, so provider support should be confirmed in a quote. [1] [6] [17] [18]

#### HR, staff and payroll

HR covers vacancy requisition/approval, online resumes, custom recruitment forms, shortlisting, merit lists, offer letters, joining documents, staff profiles, IDs, qualifications, employment history, subject/class allocation, tasks, leave and biometric attendance. Payroll processes salaries, payslips, TDS, PF/ECR, ESI, savings/investments, loans, advances, increments, arrears and bank-disbursement reports. [17] [21]

#### Transport

Transport includes route/stop and vehicle management, route vacancy/occupancy dashboards, fuel logs, maintenance/document-expiry reminders, online requests to add, withdraw or change transport, transport attendance and Android-based GPS. The transport app uses an Android phone on each bus rather than a dedicated GPS device; the bus in-charge can capture route-wise attendance while parents track the live location. [6] [9] [22]

#### Library

The library module supports DDC book records, OPAC search, acquisitions, barcode issue/return, stock verification, periodicals, library in/out registers, members, fines, employee/student transactions, overdue and circulation history, discrepancy reports and library attendance. [3] [23]

#### Hostel and boarding

The cloud hostel module allocates students to available rooms, supports custom hostel fields, is configurable for institutional requirements and has a mobile interface. Detailed mess, warden, boarding-roll and hostel health workflows are not publicly itemized. [24]

#### Inventory and assets

Inventory covers online requisitions and user-defined approval levels, purchase-order templates, goods-receiving notes, verification, stock entry, invoice processing, payment approval, item/item-kit issue, sale and return to students, staff and departments. Reports cover issue, return, stock, daily sales, item history and employee consumption. [25]

#### Health, medical and cafeteria

A dedicated medical/health-record module is not publicly evidenced in the reviewed materials. The 2024 timeline lists cafeteria management, but no detailed public cafeteria sub-feature page was found. A 2020 independent profile mentions thermal-scanner attendance and temperature alerts, not a clinical health record. [1] [15]

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care

The SIS exposes remarks, achievements, suspension and struck-off status; the parent app displays teacher observations, and the management app supports staff inspection and observations. A dedicated behavior-point, incident, restorative-discipline or pastoral-care module is not documented. [5] [8] [10]

#### Special education and safeguarding

Edunext has visitor management, contactless check-in, QR appointments, RFID child-safety tooling, SecureNext and bus tracking. No public evidence was found for an IEP/special-education case-management module, safeguarding case workflow or statutory child-protection register. [1] [26]

#### Alumni and fundraising

Dynamic website content can include alumni data and forms, and Apple lists an Edunext Alumni app. No public evidence of donation processing, campaign management or fundraising analytics was found. [10] [27]

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting

Dashboards cover fees, admissions, enquiries, follow-ups, conversion, attendance, transport occupancy, library, homework, lesson-plan coverage, staff and student performance. The academic page claims 100+ graphical/analytical reports; EducationWorld reported more than 2,000 structured reports in 2020. Reports are exportable to Excel and can be customized to school formats. [3] [15] [17] [29]

#### AI features

The official timeline says AI-based exam proctoring launched in 2021, and the product includes PowerTEST online proctoring. Alexa voice skills, launched in 2018, answer requests about homework, attendance, news, circulars and fee dues. Public material does not establish a generative AI assistant or predictive-risk model. [1] [18]

#### Government/state reporting and compliance

Edunext documents CBSE, CISCE, IB and IGCSE exam/report-card compatibility, CBSE result TXT imports, state/national curriculum support and generic compliance reports. The current llms.txt markets the platform as NEP 2020 compliant, while the admissions page cites NEP/CBSE age eligibility. I found no verified public evidence of direct UDISE+ submission, APAAR ID integration, GST invoicing, or NEP 2020 holistic progress-card templates; schools should validate these in procurement. Higher-education NAAC, NBA, NIRF and AISHE reporting is likewise not documented as a product capability. [4] [5] [19] [28]

#### Mobile apps

Native Android/iOS delivery includes Edunext Parent, teacher and management apps, with additional alumni, SecureNext and cafeteria apps listed on Apple. Parent Android has 1M+ downloads and 4.6/5 from 17.9k reviews; iOS shows 4.4/5 from 7.2k ratings. [11] [12]

#### Integrations and APIs

Named integrations are AWS hosting, Tally ERP 9, Google Meet/Google Drive/G Suite, Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Amazon Alexa, WhatsApp Business, biometric/face-recognition devices, RFID, Android GPS tracking and payment gateways. No public developer API, OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever/ClassLink connector, marketplace, Microsoft 365 directory/SSO, or Google Workspace SSO documentation was found; G2’s product FAQ explicitly says Edunext does not offer an open API. [1] [11] [15] [17]

#### Security and privacy

The vendor describes AWS hosting, SSL/TLS, encryption, automatic backups, IP restriction, OTP authentication, role-based access, audit logs, encrypted IDs in admission forms and data export in Excel on termination. Its privacy policy says it follows the IT Act/SPDI rules and claims alignment with India’s DPDP Act 2023, including consent, minimization, access/correction and breach-notification language. Public evidence of SOC 2, ISO 27001, FERPA, COPPA or GDPR certification, regional hosting choices, penetration-test reports or a public incident register was not found. Google Play’s developer-declared data-safety panel says no data is shared/collected but also says data is not encrypted and cannot be deleted, which warrants a school-level privacy and DPA review. [7] [11] [28]

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **Alexa “talking ERP”:** parents can ask for homework, attendance, circulars, school news and fee dues by voice. [18]
- **AI-based online proctoring:** PowerTEST is positioned for monitored online examinations, although public technical detail is limited. [1] [18]
- **Admission CRM:** lead capture, configurable stage workflows, weighted entrance evaluation, OTP/document validation and payment reconciliation are unusually detailed for a school ERP. [4]
- **No-device bus tracking:** the transport app uses an Android phone on the bus for location and attendance instead of dedicated GPS hardware. [22]
- **High report-card customization:** the vendor claims 1,100+ report-card formats plus board-specific CBSE/CISCE/IB/IGCSE handling. [3] [19]
- **Role-specific mobile ecosystem:** parent, teacher, management, alumni, visitor/safety and transport experiences are separated by role and use case. [9] [10] [12]
- **Task escalation:** overdue tasks trigger alerts to the responsible employee and escalate to higher management, with service-request approval workflow and dashboards. [27]

### Pricing Details

No official price list or public pricing page was found. The terms page says clients pay one-time implementation and training charges and then monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly subscription charges under the final agreement; it does not publish amounts or tier names. [7]

SoftwareSuggest likewise says there is no fixed plan and that price depends on requirements, users and customization. IndiaMART lists the product as a one-year license with online training and PC/mobile compatibility but does not show a price on the opened listing; separate search-result cards for other Edunext listings showed inconsistent figures, so they should not be treated as verified Edunext pricing. No USD price is published. Date checked: 24 Nov 2026. [12] [14]

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

The verified integration set is practical rather than standards-led: Tally ERP 9; Google G Suite/Drive/Meet; Microsoft Teams; Zoom; Amazon Alexa; WhatsApp Business; payment gateways supporting cards, net banking and UPI; biometric/face-recognition devices; RFID; Android GPS and Excel imports/exports. The platform also supports configurable school forms, custom fields and report formats. [1] [3] [17] [20]

There is no public evidence of OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever or ClassLink certification, a developer marketplace, a public API, or documented SSO with Google Workspace/Microsoft 365. G2’s product page says no open API is offered. [11]

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

Edunext states that it uses AWS, SSL/TLS, encryption, role-based permissions, OTP, IP restrictions, audit logs, automatic cloud backups and encrypted form IDs. It provides data export in Excel at termination and describes access/correction, opt-out and machine-readable-copy requests. Its privacy policy expressly references the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and claims lawful processing, consent, minimization and breach notification. [7] [17] [28]

This is policy-level readiness, not independently verified certification. No SOC 2, ISO 27001, FERPA, COPPA or GDPR certification was found, and no public breach/incident register was located. Google Play’s self-declared data-safety panel says no third-party sharing and no data collected, but simultaneously says data is not encrypted and cannot be deleted; that inconsistency makes contractual data-processing terms and technical due diligence important. [11]

### Strengths

- Broad native coverage across Indian school administration, academics, finance, HR, transport, library, hostel and mobile engagement.
- Strong board-oriented assessment support for CBSE, CISCE, IB and IGCSE, customizable report cards and CBSE result-file import. [3] [19]
- Meaningful India-specific finance workflows: UPI, ECS/NACH, concessions, refunds, Tally integration and statutory payroll calculations. [6] [17] [21]
- Mature role-based mobile experience with high-volume parent-app distribution and dedicated helpdesk. [8] [11] [12]
- AWS cloud deployment, Excel migration, configurable fields/reports and implementation/training services. [15] [17] [29]
- Distinctive Alexa voice, RFID, face recognition, phone-based bus tracking and WhatsApp capabilities.

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Pricing is quote-only; the vendor publishes no tier, per-student, per-school or implementation price, making comparisons difficult. [7] [12]
- G2 has only one review, so its 4.5 score is not a robust market signal; that reviewer liked stability but reported lag and slow data loading. [11]
- SoftwareSuggest’s review synthesis cites complex module views, slow implementation, limited notifications, server/app instability and transport-management issues; individual reviewers asked for better mess/budget functions, more streamlined finance, faster issue resolution and less manual subject allocation. [14]
- Apple reviews report slow performance, erratic notifications, stale unread status, broken leave date-picker behavior, repeated false update alerts and weak support hand-offs; one reviewer said support redirected them to the school rather than resolving app defects. [12]
- Public interoperability is weak: no open API or 1EdTech/Ed-Fi standards evidence was found. [11]
- UDISE+, APAAR, GST invoicing, Razorpay/Paytm, vernacular language packs, higher-education accreditation reporting and DPDP operational controls are not sufficiently documented publicly.
