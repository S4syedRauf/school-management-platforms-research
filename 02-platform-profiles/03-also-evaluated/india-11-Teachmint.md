### Overview

Teachmint began as a pandemic-era, mobile-first, video-first teaching app for tutors and teachers to create virtual classrooms, run live lessons, share notes, assign work, take attendance and collect fees. It then added Teachmint for Institute for schools and colleges, and by late 2026 its public positioning is centered on Teachmint X: an AI-powered interactive flat panel plus connected classroom software, EduAI, VisionX device management and Share X. The platform is relevant to Indian schools because it combines classroom delivery, student engagement and institutional workflows rather than acting only as a video-conferencing tool [3] [4].

The company raised $3.5M/$4M seed funding in October 2020, $16.5M Series A in May 2021, $20M pre-Series B in July 2021 and $78M Series B in October 2021. TechCrunch reported more than $115M raised and a $500M valuation in 2021; an independent funding database totals $118M across four rounds [9] [10]. Teachmint acquired Teachmore in 2021, acqui-hired Teachee’s Indian team in December 2021, and is reported to have acquired MyClassCampus in January 2022; the latter is not confirmed by a current Teachmint release, so it should be treated as a secondary-source claim [2] [11].

### Platform Classification Explained

- **Browser/cloud:** Teachmint provides web access and describes cloud-synchronized whiteboards, cloud storage for teaching materials and recordings, and online dashboards. Its privacy policy says data may be stored in India and transferred abroad subject to applicable law; it does not name a cloud provider [4] [12].
- **Mobile:** The Android app is **Teachmint AI Connected Classes**; it supports live video, chat, screen sharing, recordings, homework, quizzes, materials, attendance and progress reporting. The iOS app is **Teachmint : AI study app**. A separate **Whiteboard by Teachmint** Android listing and **Share X by Teachmint** iOS listing also exist [7] [8] [13].
- **Hardware/hybrid:** Teachmint X panels run Android 16 on current product pages, are Google EDLA-certified, support Google Play apps, NFC/multi-user sign-in, cloud lesson saving and wireless sharing. This is a device-plus-cloud hybrid classroom model, not an on-premise ERP [4] [14].
- **Desktop/on-premise:** Capterra lists web, Android and iPhone/iPad deployment. No current public evidence of a separately installed Windows/macOS desktop client, self-hosted edition or on-premise school server was found [5].

### Complete Feature Inventory

#### Admissions and enrollment
The legacy school suite had an Admission Management portal that passed admitted-student information into the SIS, with customizable criteria and a website/CRM lead-management workflow. The current admissions page explicitly says the standalone admission module is no longer available, so this is a historical capability rather than a current purchase assumption [15] [16].

#### Student information and records
The institute product supported a Student Information System, centralized student records, student management, ID cards, certificates and downloadable documents. Current classroom software still advertises student management, learner progress and stakeholder visibility, but a full public SIS schema is not documented [3] [4] [5].

#### Attendance
Current Teachmint X supports digital attendance, and the panel’s AI camera can recognize students from verified profile images, permit corrections before submission and reduce proxy attendance. The mobile app also advertises automatic attendance tracking; staff attendance existed in the older ERP description [4] [7] [14].

#### Timetable and master scheduling
Teachmint supports live-class scheduling and lesson planning. The older commercial package listed automated timetable generation and yearly calendars, but the current product pages do not document a full conflict-aware master-scheduling engine [4] [17].

#### Gradebook, assessment and examinations
The active classroom product supports tests, quizzes, question banks, assignments, automatic grading/attendance claims, submission tracking and learning-gap analysis. Digital content includes a 20 lakh-plus question bank. The former institute package included assessments and exam management [4] [7] [18].

#### Report cards and transcripts
The Report Card Generation page describes end-to-end exam scheduling, marks updating, customizable titles and signatures, parent/teacher visibility, printable output and bulk downloads. No public evidence of a separately maintained transcript, credit or university academic-record module was found [19].

#### Learning management and homework
The strongest current module is the LMS/classroom workflow: live classes, recordings, notes, PDFs, videos, cloud materials, lesson plans, homework, quizzes, class recaps, sharing and engagement tracking. Teachmint’s content library claims 520+ hours, 9K+ Hindi/English learning modules, NCERT mapping, offline content and 20L+ questions [4] [18].

#### Communication
Current tools include teacher-student chat, notices, announcements, live-class chat and sharing with students and parents. The older ERP page describes broadcasts for annual day, PTA meetings and fee due dates. Public materials do not verify native SMS, email gateway, WhatsApp parent messaging or Paytm/Razorpay messaging integrations; a G2 reviewer’s WhatsApp reference was to customer support, not necessarily a product channel [4] [5] [6].

#### Parent, student and teacher portals
The platform has a shared admin dashboard and a single teacher/student app, with parent visibility described in the legacy school-management page. The current public app is positioned primarily for teachers and students; no separate, role-specific parent app or staff app is publicly identified [4] [7] [16].

#### Fees, billing, payments and accounting
Historically, Teachmint offered fee collection, online payments, receipts, reminders and 20-plus filtered fee reports. The current official fee page says the standalone fee-management solution is no longer available. No current evidence verifies GST invoicing, UPI, Razorpay, Paytm, Tally or a live accounting ledger, so these India-specific capabilities should not be assumed in 2026 [5] [20].

#### HR, staff and payroll
The older school page describes a staff repository from joining to relieving, credentials/documents, staff attendance, leave tracking and access rights. Payroll processing, statutory payroll, recruiting and appraisal workflows are not publicly documented [16].

#### Transport
Transport management appeared in a third-party description of the former Advanced package, but current official pages do not explain routes, vehicles, GPS, stops or fee linkage. Treat it as legacy/uncorroborated rather than a current native module [17].

#### Library
No current official evidence of cataloging, circulation, barcode/RFID or library fines was found.

#### Hostel and boarding
Hostel management appeared in the former Advanced package reported by SoftwareFinder, but no current official details on rooms, wardens, meals or boarding fees were found [17].

#### Inventory and assets
No current public evidence of procurement, stock, fixed assets, purchase orders or asset depreciation was found.

#### Health and medical
No current public evidence of nurse/clinic, immunization, medical records or incident workflows was found.

#### Behavior, discipline and pastoral care
An independent listing mentions behavior monitoring, but Teachmint’s public product pages do not detail discipline cases, counseling, safeguarding or pastoral workflows [21].

#### Special education and safeguarding
Teachmint advertises age-appropriate, safe AI answers and Google Play data controls, but no public special-education plan/IEP, child-safeguarding case management or mandated-reporting module was verified [4] [7].

#### Alumni and fundraising
No current public evidence of alumni relations, donations, campaigns or fundraising management was found.

#### Analytics, dashboards and reporting
Current analytics cover classroom/student performance, teacher activity, device usage, homework, attendance and quiz insights. Legacy ERP pages described customizable stakeholder reports and 20-plus fee reports; CBSE Result Analyser accepts a board text file and exports performance reports to Excel [4] [16] [22].

#### AI features
EduAI provides AI Studio content, AI whiteboards, Ask Anything, lesson plans, quiz and homework generation, math/equation solving, summaries/class recaps, mindmaps, voice commands, OCR and text-to-speech. The privacy policy also names legacy GYD AI, lecture Q&A, EduBits, AI tutor courses and AI summaries/transcripts; it says AI features are optional, consent-gated and use Google to generate responses without sharing names, marks, attendance or original files [4] [12].

#### Government/state reporting and compliance
Teachmint historically advertised automated government-compliance reports and current content is mapped to NCERT; the site also offers a CBSE Result Analyser. I found no public Teachmint documentation verifying UDISE+, APAAR ID, CBSE/ICSE/state-board report-card templates, NEP 2020 Holistic Progress Cards, or higher-education NAAC/NBA/NIRF/AISHE reporting. CBSE’s own HPC guidance defines a multidimensional, cognitive/affective/socio-emotional/psychomotor card, which is more specific than Teachmint’s generic customizable report-card claim [19] [22] [23].

#### Mobile apps
Native Android and iOS delivery is verified. Android shows 10M+ downloads and a Nov. 5, 2026 update; iOS supports iPhone/iPad and lists in-app GYD and AI-tutor purchases. The current app includes live classes, recordings, notes, assignments, quizzes, chat, announcements, attendance and reports [7] [8].

#### Integrations and APIs
Google EDLA provides Play Store access; Teachmint’s panel FAQ names Google Meet, Zoom, YouTube and other LMS apps as usable third-party apps. The privacy policy documents YouTube Data API scopes. No public REST API, webhook documentation, marketplace, OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever or ClassLink certification was found [12] [14].

#### Security and privacy
Teachmint states ISO 27001 and ISO 9001 certification, encryption, firewalls, need-to-know access, India storage with possible international transfers, consent management and deletion/access rights. Google Play reports encryption in transit and deletion requests. No public SOC 2, FERPA, COPPA, GDPR certification or security-incident disclosure was found; DPDP Act readiness is not expressly claimed, so it remains unverified [1] [7] [12].

### Advanced and Distinctive Features

- **EduAI embedded in the board:** Produces curriculum/grade-aware lessons, quizzes, homework, summaries and explanations without leaving the classroom [4].
- **VisionX device management:** Centrally monitors classroom feeds, audio/screen activity, app access, OTA updates, alerts and device settings [4] [14].
- **Face-recognition attendance:** The panel camera can recognize verified student images and allow teacher corrections before submission [14].
- **Click X response clickers:** Student clickers provide 100% participation claims, live quiz responses and accuracy/attempt insights [14].
- **Google EDLA hardware:** Gives the panel Play Store/Google-services access with enterprise security controls [4] [14].
- **NCERT-mapped bilingual content:** Hindi/English notes, video, practice questions, offline content and topic-level grouping support Indian curricula [18].

### Pricing Details

No current public software price card or per-student/per-school ERP price was found; Teachmint’s pricing page is a demo/product page and the vendor asks buyers to engage sales. SoftwareFinder reports custom pricing, while Capterra displays a third-party $5 per user per year figure that is not confirmed by Teachmint and may reflect legacy software [5] [17].

The current public hardware catalog lists Teachmint X panels at INR 110,000 for 65-inch, INR 130,000 or INR 150,000 for 75-inch variants, and INR 170,000 for 86-inch, viewed 24 Nov 2026; each includes the Teachmint X ecosystem. The iOS app lists GYD Max at INR 259/month or INR 1,599/year, plus AI-tutor purchases of INR 100-199. Installation/training and 365-day support are advertised, but software implementation fees are not published [8] [14] [24].

### Integrations, Standards and Interoperability

Teachmint has practical device-level interoperability through Google EDLA, Play Store apps, Google Meet, Zoom and YouTube, plus a documented YouTube Data API use case. It does not publicly claim OneRoster, LTI, Ed-Fi, SIF, Clever or ClassLink certification; there is no public API reference, webhook guide, SSO/SCIM guide or marketplace. Legacy payment and accounting integrations such as UPI/Razorpay/Paytm/Tally were not verified in current documentation. This supports a basic-integrations score, not an open-standards score [12] [14] [17].

### Security, Privacy and Compliance

The About page claims ISO 27001 and Google EDLA certification. Teachmint’s privacy policy describes encryption, firewalls, restricted access, security monitoring, consent, deletion/access rights and India-based processing with possible cross-border transfers. Google Play states data is encrypted in transit and deletion can be requested [1] [7] [12].

There is no public evidence in the reviewed materials of SOC 2, FERPA, COPPA or GDPR certification, and no public incident disclosure was found. The policy references India’s Information Technology Act and Data Protection Rules but does not expressly say that Teachmint is DPDP Act-ready. Schools should therefore request a current DPA, subprocessor list, retention schedule, breach-notification terms, child-data controls and DPDP contractual commitments before procurement.

### Strengths

- Broad evolution from live teaching and LMS into a hardware-plus-cloud classroom ecosystem.
- Strong Indian-market fit through mobile-first delivery, Hindi/English content, NCERT mapping, CBSE result analysis and vernacular/global-language support.
- Distinctive, production AI features embedded in lesson preparation and classroom hardware.
- Native Android/iOS reach, cloud recordings and live-class continuity.
- Large dated adoption evidence: 4,000+ institutes and 10M+ users in 2021, plus a current 20M+ user claim.
- High independent ratings: G2 4.9/5 and Capterra 4.8/5, with reviewers commonly praising ease of use and breadth [5] [6] [9].

### Weaknesses and Common User Complaints

- Product scope is in transition: official pages say standalone admissions and fee management are no longer available, creating migration and procurement risk for schools expecting a classic ERP [15] [20].
- Public pricing is opaque; current software pricing, implementation fees and enterprise limits require a quote.
- Interoperability is weakly documented: no public standards certifications, open API, marketplace or verified Tally/payment-gateway connectors.
- Users report performance/stability problems, delayed or unresolved support, limited customization and constrained integrations; SoftwareFinder summarizes crashes/slow response, while Capterra notes confusing edits, rigid reports and support-ticket friction [5] [17].
- Apple reviewers cite broken UI/layout, test-upload friction and lack of dark mode; Google Play reviews cite AI-tutor limitations, missing library visibility, poll bugs and paid recordings [7] [8].
- Several requested India compliance functions—UDISE+, APAAR, NEP 2020 HPC, GST, state-board templates and NAAC/NBA/NIRF/AISHE—are not publicly verified.
